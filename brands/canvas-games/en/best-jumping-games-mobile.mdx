## Best jumping games on mobile

The best jumping games on mobile are the ones you can fail and restart with one thumb before a short break is over. If you searched for jumping games on a phone, you probably want a climb, not a tutorial and not a store category that also means runners, platformers, and anything with the word jump in the title.

This page sorts a few picks by mood. There is no single winner. It is also not another numbered list of Doodle Jump cousins. That list is already [games like Doodle Jump](/blog/games-like-doodle-jump). If you want the criteria behind "similar" rather than more names, [games similar to Doodle Jump](/blog/games-similar-to-doodle-jump) is the fairer scorecard. Here the job is narrower: which mood fits a few minutes, and which game to skip.

Those three are a filter we used, not a lab result. A vertical jumper usually means you move left and right while the character bounces or gets carried upward. An endless jumper is the same loop with no finish line: the page keeps going until you miss. A side-scrolling runner, the kind that scrolls the street toward you, is a different genre even when the store tag says jumping. Why the climb loop lasted is the subject of [endless jumping games on mobile](/blog/endless-jumping-games-mobile). This page stays with the head term, jumping games mobile, and then the moods.

## When you want the classic solo climb

**Doodle Jump 2**, from Lima Sky, is the straight answer if you want the platform bounce people mean by this genre. The [US App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/doodle-jump-2/id1542340137) and the [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.limasky.doodlejump2) both describe jumping up platforms, collecting stars, and unlocking characters and environments. The store text names a run of worlds (caves, desert, space, rain, a disco, a basketball update). I did not play every world for this article. What matters for the choice is the loop: a solo climb, platforms, a score, a restart.

Lima Sky also lists **Doodle Jump 2+** on Apple Arcade ([listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/doodle-jump-2/id6526460684)). If you already pay for Arcade, look there before you buy a second copy. I did not compare the Arcade build feature by feature with the store app, so treat them as the same family, not as proven twins.

If your search was specifically "games like Doodle Jump", stop here and use the cousin list linked above. This article stays at the broader query so you can pick a mood first.

## When the platforms feel slow

**Mega Jump**, from Get Set Games, is the pick when the classic bounce feels leisurely. The [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mega-jump/id370398167) still describes collecting coins, grabbing power-ups, and dodging monsters to climb. Early reviews described the upward motion as coming from the coins themselves: miss the chain and you fall. That is a different tension from landing on a platform. I re-checked that the iOS listing is still up. I did not re-check Google Play this week, and I am not quoting the old "millions of players" line on the store page as a current fact.

Play three runs before you decide the pace is yours. If you miss the readable platform, go back to Doodle Jump 2. Mega Jump is the wrong recommendation for someone who wants mastery of jump height. That mood is older than both of these apps.

## When you want a skill ceiling

**Icy Tower** is the reference for that mood, and it is a worse "just install it" answer than the two above. It began as PC freeware in the early 2000s. The idea is momentum: running builds speed, speed builds jump height, and chained jumps build a combo. On a phone, ports and clones vary by store and by year. I could not confirm one current official build that is on both iOS and Android under a single developer name. If you search the stores, read who published the app before you download the first result with that title.

If the combo fantasy is what you wanted from "best jumping games", and the phone port is a mess in your region, you have not failed the genre. You have hit the limit of this list. The solo platform climb in Doodle Jump 2 will feel easier on purpose.

## When you want other people in the climb

Players ask for multiplayer more often than the stores offer a good platform answer. **Doodle Jump Race** is real, and it is not the platform game with friends added. Lima Sky's [US App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/doodle-jump-race/id818669160) showed it as free when checked for this article, for iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV. The listing's own description is a four-player real-time race: tap to rise, avoid obstacles, the Doodler on a jetpack. Regional prices move, and I did not confirm an Android build.

That is a fair game if you want a sprint. It changes the question from "can I keep this run alive" to "can I arrive first". The longer split between racing and survival is in [Doodle Jump multiplayer](/blog/doodle-jump-multiplayer). Read that before you install Race expecting platforms.

## Frequently asked questions

### What counts as a jumping game on mobile?

Usually a vertical climber or an endless jumper: you move left and right, and you bounce on platforms or get carried upward. Runners that scroll sideways are a different genre, even when the store puts them in the same list. If the death is "I missed a platform" or "I missed the coin", you are in the right neighbourhood.

### Is Doodle Jump 2 the same as Doodle Jump Race?

No. Doodle Jump 2 is the solo platform climb. Doodle Jump Race, on the listing checked for this article, is a four-player tap-to-rise race. Same studio, different question.

### Do I need a controller?

No. The picks here are built for one thumb, with tilt or a tap where the game uses that. A controller is a different hobby.

### Are these games free?

The US App Store listing for Doodle Jump Race showed Free when checked in September 2026. Regional prices change, and I did not re-check the price of Doodle Jump 2 or Mega Jump. Inklets' own site says the iOS and Android apps are free, with a browser sample at [game.inklets.app](https://game.inklets.app). Read the store page before you pay for a bundle.

### Where does Inklets fit if I only have five minutes?

It fits if you want other people on the page and you are fine losing because the ink caught you, not because you missed a solo high score. It does not fit a quiet solo climb. Try the browser sample or the free app from [inklets.app](https://www.inklets.app), then come back to Doodle Jump 2 if what you missed was the classic bounce.
