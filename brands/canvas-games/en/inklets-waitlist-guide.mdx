## What game.inklets.app is for

The browser build at [game.inklets.app](https://game.inklets.app) is a **low-friction sample**. It helps when you want to see the inky page and jumping feel before any store trip, or when you want to send one link to a friend at a desk.

It is **not** a replacement for the planned mobile game. Full three-player matchmaking, the AI Inklet Generator, Sketchbook progression, and the control tuning we update against are all aimed at the iOS and Android build. The longer split is in [Inklets browser demo vs app](/blog/inklets-browser-demo-vs-app).

Use the browser sample if you want a one-minute vibe check on a laptop, or if you are comparing sketchbook climbers in [best jumping games on mobile](/blog/best-jumping-games-mobile). Join the waitlist if you want launch news, store links, and a shot at the first-100 skin.

## What the first 100 signups were promised

The published perk for early members is this: the first **100 confirmed** signups are promised a **launch-only collectible skin** that will not return to the store later. Rules we stated publicly still matter for anyone joining early:

- **Confirmed** counts, and one perk goes to each eligible signup under anti-abuse checks.
- **Order** follows confirmation time, not how fast you typed on the landing page.
- The skin is a **collectible**, not a stat boost, and not a gate to matchmaking.

We are a small indie studio in Zurich (Aurum Avis Labs). Keep your confirmation email if you joined early. Everyone else still gets the full game on release; the skin is a memento for the earliest confirmed signups.

- **Identical AI Generator previews** between the browser sample and every phone GPU.
- **Transfer of purchases from other games.** Inklets economies stand alone.
