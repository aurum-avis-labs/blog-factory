## What the waitlist was (and what still matters)

Before the apps went live, the waitlist at [inklets.app](https://www.inklets.app) was an email signup for launch news, store links, and occasional playtest notes. No charge, no APK sideload in official instructions.

The published perk for early members: the first **100 confirmed** signups were promised a **launch-only collectible skin** that would not return to the store later. Rules we stated publicly still matter for anyone who signed up early:

- **Confirmed** meant a completed double opt-in where your region required it.
- **Order** followed confirmation time, not how fast you typed on the landing page.
- **One perk per eligible signup** under anti-abuse checks.

We are a small Zurich-area studio (Aurum Avis Labs). If you joined before launch, keep your confirmation email. If you installed after release without ever signing up, you still play the full game; the skin was a memento for the earliest confirmed waitlist members, not a gate to matchmaking.
