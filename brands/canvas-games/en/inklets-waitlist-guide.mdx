The Inklets waitlist is how you hear about launch first, reserve a place in line for Summer 2026 iOS and Android release, and, if you are early enough, claim a launch-only skin that never returns to the store. ## What joining the Inklets waitlist does today

Waitlist membership is not a preorder charge in disguise. It is an email relationship plus early access to launch news as we lock dates, store links, and regional rollout order.

Practical outcomes for members:

- **Launch window email** before public social posts in most cases.
- **Store links** when Apple and Google listings go live.
- **Clear rules** for the first-100 collectible skin (below).
- **Occasional playtest or demo notes** when we have something safe to share; never spammy daily mail.

We are a small Zurich-area studio (Aurum Avis Labs). The waitlist is our main honest funnel while the app finishes certification and netcode hardening.

## Summer 2026 launch window (what is fixed vs flexible)

As of this writing, Inklets targets **Summer 2026** on **iOS and Android**, **free to download** at launch, with **competitive last-player-standing climbing** as the launch mode. Platforms and price are commitments; the exact week inside Summer is not.

Waitlist members should expect:

- A narrowing window message as soft launch regions firm up.
- Install instructions that mirror [download Inklets on iOS and Android](/blog/download-inklets-ios-android) once store URLs exist.

## First-100 skin: rules without hype

The first **100 confirmed** waitlist signups receive a **launch-only collectible skin** that we will **never draw again** after launch. It is not a power upgrade; it is a Sketchbook memory.

Fair rules we publish so nobody feels tricked:

- **Confirmed means confirmed.** If your region uses double opt-in, clicking the email link matters for counting.
- **Order is by confirmation timestamp**, not by typing speed on the landing page alone.
- **One perk per eligible signup** under our anti-abuse checks; throwaway domains may be disqualified.
- **Delivery** happens into your Sketchbook on day one you can log into the live game with the same email flow we announce at launch.
- **No resale market from us.** We are not selling the skin separately later.

The skin lives alongside other entries described in [inside the Inklets Sketchbook](/blog/inklets-sketchbook-guide). Rare pages mean more when scarcity is real.

## What the waitlist does not guarantee

Honesty keeps consideration posts useful:

- **Guaranteed day-one matchmaking density** in every time zone. We will seed lobbies, but niche genres need friends invited.
- **Feature-complete beta for every member.** Playtests may be limited and regional.
- **AI Generator identical previews** between demo and app on every device; mobile GPUs differ.
- **Transfer of unrelated game purchases.** Inklets economies stand alone.

For demo versus app expectations, see [Inklets browser demo vs app](/blog/inklets-browser-demo-vs-app).
