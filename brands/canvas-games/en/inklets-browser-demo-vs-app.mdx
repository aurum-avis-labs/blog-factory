Should you play Inklets in the browser at [game.inklets.app](https://game.inklets.app) or install the full game on iOS and Android? Both are live today, but they are not the same product shape. This comparison explains what the browser demo is good for, what the mobile app is built to deliver, and how to choose without wasting a session on the wrong version.

## Two doors into the same sketchbook fantasy

Inklets is a three-player sketchbook climber: shared page, wobbly platforms, rising black ink, last Inklet standing wins. The **browser demo** is a quick way to feel movement and tone on a desktop or laptop. The **mobile app** is the complete experience: online lobbies, progression, the AI Inklet Generator, and the Sketchbook collection that repaints your climbs.

If you already know you want the full loop, skip straight to [download Inklets on iOS and Android](/blog/download-inklets-ios-android). If you are still deciding, read on.

## What the browser demo at game.inklets.app does well

Open [game.inklets.app](https://game.inklets.app) in a modern browser when you want a **low-friction sample** without visiting an app store.

The demo shines for:

- **First impressions.** See the inky, doodle-style page and how jumping reads on screen.
- **Sharing a link.** Send one URL to a friend who wants to glance at the game before installing.
- **Keyboard or mouse play on a computer.** Useful if you are at a desk and your phone is not handy.

Treat the demo as a tasting portion, not the full menu. It exists to answer "does this sketchbook climber look and feel like my kind of chaos?" in a minute or two.

## What the iOS and Android app adds

The mobile app is where Inklets behaves like the product we ship and update. After you install from [inklets.app](https://www.inklets.app) (**Download on iOS** or **Download on Android**), you get the systems the demo does not replace.

### Real three-player online matches

The app matchmaking puts you in **lobbies with two other live players**. The social tension (who is highest, who is about to get swallowed, whether you detour for coins) depends on real opponents, not a solo sandbox.

### AI Inklet Generator and loadout

Describe a small character in a short prompt, generate a sketchbook-style climber, equip it, and jump into the next lobby. Base skins and earned unlocks sit in the same flow. That creation loop is a core reason to install, not an optional extra. Genre context lives in [AI character creator games](/blog/ai-character-creator-games).

### The Sketchbook and match rewards

Coins from risky mid-match detours feed the **Sketchbook**: backgrounds, platform styles, trinkets, monsters, stamps, and rare Inklets. Many rewards change how the page looks on future runs. The demo does not stand in for that collection meta. Details are in [inside the Inklets Sketchbook](/blog/inklets-sketchbook-guide).

### Mobile-first controls and session length

Inklets is tuned for one-thumb play and short races you can finish on a commute. The app assumes interruptions, spotty signal, and reopening between matches. Browser play on a laptop can be fun, but it is not the control scheme we optimize for daily play.

## When to use the browser demo

Choose **game.inklets.app** when:

- You are on a computer and want to see the art direction and climbing feel before you commit to an install.
- You are recommending Inklets to someone who prefers clicking a link over opening a store.
- You are comparing Inklets to other vertical jumpers and only need a sensory check.

Stop at the demo if your goal is **long-term play, custom characters, or competitive three-player survival**. Those goals need the app.

## When to install the mobile app

Choose **iOS or Android** when:

- You want **online last-one-standing matches** with two other climbers and a rising ink storm.
- You care about **creating and equipping** your own Inklet, not just watching a default climber.
- You want **coins, unlocks, and Sketchbook loadouts** that change future pages.

## A simple decision path

1. Curious but not ready to install? Spend five minutes on [game.inklets.app](https://game.inklets.app).
2. Convinced you like the sketchbook climb? Open [inklets.app](https://www.inklets.app) and download for your platform.
3. Installed? Queue a lobby, survive the ink once or twice, then try generating an Inklet before your next run.
