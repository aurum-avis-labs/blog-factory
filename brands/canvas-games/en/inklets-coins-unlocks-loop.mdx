## Where coins appear in a match

Coin clusters spawn beside the column most players climb while ink rises and rivals jostle for height.

Design intent:

- **Teach risk in context.** You feel the pull when two other Inklets are next to you.
- **Make greed social.** A detour that works alone can fail when a chaos event knocks the pack.
- **Keep sessions short.** You are making three-minute bets, not farming a map for an hour.

If match flow is still fuzzy, read [how an Inklets match works](/blog/how-inklets-matches-work) before you plan coin routes. Pair that with [how to get better at vertical jumpers](/blog/how-to-get-better-vertical-jumpers): land safe first, detour second.

## What happens to coins after the match

Post-match, coins bank toward unlocks in the Sketchbook. Exact price tables can change, but categories stay stable:

- **Page backgrounds** for new paper skies and notebook moods.
- **Platform styles** so ledges read differently mid-climb.
- **Skins and Inklets** you equip from the collection.
- **Trinkets** in dedicated loadout slots at launch.
- **Monsters, stamps, stickers** for sketchbook personality.
- **Rare Inklets** at lower frequency for long-term collectors.

Unlocks are drawings you keep, not consumables that vanish after one tap. The fantasy is pages filling in, not meters resetting every week unless patch notes say otherwise.

## Loadout: spending is not separate from playing

From the main menu, loadout slots tie earnings to the next race:

- **Skin / Inklet** you climb as.
- **Trinket** slots (two at launch; see the Sketchbook guide).
- **Page** background for this session.
- **Platform** style for this session.

Changing loadout stays fast because Inklets targets short sessions. The Sketchbook is for browsing when you want to; it should not feel like paperwork between climbs.

## Rare entries and seasonal flair

Some Sketchbook items are rare by design: lower drop or milestone pacing for special Inklets and stamps, or limited-time page art when we run a small event. We state scarcity in patch notes or store copy, not behind hidden odds.

## What the economy is not trying to be

- **Not a second job.** Sessions target minutes; banking coins should not need a daily chart unless live ops add one later and say so plainly.
- **Not pay-to-win.** Cosmetics and readability, not hidden damage boosts.
- **Not a battle pass clone by default.** If a season structure ships, we will describe it at launch.

Compare feel in the browser demo in [Inklets browser demo vs app](/blog/inklets-browser-demo-vs-app). The demo is a taste; the full release will carry the live economy.

## Loop psychology: why coins belong in survival multiplayer

In solo jumpers, coin detours only fight your high score. In Inklets, detours fight **relative height** while **ink** threatens the pack. Greed, rivals and the rising eliminator sit in the same read.

1. The match tempts you sideways.
2. You choose a risk you can still read.
3. Coins bank after the round even when you do not win; wins may speed milestones depending on tuning.
4. Unlocks change tomorrow's page art.
5. Friends spot the new platform style in the next lobby.

For rising pressure in the genre, see [rising floor mechanics in mobile climbers](/blog/rising-floor-climbing-games).

## Practical player habits (no grind guide)

- **One unlock category per week** is enough; you do not need the full catalog on night one.
- **Detour when margin exists:** ink low, rivals on the safe line, next platform wide.
- **Stop chasing clusters** after two failed detours in the same match; frustration breaks reads.
- **Show friends loadout changes**; social feedback beats spreadsheet optimisation.
