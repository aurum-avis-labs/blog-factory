DO-4-ME works in three moves: you post what you need and when, you choose a verified student and agree the details, they do the task and you pay them directly. If you have never posted a task online, the useful question is what actually happens, not whether "platforms" work in the abstract.

This is for someone in Zürich with one everyday job: an errand, boxes, a dog, homework help, cleanup after a gathering. It is a local task help flow. It is not a contract and not a staff hire.


## Before you post: decide if this task belongs here

A good first listing is legal, reasonable, and explainable in a few sentences. Groceries, a pharmacy bag, laundry, tidying, moving help, a few hours at an event, device setup while you sit there, simple assembly. DO-4-ME's own list is everyday work a student can do with a clear brief.

Skip DO-4-ME when:

- the work needs a licence (electrical, gas, and similar trades),
- you would have to share passwords, banking, or private records,
- you want someone employed for the semester,
- or the "task" is a decision only you can make.

If you are unsure the shape fits, use [the checklist](/en/blog/is-a-student-based-task-platform-right-for-you-a-simple-checklist) before you write the listing.

## Step 1: Describe the task and pick a time

<Image src={img2} alt="Simple task description on screen" width={500} quality={80} class="w-full" />

You do not fill out a long job spec. You say what needs doing, where in the city, and when you need the help.

Write it so a student who does not know your flat can follow it:

- the outcome (the bags in the kitchen, the boxes on the first floor, the dog back by 16:00),
- the address or a precise meeting point,
- what they should bring, or what you will leave out (keys, a harness, a trolley),
- what is out of scope.

If you can say that in a short paragraph, you are ready. If the window is tight, mark the task as time-sensitive. DO-4-ME says students who are free sooner see those first.

Posting is free. You can post more than one listing. There is no signup fee and no commission added by the platform.


## Step 2: Students nearby see it, and you choose

<Image src={img3} alt="Task visible to local students" width={500} quality={80} class="w-full" />

After you post, students looking for short, flexible work can see the task. Because the focus is Zürich, the replies are from people who can actually reach you.

You review profiles of verified students. Verification is a university email plus DO-4-ME's vetting, not a feeling. Profiles can show reviews from other users. Read them, and do not treat a blog as the source of a score. This page will not invent one.

DO-4-ME says most tasks get a response within a few hours. That is their claim about replies. Your afternoon can still be too short. If nobody suitable answers, do the job yourself or move the time. There is no obligation to take the first reply.


## Step 3: Chat, then agree what "done" means and what you will pay

<Image src={img4} alt="Message exchange between task poster and helper" width={500} quality={80} class="w-full" />

When someone is interested, you chat before you confirm. Use the chat for:

- the exact timing,
- anything awkward about the building (no lift, a code, a dog that pulls),
- the amount you will pay them directly.

The point is alignment, not a negotiation sport. Payment is between you and the student. DO-4-ME does not take a cut. People often pay cash or with Twint after the work is finished. Agree that in the chat so nobody is guessing at the door.

If the chat feels wrong, stop. You have not hired anyone until you confirm.


## Step 4: They do the task

At the time you agreed, the student does the job you described. Errands get run. Boxes get carried. The dog gets walked. Homework gets a defined session. An event gets another pair of hands.

You should not have to stand over them and rewrite the task. If you must, the listing was vague. The value is that this hour is no longer inside your own calendar.

Help with errands only works if you are reachable for a real question (which entrance, which bag). It fails if you disappear and expect mind-reading.


## Step 5: Pay them, close it, and stop

When the task is finished, you pay the student directly. Then there is nothing else to manage.

There is:

- no subscription,
- no long-term commitment,
- no requirement to post again.

You use DO-4-ME when you need help, and you leave it when you do not. That is the design of a local task help platform for ordinary weeks.

If something goes wrong, DO-4-ME says you can contact them and they will work with both sides on a fair outcome. Do that while the facts are fresh. Do not wait and turn it into a story you cannot reconstruct.

## What the first task is for

People delay because posting feels like a lifestyle change. The first task answers a smaller set of questions: whether you can write a clear brief, whether a student nearby responds, and whether the chat is enough for you.

Pick something low drama. A pickup. A pile of boxes with a friend still in the flat. A dog walk on a route you know. After that you will know if you want a second listing. You might not. Both results are fine.
