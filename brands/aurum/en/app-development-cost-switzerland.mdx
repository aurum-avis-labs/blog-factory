## App development cost in Switzerland

**App development cost in Switzerland** is the sum of what you pay to have software built, what you pay to reach the App Store and Google Play, and what you keep paying to host, integrate, and maintain it. A single franc total without a written scope is marketing, not a budget. For a Swiss SME turning internal know-how into a customer app, a portal, or a small SaaS product, the useful question is which buckets belong in year one and which ones continue after launch.

This page is about **commissioned app work and production run costs**, not the narrower question of how much an early MVP validation budget should be. That comparison, partner types, and what «MVP» meant in each quote, is in [MVP cost in Switzerland](/blog/mvp-cost-switzerland). Here the focus is the full app path: build, stores, maintenance, and running bills.

## App development cost in Switzerland: what the build quote actually buys

Partners price from **scope**, not from the word "app". Roles, integrations, and admin surfaces matter more than screen count. A **production bar** (authentication, logging, backups, deploy paths) costs more than a demo on one laptop. Fixed price and time and materials shift who pays when the spec moves; see [fixed price vs time and materials](/blog/fixed-price-vs-time-and-materials) and [paying a partner in equity](/blog/paying-a-partner-in-equity). Quotes often omit **project management**, QA, migration, documentation, and first store submission. Treat unnamed work as zero until it is priced.

## Store path: accounts, review, and calendar time

Publishing is not a one-line item at the end of development.

**Apple.** Organisation enrollment needs a legal entity, a D-U-N-S number, authority to bind the company, a work email on the company domain, and a public site; the seller name on the store is that entity ([Apple enrollment](https://developer.apple.com/support/enrollment/)). The programme membership is stated as **99 USD** per year on the same page (Enterprise is listed separately). Review looks at **minimum functionality**: more than a repackaged website; pure marketing shells fail ([guideline 4.2](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)). A wrapped web app still has to behave like an app.

**Google Play.** For **personal** developer accounts created after 13 November 2023, production access requires a closed test with at least **12 testers** opted in **continuously for 14 days** ([Play Console help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en)). That is recruitment, reminders, and calendar time, even when the build is ready. Organisation accounts may differ; check the console for your account type before you promise a launch date.

Each rejection or policy change can mean another build and another review cycle. Budget **people time**, not only store fees.

## Running costs after go-live

Once users sign in, cost moves from project to **operations**.

**Hosting and data** on Azure or a managed database recur monthly; Supabase can use Zurich (`eu-central-2`) ([regions](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/regions)). **APIs** for maps, email, SMS, identity, or AI bill per use. **Payments:** TWINT via Stripe ([Stripe TWINT](https://stripe.com/en-ch/payment-method/twint)) is integration plus transaction fees. **Analytics** storage is ongoing; Swiss cookie rules differ for profiling and cross-site tracking. Turnover above **CHF 100'000** triggers VAT duty; this is not ESTV advice.

## Maintenance: the line item missing from the first quote

An app is not «finished» at v1.0.

OS updates, dependency patches, and store policy changes need scheduled work. Maintenance is usually a **retainer** or change orders. Without an owner, the app drifts until login breaks or a store build is rejected.

Cash next to the build matters too. A GmbH needs at least **CHF 20'000** share capital fully paid in at formation ([KMU guidance](https://www.kmu.admin.ch/de/gmbh-haftung-stammkapital-gruendung)). A trademark in one class is listed at **CHF 450** for ten years on the IGE FAQ (recheck before you file). Those are not development hours, but they sit on the same treasury plan if you trade as a company and care about the name on the store.

## What the data still will not prove

Paying for a full build does not show product-market fit. It shows you can ship. Store approval does not show retention. A polished UI does not show willingness to pay. Code ownership still needs a written assignment for external authors ([Art. 17 URG](https://www.rentschpartner.ch/ict-law/schutz-von-software), summarised on the linked page). None of that replaces a validation phase when the risk is still «do strangers want this».

<div class="blog-faq">
  <h3>Is the cheapest quote the cheapest outcome?</h3>
  <p>Not when the quote excludes stores, hosting, or maintenance and you buy those later under time pressure. The expensive mistake is often rebuilding scope or architecture after a false saving on the first contract.</p>
</div>
