## Digitise incoming paper mail

<p class="blog-lede">You digitise incoming paper mail by getting each envelope into one PDF the same day, noting what it is and who must act, and only then deciding whether the paper stays. The scan is not the filing system.</p>

This is for Swiss firms whose post is still opened by whoever is at the desk: invoices, contracts, authority letters, insurance, and a share of advertising. Supplier invoices that software already receives by email are a narrower problem, described in [invoice capture: what software misses](/prozess-check/blog/invoice-capture-what-software-misses). A contract that has to be watched for a notice date, once you hold the PDF, belongs in [track contract notice periods and renewals](/prozess-check/blog/contract-deadlines).

<ol class="blog-steps">
  <li data-step="1"><h3>One scan path</h3><p>Either someone in the office scans the opened mail, or a mail service returns PDFs. Not both for the same envelope.</p></li>
  <li data-step="2"><h3>What it is</h3><p>Invoice, contract, authority, insurance, or other. A model may propose the type. A person confirms anything that is unclear.</p></li>
  <li data-step="3"><h3>Who acts</h3><p>A person or a case number. Unassigned PDFs are a queue, not a finished archive.</p></li>
  <li data-step="4"><h3>The paper</h3><p>Keep, or destroy only when your fiduciary agrees that a readable copy is enough for that kind of document.</p></li>
</ol>

## Scan in the office, or have the mail scanned for you

If one person already opens the post and the volume is a small daily pile, an office scanner is enough. Open, remove staples, scan to a single folder named by date, and do not leave the paper on a sideboard "for later". The later pile is how a payment deadline sits unseen.

If the office is often empty, or the pile is a daily hour, a service that scans business mail can take that step. Swiss Post's ePost ScanningService scans incoming business mail and provides it as PDF for further handling. The Post's own description uses a digitised invoice or contract moved into accounting or ERP software so a workflow can start ([Swiss Post](https://www.post.ch/de/geschaeftsloesungen/posteingang)). Read the current terms before you sign: the page we checked does not state the price, and it does not say whether the paper original is returned or destroyed. Ask that in the offer. Two scan paths for the same address create duplicates.

Advertising and obvious circulars need no PDF. Agree a short list of what is thrown away unopened, and have the same person apply it. Everything else is scanned, including envelopes that look routine. The letter you skip is the one with a deadline.

## Assign a person or a case, do not only store the PDF

A folder of "scans 2027" is not an inbox. Each item needs a type and an owner.

| Type | Usual next step |
| --- | --- |
| Supplier invoice | Into the capture path your accounting software already uses, then approval |
| Contract or policy | To whoever watches notice dates, with the PDF linked from the register |
| Authority or tax | To the person who answers, and the paper kept until that person says otherwise |
| Customer letter | To the case or the account manager |
| Unclear | Stays in a daily queue until someone names it |

A model can propose sender, date and type from the first page. It fails on handwriting, on a letter that refers to an earlier case, and on two documents in one envelope. Those rows are marked, not guessed. The person who knows the customers spends time on the marked rows. The rest are a short confirm.

Do not email the PDF to three people "for info". One owner is accountable. Others get a copy only when the owner asks.

## What happens to the paper original

Books and accounting vouchers are kept for ten years, starting at the end of the financial year. The annual report and the audit report are kept in writing and signed. Books and vouchers may instead be kept electronically if they still match the underlying transactions and can be made readable again at any time ([Art. 958f OR](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/27/317_321_377/de)). The federal SME portal explains the ordinance that sits under that article: an unmodifiable carrier has fewer extra conditions, while a normal disk or a cloud needs a way to show the file was not changed, a timestamp, and logs, and the records have to be inspectable within a reasonable time until the ten years are up ([kmu.admin.ch](https://www.kmu.admin.ch/de/elektronische-aufbewahrung-der-geschaeftsbuecher)). A PDF in a shared drive is not automatically that archive.

Until that answer exists, keep non-advertising paper in a box labelled by month, next to the PDF. The box is provisional. That way you can undo a bad scan. Once retention is settled, the box shrinks to what you must still hold.

<div class="blog-faq">
  <h3>Can I throw away the original after scanning?</h3>
  <p>Sometimes, for certain vouchers, if your fiduciary accepts a readable digital copy for retention and you meet the rules for electronic archiving. Signed reports, some contracts and authority mail often still need paper. Settle the categories in writing once before you shred a large pile.</p>
</div>

## Who should leave the pile physical

Leave it physical if you receive a few letters a week and the same person opens them the day they arrive. A scanner subscription will not beat a tray.

Wait if you have no place the PDF will be read. Scanning into a folder nobody opens is a second archive of the same neglect. Assign an owner for the daily queue before you buy a service.

Do not start with a new document system. The aim is one PDF, one type, one person. Names and long-term folders are a later tidy-up, once this inbound step is boring.

## A made-up example: the morning tray

A made-up example: fifteen people, not a client. Twenty envelopes on a weekday. The office lead opens them beside the scanner. Ads go in the bin; the rest become one PDF per envelope in the day's folder. A list proposes type and recipient. Twelve lines confirmed in minutes; three invoices go into the existing capture mailbox, two contracts are linked from the notice register. One cantonal letter stays paper and goes to the person who signs tax replies. Two envelopes stay unclear because the model read the letterhead and missed the case number in the second paragraph. Someone rereads them on screen and assigns them before lunch. The month's paper waits in a box until the fiduciary names which categories the PDF replaces. The gain is a deadline not buried under a magazine.
