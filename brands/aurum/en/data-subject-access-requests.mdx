## Answer a data subject access request under the Swiss FADP

<p class="blog-lede">You answer a data subject access request under the Swiss FADP by logging receipt, matching the person, searching the places your firm actually uses, and replying in time with what the Act requires.</p>

The usual period is 30 days from receipt, unless statute or ordinance sets something different for your case. On each request, check [Art. 25 para. 7 FADP](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de) and the clock in [Art. 18 DPO](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/568/de). Hiring records, marketing tools and accounting are all places data may sit. The answer is a document a person checks.

For owners and office leads in a small Swiss firm who receive mail such as "what data do you hold about me". Applicant files: [screening job applications with AI](/prozess-check/blog/screen-job-applications-ai). Refusal, redaction or a cost contribution needs your adviser.

## What the answer has to contain

Any person may ask whether you process personal data about them. The reply makes the processing transparent. It includes the controller's identity and contact, the personal data, purpose, retention or criteria, origin if you did not collect from the person, any solely automated decision and its logic, and recipients or categories ([Art. 25 FADP](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de)).

If a supplier processes for you, you must still answer (Art. 25 para. 4). The right cannot be waived in advance. Information is free unless the Act allows otherwise (para. 6: Federal Council exceptions where effort is disproportionate).

The ordinance sets form and time. Request in writing, oral if you agree. Answer in writing in the form the data already have, or orally with consent. Electronic is allowed ([Art. 16 DPO](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/568/de)). If you cannot meet 30 days, you name the later date. Refusal, restriction or deferral goes out inside the same period ([Art. 18 DPO](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/568/de)).

On retention: name a duration when you know it. Many firms point invoices at bookkeeping periods. Marketing consent or complaints may have shorter periods. Write "until the matter is closed" only if you actually keep data that way.

Include automated decisions only if you make them. A simple rule in accounting is not necessarily a "profiling" decision under the Act. When unsure, ask the adviser instead of inventing a long technical explanation.

## Where you look

A complete answer fails if you only search the inbox of whoever read the request. List the systems your firm uses:

- Shared mailbox and mailboxes of anyone who writes to customers
- CRM or the spreadsheet that stands in for one
- Accounting: invoices, debtors, creditors
- Shared drives and personnel or applicant folders
- Tools on the [allow list](/prozess-check/blog/approve-ai-tools-allow-list), including AI accounts where someone pasted a name

A search helper lists hits. It does not decide whether a hit is the same person. A person confirms the list before copying into the reply. If an export is incomplete, say what you searched and what you could not open.

Ask processors who handle data for you whether they hold copies that belong in your answer. The clock runs for you even when a supplier is slow. Plan that chase in the first days.

## What not to attach

Internal notes about the person, staff opinions, or team arguments are not "personal data about the data subject" when they only concern the firm. A person still checks the draft before it goes out. Data about other people (a competitor in the same email, colleagues named in minutes) you redact or separate when your adviser says so for that case.

## Identity without a new dossier

The articles cited do not require a passport scan for every request. You still must not send the file to an unverified address. If the request comes from the customer's usual email and matches the file, that is often enough for small correspondence. A new address or someone acting for the person: ask your adviser what to request. Identity copies are sensitive. Delete them after the check unless your adviser names different retention.

## Cost, refusal, ten-day pause

Where effort is disproportionate, the ordinance allows a contribution toward cost. State the amount before you answer. If the person does not confirm within ten days, the request is treated as withdrawn without cost, and the 30-day period starts after that pause ([Art. 19 DPO](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/568/de)). The Act does not name a franc figure for "disproportionate". Do not invent a fee. For a normal customer file in a small firm, you usually answer without charge.

Art. 26 FADP allows refusal, restriction or deferral in defined cases, including professional secrecy. You still communicate within the period. Which case applies is for the adviser.

## A made-up example

A made-up example: a firm with 12 people. On Tuesday a customer writes from the invoice address: what data, for what. Day one is logged. That afternoon someone searches the shared mailbox, order spreadsheet, accounting and customer folder. An AI account on the allow list is checked because a complaint was pasted there in March.

The draft lists controller and contact, customer record, invoices, complaint thread, purpose, retention notes, and that no automated decision was made. The owner removes an internal staff note that is not data about the customer and sends on day 12. The log stores date, places searched and a copy of the reply. Firm and numbers are invented.

## Who should not build a workflow yet

If you have never had a request and hold little beyond invoices at your fiduciary, write the search list on one page. A workflow pays when requests repeat or data are scattered.

Do not promise a 48-hour answer in your privacy notice if you cannot keep it. When time is tight, the later date under Art. 18 DPO is the path the ordinance describes.

Repeat requests from the same person may need extra checks. Whether a request is abusive is for the adviser. As a firm you can still use the same search list and log each time so nothing is forgotten.
