## Answer a data subject access request under the Swiss FADP

You answer a data subject access request under the Swiss FADP by recording the day it arrived, making sure you are writing to the right person, finding the personal data you actually hold, and sending what the Act lists, in time. The usual period is 30 days from receipt. The hiring decision, the marketing tool and the accounting program are all places the data may sit. The answer is a document a person checks. It is not a dump of every folder you can open.

This is for owners and office leads in a small Swiss firm who receive a message such as "please tell me what data you hold about me". Which AI tools are allowed to see customer data is a different page: [ChatGPT and customer data under the Swiss FADP](/blog/chatgpt-customer-data-swiss-dsg). Applicant files, if you hire, are one more place to look, described in [screening job applications with AI](/blog/screen-job-applications-ai). Nothing here replaces your adviser when you want to refuse, redact, or charge a contribution.

## What the answer has to contain

Any person may ask whether you process personal data about them. The reply gives what they need to exercise their rights and to see that processing is transparent. In every case it includes the controller's identity and contact details, the personal data themselves, the purpose, how long you keep them or the criteria you use if you cannot name a duration, the available information on where the data came from if you did not collect them from the person, any exclusively automated decision and the logic behind it, and the recipients or categories of recipients ([Art. 25 FADP](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de)).

If a supplier processes data for you, you are still the one who must answer (Art. 25 para. 4). The right cannot be waived in advance. The information is free of charge. The Federal Council may allow exceptions where the effort is disproportionate (para. 6). The Act says the information is provided as a rule within 30 days (para. 7).

The ordinance is more precise on the clock. The request is in writing, and it may be oral if you agree. You may answer in writing, in the form the data already have, or orally if the person agrees. Electronic is allowed ([Art. 16 DPO](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/568/de)). The 30 days run from receipt. If you cannot meet them, you tell the person and you name the later date. A refusal, a restriction or a deferral is communicated inside the same period ([Art. 18 DPO](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/568/de)).

## Where you look, and where people forget

A complete answer is impossible if you only search the inbox of the person who read the request. Write down the places your firm actually uses, and look in each one that could hold this person:

- The shared mailbox and the mailbox of anyone who writes to customers
- The CRM or the spreadsheet that stands in for one
- Accounting: invoices, debtors, creditors
- Shared drives and the personnel or applicant folder, if the person worked for you or applied
- Tools on the [allow list](/blog/approve-ai-tools-allow-list) that received a copy, including an AI account if someone pasted the name into it

A search helper can list hits. It cannot decide that a hit is the same person, or that a namesake is not. A person confirms the list before anything is copied into the reply. If a tool's export is incomplete, say what you searched and what you could not open. Silence about a system you use is how the answer becomes wrong.

## Identity, without collecting a new dossier

The ordinance does not, in the articles above, tell you to demand a passport scan for every request. Sending the file to an unverified address is still a disclosure to the wrong person. If the request comes from the email address you already use with this customer, and the content matches the file, that is often enough for a small correspondence. If the request comes from a new address, or from someone claiming to act for the person, pause and ask your adviser what to ask for. A copy of an identity document is itself sensitive. Do not leave it in the same folder as the answer for years. Delete it when the check is done, unless your adviser says a different retention applies.

## Cost, refusal, and the ten-day pause

Where the effort is disproportionate, the ordinance allows you to ask for a contribution toward the cost. You state the amount before you answer. If the person does not confirm within ten days, the request is treated as withdrawn without cost, and the 30-day period starts only after that ten-day reflection time ([Art. 19 DPO](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/568/de)). There is no figure in that article for what "disproportionate" costs in francs. Do not invent a fee. For a normal customer file in a small firm, the straightforward path is to answer without a charge.

Article 26 of the Act allows refusal, restriction or deferral in defined cases, including where a formal statute provides for it in order to protect a professional secret ([Art. 26 FADP](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de)). If you think one of those cases applies, the ordinance still expects you to say so within the 30 days. Which case applies is the adviser's work. Do not answer with "we cannot, data protection" and no more.

## A made-up example: one email on a Tuesday

A made-up example: a workshop with 12 people, not a client. On Tuesday a customer writes from the address on the invoices: what data do you hold, and for what. The office logs Tuesday as day one. The same afternoon someone searches the shared mailbox, the order spreadsheet, the accounting program and the drive folder for that customer. An AI account is on the allow list. They check it too, because a colleague had pasted a complaint there in March. The draft lists: the firm's name and address as controller, the contact details, the customer record, the invoices, the complaint thread, the purpose (orders and payment), a retention note that points at the bookkeeping period for the invoices and a shorter period for the complaint once the matter is closed, and that no automated decision was made. The owner reads it, removes an internal note about a staff conflict that is not data about the customer, and sends the answer on day 12. The log stores the date, the places searched, and a copy of what was sent.

The 30 days were not close. The point of the log is the next request, when the person who searched this time is away.

## Who should not build a workflow yet

If you have never had a request and you hold little personal data beyond invoices your fiduciary already stores, write the search list on one page and stop. A workflow pays when requests repeat or when the data are scattered.

Do not promise a 48-hour answer in your privacy notice if you cannot keep it. The statute gives 30 days, and a late honest answer with a named new date is the path the ordinance describes when 30 days are not enough.
