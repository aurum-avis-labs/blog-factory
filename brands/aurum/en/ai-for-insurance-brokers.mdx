## AI for Swiss insurance brokers

AI for a Swiss insurance broker belongs in the office around the advice. The repeating work is a quote request sent to several insurers from one client need, a comparison laid out so you can read it, the chase before a renewal, and a claims file that is complete before you take a position. The recommendation, and the information the client must receive before a contract, stay with a person and on the record. This is practical orientation, not regulatory advice and not insurance advice. [FINMA's pages](https://www.finma.ch/de/bewilligung/versicherungsvermittlung/) are the source for the duties below.

This is for the owner of a small independent brokerage, untied intermediation, a few people, client files in a broker system or in folders. It is not written for staff who sell only one insurer's products. FINMA states that tied intermediaries are generally no longer entered in its register. Several duties below still apply to intermediaries in general. The register, the remuneration disclosure and the annual figures are stated for the untied activity.

## What stays with a person, and in the file

[FINMA's duties page](https://www.finma.ch/de/ueberwachung/versicherungsvermittler/pflichten-fuer-versicherungsvermittlerinnen-und--vermittler/) is the list to keep beside any automation.

<ul class="blog-facts">
  <li><span class="blog-kicker">Register</span><strong>Untied</strong><p>To act untied you are entered in FINMA's public register. Staff who intermediate must be entered too. Unregistered activity is unauthorised.</p></li>
  <li><span class="blog-kicker">Before the contract</span><strong>Art. 45 VAG</strong><p>Information goes to the client in an understandable form before the contract. Untied brokers also disclose remuneration from insurers or third parties.</p></li>
  <li><span class="blog-kicker">Conflicts</span><strong>Art. 45a VAG</strong><p>Organise so conflicts are avoided or, if that fails, disclosed before the contract. You cannot be tied and untied at the same time.</p></li>
</ul>

The same page points to qualified life insurance as a line with further duties of its own. Do not let a general renewal letter stand in for those. The page also says the firm keeps internal rules and an organisation that actually meets the duties. A workflow that nobody can describe does not meet that test.

One limit is easy to miss. FINMA asks untied intermediaries to give advance notice of a material cyber incident within 24 hours, and the report within 72 hours. A mail rule that files "FINMA" into a quiet folder can hide that. A person sends it.

Where client data sits when a tool reads a policy is a separate question, in [does AI have to run in Switzerland](/prozess-check/blog/ai-data-location-switzerland). Policies, claims and health questions do not go into a free chat account.

## Annual figures, the file, and what a tool cannot replace

Untied intermediaries report annual figures to FINMA. Deadlines and the form are on the same duties page. An October diary entry is often not enough: the numbers come from accounts and from policy statistics, and someone must check they match the register entry. AI can prepare tables. Submission and responsibility stay with the broker.

The file must show what the client received before the contract and what you recommended. If a tool drafts a comparison, store the result with a date, the insurers' source PDFs, and the version you sent. "The AI wrote it" is not a file note. Who approved the draft belongs in the file.

## The office work that is still done by hand

The broker system stores the client, the policies and often the renewal date. The gap is everything that arrives as a PDF from an insurer, a mail from the client, or a spreadsheet because the system will not hold a comparison.

### Quote requests to several insurers

A client needs cover: contents, fleet, liability. You ask several insurers, often with the same facts typed again into each portal or each email. The answers come back in different layouts. Structuring the ask, sending it, and lining the answers up is office work. Which insurer you recommend is the advice.

### A comparison the client can be shown

A comparison is a table of covers, exclusions, premiums and gaps, drawn from the answers, with the source PDF kept beside each row. A workflow can propose rows when the answers are clear. It does not decide that two clauses mean the same thing when the wording differs. It does not drop an exclusion because the cell was hard to fill. The note you send the client is written or checked by the broker.

### Renewals

Renewals cluster. The work is to see which policies end, which facts you no longer have, and to chase those facts before you ask the insurer. A workflow can list dates from the file, draft the chase, and attach last year's schedule. Whether the cover is still right stays with you. A renewal that goes out silent because "it always renews" misses changes in the client's business.

### A claims file

The client writes, often upset, with photos and a date. The insurer asks for a list. Someone collects what is there and sees what is missing. A workflow can file the mail on the policy, list the insurer's questions against the documents you hold, and draft a status line. Whether the claim is notified in time and what you advise the client to say stay with the broker.

## Where to start

Count a normal month: quote requests you typed more than once, renewal chases, claims mails that waited for a document. Use the four questions in [which process to automate first](/prozess-check/blog/which-process-to-automate-first): it repeats, it hurts, it has a clear end, and the data is already there. If the only saving is to let a tool write the recommendation, do not start.

  <h3>Can ChatGPT inform the client?</h3>
  <p>Not as a substitute for your duty. Art. 45 VAG requires understandable information before the contract. A draft can help if you check content, disclosure and sending, and record that in the file.</p>
