## AI for Swiss insurance brokers

AI for a Swiss insurance broker belongs in the office around the advice, not in the advice. The repeating work is a quote request sent to several insurers from one client need, a comparison laid out so you can read it, the chase before a renewal, and a claims file that is complete before you take a position. The recommendation, and the information the client must receive before a contract, stay with a person and on the record. This is practical orientation, not regulatory advice and not insurance advice. [FINMA's pages](https://www.finma.ch/de/bewilligung/versicherungsvermittlung/) are the source for the duties below.

This is for the owner of a small independent brokerage, untied intermediation, a few people, client files in a broker system or in folders. It is not written for staff who sell only one insurer's products. FINMA states that tied intermediaries are generally no longer entered in its register. Several duties below still apply to intermediaries in general. The register, the remuneration disclosure and the annual figures are stated for the untied activity.

## What stays with a person, and in the file

[FINMA's duties page](https://www.finma.ch/de/ueberwachung/versicherungsvermittler/pflichten-fuer-versicherungsvermittlerinnen-und--vermittler/) is the list to keep beside any automation.

<ul class="blog-facts">
  <li><span class="blog-kicker">Register</span><strong>Untied</strong><p>To act untied you are entered in FINMA's public register. Staff who intermediate must be entered too. Unregistered activity is unauthorised.</p></li>
  <li><span class="blog-kicker">Before the contract</span><strong>Art. 45 VAG</strong><p>Information goes to the client in an understandable form before the contract. Untied brokers also disclose remuneration from insurers or third parties.</p></li>
  <li><span class="blog-kicker">Conflicts</span><strong>Art. 45a VAG</strong><p>Organise so conflicts are avoided or, if that fails, disclosed before the contract. You cannot be tied and untied at the same time.</p></li>
</ul>

The same page points to qualified life insurance as a line with further duties of its own. Do not let a general renewal letter stand in for those. And the page says the firm keeps internal rules and an organisation that actually meets the duties. A workflow that nobody can describe is not that organisation.

One limit is easy to miss. FINMA asks untied intermediaries to give advance notice of a material cyber incident within 24 hours, and the report within 72 hours. A mail rule that files "FINMA" into a quiet folder can hide that. A person sends it.

Where client data sits when a tool reads a policy is a separate question, in [does AI have to run in Switzerland](/blog/ai-data-location-switzerland). Policies, claims and health questions do not go into a free chat account.

## The office work that is still done by hand

The broker system, if you have one, already stores the client, the policies and often the renewal date. The gap is everything that arrives as a PDF from an insurer, a mail from the client, or a spreadsheet someone keeps because the system will not hold a comparison.

### Quote requests to several insurers

A client needs cover: a contents policy, a fleet, a liability. You ask several insurers, often with the same facts typed again into each portal or each email. The answers come back in different layouts. Putting the ask into one structured request, sending it, and lining the answers up is office work. Which insurer you recommend, and why, is the advice. The method of that request is its own job. Here it is enough to see that the typing is not the recommendation.

### A comparison the client can be shown

A comparison is a table of covers, exclusions, premiums and what is missing, drawn from the answers, with the source PDF kept beside each row. A workflow can propose the rows when the answers are clear. It cannot decide that two clauses mean the same thing when the wording differs, and it cannot leave out an exclusion because the cell was hard to fill. The note you send the client, including the disclosures FINMA describes for the point before a contract, is written or checked by the broker. The table is not that note.

### Renewals

Renewals cluster. The work is to see which policies end, which facts you no longer have (a payroll, a turnover, a vehicle list), and to chase those facts before you ask the insurer. A workflow can list dates from the file you already keep, draft the chase, and attach last year's schedule. Whether the cover is still right, and what you tell the client, stays with you. A renewal that goes out silent, because "it always renews", is how a change in the client's business never reaches the policy.

### A claims file

The client writes, often upset, with photos and a date. The insurer asks for a list. Someone collects what is there and sees what is missing. A workflow can file the mail on the policy, list the insurer's questions against the documents you hold, and draft a status line for you to edit. Whether the claim is notified in time, how you describe the facts, and what you advise the client to say, stay with the broker. Speed is not a substitute for the file being true.

## Where to start

Count a normal month: quote requests you typed more than once, renewal chases, claims mails that waited for a document. Use the four questions in [which process to automate first](/blog/which-process-to-automate-first): it repeats, it hurts, it has a clear end, and the data is already there. If the only saving is to let a tool write the recommendation, do not start.

If one of those office jobs sits with the same person every week, the [Process Check](/prozess-check) is a calm way to look at that single task.
