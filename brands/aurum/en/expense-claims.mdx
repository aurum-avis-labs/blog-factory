## Automate the expense claim, and leave the certificate rules intact

<p class="blog-lede">Automating expenses means a receipt the same day, the card line matched, one approval, a closed batch for payroll or the accounts. Tax and the salary certificate stay with whoever prepares the certificate.</p>

You automate employee expense claims by turning each outlay into a line with an image and an approval before the payroll run asks. The scanner reads the total. It does not decide whether the amount belongs in field 13 or whether a cross in 13.1.1 is enough.

This is for the office that reimburses staff or reconciles a company card in a Swiss firm. Supplier invoices are a different pile: what accounting software already reads is in [invoice capture](/prozess-check/blog/invoice-capture-what-software-misses). Approving vendor bills is in [invoice approval](/prozess-check/blog/invoice-approval-workflow). An expense is money spent by a person for the firm, not an invoice addressed to the company.

How the closed list sits among hours and absences is in [preparing payroll inputs](/prozess-check/blog/payroll-preparation).

## The workflow in four stages

<ol class="blog-flow">
  <li><strong>Capture</strong><p>Photo or form on the day of spend. Amount, currency, short purpose.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Match</strong><p>Card line to the row when the firm paid. Same franc amount.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Approve</strong><p>Line manager confirms business purpose, not tax treatment.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Close</strong><p>Sealed period to payroll (reimbursements) and accounts (card already paid).</p></li>
</ol>

Open lines are named as deferred, not dropped. The same cut-off as the payroll pack stops arguments on the morning of the run.

## What every line needs

If a field is missing, the line waits. Nothing is guessed.

<ul>
  <li>Date, amount, currency and what was bought, in a short plain phrase</li>
  <li>Who spent it, and whether the employee or the firm paid</li>
  <li>The receipt image, readable, including the supplier and the total</li>
  <li>The card line, when a company card paid, with the same amount</li>
  <li>A type your rules already use: travel, meal, overnight, minor, client</li>
  <li>The name of the manager who approved, and the date</li>
</ul>

A month of paper in a glovebox arrives after the cut-off. One path is enough: the form on the phone, or the photo to a fixed address that creates the line. A second path in a chat means double entry or no line at all.

## Company card and employee money

If the employee paid, the closed line is a reimbursement for payroll or a payment run. It may belong on the salary certificate. If the firm paid by card, the money has already left the bank. The line still needs a receipt and an approval: cost type for the books, and the certificate rules do not ignore card spending.

The [2026 federal guide to the salary certificate](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/afP1GDFr8gE3/dbst-form-lohna-wegleitung-2026-de.pdf) requires effective expense reimbursements to be declared, including on a company credit card. There is a narrow case with a cross in field 13.1.1 and no amount on the form. Every condition must hold: overnight against a receipt; a meal at generally no more than CHF 35 or a flat main meal of no more than CHF 30; client invitations against the original receipt; public transport against a receipt; a private car at no more than 75 rappen per kilometre; minor costs against a receipt or a daily flat of no more than CHF 20. Those per-case flats may not be scaled to a year's working days. There has to be real travel.

If any condition fails, or there is no approved expense regulation, effective travel, meal and overnight amounts go on the certificate as figures. This page is not tax advice. Your fiduciary applies the guide.

## Flat amounts and an approved regulation

Flats outside that narrow list need a regulation approved by the canton where the company is seated. The Swiss Tax Conference templates are the recommended shape. With approval, flats appear in field 13.2 and field 15 states canton and date. You then do not use the cross in 13.1.1. Other cantons recognise the approval when the templates of 1 February 2024, in force from 1 May 2024, are followed.

A workflow can store "regulation approved, canton, date" on the company. It cannot approve the regulation. That request is a letter to the cantonal tax authority, usually with the fiduciary. If you pay monthly representation flats without approval, fix that before you automate photos. Automating a flat the certificate cannot carry turns January into a correction.

## Foreign currency

Write the rate on the line and one source. If the card statement shows Swiss francs, that amount is the line. If the employee paid in euros and you reimburse francs, use the rate your books, the card or your fiduciary already use, and note which. Do not let each claimant pick a different website rate. If the statement has not arrived, the line waits.

## Approval and the closed batch

The line manager approves business purpose. A missing receipt, a meal that looks private, or a weekend with no job in the calendar goes back. The workflow routes the question. It should not approve by silence.

Private items on a company card are marked private and recovered. They do not sit in travel costs because the receipt was hard to read.

Once a period is closed, payroll receives reimbursements and the accounts receive card lines that are already paid. Whoever prepares the certificate needs the batch, not every photo separately in the inbox.

## Payroll module or a separate app

Many payroll programs include a receipt field. If people use it and travel is rare, the module is often enough. A second app that exports into the first is double entry. A dedicated expense app is worth describing when the chase is monthly work: card lines without photos, claims after the cut-off, a batch still open when payroll asks.

## A made-up example

A made-up example: one field technician, one month, three costs. Lunch CHF 28, paid by the technician. Train ticket CHF 64, company card. Forty kilometres in a private car at the firm rate, under 75 rappen in the guide. Lunch receipt the same evening, kilometres in the form. Card line matches the ticket image. Manager approves the next morning. Payroll: reimbursement for lunch and kilometres. Train not paid again. Accounts: train as travel, already paid. Whether the certificate needs a figure or only the cross is for whoever runs payroll, with the fiduciary rule.

## Who should skip a separate workflow

Skip it if two people submit a handful of receipts a year and the office is not chasing them. Skip it if the real problem is an unapproved flat paid every month. The photo workflow will not make that flat correct.

The recurring task is the chase and the match: receipts without a card line, card lines without a receipt, a batch still open when payroll asks.
