## Handle returns and customer enquiries in an online shop

<p class="blog-lede">Handling shop mail means classifying each message into one of four types, drafting from that order, and sending money and disputes to a person.</p>

You handle returns and customer enquiries in an online shop by sorting each message into one of four types, drafting the reply from that order, and sending only the remainder to a person. The four types are: where is my order, a return label, the status of a refund, and an exchange. Money, a promise you did not mean, and a damaged item stay with a person.

This is for the small team that reads the shop inbox, not for a complaints desk in a factory. What the shop system already sends is in [AI for small retailers and online shops](/prozess-check/blog/ai-for-retail-ecommerce). Sorting a mixed company inbox is in [sorting a shared inbox](/prozess-check/blog/shared-inbox-sort-emails). Here the answer must match one order, and a return must follow rules you wrote down.

<ol class="blog-steps">
  <li data-step="1"><h3>Classify</h3><p>Four types, plus a pile for everything else. Unclear messages do not get a guess.</p></li>
  <li data-step="2"><h3>Open the order</h3><p>Number, status, shipment, items, what the customer already paid.</p></li>
  <li data-step="3"><h3>Draft, do not send</h3><p>The reply uses those fields. A person sends it.</p></li>
  <li data-step="4"><h3>Apply your return rule</h3><p>Label, exchange or refusal only if your written terms say so.</p></li>
  <li data-step="5"><h3>Route the rest</h3><p>Damage, a missing order, a legal threat, a price you never offered.</p></li>
</ol>

## Step 1: Four types, and a pile for the rest

Read the message for the job, not for the mood.

- Where is my order, or can I change the address before it ships.
- I want to send it back: a label, a deadline, what happens to the postage.
- Where is my refund, after a return you already accepted.
- I want a different size or colour, an exchange rather than money.

Everything else goes to a second pile: broken goods, a claimed promise, no order number, or no order involved. [ch.ch](https://www.ch.ch/en/safety-and-justice/returning-or-exchanging-goods) is clear that Switzerland has no statutory right to return or exchange goods, online or in a store. Each shop sets its own rules. A model that has read other countries will offer 14 days unless you tell it your rule. Do not let it.

<ul class="blog-scenarios">
  <li><h3>If the order number is in the mail</h3><p>Open that order. Draft from its status. Do not ask the customer to repeat it.</p></li>
  <li><h3>If the number is missing</h3><p>Ask for the checkout email. Do not search three systems by a first name.</p></li>
  <li><h3>If the goods are said to be faulty</h3><p>That is not a change-of-mind return. Put it on the person pile. Warranty is a different job.</p></li>
</ul>

## Step 2: Where is my order

If the shop or carrier record shows a status, the draft states that status, the date it changed and where the customer can see it. If the status is "not shipped", the draft says that and does not invent a delivery day. If two parcels went out, the draft names which one the question is about, or asks which.

The person sending the mail checks that the draft matches the order in front of them. A helpful-sounding sentence that is not on the order is deleted. Address changes before shipment can be a draft too, if you have not yet handed the parcel to the carrier. After that, the draft states what the record shows and a person decides whether the carrier can still stop the parcel.

Customers often write twice on the same day when the first reply was vague. A draft that only says "we are checking" without status and date creates the second mail. The draft should name the status you already hold in the shop, even when it is unwelcome.

## Step 3: A return label only when your terms say yes

Write the return rule once in fields a workflow can check: days from delivery, excluded articles, who pays postage, whether you need the item back before refund, whether exchange is allowed. Inside the window, right article, not excluded: the draft confirms the return and, if you use labels, that a label will follow. Outside the window or an excluded article: the draft says no in your approved words, and a person still sees it before it goes.

A voluntary 14-day window is your term, not the rule for every Swiss online order. The 14-day cancellation [ch.ch](https://www.ch.ch/en/safety-and-justice/returning-or-exchanging-goods) describes for door-to-door sales and telephone contracts over CHF 100 does not automatically apply to an ordinary website order. Do not copy that deadline into shop mail unless it is actually your term. This is a practical reading, not legal advice.

The label is a file from the carrier or your shop. The workflow prepares it when the rule passes. A person confirms it when the case was flagged. Hygiene items, personalised goods or stock you listed as excluded do not belong in the same draft as a standard return. The draft declines in your sentence, not in wording the model learned from another shop.

## Step 4: Refund and exchange as facts

"Where is my money?" is answered from the refund record. If it is marked sent, the draft states date and amount. If the return has arrived but the refund is not released, the draft says that. If there is no return in the system, the draft says so and asks for return-parcel tracking, instead of promising "as soon as possible" with no date.

Exchange offers size or colour only if your rule allows that variant and the order shows the original item. A different product, goodwill or a price difference is a person's decision. The workflow can show the price difference. It should not waive it.

## Step 5: What never goes out automatically

These messages always go to a person, with no draft sent alone:

- Item damaged, incomplete or described as dangerous.
- Promise, phone agreement or price not on the order.
- Threat of a complaint to an authority, lawyer or review with staff personal data.
- Order not found, or two customers seem to share one number.
- Language your team cannot check. Replies across languages once a person will check them: [multilingual customer messages](/prozess-check/blog/multilingual-customer-communication).

The shared-inbox article is the general method. The shop adds the order as the source of the draft and your terms as the source of yes or no.

## A made-up example

A made-up example: a Swiss online shop for household goods, two people on the inbox, about 40 messages on a busy day. Roughly half ask where the parcel is; the rest are returns, refunds and exchanges. Before, both opened the shop, searched the name and wrote from scratch. On a Monday the same sender wrote twice; the second reply contradicted the first.

In the changed route each message is marked as one of the four types or "person". With an order number the draft quotes stored status. Without a number it asks for the checkout email and does not search three systems by surname. Return inside 30 days per the shop terms, article not on the exclusion list: draft confirms and a label is prepared. Day 41: decline in the approved sentence the shop signed off, and a person still sees it before send.

Three messages skip the draft: broken glass, claimed free return postage on the phone, refund question with no payment sent in the system. One person works that list; the other sends matching drafts. What stays is judgement. Gone is searching for an order whose number was already in the mail, and the second contradictory reply on a Monday.

How much time that saves depends on message volume and how long one reply takes today. Count half a day with real mail before you buy a tool. The difference from the shared-inbox article is the order as source: there you sort channels, here you pull fields from one order.

## When to leave it

Leave it if a handful of messages a week are cleared by the person who also packs. A shared folder and your terms on screen cost less. Leave it if terms are not written down. A workflow cannot apply a rule that lives in a head. Write the window, the exclusions and the postage first, use them for a few weeks, then automate the check. Without a written rule, the draft will offer the 14-day window from other countries.
