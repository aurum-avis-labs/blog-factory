Between two systems with no interface, Excel is often the honest source of truth. Finance has a tool, field sales a CRM, production a list. The week lives in a workbook on a SharePoint three people know.

The goal is not to ban Excel. The goal is to name the glue: which columns, which import, which system should be the source afterwards.

## Candidate

Same sheet, same columns, same import, every week. Someone copies from export A into sheet 2, calculates, sends a PDF to leadership. Frequency and a boundary are there. The file exists. That is the same filter as in [which process to automate first](/blog/which-process-to-automate-first).

Often the Monday report is exactly that: [reporting from five sources](/blog/reporting-from-five-sources). Or invoices that land in a holding workbook before they reach the accounting tool: [invoice capture](/blog/invoice-capture-what-software-misses).

## Not a candidate

The one model only one person understands, reinvented every week. Different questions, different columns, different exceptions. When that colleague is on holiday the operation stops, because the logic lives in a head. Then the first step is to write it down, not to point a script at unknown logic.

## The workbook and two systems into the Check

Which two systems, which file, how often, who may overwrite. That is the [Process Check](/prozess-check). Not "we replace Excel with a platform" as a sales line. A bounded piece of glue.
