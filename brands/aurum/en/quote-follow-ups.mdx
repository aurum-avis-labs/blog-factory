The quote is out. The reminder sits on one person and a task nobody else can see. The CRM is incomplete because "quickly the PDF from Outlook" was the real process. Two weeks later the quote is cold, and nobody knows whether anyone followed up.

Automation may remind, put a draft on the table, set a status when someone clicked. It may not invent the close, and it may not tell a customer that "we are just checking internally" when nobody checked.

In overview: [three workflows worth automating](/blog/three-workflows-worth-automating).

## Copilot writes the text. The rhythm is missing.

A mail draft from Copilot helps when the quote is already in someone's head. It does not set a follow-up when that person is on the road. It does not fill the CRM if the source stays Outlook.

When a licence is enough and when you need a built flow: [Copilot or build a workflow](/blog/copilot-or-build-a-workflow).

Customer files do not belong in the Free chat. The data-protection angle is in [ChatGPT and customer data](/blog/chatgpt-customer-data-swiss-dsg). One sentence here, not the main part.
