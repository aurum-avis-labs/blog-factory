The **Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection** (FADP / DSG) protects personal data of natural persons. Since **1 September 2023** the total revision applies (often called “nDSG” or “revDSG”). For SMEs that means: no exemption merely because of company size, while many corporate formalities stay optional. This overview is orientation, not legal advice. Statute text: [Fedlex](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de).

## What the revision changed

Changes SMEs feel:

- Stronger transparency duties when collecting data (Art. 19).
- Duty to notify the FDPIC as soon as possible of data security breaches likely to pose a high risk (Art. 24).
- Data protection impact assessment for high risk (Art. 22).
- Rules on disclosure abroad (Arts. 16 and 17).
- Fines up to CHF 250,000 against natural persons for intentional offences in listed cases (Arts. 60 ff.).

Checklist: [FADP obligations for SMEs](/security/blog/swiss-fadp-obligations-for-smes). Versus GDPR: [FADP vs GDPR](/security/blog/swiss-fadp-vs-gdpr).

## What every SME needs first

1. Clarity on which personal data sits where (customers, staff, applicants, newsletters).
2. A privacy notice that matches the current Act.
3. Contracts or safeguards with processors and cloud providers.
4. A breach path: who decides, who documents, when the [FDPIC portal](https://databreach.edoeb.admin.ch) is used.
5. Access control at join and leave.

A full processing inventory is often optional under 250 employees when risk is low (Art. 12(5)). Customers and tenders may still demand one.

## What “revDSG” means in search

Searchers mean the 2023 revision. Legally the Act is still the DSG, SR 235.1. “nDSG” is the common short name for the new text. Use the words readers type, and link Fedlex.

## Common misconceptions

- “We only have B2B customers, so nothing applies.” Contact persons’ personal data still counts.
- “The GDPR 72-hour clock applies here.” In Switzerland: as soon as possible for high risk; no fixed 72-hour FADP deadline.
- “ChatGPT Free is fine if we omit the company name.” Identifiability is enough. See FDPIC on [AI and data protection](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/de/ki-und-datenschutz).

## FAQ

<div class="blog-faq">
  <h3>Since when does the new FADP apply?</h3>
  <p>Since 1 September 2023 (entry into force per Fedlex).</p>
</div>

<div class="blog-faq">
  <h3>Must every SME appoint a data protection adviser?</h3>
  <p>No. Art. 10 allows appointment; it is optional but can ease FDPIC consultation exceptions.</p>
</div>

<div class="blog-faq">
  <h3>Is the FADP checklist enough?</h3>
  <p>As a start, yes. Professional secrecy, health data and pure EU business need extra review.</p>
</div>

<div class="blog-faq">
  <h3>What if there is a breach?</h3>
  <p>Contain, assess, document; if a high risk is likely, notify the FDPIC as soon as possible via the portal. Detail: [Report a data breach to the FDPIC](/security/blog/report-a-data-breach-to-the-fdpic).</p>
</div>
