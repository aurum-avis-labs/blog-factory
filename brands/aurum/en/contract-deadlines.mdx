## Track contract notice periods and renewals

You track contract notice periods and renewals by keeping one register, not by hoping someone remembers the PDF. Each row names the contract, the party, the end date, whether it renews if nobody acts, the last day a notice still counts, who must send it, and where the signed file sits. A reminder goes out before that last day, to a role the office still has if one person is away. The decision to end, change or let the contract run stays with a person.

This is for owners and office leads in Swiss firms of about 5 to 80 people. Typical rows are the premises lease, vehicle or equipment leasing, insurance, software licences and maintenance agreements. The letter you send, if you do send one, is a separate job: a fixed text with fields, described in [documents from templates](/prozess-check/blog/generate-documents-from-templates). This page is about not missing the window.

## What belongs in the register

A register is short on purpose. If it tries to be the full contract file, nobody keeps it current.

| Field | What you write |
| --- | --- |
| Contract | Premises lease, leasing, policy, licence, maintenance, or a name your office already uses |
| Party | Landlord, insurer, vendor |
| Start and end | As written, including a fixed term |
| Renewal | Ends, or continues unless notice is given, and for how long |
| Notice | How long before, and the form: letter, email, portal |
| Last day | The last date a notice still counts, counted from the clause |
| Owner | The role that decides, not a private inbox |
| File | Link or path to the signed PDF |
| Status | Open, notice sent, ended, or left to renew |

Annual cost is useful if finance already has the number. It is not required to catch the date.

## The last day comes from the clause

There is no single Swiss notice period for every contract a small firm signs. Insurance, software and maintenance follow the text you signed. Read that text, write the last day in the register, and do not copy a period from a neighbouring row.

Leases of premises are the exception you can check against the law when the contract is silent. Under the Code of Obligations, a fixed term ends without notice when that term ends. If both sides simply carry on, the lease becomes open-ended ([Art. 266 OR](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/27/317_321_377/de)). An open-ended lease is ended on the statutory dates unless the parties agreed a longer period or a different date ([Art. 266a OR](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/27/317_321_377/de)). For business premises the default is six months' notice, on a locally customary date or, if there is no local custom, at the end of a three-month rental period ([Art. 266d OR](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/27/317_321_377/de)). A dwelling is three months, on the same idea of a customary date ([Art. 266c OR](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/27/317_321_377/de)). These lines are the statute, not advice on your lease. If your contract says something stricter, the contract is what you put in the register. A lawyer or your fiduciary reads the clause if the wording is unclear. We do not.

## What a reader can pull from the PDF

A model can propose fields from a PDF: party, end date, a sentence that says the contract renews unless notice is given, and a number of months. That saves typing when you are loading a pile at year-end. It does not replace the row.

The person who confirms looks for four failures:

- The date sits in a schedule, and the model read a different date from the cover.
- Renewal is in an amendment, not in the original.
- Notice must be a signed letter to a named address, and the model only found "in writing".
- The PDF is a quote, not the signed contract.

Mark those rows. Do not let a reminder go out on an unconfirmed date. Once the row is confirmed, the reminder is ordinary office work: a shared list, a calendar entry, an email to the role. If you already work in Microsoft 365, a list with a date column can be shown as a calendar, with a start and an end ([Microsoft Support](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/lists/create-a-calendar-view-from-a-list)). The picture is the same in a spreadsheet the whole office can open. The tool is not the point. The point is that the reminder survives a holiday.

## Who should leave this as a list

Leave it as a maintained list if you have fewer than about ten live contracts and someone already checks the sheet once a month. A reminder flow does not pay for itself when the list is already trusted.

Wait if the PDFs are not in one place. Collect them first. A model cannot remind you about a contract that only exists in a drawer.

Skip a special contract product when your accounting or document system already stores these dates and people actually use the reminder. Add a row only for what that system does not see, usually the lease and the insurance policies that never passed through accounts payable.

Do not automate the decision. Ending a lease, dropping a policy or letting a licence renew is a business call. The register's job is to put that call on a date while there is still time to send the notice in the form the contract asks for.

## A made-up example: the December stack

A made-up example: an office of 20 people, not a client. In November the office lead finds 18 live contracts: the business premises, two vehicle leases, four insurance policies, seven software licences and four maintenance agreements. Renewal clauses differ. Two licences renew for a year unless email notice arrives 30 days before the end date. The premises lease states six months, which matches the default for business premises, and notice must be a letter. One maintenance contract ends on a fixed date and does not renew.

A colleague drops the PDFs into a folder. The draft register fills party, end date and a proposed last day. The office lead opens every row beside the PDF. Three dates are wrong: one was the signature date, one was in an unsigned quote, one ignored an amendment that shortened the notice period. Those three are corrected. Reminders sit on a shared calendar, addressed to the office role, six weeks before each last day, and three months before for the lease. In December the lead decides two licences will end, and asks the person who will sign to send the notices. The rest are marked "leave running" so the same question does not return next week by accident.

Nothing here is a measured saving. It is the shape of the work: read once, confirm, remind a role, decide while the window is open.
