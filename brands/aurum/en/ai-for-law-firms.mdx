## AI for Swiss law firms: what professional secrecy allows

AI in a Swiss law firm is worth a look only for office work that stays inside a setup professional secrecy allows: sorting a new enquiry, filing incoming mail on the matter, proposing a deadline for a person to confirm, preparing a draft bill from time already recorded. It is not a licence to paste a mandate into a chat account. The legal judgement stays with the lawyer. This page is practical orientation for the office, not legal advice. If a tool would touch client material, read the sources below and ask the adviser your firm already uses.

The reader is a partner or the person who runs the secretariat in a small firm, a few lawyers and assistants, matters in a practice system and a lot of mail. Research drafts and long document review are a different question. This page is the repetitive office work around the file.

## Secrecy comes before the tool

Two rules frame the office, and a bar guide says what to do before any software, including AI, sees client material.

<ul class="blog-facts">
  <li><span class="blog-kicker">Criminal Code</span><strong>Art. 321</strong><p>Lawyers, defence counsel and their assistants who reveal a professional secret are punishable on complaint. The consolidated article is linked below.</p></li>
  <li><span class="blog-kicker">Lawyers Act</span><strong>Art. 13 BGFA</strong><p>Secrecy has no time limit and runs towards everyone, for what clients entrusted in the course of the profession. Assistants are included.</p></li>
  <li><span class="blog-kicker">Swiss Bar</span><strong>14 June 2024</strong><p>The SAV board adopted an AI guide: know who can access input data and where it is stored, before you use a tool.</p></li>
</ul>

[Art. 13 of the Lawyers Act (BGFA)](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2002/153/de#art_13) says lawyers are bound, without time limit and towards everyone, by professional secrecy for everything clients entrusted to them because of the profession. Being released from secrecy does not oblige them to disclose it. They must see that their assistants keep it too. The secretariat is inside that sentence, not beside it. [Art. 12 let. a](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2002/153/de#art_12) adds that lawyers practise carefully and conscientiously. A draft that nobody checks fails that line even if the software is tidy.

[Art. 321 of the Criminal Code](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/54/757_781_799/de#art_321) is the criminal side. The wording reprinted in the [Federal Gazette](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/fga/2025/2004/de) covers lawyers, defence counsel and their assistants who reveal a secret entrusted in the profession or perceived while practising it. On complaint, the penalty is a custodial sentence of up to three years or a monetary penalty. This is the shape of the rule, not a reading of your matter.

## Which office jobs can move, and where

The jobs are ordinary. The constraint is where the document goes.

### A new enquiry

A person writes to the firm: a name, a short description, sometimes a court letter attached. Someone checks whether the firm already acts for the other side, who should see it, and whether the firm takes the matter. A workflow can register the mail, list the names for a conflict check, and put the thread in a queue. It should not accept the mandate, and it should not send the attachment to a service outside the routes below.

### Filing on the matter

Once a matter exists, incoming letters and emails need the file number, the date and the folder. That is sorting against a list the firm already keeps. A workflow can propose the file from the reference in the subject line and leave the doubtful ones for the secretariat. The proposal is only as safe as the system it writes into. If that system is the firm's own, the sort is the same class of work as a careful clerk. If the sort means uploading the letter to a consumer chat, it is not.

### A deadline, as a proposal

Court and authority mail often contains a date. Pulling that date into a list, with the source page attached, saves the search. Entering it as the firm's deadline, and calculating what the procedure allows, stays with the lawyer. [Art. 12 let. a BGFA](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2002/153/de#art_12) is why the second look is not optional. This page does not explain how a time limit is counted.

### A draft bill

Time is written on the matter, in the system or on a sheet. A draft fee note that gathers those entries, for the lawyer to correct, is preparation. Deciding what is billable, what was an estimate, and what the client was told is not. [Art. 12 of the BGFA](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2002/153/de#art_12) also expects the client to be informed about billing principles when the mandate starts, and periodically or on request about the amount of the fee. A workflow does not replace that conversation.

## What must not go into an open chat

Free and personal chat accounts are the wrong place for a mandate, a draft pleading or a client's letter. The general office version of that line, under the Data Protection Act, is in [ChatGPT and customer data](/blog/chatgpt-customer-data-swiss-dsg). For a law firm the bar is higher than "personal data", because of Art. 321 SCC and Art. 13 BGFA, including for assistants.

The [SAV guide](https://www.sav-fsa.ch/documents/672183/2025869/SAV_KI+Guidelines+2024.pdf/68d5eab4-c2ad-50bd-506f-7ba6af15a839), which the [SAV board adopted on 14 June 2024](https://www.sav-fsa.ch/news/-/asset_publisher/97aPCN6lT9LD/content/id/3812477), says to clarify who can access input data and where it is stored before any software is used. It describes three routes: the software runs on the firm's own network and nothing leaves it; the firm follows the SAV's rules on outsourcing and cloud (the guide points to its own cloud note); or the firm has informed client consent and a waiver. Otherwise, the guide says, confidential information is not entered. It also says results can be wrong or incomplete and must be checked, and that a firm should set an internal instruction for AI. Where a service is hosted is a separate question, covered in [does AI have to run in Switzerland](/blog/ai-data-location-switzerland).

## Where to start

Pick one job that does not need the content of the mandate in order to be tried: filing by reference, or a draft bill from time entries already in your system. If the only way to save time is to upload letters to a tool you cannot explain, do not start there. Count for a fortnight how many items the secretariat files, and how many times a date is copied off a letter. If the number is small, leave it.

If one of those jobs is heavy and you can describe it without showing a file, the [Process Check](/prozess-check) is a way to look at that single task.
