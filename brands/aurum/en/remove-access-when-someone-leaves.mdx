## Remove access when someone leaves the company

Remove access when someone leaves the company at the agreed time, not «when IT has time next week». Former staff with active VPN, mailbox, or admin rights are a common source of data leaks, invoice fraud, and later password-reset abuse. Under [Art. 8 FADP](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de), only people who need data for their role should still reach it. That is an organisational measure alongside firewalls and training.

This article is for HR, IT, and owners in Swiss firms with about 20 to 250 people. It covers offboarding, not day-one onboarding. Orientation, not legal advice.

<ul class="blog-checklist">
  <li>Disable Entra ID or AD account at the agreed time; revoke all sessions</li>
  <li>Convert mailbox to shared or forward per policy; no silent access to personal addresses</li>
  <li>Remove MFA, VPN, guest Wi-Fi, cloud admin, and building badges</li>
  <li>Rotate passwords they knew: shared shop logins, Wi-Fi, vendor portals, API keys in notes</li>
  <li>Collect devices; wipe or reassign company hardware</li>
  <li>Remove from Slack, Teams, CRM, payroll admin, backup alerts, and shared calendars</li>
  <li>Check SaaS user lists for contractors; delete users in Mailchimp, accounting, time tracking</li>
  <li>Document completion with HR and IT date, time, and list of systems disabled</li>
</ul>

## Why delays hurt

A dismissed employee can still export a client list the evening notice is given if the account stays open. A contractor keeps reading Teams until someone notices the licence. A bookkeeper who left six months ago may still receive password reset mails and use them if nobody closed the account. BACS describes [business email compromise](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/de/business-email-compromise-de) risks including forwarding rules in taken-over mailboxes. Training does not close accounts; offboarding does.

## When to disable: last day, notice period, or immediate

<ol class="blog-steps">
  <li data-step="1"><h3>Fix the date with HR</h3><p>Last working day, time of immediate dismissal if relevant, and whether conflict is likely. IT needs this in writing, not only in a corridor conversation.</p></li>
  <li data-step="2"><h3>Disable at the minute</h3><p>On a normal exit, at end of the last working day. On immediate dismissal, in parallel with the meeting when leadership and HR agree. Read-only mode during notice is for counsel to approve on sensitive roles.</p></li>
  <li data-step="3"><h3>Mailbox and forwarding</h3><p>Shared mailbox with manager access and an end date. After finance staff leave, [check mailbox forwarding rules](/security/blog/check-mailbox-forwarding-rules): leavers sometimes set private forwards weeks earlier.</p></li>
  <li data-step="4"><h3>Store proof</h3><p>Checklist signed by HR and IT. Keep at least two years, in line with internal breach documentation under Art. 24 FADP.</p></li>
</ol>

## A made-up example: Friday 17:00 termination

A made-up example: a sales lead is terminated Friday at 17:00. HR opens a ticket at 16:30. At 17:05 accounts are disabled, sessions revoked, laptop collection is booked for Monday, and the shared CRM password is rotated because the team once used «Spring2024» for everyone. The sales manager reviews open opportunities. No drama, only a form both sides signed and an IT timestamp.

## HR and IT handoff

HR should open a mandatory ticket with last day, time, and litigation risk. IT returns the completed checklist. Between notice and exit, people copy files to private storage; that is not a substitute for later disable, but a reason to watch sensitive exports in that window when counsel allows.

Freelancers, interns, caretakers, and agency recruiters with production access need the same process as employees. They are often missing from the Entra «Staff» group but still on VPN or the building badge system.

## SaaS and shadow IT

Marketing forgets Mailchimp. Sales forgets the trial CRM. The leaver list should mirror the onboarding list in reverse. Anyone who reached personal data in a tool should also be removed from the vendor side covered by your [data processing agreement](/security/blog/data-processing-agreement-under-the-fadp).

Passwords on sticky notes for «tools we will cancel later» keep access alive. On exit, rotate shared logins for shop, social media, or vendor portals even if only one person knew the password.

## Physical access and devices

Keys, alarm codes, parking gates, and server-room badges belong on the same checklist as Entra ID. BYOD may still hold mail profiles on a private phone. Remote wipe only where policy allows; document what was removed.

Collect laptops, tokens, and company phones. For part-time staff on shared devices, clarify before exit which profile holds company data and whether a reset is needed.

## Privacy and sensitive data

If the leaver handled pupil, patient, or client data, record what you checked for plausible private USB or cloud copies. Schools and course providers can use [data protection for schools](/security/blog/data-protection-for-schools-and-course-providers). Medical practices have extra duties; see [data protection and security for a medical practice](/security/blog/data-protection-and-security-for-a-medical-practice).

A [DPIA](/security/blog/when-a-dpia-is-required-in-switzerland) is rarely the first question on exit. It becomes relevant when exit shows someone had broad access for years without logs.

## Team behaviour and reporting

Tell the team not to forward mail «to help» the leaver to a private address. That is a data breach path. If former colleagues still appear in chat channels, the disable was incomplete.

  <span class="blog-kicker">Note</span>
  <p>Employment law may require access to payslips or references. Separate that from operational systems and agree with HR how documents are handed over outside mail.</p>
</aside>

## External IT partner and MSP

Many firms have no full-time IT employee. The managed service provider then needs written offboarding instructions with time before HR communicates termination. Clarify in the service contract who disables accounts within which deadline and who escalates when the partner is on holiday. If the MSP uses its own admin account, check whether the leaver ever knew partner passwords or used shared break-glass accounts.

## Test the process once a year

Use a PSA ticket HR cannot close without IT confirmation. Run a yearly test with a fictional name before a real conflict case. Show leadership the leaver pack next to the onboarding pack once a year so the process survives HR turnover.

When a director leaves, an external IT partner or fiduciary should confirm the disable to avoid conflicts of interest.

Keep a one-page template ready: name, last day, disable time, systems with checkboxes, HR and IT signatures. Without a template, every exit is improvised and SaaS accounts stay open.

If offboarding incidents show nobody reports phishing, refresh [security awareness](/security/security-awareness) so teams flag logins from former addresses.
