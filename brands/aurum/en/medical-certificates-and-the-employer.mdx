## Medical certificates and the employer: what HR may process

Medical certificates and the employer under Swiss data protection usually means you may check whether someone is unfit for work and until when, not the full diagnosis or treatment plan, unless law or insurance requires more. Health data is particularly sensitive ([Art. 5 FADP](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de)); employee data only as far as the employment relationship needs ([Art. 328b CO](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de)). The FADP requires appropriate measures ([Art. 8 FADP](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de)). This text is for owners, HR, and office staff in firms of about 20 to 250 people. It does not replace labour law advice on continued pay or termination. It is orientation, not legal advice.

## What the certificate states and what HR needs

Swiss medical certificates typically state incapacity for work and the period. Diagnosis, medication, or therapy plans are usually unnecessary for payroll and rostering. If an employee volunteers more, do not store more than the purpose requires.

Line managers need absence dates for planning, not a medical narrative. Train the line «HR handles certificates» and stop forwards into team WhatsApp or Slack.

Many firms handle short absences without a certificate (number of days, who reports). Once a certificate arrives, apply the strict data rule: only HR and payroll store the document; the calendar shows «absent». Do not mix the two stages or every «I am sick» message ends in a medical folder.

## Intake, e-mail, and shared folders

Certificates by e-mail are common but risky when many people read the mailbox or forwards stay unencrypted. Better: a dedicated HR address, upload in the HR tool, or an encrypted channel mentioned in your privacy notice. Same care as other sensitive attachments in [sharing files that contain personal data](/security/blog/sharing-files-that-contain-personal-data).

## Insurance, SUVA, and disability benefits

Medical detail for insurers, SUVA, or disability offices belongs in the defined process, not the sales chat. HR or payroll forwards what the insurer requires; managers only see that «the case is with HR».

A made-up example: a 40-person services firm receives sick notes as photos in the team lead’s private WhatsApp. HR writes a half-page rule: intake only to hr@…, the lead logs «sick until date X» in the calendar, the photo is deleted. IT restricts HR folders. No new software.

## Retention and deletion

Certificates belong in the personnel file with the same retention logic as other HR documents (see the personnel file article). After expiry, destroy or anonymise, including copies in mail and backup. Job applications are separate: [how long to keep job applications in Switzerland](/security/blog/how-long-to-keep-job-applications-switzerland).

For an employee access request, the dossier may contain health data. Reply only with what you hold and may disclose; internal comments on the «real» illness without a document do not belong in the export. Disputed or incomplete certificates are clarified with the employee and counsel if needed, not broadcast to the team.

If the employee sends the certificate to a line manager’s private mail, do not forward «for info». HR takes over, confirms receipt, and deletes copies from the manager’s mailbox after filing. That cuts risk alongside [sending personal data by e-mail](/security/blog/sending-personal-data-by-email) and shared folders.

## Data protection training

A live data protection session covers sick notes, certificates, and typical mail and chat mistakes with your examples, in one to two hours. See [data protection awareness training](/security/datenschutz).

## Not a guide for medical practices as employers

This text is for employers in SMEs, not for how a practice or clinic organises patient data. Professional secrecy and patient files add layers this page does not cover. If your firm delivers healthcare services, you need separate orientation.

## Photos, scans, and phone cameras

Employees photograph certificates on private phones and send them to HR. Ask for upload in the defined channel or mail to the HR address, then delete the photo on the phone. HR keeps one version in the system, not three copies in mail, chat, and paper.

Scans need the same access restriction as the original. A «Health» folder on the desktop without a password is a common finding in internal reviews.

## Payroll and planning without clinical detail

Payroll needs absence days and whether continued pay applies, not therapy detail. Rostering needs «person X unavailable until Friday». Both fit a calendar without mailing the certificate to everyone. If someone volunteers a diagnosis, do not record more than the purpose requires.

Internal policy: who may open a certificate, who only updates the calendar, how long it may sit in the inbox before filing. One paragraph in the employee privacy notice is often enough as a pointer. Repeated breaches (forwards to team chat) call for training, not a public reprimand that repeats health content.

Cantonal or sector rules on when a certificate is required (beyond data protection) need labour advice if you employ people in several cantons. This article stays on data, not the detail of continued pay days.

The employer usually needs the length of absence and incapacity for work, not the diagnosis. Do not put certificates in group inboxes the whole department reads, and keep retention rules in the personnel file.
