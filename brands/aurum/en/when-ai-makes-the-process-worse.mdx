Not every workflow gets better with a model in the middle. Some get slower, more expensive or embarrassing, because the work was already unclear.

## Four stop signs

**Bad data.** If the sheet has different columns every week and nobody owns the source, the model invents figures someone then forwards internally. The [adesso GenAI Impact Report 2026](https://www.adesso.ch/adesso-ch/whitepaper/genai-impact-report-2026-ch.pdf) names data quality and missing processes as the most common hurdles, not "the model is too dumb". That is orientation, not our survey.

**Too many exceptions.** Every second instance needs a special rule: you are not building automation. You are building a second, more expensive manual process with extra risk.

**No owner.** Nobody may say when a run is "done" and when it is "wrong". Then responsibility lives in a chat log.

**Model into the customer mail with no check.** An internal draft is one thing. Sending to a client without a human reading it is another. Personal data and reporting figures: human-in-the-loop as the default.

The filter for when you try *anyway*: [which process to automate first](/blog/which-process-to-automate-first).

## Better a no in the Check

The [Process Check](/prozess-check) may end with "not this one". That is the product, not a failure. Six weeks of a tool that cements a chaotic process costs more than 60 minutes of clarity.
