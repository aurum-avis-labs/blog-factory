## WhatsApp for business and data protection: the SME risk in one sentence

WhatsApp for business and customer chats is convenient. Consumer WhatsApp is not your company archive, access control layer, or a clearly documented processing agreement on Swiss terms. Names, phone numbers, orders and health hints in group chats are personal data under the FADP ([Art. 8 FADP](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de)). Most SMEs of 20 to 250 staff should keep customer dossiers out of private chats and route work through channels you can govern. Orientation only, not legal advice.

## Why finance and support reach for WhatsApp

Customers already use it. A quick photo of a delivery note or IBAN «just this once» becomes habit. Without a rule, several teams run shadow CRMs in chat. When someone leaves, nobody removes them from the «urgent clients» group. When a phone is stolen, chat history may lack a lock screen.

## Consumer app, Business profile and API

The consumer app on a private phone stores chats locally and in cloud backups your IT does not manage. A WhatsApp Business profile helps reachability and catalogues; it does not replace retention rules, permissions or proof for access or deletion requests.

The WhatsApp Business API through a documented partner is a different setup: processing agreements, defined data flows, often CRM or ticket integration. Whether that pays off depends on volume and regulated data. This article does not compare vendors.

## What the FADP expects in practice

You need purpose, information ([Art. 19 FADP](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de)), appropriate security, and often a processing agreement ([Art. 9 FADP](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de)) when a vendor processes personal data for you. Consumer WhatsApp rarely matches auditor questions on deletion, access or data location.

## Backups, device changes and screenshots

iCloud or Google backups copy threads to accounts the company does not own. On a new phone, groups migrate with old customer data. Screenshots on private devices multiply copies without access control. Ban screenshots for customer dossiers in policy and name one official channel.

## Safer habits this month

<ul class="blog-checklist">
  <li>Pick one channel for customer contact (ticket, mail, Teams with retention)</li>
  <li>State it in signatures and on the website</li>
  <li>Remove work WhatsApp groups on the same day someone exits</li>
  <li>Ban CRM exports into WhatsApp in acceptable use</li>
</ul>

## A made-up example: the sales group

A made-up example: a 35-person trades firm in Lucerne runs a WhatsApp group with installers and the sales inbox number. A customer sends passport photos for a credit check. Six months later an installer leaves; the group still has the photos. The firm moves credit checks to the portal, deletes the group, and trains sales to reply «please use the link» from templates.

## Incidents and notification

If a customer sends medical or financial data in WhatsApp, treat it like any channel: document internally, limit further spread, and check whether notification to the [FDPIC](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch) is needed when high risk is likely. The deadline is «as soon as possible», not 72 hours.

## Who chats internally only, who serves customers daily

A team with no customer data in chat mainly needs a clear line: from the first customer number in the thread, business rules apply. Teams that serve customers on WhatsApp daily soon need tickets or CRM, vendor contracts, clean offboarding and training that makes «please use the portal» normal. Regulated professions, health data or frequent EU contacts under GDPR pressure should not build workflows on consumer WhatsApp.

## IT and data protection together

Data protection writes the rule; IT supplies the technical alternative. If policy says «no WhatsApp» but no portal exists, policy is ignored. A customer link to systems you already run is often enough as a first step.

## Datenschutz training

A live session explains which chat tools fit customer data and how to redirect politely, in one to two hours. See [Datenschutz awareness training](/security/datenschutz).

A client address in a private group chat leaves the firm with the phone. Use the channel the business controls, and do not treat the device as the company archive.
