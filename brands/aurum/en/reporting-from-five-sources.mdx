The picture is almost always the same. A CRM export. Two spreadsheets nobody merged. A supplier PDF. A mail with the figures "that are not in the system". The same glue every Monday, often in Excel, often by the same person.

Copilot in the document writes a nicer paragraph about the numbers. It does not pull the numbers from five systems with different permissions. The glue stays.

In the trio with invoices and follow-ups: [three workflows worth automating](/blog/three-workflows-worth-automating).

## What "good enough" means

A defined output: the same columns, the same order, the same recipients. A rhythm: weekly, not "when leadership asks". A person who checks before the report goes out. Figures that enter reporting do not come unchecked from a model.

If the truth between systems lives in a workbook only one person understands, that is often [Excel as a shadow system](/blog/excel-as-shadow-system). Then the candidate is not "an AI that explains Excel". It is the glue: the same columns, the same import.

## When to leave it

Different questions every week, different files, different recipients. Then the report is a conversation, not a workflow. Automation turns that into expensive, false certainty.

Same filter as in [which process to automate first](/blog/which-process-to-automate-first): does it repeat, does it hurt, is it bounded, do the files already exist.
