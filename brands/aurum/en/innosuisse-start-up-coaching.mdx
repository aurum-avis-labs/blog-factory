## Innosuisse start-up coaching

Innosuisse start-up coaching, in practice the Initial Coaching programme, is a voucher of up to CHF 10'000 for sessions with Innosuisse-accredited coaches over up to twelve months. It pays for structured coaching on your business model, team, and milestones. It does not pay for software development, design sprints, or cloud invoices. Approval is a gate into coached planning and, if you complete the programme, a possible application for Core Coaching. It is not a substitute for market tests or a promise that investors will follow.

This is for founders and innovation leads in Switzerland who already treat the idea as a company: a Swiss address, a team that can commit time, and a story that links innovation to a scientific or technical discipline. The wider map of grants and programmes sits in [Swiss startup funding programmes](/blog/swiss-startup-funding-programmes). If your next bottleneck is how to present traction in slides, build the deck around your validation data. What an MVP build costs in Switzerland is a separate budget line, covered in [MVP cost in Switzerland](/blog/mvp-cost-switzerland).

## What Innosuisse Initial Coaching actually covers

The voucher redeems against accredited coaches you invite through the Innosuisse portal. A lead coach guides the programme; additional coaches can join when needed. The aim is to question the whole company: strengths, weaknesses, market logic, and the next milestones that matter for development. At the end you submit an evaluation report with forward milestones, usually within twelve months of starting.

Coaching can sharpen positioning, challenge assumptions, and force a written plan. It does not ship code, run ads, or produce usage data from strangers.

## How to apply for Innosuisse start-up coaching

Treat the application as a serious dossier, not a formality. Innosuisse expects a clear description of the innovative idea, the product or service promise, competitive advantage, team commitment, and supporting documents such as a pitch deck or company presentation, all submitted through the [official Initial Coaching page](https://www.innosuisse.admin.ch/de/initial-coaching-fur-start-up) and input portal.

<ol class="blog-steps">
  <li data-step="1"><h3>Check eligibility before you write</h3><p>Confirm the science-based innovation angle, Swiss company timing (five years since formation, ten in justified life-science cases), scalability, and team commitment match the published criteria.</p></li>
  <li data-step="2"><h3>Align the deck with the questions</h3><p>Each slide should answer something evaluators score: novelty, defensibility, market size, sustainability, and whether the team can execute.</p></li>
  <li data-step="3"><h3>Submit through the portal</h3><p>Missing documents or weak formal compliance delay or stop the process before substance is judged.</p></li>
  <li data-step="4"><h3>Prepare for the expert conversation</h3><p>A short discussion with an independent expert is part of evaluation. Know your risks and what you still need to prove in the market.</p></li>
  <li data-step="5"><h3>Plan coaching like a project</h3><p>If approved, choose a lead coach, book sessions against the voucher, and work toward the evaluation report instead of treating the voucher as open-ended advice.</p></li>
</ol>

A rejection can be resubmitted after you change what failed. Treat feedback as a checklist, not as a verdict on the idea forever.

## What the voucher does not prove

<aside class="blog-callout blog-callout--limit">
  <span class="blog-kicker">Limit</span>
  <p>Innosuisse coaching validates that your dossier met programme rules and that accredited coaches helped you structure next steps. It does not prove product-market fit, repeatable acquisition, or that paying customers will arrive. It also does not fund building the product.</p>
</aside>

Approval does not replace experiments with real users. Ideas as such cannot be protected; execution, contracts, and brand can ([IGE on ideas](https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden)). Coaching may help you see that gap clearly. It does not close it.

Investors may like seeing Innosuisse on the timeline. They still ask for evidence from the market, not from coaching hours alone.

## A made-up example: logistics software before the build

A made-up example: two founders in Bern plan B2B software for mid-sized freight forwarders. They have a GmbH less than two years old, a causal diagram of dispatch delays drawn from the founder's operations research background, and ten discovery calls that sound interested but no paid pilot. They apply for Initial Coaching with a twelve-slide deck: problem, science link, wedge segment, competition, business model sketch, team, risks, and three milestones for the next year.

Evaluation takes four weeks. They receive the full voucher, select a lead coach with manufacturing supply-chain experience, and spend the first two months rewriting the segment definition and pricing logic. Month four ends with a decision to run a concierge pilot with three firms before any custom code. The evaluation report lists milestones: signed pilot letters, one workflow automated manually, then a build budget discussion. They have sharper slides and a coach-backed plan. They still have no deployed app and no proof that strangers will pay at scale.

That outcome is a success for coaching. It is not yet validation you can bank in a fundraise deck without usage numbers.

## Who should apply, and who should skip

<div class="blog-pair">
  <div>
    <h3>Who this is for</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Swiss start-ups with an innovative, science-linked story and a team that can commit to the programme</li>
      <li>Founders who need structured challenge on model, market, and milestones before a large build</li>
      <li>Teams that will use coaching time to decide what to test next, not to outsource thinking</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div>
    <h3>Who should skip or wait</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Projects with no credible link to science or technology as Innosuisse defines it</li>
      <li>Teams that only need developers to execute a fixed spec; coaching will not pay those invoices</li>
      <li>Founders who already have strong usage data and mainly need scale; their question is growth mechanics, not Initial Coaching</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="blog-faq">
  <h3>Does Innosuisse start-up coaching pay for an MVP?</h3>
  <p>No. The voucher is for accredited coaching services. Product design, engineering, hosting, and paid acquisition stay on your balance sheet or on other programmes, if any apply.</p>
</div>

<div class="blog-faq">
  <h3>Is Initial Coaching the same as Innosuisse innovation projects?</h3>
  <p>No. Initial Coaching is the entry voucher programme for start-up coaching. Collaborative research and development funding follows different tracks and rules on Innosuisse's site.</p>
</div>

## When the product validation package fits

The [product validation package](/services/mvp-validation) fits when coaching has narrowed the question and you need a deployed product with analytics, session recordings, paid acquisition tests, and pivot room over several weeks, ending in a Go, Pivot, or Stop recommendation with honest limits on what the data show. Innosuisse coaching can help you decide whether that spend is the right next step. It does not replace the build or the measurement. If strangers still need to prove they will take a concrete action, start with a measured landing test, then book a discovery conversation when the open question is behaviour inside software, not slide wording alone.
