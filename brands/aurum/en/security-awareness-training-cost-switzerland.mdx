## Security awareness training cost Switzerland

Security awareness training cost in Switzerland for Aurum Avis Labs live packages is CHF 1'190 for up to 25 participants and CHF 2'190 for up to 50, with custom quotes above that. Price includes preparatory review, a live session of one to two hours, and a follow-up conversation weeks later, remotely or on site in Switzerland with travel included.

## What is included in the price

Preparatory review tailors examples to your sector. The live presenter covers phishing, payments, reporting and passwords. A follow-up conversation weeks later clears questions from daily work. ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A 6.3 or NIST mapping on request; no company certification.

## What the price is not

It is not a per-seat three-year LMS unless you buy that elsewhere. It is not certification. A preparatory phishing test is an extra (CHF 600 per the service page) and not included in the package price; decide in discovery whether you need it.

## CHF per head: when the maths misleads

Divide CHF 1'190 by 22 people in the room, not by 80 on payroll if only 22 attend. E-learning licences with no logins look cheap per head but often get zero attention. Compare total staff time and the service list, not only the headline figure.

## Compare total cost

Divide platform licences by actual logins before comparing to one live day. A full room often beats cheap seats nobody opens. Internal IT training looks cheap but burns preparation time without external examples.

## Hidden costs of other models

LMS licences without logins, internal prep without an experienced presenter, video plus live with no coordination. Budget one live package per year plus optional e-learning; do not hide follow-up in "internal hours" nobody has.

## Custom above fifty people

Several sessions, extra languages or multiple sites lead to custom quotes. Share realistic headcount, including leaders who stay in the room. A second site on the same day via remote sometimes avoids a plus upgrade if no more than fifty listen at once.

## Combined with privacy training

A combined security and privacy day saves travel; confirm price in discovery via the [security and privacy overview](/security). Security and privacy packages are separate services; the combined day is described and priced there.

## Who should not book

Enterprises with global awareness teams, firms wanting covert HR click scoring, or buyers needing only a recorded video with no follow-up should skip this package. Procurement that compares only CHF per head without session content, or a mature global programme with internal owners, gains little from this package.

## Example without hidden seats

Twenty-two people in the room fit the standard band CHF 1'190. If forty-eight attend, plus at CHF 2'190 is often cheaper than two separate mini-sessions on the same day. Count people who actually listen, not payroll.

## Travel, remote and staff time

Travel in Switzerland is included for on-site sessions per the service page. Remote saves travel, not preparation. Add participants' time away from work: two hours for 25 people is visible; a per-head licence with no attendance is not.

## Contract clarity

Put scope in writing: date, language, max participants, topic focus, mapping if wanted. Extras such as simulations separately. CHF 1'190 and CHF 2'190 refer to the live packages described here, not unlimited follow-up sessions in the same year.

## After purchase and proof

Follow-up is in the package; use it instead of hunting a second budget for "remedial" training. Ask for mapping before an audit, not the night before. Keep invitation, attendee list, topic summary and follow-up notes per year in one folder. When leadership changes, the folder outlasts chat threads.

## Transparency with staff

Tell people internally that live training is paid for and that covert monitoring does not follow. Inform staff before simulations if you commission them separately. That supports culture and justifies the price better than a silent LMS with no explanation.

## Onboarding new hires

One live day does not automatically cover every new starter in the year. Decide whether newcomers get a short video, a buddy conversation or the next live date. Budget that separately from the CHF 1'190 or CHF 2'190 package instead of pretending one day lasts three years.

## When two standard days beat custom

If you need thirty-five people but at two sites with eighteen each, two standard sessions plus remote dial-in can cost less than custom with travel on two days. Work both options in discovery instead of rounding headcount up to fifty.

## What procurement should check in the quote

Do you see date, on-site or remote, language, max participants, focus topics (phishing, payments, reporting) and a follow-up window? If follow-up is missing, the price is not comparable to a package that includes it. Ask before you mark a bid "more expensive".
