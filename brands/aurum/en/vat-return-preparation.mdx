## Prepare the VAT return on the firm's side, before anyone files

<p class="blog-lede">Prepare the Swiss VAT return by closing the period in the accounts first: every receipt in, turnover and purchases reconciled, input tax only with a receipt. A person still files.</p>

A workflow can chase the missing pieces and, where your software allows it, export a file the portal can import. This page does not calculate the tax you owe.

VAT-registered firms file and pay unsolicited, within 60 days after the settlement period ends. The federal tax administration states that on its pages for [extensions](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/mwst-fristverlaengerung-einreichung-abrechnung) and for [payment](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/mwst-bezahlen). There is no bill in the post. The payment page's own example treats the first quarter as due on 31 May. Your other periods follow the same 60-day rule from the end date you were assigned. This is not tax advice.

## What to have ready before the portal

The portal does not know which invoice is still in a mailbox. That is the firm's work. Agree with the fiduciary which of these lines they want from you and which they pull themselves.

<ul class="blog-checklist">
  <li>Sales and purchase invoices of the period booked, including the last day</li>
  <li>Bank and card movements reconciled to those invoices, with a note for what does not match</li>
  <li>Input tax only on receipts that show it, not on a guessed rate</li>
  <li>Credit notes and cancelled invoices in the same period as the original, or flagged if they are not</li>
  <li>Purchase invoices showing the supplier's UID or VAT number where your fiduciary requires it</li>
  <li>Import and acquisition tax: customs and import documents for the period, filed as your fiduciary asks (details: [ESTV VAT](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/mehrwertsteuer))</li>
  <li>Reverse-charge invoices from abroad, if you book such purchases, with the wording and VAT line your fiduciary expects</li>
  <li>Cash sales and tills: daily closings or Z-tickets for the period if you take payment at the counter</li>
  <li>Free deliveries, own use and other non-invoiced withdrawals that sit in the ledger for the period</li>
  <li>A list of open questions: mixed private use, a foreign supplier, a deposit, an exempt supply against a taxable one</li>
  <li>The name of the person who may submit in the portal, and the fiduciary's access if you use one</li>
</ul>

Missing supplier invoices are the usual delay. How those invoices fail to reach the ledger in the first place is covered in [invoice capture](/prozess-check/blog/invoice-capture-what-software-misses). The year-end folder, bank file and open items, is a related pack, covered in [documents for the fiduciary](/prozess-check/blog/year-end-documents-for-fiduciary). The VAT period is not always the calendar year. Do not wait for the annual folder if the period ends in March.

## What a workflow can prepare, and what it must not send

A workflow can list invoices dated in the period and not yet booked, and card lines with no receipt. It can remind the person who has the document, once, with the supplier and the date. It can export, if your accounting program does this, the period as an eCH-0217 file for import into the portal. The tax administration describes that import: you choose the file in MWST-Abrechnung pro, then you check the figures and complete what is missing. The check is the point. An export that nobody reads is how a wrong turnover gets filed.

The workflow does not click submit. Since 11 May 2026 the only place to file is MWST-Abrechnung pro in the ESTV portal. The older MWST-Abrechnung easy, with a filing code, stopped on 8 May 2026. Filing has been online since 1 January 2025. Login uses AGOV, through the myESTV service. The administration describes two roles, someone who fills the return and someone who submits it, and a way to invite the fiduciary. It also recommends that someone in the firm keeps access, so a change of fiduciary does not lock you out. Those steps are on the page about [filing VAT online](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/mwst-online-abrechnen).

## Deadline, extension, correction

If the 60 days are tight, the extension is online and free, inside the same portal service. The administration states that a request by any other channel is not granted, because the electronic request is part of filing. Firms on MWST-Abrechnung pro request it from the overview.

If, while drawing up the annual accounts, you find errors in returns already filed, the correction is a Berichtigungsabrechnung. The administration states that this has to be done, at the latest, in the return for the period that includes the 180th day after the end of that financial year. Do not bury a known error until the next annual meeting on the assumption it can wait forever.

Late payment can draw interest from the due date. The rate is set in a federal ordinance and reviewed each year. The payment page says interest is in principle only charged, or paid to you, from CHF 100. The percent is not repeated here, because it changes. Pay from the payment information on the filing, not from an old slip.

## A made-up example

A made-up example: a firm files quarterly. Ten days before the due date the accounts still miss three supplier invoices that appear as card payments. A list names the three suppliers and the person who usually receives that mail. Two PDFs arrive and are booked. The third is marked as a question, because nobody can find a receipt and the amount might be private. The fiduciary imports the period file into MWST-Abrechnung pro, checks the totals against the ledger, and leaves the private question off the input tax. A person in the firm, set up as submitter, files. The workflow never had the portal password, and it did not decide the private line.

## Who should skip a workflow

Skip it if the fiduciary already receives a complete ledger on a fixed day and the only remaining act is their submit button. Adding a second checklist that nobody reads does not move the due date. Skip it if you are not VAT-registered. Skip it if the ledger is months behind. The return cannot be prepared on top of a gap you already know about. Close the bookings first.

The recurring task worth a workflow is the same missing invoices each period, and the export that still needs a human check before it is filed.

## When a Process Check fits

A [Process Check](/prozess-check) fits when that chase is the recurring task: the same three suppliers missing every quarter, or a file prepared by hand because nobody trusts the export. You bring this one task. The meeting is 30 minutes on Microsoft Teams. You leave with a concrete case, whether it pays off, a price range and a rough duration. The half hour does not commit you to a project.
