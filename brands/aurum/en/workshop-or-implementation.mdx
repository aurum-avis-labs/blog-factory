Two purchases often get stuffed into the same sentence: "we have to do something with AI". One is a day where the team gets a shared language and shared rules. The other is a workflow that should run afterwards. Both are legitimate. They are not the same job.

## When a workshop fits

The [AI in Business workshop](/services/ai-in-business) is for leadership and specialist teams who already touch tools, or should, but without shared guardrails. You leave with what may go where internally, which one to three experiments you run yourselves, and which tools sit on the allow-list.

The job is "everyone should be allowed to use this without dumping customer data into a private chat". Not "this Monday report should assemble itself next week".

An engineering team that wants agents in the repo is a different format: [Agentic Coding](/services/agentic-coding). This article is about the office, not the repo.

## When implementation (via the Process Check) fits

The [Process Check](/prozess-check) is about one recurring workflow. 60 minutes, a case, a package size. After that you may buy a fixed-price build. You do not have to. Detail: [what the Process Check is](/blog/what-the-process-check-is).

The job is "this glue should run". Invoices, follow-ups, a report from several sources. Copilot can still be the right answer in between. When a licence is enough and when you need a built flow: [Copilot or build a workflow](/blog/copilot-or-build-a-workflow).

## The fork, short

Need rules for the whole team: workshop. Need a bounded workflow with existing input: Process Check. Need a product idea or an MVP: that is a different offer, not this cluster.
