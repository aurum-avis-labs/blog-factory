Many Swiss SMEs already use ChatGPT, often on private Plus accounts, sometimes with customer names in prompts. **ChatGPT Business** is the company workspace that ends that pattern and adds admin, billing, and a clearer data boundary. This guide covers plans, pricing, and a sensible rollout. It is not legal advice.

## What ChatGPT Business is

OpenAI renamed ChatGPT Team to ChatGPT Business on 29 August 2025. It is the same product line for teams of two or more: a shared workspace, central administration, SAML/SSO and MFA, and connectors to Google Workspace, Slack, GitHub and Microsoft 365. By default, OpenAI does not train models on your Business data.

Enterprise is the next tier, with controls such as SCIM, broader residency options and support SLAs. OpenAI does not publish a public list price; you talk to sales.

As of October 2026, [OpenAI Business Pricing](https://openai.com/business/chatgpt-pricing/) lists these USD prices (excluding VAT and FX):

| Seat | Billed annually | Billed monthly |
|------|-----------------|----------------|
| Standard | USD 20 per user per month | USD 25 |
| Premium | USD 100 | USD 125 |

Premium provides about five times the usage of Standard and no five-hour limit. You can mix seats. Minimum: two seats. Business covers roughly 2 to 200 paid seats; beyond that, Enterprise is typical.

In CHF, convert at the spot rate and allow for card fees. OpenAI does not publish a fixed CHF price list.

## Free and Plus are the wrong company channel

On Free and Plus, OpenAI may use content for training unless someone opts out. The opt-out sits on a private account. The company neither controls retention nor offboarding. Customer files, named quotes, medical notes and payroll lists do not belong there. More detail: [ChatGPT and customer data](/prozess-check/blog/chatgpt-customer-data-swiss-dsg). The [FDPIC](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/de/ki-und-datenschutz) states that the Swiss FADP applies to AI-assisted processing and is technology-neutral.

ChatGPT Business changes three things SMEs care about:

1. Workspace instead of private accounts: seats, roles, central billing.
2. No training on your Business data by default.
3. Admin controls before the next wave of shadow AI.

Business does not replace a short allow-list or a rule for what may enter the chat. It provides the technical frame.

## Which plan you need

**Business Standard** is enough when five to thirty people draft text, summarise meetings and use internal templates, without all-day high-volume coding.

**Business Premium** fits the few people who constantly hit limits: developers on Codex, research roles, very long contexts.

**Enterprise** when you need residency terms, SCIM, richer audit logs or volume discounts. Many SMEs under 80 people start on Business.

## Checklist before you buy (Switzerland)

1. Decide which roles need a seat. Not everyone in the company.
2. Write three allowed and three forbidden prompt examples (customer name, AHV, health data).
3. Name an owner for seats and offboarding.
4. Review processor terms and cross-border disclosure under [Art. 16 FADP](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de).
5. After 30 days, measure how many recurring tasks still run by hand.

Point 5 is the real lever. Licences alone do not change operations. See [AI in operations, nothing tangible](/prozess-check/blog/ai-in-operations-nothing-tangible).

## What Business does not solve

Business does not reconcile invoices or build a weekly report. Automating a concrete workflow needs integration. Workshop rules versus build: [Workshop or implementation](/prozess-check/blog/workshop-or-implementation).

## FAQ

<div class="blog-faq">
  <h3>Is ChatGPT Plus enough for a small team?</h3>
  <p>Not for shared company work with customer data. Plus is a single-user plan without workspace admin. From two people with company use, Business is the right entry.</p>
</div>

<div class="blog-faq">
  <h3>Does OpenAI train on our Business chats?</h3>
  <p>According to OpenAI, not by default for Business and Enterprise. Free and Plus may allow training unless opted out. Check the current privacy page before rollout.</p>
</div>

<div class="blog-faq">
  <h3>Must the data stay in Switzerland?</h3>
  <p>OpenAI is not Swiss hosting. Business adds admin and contract framing, not automatic Swiss residency. If you need data location in Switzerland, evaluate sovereign platforms or self-hosted models.</p>
</div>
