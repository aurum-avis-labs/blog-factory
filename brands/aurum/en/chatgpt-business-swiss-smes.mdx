**The short answer:** ChatGPT Business, formerly ChatGPT Team, is OpenAI's company workspace for 2 to 200 people. A standard seat costs USD 20 per user per month on annual billing, or USD 25 billed monthly. OpenAI does not train on workspace data by default, and you can sign a data processing addendum (DPA). Enterprise adds SCIM, custom retention and data residency in Europe, which OpenAI defines as the EEA plus Switzerland, but only through a sales quote. For most Swiss SMEs, Business is the right starting point, provided you decide first which data may go into the chat. Everything below is as of October 2026.

This guide is for owners, managing directors and IT leads at small and mid-sized Swiss companies who want to move staff off personal ChatGPT accounts. It is not legal advice.

## What ChatGPT Business is

OpenAI renamed ChatGPT Team to ChatGPT Business on 29 August 2025. According to the [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8792828-chatgpt-business-overview), the rename did not change the plan itself. If you search for "ChatGPT Team" today, you end up at the same product.

A Business workspace belongs to the company, not to the individual user. It includes:

- central billing and an admin console with roles
- SAML SSO, MFA and domain verification
- connectors to Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Slack and GitHub
- projects, custom GPTs, deep research, ChatGPT agent and Codex
- no training on your business data by default

OpenAI's [pricing page](https://openai.com/business/chatgpt-pricing/) positions Business "for teams of 2–200 employees". You pay by credit card. Non-profits can get up to 75% off through OpenAI for Nonprofits, according to the pricing page, which is worth knowing for associations and foundations. If you need invoicing, bank transfer or purchase orders, the Help Center points you to a contracted offering such as Enterprise.

## How much ChatGPT Business costs

OpenAI's list prices as of October 2026, per user per month:

| Plan | Billed annually | Billed monthly | Who it suits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business, standard seat | USD 20 | USD 25 | most staff |
| Business, premium seat | USD 100 | USD 125 | heavy users: five times the usage of a standard seat, no 5-hour limit, according to OpenAI |
| Enterprise | on request | on request | more than 200 seats or strict compliance requirements |

Sources: [OpenAI business pricing](https://openai.com/business/chatgpt-pricing/) and the [Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8792828-chatgpt-business-overview).

A workspace needs at least two paid seats: two standard, two premium, or one of each. You can mix seat types and reassign seats later. API usage is not included and is billed separately.

**What about Swiss francs?** OpenAI lists prices in USD and notes that pricing "may vary depending on your country and currency type". You will only see at checkout whether you are charged in CHF or USD. If you are billed in dollars, budget for the exchange rate and any foreign-currency card fees, and ask your accountant how VAT applies to the subscription.

A worked example for an eight-person fiduciary firm: seven standard seats plus one premium seat for the person who analyses long documents every day. On annual billing that comes to 7 × 20 + 1 × 100 = USD 240 a month, or USD 2,880 a year.

## Business vs Enterprise vs Plus

Plus is a personal subscription. It works for one person or a sole trader. A team that handles client data needs three things Plus lacks: a contract with the company, central control over access, and a training exclusion that applies to everyone.

| | Plus (personal) | Business | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Training on your content | possible unless the user turns it off | off by default | off by default |
| Data processing addendum (DPA) | no | yes | yes |
| SSO and MFA | – | yes | yes |
| SCIM, role-based access control | – | no | yes |
| Retention controls | – | yes, set by admins | yes, custom policies |
| Data residency in Europe (incl. Switzerland) | – | rolling out gradually, not for everyone | yes, for eligible new workspaces |
| ISO 27001/27017/27018/27701 | – | no | yes |
| SOC 2 Type 2 | – | yes | yes |
| Payment | card | card | invoicing available |

Sources: [pricing page](https://openai.com/business/chatgpt-pricing/), [Enterprise privacy](https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy/), [Data controls FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-data-controls-faq).

**Rule of thumb:** choose Business if you need up to 200 seats, can pay by card and do not have to guarantee European data residency in a contract. Choose Enterprise if you need SCIM for automated joiners and leavers, require ISO certificates, want to pay by invoice, or must keep data in Europe.

## What happens to your data

### No training by default

On its [Enterprise privacy page](https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy/), updated 8 January 2026, OpenAI states: "We do not train our models on your data by default." This covers Business, Enterprise and the API. OpenAI names one exception itself: if you explicitly share data, for example through feedback, that content can be used for training. The [Data controls FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-data-controls-faq) shows how far that goes: a thumbs up or down can make the entire conversation it belongs to available for training. Put that in your internal rules.

On Free, Go and Plus, each user decides: the "Improve the model for everyone" toggle under Settings → Data controls determines whether their chats are used for training. A toggle on a personal account is not a company policy.

### Who can read your chats

This is often overlooked: in Business, workspace admins can "view, access, export, and delete" employees' conversations, according to OpenAI. Tell your team before you roll out. At OpenAI itself, authorised employees can access conversations for engineering support, abuse investigations and legal compliance, and specialised contractors bound by confidentiality can access them solely to review for abuse.

### Retention

Admins decide how long conversations are kept. OpenAI removes deleted conversations within 30 days, unless the law requires longer retention or keeping them is reasonably necessary to protect the service or third parties.

### Data residency: Business vs Enterprise

This changed in 2026, and many guides are out of date:

- **Enterprise:** eligible new workspaces can store content at rest in the "Europe (EEA + Switzerland)" region. Europe also supports inference residency, meaning GPU processing stays in the region too. Data residency is included at no extra cost on Enterprise ([OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9903489-data-residency-and-inference-residency-for-chatgpt)).
- **Business:** a [new Help Center article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001418-where-your-chatgpt-business-content-is-stored) says you can pick a storage region at checkout, but the feature is "rolling out gradually and is not yet available to all customers". Two caveats: the region covers storage only, not where requests are processed, and a copy of every prompt and response is kept in the US for a limited time for abuse monitoring. The pricing page still lists data residency as "No" for Business.

In practice, assume that Business processes your data in the US. Whether AI has to run in Switzerland at all is covered in [Does AI have to run in Switzerland?](/prozess-check/blog/ai-data-location-switzerland)

## ChatGPT Business and the Swiss FADP

The [FDPIC](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/de/ki-und-datenschutz), Switzerland's data protection authority, states that the Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP, often called revDSG or nDSG) is technology-neutral and applies directly to AI-based processing. No separate AI law is needed for that. Four points matter when an SME rolls out ChatGPT Business.

**1. Processing on your behalf (Art. 9 FADP).** When staff enter personal data, OpenAI processes it on your behalf. Under [Art. 9 FADP](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de) this requires a contract, and you must satisfy yourself that the provider can guarantee data security. OpenAI offers a [Data Processing Addendum](https://openai.com/policies/data-processing-addendum/) for Business, Enterprise and the API, signed via a web form. For customers based in Switzerland, the contracting entity is OpenAI Ireland Ltd. More on these contracts: [Data processing agreements under the FADP](/security/blog/data-processing-agreement-under-the-fadp).

**2. Transfers abroad (Art. 16 and 17 FADP).** Where OpenAI Ireland passes data outside the EEA and Switzerland, the DPA relies on standard contractual clauses (SCCs). The FDPIC recognises the EU SCCs for Switzerland. Since 15 September 2024, the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework has also covered certified US companies. Check whether a specific provider is certified on the official [DPF list](https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov/list). More: [Transferring personal data to the United States](/security/blog/transferring-personal-data-to-the-united-states).

**3. Notice and records.** Your privacy notice must name the countries data goes to (Art. 19(4) FADP). Your record of processing activities must list the country and safeguards (Art. 12 FADP), although companies with fewer than 250 employees and low-risk processing may be exempt.

**4. What still stays out of the chat.** A DPA does not clear the tool for everything. Sensitive personal data such as health data (Art. 5(c) FADP) and anything under professional secrecy needs its own assessment. For medical practices, law firms and fiduciaries, that is the real hurdle, not the licence. A simple traffic-light rule is in [ChatGPT and customer data](/prozess-check/blog/chatgpt-customer-data-swiss-dsg).

## Rollout checklist for SMEs

How to roll out the licence without creating a new shadow-AI problem:

1. **Take stock.** Ask openly who uses which AI tools on which account. Don't assign blame, or you'll only hear half the story.
2. **Plan seats.** Not everyone needs one. Start with the roles that write, summarise or research every day. Premium only for the few who hit limits.
3. **Sign the DPA.** Before the first prompt that contains personal data, using OpenAI's form. File it with your contracts.
4. **Set data rules.** Three allowed and three forbidden examples from your own work: "Rewrite a quote without the client's name: yes. Summarise a doctor's note: no." Template: [AI policy for SMEs](/prozess-check/blog/ai-policy-template-sme).
5. **Configure the workspace.** Turn on SSO, set retention, enable apps and connectors only where needed, and switch off public GPT sharing.
6. **Brief the team.** Admins can read chats, feedback buttons can share data, and personal accounts are off-limits for company data.
7. **Update your privacy notice and records.** OpenAI as processor, countries, safeguards.
8. **Retire personal accounts.** Have staff delete company data from personal Plus accounts, then update the allow-list: [Approving AI tools](/prozess-check/blog/approve-ai-tools-allow-list).
9. **Plan offboarding.** Decide who removes the seat and exports or deletes chats when someone leaves. Without SCIM, this is a manual step.
10. **Measure after 30 days.** How many seats are actually used? Which recurring tasks are still done by hand?

Step 10 matters most. A licence makes people faster at writing. It does not capture receipts, move data between systems or produce the weekly report on its own. That takes a built workflow.

## ChatGPT Business or Microsoft Copilot?

Many SMEs already run Microsoft 365 and wonder whether Copilot is enough. The short answer: it depends on where the work happens.

| | ChatGPT Business | Microsoft 365 Copilot Business |
|---|---|---|
| Price (October 2026) | USD 20 per user per month, annual, ChatGPT only | bundle price including the Microsoft 365 licence: CHF 19.03 with Business Standard, CHF 25.92 with Business Premium, per user per month, annual, excl. VAT |
| Strength | open-ended tasks, research, writing, analysis, Codex | works inside Outlook, Word, Excel and Teams on your own emails and files |
| Training | off by default | not used to train foundation models, according to Microsoft |
| Data location | US; Europe only reliably on Enterprise | EU Data Boundary for EU users; for customers outside the EU, queries may be processed in the US, the EU or other regions |

Sources: [Microsoft Switzerland pricing](https://www.microsoft.com/de-ch/microsoft-365-copilot/pricing), [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/microsoft-365/microsoft-365-copilot-privacy).

A word of caution on price: the Microsoft figures include the full Microsoft 365 licence with Outlook, Teams and SharePoint. If you already pay for M365, compare the difference to your current plan with ChatGPT's USD 20, not the whole bundle price.

If your people live in Outlook and Excel and your files sit in SharePoint, Copilot is often the more natural fit. For open research, drafting and analysis, ChatGPT is usually stronger. Neither replaces an automated workflow: see [A Copilot licence, or a built workflow?](/prozess-check/blog/copilot-or-build-a-workflow)

## FAQ

<div class="blog-faq">
  <h3>What is the difference between ChatGPT Team and ChatGPT Business?</h3>
  <p>None. OpenAI renamed ChatGPT Team to ChatGPT Business on 29 August 2025. Existing Team workspaces became Business workspaces without any data migration.</p>
</div>

<div class="blog-faq">
  <h3>What can I do with ChatGPT Business?</h3>
  <p>Everything ChatGPT does, inside a company workspace: draft quotes and emails, summarise long contracts or minutes, explain spreadsheet analyses, research market questions with deep research, and build custom GPTs for recurring tasks such as payment reminders at a fiduciary firm. Connectors let it work with files in Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace.</p>
</div>

<div class="blog-faq">
  <h3>Is ChatGPT Business better than Plus?</h3>
  <p>For a company, yes. The models are similar; the difference is the contract: no training by default, a DPA, an admin console and SSO. Plus is a personal subscription with no company agreement.</p>
</div>

<div class="blog-faq">
  <h3>Can I cancel ChatGPT Business monthly?</h3>
  <p>Yes, on monthly billing (USD 25 per standard seat). The cheaper annual plan (USD 20) commits you for a year. Start monthly until you know how many seats get used.</p>
</div>

<div class="blog-faq">
  <h3>How much does ChatGPT Enterprise cost?</h3>
  <p>OpenAI does not publish a list price. Enterprise is negotiated with sales, priced per seat or through credits or tokens depending on the agreement.</p>
</div>

<div class="blog-faq">
  <h3>Is ChatGPT Business data stored in Switzerland?</h3>
  <p>No. Residency in the "Europe (EEA + Switzerland)" region is reliably available on Enterprise. On Business, a region choice is rolling out gradually, covers storage only, and a copy is kept in the US for abuse monitoring.</p>
</div>

<div class="blog-faq">
  <h3>Can a Swiss SME use ChatGPT Business in line with the FADP?</h3>
  <p>In principle, yes: with a DPA, a privacy notice that discloses the transfer, and clear rules on which data may be entered. Sensitive data and professional secrets need a separate assessment.</p>
</div>
