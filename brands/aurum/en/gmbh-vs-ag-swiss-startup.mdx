<p class="blog-lede">For most Swiss software startups, a GmbH is the default: CHF 20'000 Stammkapital fully paid in at formation, limited liability for shareholders, and a name that partners and app stores recognise. An AG fits when you need registered shares that move without a Gesellschafter vote on every transfer, or when your cap table and future investors expect that structure, with CHF 100'000 nominal capital and at least CHF 50'000 paid in at the start.</p>

## GmbH vs AG for a Swiss startup

You choose between GmbH and AG when the product is real enough to invoice, employ someone, or enroll Apple and Google as a company, not when you are still proving demand in a personal sandbox. Both forms need a notarial deed, statutes, auditors where the law requires them, and entry in the commercial register. Neither form replaces a validation plan or fixes weak unit economics.

This is for founders who already shipped a Lovable, Bolt, v0, Replit, Cursor, or Claude Code prototype and are deciding which legal shell should own the repository, the brand, and the store accounts. How that product is built in Switzerland sits in [startup software development in Switzerland](/blog/startup-software-development-switzerland). Privacy paperwork for a first app is a separate track in [privacy requirements for a Swiss MVP](/blog/privacy-requirements-swiss-mvp) and [App Store privacy labels](/blog/app-store-privacy-labels). This page compares the two capital companies; it is not tax or legal advice.

## What changes for a product team

The form matters for who appears on contracts, who can sign for the company, and how cleanly you can move equity later. It does not tell you whether anyone will pay for the app.

<div class="blog-pair">
  <div>
    <h3>GmbH tends to fit</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>One or two founders and a small team, no imminent large share round</li>
      <li>You want the lowest statutory locked capital that still limits personal liability</li>
      <li>Shareholders accept that Stammanteil transfers usually need Gesellschafter approval</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div>
    <h3>AG tends to fit</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>You expect frequent share transfers, options, or investors who insist on Aktien mechanics</li>
      <li>You can fund the higher minimum paid-in capital without starving the product budget</li>
      <li>You accept more formal governance (Verwaltungsrat, Generalversammlung) from day one</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

Shareholders in a GmbH are named in the commercial register. That transparency is fine for a two-founder studio; it is awkward if you wanted silent co-founders. An AG's statutes set how registered shares are transferred, but banks and compliance still identify who matters. Do not pick AG only for formality without reading what your bank and future investors will ask for.

## Code, contractors, and the entity that owns the repo

Software written by employees in the course of work belongs to the employer under Swiss copyright practice ([Art. 17 URG](https://www.rentschpartner.ch/ict-law/schutz-von-software)). Contractors and agencies keep rights until a written assignment says otherwise. A vibe-coded MVP in a personal GitHub account does not move to the company because you filed the GmbH yesterday. Align repository, domain, App Store Connect organisation, and assignment clauses with the entity you chose.

Apple organisation enrollment expects a legal entity name that matches the register, not a product nickname ([enrollment support](https://developer.apple.com/support/enrollment/)). Google Play organisation profiles follow the same principle. The legal form is housekeeping that unblocks those accounts; it does not pass App Store review or prove retention.

## Cost and friction beyond the capital block

Both forms pay notary fees, register fees, and ongoing bookkeeping and audit rules that depend on size. KMU guidance describes GmbH founding costs as somewhat lower than AG; exact francs vary by canton and advisor. Budget the admin line separately from cloud, ads, and contractor spend. Locking CHF 20'000 or CHF 50'000 in share capital is not the same as having runway for twelve weeks of learning.

Ordinary revision thresholds (balance sheet, turnover, headcount) apply to both forms at the levels in the Code of Obligations. Early startups usually sit below them, but the Revisionsstelle is still part of the founding checklist on the KMU pages.

## What neither GmbH nor AG proves

A registered company can still ship a product nobody wants. The form does not replace interviews, a deployed build, or paid acquisition with a clear metric. It also does not force investors to fund you; it only gives them a standard instrument to buy into.

If you are pre-revenue and the hypothesis is still "will anyone sign up", stay in a lighter structure until the measure needs invoicing, payroll, or a company developer account. If you are post-prototype and every week of delay pushes IP and store identity into personal accounts, incorporation timing is a risk question, not a badge of seriousness.

<div class="blog-faq">
  <h3>Can we stay sole proprietorship while we use Lovable in production?</h3>
  <p>Some founders do for a short browser-only test. Once you handle other people's personal data at scale, pay contractors as a team, or need the store seller name to match a cap table entity, the sole prop label becomes a bottleneck. The trigger is liability and contracts, not the tool brand.</p>
</div>

<div class="blog-faq">
  <h3>Do we need an AG before we talk to Swiss venture capital?</h3>
  <p>Not automatically. Many early rounds use convertible instruments or GmbH shares. Some funds prefer AG mechanics later. Ask the lead investor what they need for closing instead of incorporating AG on speculation.</p>
</div>

## When a discovery conversation fits

Use the [contact page](/contact) when the legal entity, the codebase, and a measurable validation window need to line up: you have a deployed or deployable product, you know what you are trying to learn in the next quarter, and you want to align incorporation timing with build, analytics, and store setup. Skip booking if you only need a notary quote, if the product name and shareholder split change every week, or if you have not decided whether the next step is still a smoke test. Choosing GmbH or AG is admin; the open question is still whether the product earns the capital you lock in.
