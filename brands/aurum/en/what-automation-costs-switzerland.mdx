## What the Check costs

For the first 10 calls the launch price is CHF 49. After that the Process Check is CHF 99.

If you later book an implementation package, the Check price is credited in full. It is then not an extra invoice on top.

Money back only if the call took place and no tangible automation case came out of the hour. No call, no refund. If a case exists and you still do not build, the Check price stands until a package triggers the credit.

What happens in the hour: [what the Process Check is](/blog/what-the-process-check-is).

## Packages as guide prices, not hourly run-on

Implementation after the Check is fixed price once the case is bounded. The sizes on the [offer page](/prozess-check) are guide prices, internally sized around about CHF 200/h of effort. What you buy is still a package, not a timesheet:

| Package | Duration | Guide price |
|---------|----------|-------------|
| Small | 2–3 days | CHF 3,200–4,800 |
| Medium | 7 days | CHF 14,000 |
| Large | 10 days | CHF 20,000 |
| Max | from ~12 days | from CHF 25,000 |

The final size is in the Check, not in this article. Buying a package without a case means guessing the scope.

## What shows up monthly afterwards

Token cost sits with the customer (your model subscription or API). Hosting as a guide is CHF 50–150 per month and can be cancelled. That is operations, not the build.
