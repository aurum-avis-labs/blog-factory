## What automation costs before you build

For a Swiss SME, the amount you can pin down before a build is the Process Check at CHF 99. A build, if you want one, is a fixed-price package chosen after that hour, from Small through Max, at the guide prices below. Hosting afterwards is a guide of CHF 50 to 150 a month. Anything more precise than that, for your workflow, is guessed until the case is written down.

This is for owners, ops and finance leads who have a recurring office workflow and need a number they can take to a partner or a board. It is a poor fit if you want a day rate for "some AI", a workshop, or a quote to build a product. The packages are for one bounded workflow, after the Check has named it.

What happens in the hour, and what you leave with, is in [what the Process Check is](/blog/what-the-process-check-is). The offer, in short form, is on the [Process Check page](/prozess-check).

## The Check: CHF 99 for the hour

The Process Check costs CHF 99. That is the price of the hour, not a deposit on a larger project until you decide to build.

If you later book an implementation package, the Check price is credited in full. You do not pay the hour again on top of the package. The credit applies when a package is booked. It does not turn the Check into a free sample.

Money back only if the call took place and no tangible automation case came out of the hour. No call, no refund. If a case exists and you still do not build, the Check price stands. The refund is tied to a missing case, not to a decision against implementation. The amount stays the Check price until a package triggers the credit.

You pick a slot, pay through Stripe, and the appointment is confirmed after payment. The same path is [book a Process Check](/prozess-check/buchen).

## Packages as guide prices

Implementation after the Check is a fixed price once the case is bounded. The sizes on the offer page are guide prices. They are sized internally around CHF 200 an hour of effort. You still buy a package, not a timesheet, and you should not multiply days by that rate and treat the product as hourly billing. The final size is the one named in the Check, on your workflow.

| Package | Duration | Guide price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small | 2 to 3 days | CHF 3'200 to 4'800 |
| Medium | 7 days | CHF 14'000 |
| Large | 10 days | CHF 20'000 |
| Max | from about 12 days | from CHF 25'000 |

How to read the bands without pretending they are a quote for your firm:

Small is the guide for a narrow glue that is already repetitive. Two stable exports into one known sheet. A reminder that opens a draft a person still sends. Few systems, few exceptions, one owner.

Medium is the guide when the path is still one workflow, but more than a paste: several sources, a review queue, rules you can list. Invoices that arrive from more than one portal, with a person confirming before anything is booked, often lands here once the path is clear. It can also be a harder Small, or it can fall apart into "not yet" if the exceptions are not listed. The hour decides.

Large and Max are the guides when that same single workflow touches more systems, more permissions and more written rules, and the build runs longer. They are not a bundle of five unrelated ideas. Five topics in one package means five half-results. The Check stays on one process. If the list is longer, you pick one and the rest wait.

Buying a package with no case means guessing the scope. The guide table cannot see your portals, your logins or the column that changes name every week.

## What you pay monthly after it is live

Token cost sits with you: your model subscription or API, on the account the firm already chose. We do not fold a vendor's token price into the package, because that bill moves with how often the flow runs and which model you use.

Hosting, as a guide, is CHF 50 to 150 per month, and it can be cancelled. That is the running cost after the build. The final figure is in the Check, the same way the package size is. Treat the band as a planning number for a small workflow host.

Licences you already pay stay yours. Microsoft 365, bexio, Abacus, a CRM. A package does not replace those subscriptions, and this page does not price them.

## Two scopes, so the bands mean something

These illustrations show the shape of a scope. The Check is what places your file in a band.

A Monday report from two stable exports, eight columns that have not moved in a month, one person who checks totals before send. That is the shape of Small: a few days, a start and an end you can say in one sentence, systems you can already log into.

Invoices from five portals, a Teams approval, and a match to a purchase order that lives in another tool, with a human confirm before bexio or Abacus gets a booking. That is a larger shape. More logins, a queue, rules about VAT and project codes when they are unclear. It may be a Medium or it may be the wrong workflow until the exceptions are written down. The guide price of a larger band exists so you do not budget a two-day paste job for a five-portal path.

The reason the fixed price waits until after the case: those two Mondays are not the same purchase. A price picked from a menu, before anyone has seen the file, is a guess with a Swiss franc sign on it.

## What stays outside the package

You are here for a number on one workflow. Closing, odd VAT positions and the year-end stay with the trustee or with the person who already signs them. A package can move a PDF from a portal into a review queue. Microsoft 365, bexio, Abacus and the CRM stay subscriptions you already pay.

A product idea or an MVP sits outside this cluster. The figure on this page is for a bounded build after the Check.

Walk away from a package, including ours, when the workflow has no owner, the columns change every week, or you hope the build will decide which process matters. Pay for the Check if you want that answer. Do not pay for twelve days of construction in order to find out.
