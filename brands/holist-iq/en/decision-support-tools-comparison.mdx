Teams already own spreadsheets and BI. The question is which representation fits **recurring system problems** without hiding feedback.

| Approach | Best for | Weak when |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| BI dashboards | Tracking metrics | Explaining co-movement |
| Spreadsheets | Scenarios, budgets | Shared mental models |
| Causal loop maps | Feedback, delays, alignment | One-off sequencing |

<Image src={img2} alt="Icons comparing dashboards spreadsheets and loop tools" width={500} quality={80} class="w-full" />

Evaluate loop semantics, collaboration on disputed links, living models, decision trace, and governance. Dashboards answer *what moved*; loop maps answer *what keeps moving it*.
