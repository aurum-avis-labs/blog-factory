Pair scenario thinking with causal loops to see which loops strengthen when you pull one lever.

## Five steps

1. Baseline map (≤15 variables)
2. Two plausible scenarios (full vs partial adoption)
3. Walk each scenario around the loops
4. Name 30–60 day early warning signals
5. One-page decision memo
