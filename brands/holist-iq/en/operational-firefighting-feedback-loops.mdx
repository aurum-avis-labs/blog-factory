Ops leaders often joke that their job is putting out fires. Operational firefighting loops explain why heroic effort can *increase* future fire load.

## What operational firefighting looks like

On-call pages cluster around the same services, runbooks grow but incident frequency does not fall, and capacity planning assumes permanent reactive slack. The team is busy; the system is unchanged.

## The core reinforcing loop

Incidents consume engineering time → preventive work slips → fragility rises → incident rate increases → visible firefighting is rewarded → short fixes dominate → back to incidents.

<Image src={img2} alt="Reinforcing loop linking incidents deferred prevention and rising fragility" width={500} quality={80} class="w-full" />

Track **repeat incident rate** and **preventive vs reactive time**, not MTTR alone.

## Breaking the loop

Blameless postmortems with loop sketches, protected prevention capacity, incentives for eliminated recurrence, architecture reviews after repeat pages.
