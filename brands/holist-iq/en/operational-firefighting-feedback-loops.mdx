Ops teams often celebrate heroic clears while backlog reforms overnight. **Operational firefighting feedback loops** describe how urgency rewards visible saves and starves prevention.

## The reinforcing firefighting loop

Incident visibility → heroics rewarded → prevention time cut → more incidents → more visibility.

## The balancing loop that never catches up

Backlog → temporary surge hiring or overtime → throughput rises → backlog falls → surge ends → backlog returns if root loops unchanged.

## Delays hide prevention payoff

Prevention metrics move slowly. Firefighting metrics move daily. Leadership attention follows the fast clock unless you instrument leading indicators.

See [delays in feedback loops](/blog/delays-in-feedback-loops).

## Break the loop without blame

Shift recognition to prevented incidents, fund small prevention slices protected from redeployment, map one structural link per quarter.

## When firefighting is appropriate

True emergencies and safety incidents require surge mode. The issue is chronic recurrence, not occasional spikes.

## Mid-step: time-box last week
