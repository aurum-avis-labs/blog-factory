When teams stay stuck on symptoms, **symptom to structure** elicitation moves them to testable loops. Five steps keep blame out and delays visible.

## Step 1: Anchor the recurring metric

Write the outcome and last three times it returned. If it never returned, stop: use brainstorming tools instead.

## Step 2: Symptom wall, then neutral rename

List visible pains. Rename each into a measurable or observable factor.

## Step 3: Story links before polarity

Tell stories along arrows. Only then mark +/- . Disagreement becomes alternate dotted links.

## Step 4: Close loops and mark delays

Name R/B loops. Add delay notes on the longest administrative or trust link.

## Step 5: Choose one link to instrument

Pick a leading indicator and owner for a six-week test.

## Common failures

Skipping recurrence check, too many variables, debating software before structure.

## Mid-step: retro one incident

Run steps 1 to 3 on a past outage or policy surprise.

## Frequently asked questions

### How is this different from root cause analysis?

RCA targets one incident. This method targets recurring metrics and adaptation.
