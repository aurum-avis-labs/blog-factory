Sustainability pledges fail when lock-in loops outmuscle annual targets. Efficiency gains get eaten by rebound; pilots stall at scale; suppliers revert when price pressure returns.

## Lock-in patterns

Infrastructure sunk costs, supplier contracts, convenience defaults, legacy subsidies — each maintained by loops, not single villains.

## Rebound loop

Efficiency lowers unit cost → demand rises → emissions flat → confidence in efficiency-only falls → structural budgets shrink → rebound continues.
