**Sustainability transition feedback loops** explain why incremental wins stall: reinforcing lock-in from assets, habits, and politics competes with balancing pressures that move slowly.

## Lock-in reinforcing loops

Sunk infrastructure → continued use → scale economies → political support for status quo → deferred retrofit investment.

## Balancing loops that arrive late

Carbon price or regulation → efficiency investment → emissions fall → but only after capital cycles and supplier retooling delays.

## Map physical and belief variables together

Emissions link to operations; perceived feasibility links to adoption. Omit either and the map misleads.

## Honest limits of CLDs in climate work

Qualitative loops clarify stories; quantified stock-flow may be required for budgets. See [stock and flow vs causal loops](/blog/stock-and-flow-vs-causal-loops).
