Executives lack **shared structure** under time pressure. Extract a one-page brief without exporting every node.

## Template

1. Outcome and trend (2 sentences)
2. Two dominant loops (plain names)
3. One leverage point
4. Risks and 90-day delays
5. Single ask (decision, budget, or evidence gate)

Attach the full map as appendix.
