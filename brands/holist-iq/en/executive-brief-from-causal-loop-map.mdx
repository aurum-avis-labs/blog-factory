Executives rarely need the full wall of sticky notes. They need a **executive brief from a causal loop map**: one page, dominant loops, delays, trade-offs, and a recommended test with owners.

## Page structure that works

1. Outcome and decision date
2. Dominant R and B loops in plain language
3. Longest delay on the critical path
4. Options with loop impact, not feature lists
5. Recommended test, owner, leading indicator

## Translate notation out, keep structure in

Move +/- to appendix if needed. Keep loop names in the body so readers can ask structural questions.

## Attach evidence and dissent

Include dotted alternate links skeptics proposed. Executives decide with visible uncertainty, not false consensus.

## Workflow with Holist-IQ
