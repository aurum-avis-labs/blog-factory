Misalignment is often incompatible stories, not incompatible people. A **shared causal map** gives stakeholders one diagram, shared variable names, and visible loops so debates move from anecdotes to structure.

## Contract the map must satisfy

- One outcome header everyone accepts
- Neutral variable names (no blame labels)
- At least two named loops
- Delay notes on contested links
- One decision or test attached

## Facilitation sequence for mixed seniority

Executives define outcome and decision date. Operators supply links. Skeptics mark dotted alternate polarities.

## Governance: who may edit what

Assign map owner, link owners for high-risk edges, and version notes when incentives change.

## Honest limits

Maps do not replace politics. They make trade-offs explicit: which loop each stakeholder prioritizes.

## Mid-step: alignment pre-read

Send a one-page variable list before the session. Collect vetoed names asynchronously.

## Frequently asked questions

### How is this different from a RACI?

RACI assigns tasks. Causal maps assign causal claims about why metrics move.
