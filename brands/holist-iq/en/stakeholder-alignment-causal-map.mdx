Stakeholder meetings fail when everyone argues solutions while picturing different systems. A shared causal map aligns stakeholders on *structure first*.

## Session agenda (75 minutes)

Metric charter (10m), variable elicitation (25m), link and loop building (25m), dispute register (10m), decision gate (5m).

<Image src={img2} alt="Stakeholder workshop causal map with loop ownership" width={500} quality={80} class="w-full" />

## Decision rules

No solution proposals until reinforcing and balancing loops are named. Dissent stays on the map as dotted links. Update the map when data arrives.
