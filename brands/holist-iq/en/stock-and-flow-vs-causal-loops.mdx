Teams modeling dynamic problems choose between **stock-and-flow diagrams** and **causal loop diagrams (CLDs)**. Both show feedback; they emphasize different questions. **Stock and flow vs causal loops** is not a beauty contest. It is a fit decision for accumulation, units, and audience.

## What stock-and-flow emphasizes

Stocks accumulate inflows and outflows. Flows have units per time. The question is "how much is on hand, and what rates change it?"

Good for inventory, cash, population cohorts, and energy balances when conservation matters.

## What causal loops emphasize

CLDs show signed influence among factors, including non-physical variables like trust or perceived risk. The question is "what influences what, including loops?"

Good for alignment workshops and policy narratives before full quantification.

## Honest tool alternatives

| Need | Often fits | Trade-off |
|------|------------|-----------|
| Quantified accumulation | Stella, Vensim, AnyLogic | Learning curve, data demand |
| Executive alignment CLD | Holist-IQ, kumu, dedicated CLD tools | May start qualitative |
| Quick numbers | Spreadsheets | Feedback structure easy to hide |
| Whiteboard speed | Miro, Lucid, pen-and-paper | Loop semantics inconsistent |

Holist-IQ focuses on decision-ready CLDs from plain language. Stella-class tools excel when equations and units are non-negotiable. Spreadsheets win one-off budgets but obscure loops unless you design them explicitly.

## Hybrid workflow many teams use

Sketch CLDs for stories and disagreement, then quantify one stock-flow slice where data supports it. Keep variable names aligned so both views stay linked.

## When not to force stock-and-flow

Skip full stock-flow when data is sparse, the audience needs fast alignment, or the problem is dominated by belief and incentive loops without reliable flows.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can CLDs become stock-and-flow?

Yes. Promote heavily quantified variables into stocks and define flows between them.

### Which is better for climate transitions?

Often both: CLDs for lock-in narratives, stock-flow for carbon and energy balances.
