These causal loop diagram mistakes look impressive in slides but collapse under scrutiny.

## Six mistakes

1. **Spaghetti without loops** — force two closed loops before optional links
2. **Missing polarity** — every arrow needs +/− or explicit dispute
3. **Ignored delays** — annotate lags where early metrics lie
4. **Static maps** — assign an owner and monthly review
5. **CLD vs flowchart confusion** — sequence ≠ feedback
6. **Too many variables** — cap working maps at ~15 nodes
