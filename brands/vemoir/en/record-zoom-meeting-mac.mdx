You **record a Zoom meeting on Mac** during the call in two different ways, and they should not be collapsed into one. Zoom's Record button writes Zoom's own file, on this computer or in the cloud, and Zoom's banner tells the room. A menu-bar app records the microphone and the sound coming out of the speakers while the call runs, and Zoom does not treat that as its recorder. This page is the during-the-call path. If you already finished the meeting and you are looking for `Documents/Zoom` and an `audio_only.m4a` Zoom saved itself, use [transcribe a local Zoom recording](/blog/transcribe-zoom-local-recording) instead. That article starts after the file exists. This one starts while people are still talking.

## What Zoom records during the call

If you are the host or a co-host, Zoom shows Record. "Record on this computer" writes a local file. "Record to the cloud" writes to Zoom's cloud, and a cloud transcript is part of that cloud feature set, not of the free local file. Local recording is audio and video that Zoom saved. It is not a text transcript by itself. You still have to transcribe the file if you want minutes. The folder Zoom uses, and how to transcribe the local audio afterward, is the other article linked above. Do not hunt for that folder while the call is still open. Let Zoom finish writing first, or record with a second tool that you stop yourself.

Zoom's recording indicator follows Zoom's recorder. It does not follow every app on the Mac that can hear the speakers. If you only start a third-party capture, the banner stays off. That is a reason to say out loud that you are recording, not a reason to skip consent.

If your Zoom account does not show Record, the host has not given you the right, or the account type does not include recording. You cannot grant that to yourself as a guest. You can still capture system audio on the Mac, and you still have to tell the room.

## Record a Zoom meeting on Mac from the menu bar

The Mac app is from Aurum Avis Labs GmbH in Oberägeri. It sits in the menu bar. Before the first call, macOS asks for the microphone and for System Audio Recording. Those two permissions are the capture: your voice on one local track, Zoom's playback on another. There is no screen recording in this path, and no bot joins the Zoom participant list.

Start the capture when the conversation should be kept, not when you remember ten minutes later. You stop it from the same menu. The app does not detect the meeting for you and it does not announce itself in the Zoom chat. Say that you are recording. If someone objects, stop.

After models are installed, transcription and speaker separation run on the Mac. Minutes, decisions, and tasks are written locally unless you choose, with your own key, to send only the text step to OpenAI or Anthropic. There is no silent fallback, and the audio does not go with that text. The interface can be English, German, French, Italian, or Dutch. The transcript language follows the speech model, not the menu language. Parakeet v3 covers 25 European languages. Other models cover their own lists. Swiss German is not a dialect output you should expect.

Price on the Mac App Store, checked September 2026: CHF 20 in Switzerland, €24.99 in Germany, Austria, France, and Italy, $22.99 in the United States. One purchase, no per-meeting fee ([pricing](/#pricing)). You need Apple silicon, macOS Sonoma 14.2 or later, 8 GB of memory, and 10 GB free. Long meetings are more reliable with 16 GB. Intel Macs, Windows, iPhone, iPad, and Android are not supported. The install link is the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794693630).

If you also press Zoom's Record, you will have two files: Zoom's, and the menu-bar capture. That is fine for a test. For a normal meeting, pick one, so nobody has to decide later which transcript is the record.

## After the call: a short file versus the whole meeting

When the meeting is over and you only have a short clip, the browser tool [transcribe a Zoom recording](/tools/transcribe-zoom-recording) turns that file into text without uploading it. The speech model downloads once. Later runs can work offline. Limits: 15 minutes, 3 successful runs a week, MP3, M4A, WAV, or MP4, no speaker labels. Use the Network tab if you want to see that the audio stays in the tab. A one-hour Zoom call does not fit that tool. The during-the-call Mac app is the path for the whole meeting.

If Zoom itself already wrote `audio_only.m4a` because you used Record on this computer, you do not need to record the call a second time to get text from that file. Follow [transcribe a local Zoom recording](/blog/transcribe-zoom-local-recording) for the Finder path and the transcription of that Zoom file. Use this article when the decision is still in front of you, before Record or the menu bar.

Own notes you typed during the call can be folded into the Mac record. Sharing the finished notes uses the macOS share sheet. There is no team workspace and no push into a CRM.

## What this does not do

It does not replace Zoom's cloud transcript for a company that has already standardized on Zoom cloud and is allowed to use it. If the host is recording to the cloud and that policy is the one your customer signed, leave it. A second local copy is extra data you would then have to retain or delete on purpose.

It does not make the recording lawful. You tell participants. The app does not claim to be a compliant or certified transcript.

It does not label speakers in the 15-minute browser tool. Speaker separation is on the Mac, after you stop the capture.

It does not run on a Windows laptop in the guest's meeting. If you are on Windows, Zoom's own recorder is the control you have, subject to the host.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I record a Zoom meeting on Mac if I am not the host?

Zoom's Record button follows the host's settings. A menu-bar capture of system audio does not need that button, and it does not add you as Zoom's recorder. You still need permission from the people in the call.

### Will attendees see a recording banner?

They see Zoom's banner if the host uses Zoom's recorder. They do not see that banner only because a Mac app is listening to system audio. Tell them yourself.

### Is this the same as transcribing Zoom's local file later?

No. That workflow starts when Zoom has already written the file. This workflow is while the call is running. Both can end in text. They do not start in the same place.

### Does the browser tool upload the Zoom file?

No. [Transcribe a Zoom recording](/tools/transcribe-zoom-recording) keeps the file in the tab, up to 15 minutes, with 3 successful runs a week and no speaker names. The Network tab is the check.

### What does the Mac app cost?

CHF 20 in Switzerland, €24.99 in Germany, Austria, France, and Italy, $22.99 in the United States, as a one-time Mac App Store purchase (September 2026). No subscription and no per-seat fee.

### Can I record the call and use Zoom cloud too?

Yes, you will have two recordings. Pick the one that matches the rule you already told the room about, and delete the other if you were only testing.

For a short file after the call, open [transcribe a Zoom recording](/tools/transcribe-zoom-recording). For the next live Zoom meeting on Apple silicon, start the menu-bar capture before the discussion you need to keep, and say so when people join.
