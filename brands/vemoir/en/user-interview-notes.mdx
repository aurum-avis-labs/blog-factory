**User interview notes** here are notes from a product conversation: someone using the thing, or deciding whether to. They are not a hiring screen, and they are not a coded academic study. Those are different jobs, with different rules about who may hear the tape. This page is the file you are allowed to keep, the quotes you can point at, and the places a notes tool should stop.

Consent stays with you. Tell the person the recording exists, what you will keep, and who on the product team will read the notes. A local transcript does not replace that sentence.

## What belongs in user interview notes

A useful note from a product interview answers a small set of questions.

<ul class="blog-checklist">
  <li>Get consent before you record research calls</li>
  <li>Separate quotes from your interpretation in the notes</li>
  <li>Keep raw audio local when ethics boards restrict upload</li>
</ul>

What were they trying to do? Where did the product get in the way? What did they do instead? What would they need before they would pay, switch, or tell a colleague? Which line is a quote, and which line is your interpretation?

The recording is how you separate those. If you only have memory, the second sentence tends to become what you hoped they said. Write the hope in your own words, labeled as yours. Leave the quote as the words on the tape.

<ol class="blog-flow">
  <li><strong>Record</strong><p>Remote call on the Mac, or import a file you already have.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Transcribe</strong><p>On device for the full session, or a short clip in the browser.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Mark</strong><p>Highlight the lines that belong in the spec.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Ship</strong><p>Paste checked quotes into the doc your team already uses.</p></li>
</ol>

A remote interview on Zoom or Meet can be captured on the Mac while it happens: menu bar, microphone plus system audio, no bot in the participant list. You start and stop. An interview you already recorded elsewhere is a file. Import audio (MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF, CAF, or FLAC) into the same local pipeline, or send a short clip through the browser.

In person, one microphone in the room is the capture you have today. There is no separate in-person mode. Names will be weaker than on a call with two tracks. Say who is who in a manual note, marked as yours. That note is not verbatim evidence.

Do not promise dialect output. Parakeet v3 on the Mac covers 25 European languages as standard speech. Swiss German is not a separate transcript mode. If the person codeswitches, read the passage before you quote it in a slide.

## A clip in the browser, the tape on the Mac

The browser page [transcribe interview](/tools/transcribe-interview) takes a file you drop in. The audio is not uploaded to Vemoir. A public speech model downloads once into the tab. The cap is 15 minutes and three successful runs a week. There are no speaker labels. This page itself is speech to text.

Use it for the five minutes where they described the workaround, not for a 50-minute session you then lose in the middle. A full product interview belongs on the Mac.

[Vemoir](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794693630) keeps the library on Apple silicon, macOS Sonoma 14.2 or later. Transcription and speaker separation stay on the device. Summaries stay on the device unless you turn on your own text API key. That key receives text only, with no silent fallback and no audio. After the models are downloaded, processing does not need a network.

Put the interview in a project for that product or that round of conversations. Search covers meetings, transcripts, artifacts, actions, and decisions in the project. Ask can look at one meeting, one project, or the active library, and it leaves out archived and deleted items. Answers can cite, up to 24 unique sources. A citation you cannot resolve is not shown as a link.

## Quotes you can check

A product note that cannot be checked becomes a slide. Treat three kinds of text differently.

The transcript line is what the model heard. Open the meeting, switch to the transcript, and read the highlighted passage before you paste the quote into a spec. If the line is wrong, fix the quote from the audio. Do not smooth it into a clearer complaint they did not make.

Your manual note is your inference: "this sounds like the same billing confusion as interview 4." It may sit in the minutes. It must not be exported as if the person said it. Evidence links point at speech, not at notes you typed.

Decisions and actions are your team's, not the participant's, unless they explicitly agreed to something. "We will change the empty state" is a product decision. It needs wording, a responsible person, and a date. "They will send the screenshot" is an action with an owner and a due date, and only if they offered. Do not turn a shrug into a commitment.

Speaker rename is allowed without a profile. "Remember this speaker" is opt-in, encrypted, and deletable. For a one-off research participant, leave them unnamed or use a code you control. Unknown speakers stay anonymous. The app does not build a voice profile from the sample meeting, and you should not build one from a participant who was promised anonymity.

Retention is chosen when the meeting is created. Audio cleanup can run after processing succeeds. You can purge audio later and keep the transcript, which removes playback. You can delete the meeting, which removes the transcript, the generated record, and actions or decisions attached to it, plus the recording folder. If your consent said the audio would be deleted after notes, do that deletion. Clipboard, Markdown, or an Apple Notes folder are the ways out. There is no PDF or DOCX from the app.

## What this page does not cover

Hiring interviews, reference calls, and anything a candidate could treat as an employment record are out of scope here. Use the process your company already has for those. Do not drop a hiring tape into a product-research folder because the tool happens to accept audio.

A qualitative study with a codebook, dual coding, and an ethics board is also out of scope. The browser tool will not do that work. If that is the job, follow the research protocol. [Qualitative research transcription](/blog/qualitative-research-transcription) explains how a transcript fits a study design without pretending the Mac app is NVivo.

Privilege, medical confidentiality, and "compliant" labels do not belong on this product. Keeping a file on a Mac is a storage choice. It is not a certification, and it is not permission to record someone who refused.

Gong-style coaching on sales calls is a different article. A user interview is not a deal review.

If you only need a blank page to type while they talk, and you will not record, use the [minutes sheet](/tools/minutes-sheet). No model, no upload, Markdown when you are done.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use this for a job interview?

No. This page is product and user interviews. Hiring has its own consent, retention, and who-may-listen rules. Do not mix the tapes.

### Does the browser tool label the interviewer and the participant?

No. Fifteen minutes, no speaker names, three runs a week, nothing uploaded. Rename and separate voices on the Mac, and expect weaker names from a single room mic or a single mixed file.

### Is the transcript allowed in the spec?

Only the lines you have checked against the audio, and only if your consent covers that audience. Mark your interpretation separately. Unresolved citations are not shown as links.

### What if they asked me to delete the recording?

Delete the meeting in the app, and delete the original file in Downloads or on the recorder. Export to Apple Notes first only if they agreed the notes may remain. Deleting the meeting does not reach into Notes afterward.

### Will names be accurate in a café?

Unlikely. Noise suppression on the Mac mic has Off, Balanced, and Strong for steady noise. It does not sort two voices at one table. Move, or take the interview where the mic can hear one person at a time.

### Do I need Windows?

The live and library path is an Apple silicon Mac. The browser tool runs a short file in the tab, still without speaker labels. Intel Macs are not supported by the app.

### Can I claim the notes are confidential in a legal sense?

You can tell the participant what you will do with the file. You cannot stamp the app as privileged or as a compliance product. Say the practical limit: the tape stays on the Mac unless you export text or turn on a text API key you control.

Record only what you may keep. Short proof clips can go through [transcribe interview](/tools/transcribe-interview). The full conversation belongs in a project on the Mac, with quotes you have re-read. The app is on the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794693630).
