A **Granola alternative** helps when you like Granola’s no-bot, live note-taking feel, but you want different data handling, pricing, or a Mac-first workflow where meeting audio stays on your device. Granola listens through your computer’s microphone and system audio, builds an AI notepad tied to your calendar, and avoids a visible notetaker in the participant list. The tools below trade some of that cloud convenience for local capture, team bots, or file-based transcription after the call.

This guide compares seven options as of September 2026. Plan limits and prices change, so follow the linked vendor pages before you standardize on a tool.

## What Granola does well

[Granola](https://www.granola.ai/) is an AI meeting notepad for desktop and mobile. You start it manually for a call; it does not auto-join as a meeting guest. On desktop it captures microphone and meeting audio from your computer, transcribes through third-party speech providers, and summarizes with third-party AI models ([Granola security](https://www.granola.ai/security), checked September 2026). Granola states it does **not** store meeting audio long term: it keeps transcripts and your notes in encrypted cloud infrastructure on AWS in the US.

That combination is appealing when client policies forbid a bot in the roster but you still want live notes during Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or similar apps. Granola also ships mobile capture for in-person conversations and syncs notes across devices ([Granola homepage](https://www.granola.ai/), checked September 2026).

## Granola pricing as of September 2026

[Granola's pricing page](https://www.granola.ai/pricing), checked in September 2026, lists **Basic** at $0 per user per month, **Business** at $14, and **Enterprise** at $35. Basic **meeting history is 30 days**. Business and Enterprise show unlimited history on the grid. Bot-free device-audio capture is listed on every plan, as are desktop, iOS, Android and Apple Watch apps. "Audio deleted after transcription" is listed as included. Notes are private until you share them.

The [security page](https://www.granola.ai/security) adds product detail: you start Granola manually, it does not auto-join as a guest, and it does not add a bot. Desktop works with any meeting platform; mobile is aimed at in-person meetings. Transcription uses providers such as Deepgram and Assembly. Summaries use providers such as OpenAI and Anthropic. Granola states those providers may not train on your data. Granola itself may train on anonymized data unless you opt out in Settings. Enterprise turns training off by default. Notes live in US-hosted AWS, encrypted at rest and in transit.

## How to choose a Granola alternative

Three questions decide it.

1. **Must the file stay on your Mac?** Granola sends audio to transcription providers to produce text. Local apps keep recordings and transcripts on the device unless you export or enable cloud AI with your own key.
2. **Do you need live notes during the call?** Live capture is different from transcribing a file after the meeting. The free [transcribe recording tool](/tools/transcribe-recording) is an after-the-call trial in the browser (no upload). It does not listen live like Granola on desktop.
3. **Can a bot join the call?** If yes, Otter or Fireflies may be simpler for a shared team library. If no, see [meeting notes without a bot](/blog/meeting-notes-without-a-bot).

## Seven Granola alternatives

### Vemoir (Mac app)

**Best for:** keeping the recording, the transcript and the minutes on the Mac, with no bot in the call.

Vemoir records the microphone and system audio from the menu bar. You start and stop each session. Transcription and summaries run on the Mac with local models. A cloud model is opt-in, with your own API key. The record is minutes, decisions, action items and a follow-up draft. Speaker separation runs on the Mac ([the record](/#record), [Ask Vemoir](/#chat)).

It works with any app that plays audio on the Mac ([platforms](/#platforms)). macOS only allows another app's audio through the Screen Recording permission. Vemoir uses that permission for meeting audio and does not record the screen.

Price, as of September 2026: one-time Mac App Store purchase. CHF 20 in Switzerland, €24.99 in Germany, Austria, France and Italy, $22.99 in the US ([pricing](/#pricing)). Apple silicon, macOS Sonoma 14.2 or later, 8 GB RAM minimum, 10 GB free storage. 16 GB or more recommended for long meetings.

**Skip Vemoir** if you need Windows, an Intel Mac, or a shared cloud library with CRM fields.

### Otter.ai

**Best for:** teams that accept a calendar notetaker bot and want mobile apps plus shared workspaces.

Otter joins Zoom, Teams and Meet from the calendar. The desktop app can record without a notetaker in the room, per Otter's help, but the transcript still goes to Otter's cloud ([Otter pricing](https://otter.ai/pricing), [recording permissions](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/39339238308503-Recording-Permissions-with-Otter), September 2026).

**Skip Otter** if the issue is cloud storage, not the visible bot.

### Fireflies.ai

**Best for:** conversation intelligence, CRM workflows and a mature cloud library when a bot or Chrome extension is allowed.

Fireflies stores recordings and transcripts in its workspace, sells per-seat plans, and documents a Chrome extension path for some Google Meet capture without the bot in the roster ([Fireflies pricing](https://fireflies.ai/pricing), [security](https://fireflies.ai/security), September 2026). Pro and Business users have no monthly rate limit on the bot or extension per the pricing FAQ; API and bulk uploads have separate caps.

**Skip Fireflies** if bots and extensions are blocked equally, or if audio must not sit in a vendor cloud.

### Fathom

**Best for:** generous free recording, clips, and optional bot-free beta capture on Mac.

[fathom.video/pricing](https://fathom.video/pricing), September 2026: free plan with unlimited recordings and transcriptions; bot or bot-free (beta, Mac only). Paid individual and team rows from the $15–$20 band with annual toggle. Business adds CRM sync.

**Skip Fathom** if audio must stay on the Mac or you need bot-free capture on Windows.

### MacWhisper

**Best for:** local transcription and optional cloud AI via your own keys.

[MacWhisper](https://www.macwhisper.com/) records online meetings without a bot, transcribes locally, lists Pro at €64 once on the site in September 2026. Summaries can use connected cloud AI or local models via Ollama or LM Studio.

**Skip MacWhisper** if you need a team cloud workspace or Windows.

### tl;dv

**Best for:** EU-hosted cloud libraries with CRM integrations.

[tl;dv pricing](https://tldv.io/app/pricing/) and [features](https://tldv.io/features/), September 2026: EU storage, no training on recordings per vendor, multilingual transcription. Pro €18 or €29 per seat depending on billing.

**Skip tl;dv** if EU cloud is still too far, or you need offline capture.

### Jamie

**Best for:** no bot, EU processing, audio deleted after transcription.

[meetjamie.ai](https://meetjamie.ai/) and [pricing](https://meetjamie.ai/pricing), September 2026: processing in the EEA, Switzerland and the UK; ISO 27001 stated on the homepage. Plus €21, Pro €39, Team €33 on the pricing page; confirm monthly vs annual on the toggle.

**Skip Jamie** if you need the audio file after the call or a fully offline meeting.

If you already have a short audio export and only need text locally in the tab, try the free [transcribe recording tool](/tools/transcribe-recording). It is not a live Granola replacement; compare it with live capture on [platform support](/#platforms).

## Where Granola is still the better choice

Granola remains a strong default when you want a lightweight notepad experience, no bot in the participant list, and live transcription on both Mac and Windows with mobile capture for in-person meetings. If you already rely on Granola’s calendar flow, shared note links and cross-device sync, switching purely for privacy may cost more convenience than you gain.

Granola also documents SOC 2 Type 2, GDPR-oriented controls, enterprise model-training defaults and clear statements about third-party AI providers ([Granola security](https://www.granola.ai/security), checked September 2026). Teams that accept US-hosted notes and cloud transcription may prefer Granola’s product polish over rebuilding a workflow on a local app.

If your buyers only asked for no bot in the roster, Granola already answers that. A Granola alternative search usually means the next question: where the transcript lives, whether you keep audio, offline capture, or subscription math. Name which of those blocked approval before you replace a workflow that already works.

## Honest limits to plan for

No tool on this page makes a recording lawful. You tell people, you keep what you can justify, and you delete the rest. This is not legal advice.

Granola depends on network access during capture and on third-party transcription providers even when it does not store raw audio. The Basic plan drops meeting history after 30 days on the pricing grid we saw in September 2026. Export or upgrade before you lose context.

Local apps need disk and model downloads. Vemoir needs Apple silicon and Screen Recording permission for system audio. It does not record the screen contents ([capture details](/#capture)). It does not replace Granola on Windows or phone for live capture.

For structured minutes rather than a raw transcript alone, see [the record view](/#record).

Want live capture without sending audio through Granola's cloud stack? [Vemoir records from the Mac menu bar](/#how). [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794693630) and [pricing](/#pricing) for your storefront.



Before you shortlist tools, export one week of Granola notes and list which fields your CRM or wiki still needs manually. That gap tells you whether you need Fireflies-style intelligence, Mac-local files, or only a cheaper seat.
## Frequently asked questions

### What is the closest Granola alternative without cloud transcription?

Vemoir and MacWhisper default to on-Mac transcription for live or imported audio. Jamie advertises EU-hosted cloud processing without a bot. Granola itself sends audio to transcription providers to produce text, per its security page.

### Is there a free Granola alternative?

Granola, Otter, Fireflies, Fathom and tl;dv offer free tiers with different limits. MacWhisper has a free Mac tier. Vemoir sells a one-time Mac app for live capture; [/tools/transcribe-recording](/tools/transcribe-recording) is a free after-the-call browser trial, not live desktop capture like Granola.

### Can Vemoir replace Granola’s live notes during a call?

On the Mac, Vemoir records live system audio and your microphone and generates minutes, decisions, action items and a follow-up draft locally ([the record](/#record)). The browser tool only transcribes a file you already have after the call; it does not listen live like Granola on desktop.

### Does Granola store my meeting recordings?

Granola’s security FAQ states it does not store meeting audio long term and keeps transcripts and your notes in encrypted cloud storage ([Granola security](https://www.granola.ai/security), September 2026). Verify current wording before a client audit.

### How does pricing compare to Granola?

Granola publishes free and paid tiers on its site (September 2026). Vemoir is a one-time Mac App Store purchase. Otter, Fireflies, Fathom, tl;dv and Jamie use subscription seat models. MacWhisper Pro is a one-time license on the vendor site.

### Can I use a Granola alternative offline?

Vemoir and MacWhisper support offline workflows once local models are installed. Granola and most cloud notetakers need network access during capture.

### Do these tools train AI on my meetings?

Granola documents anonymized training with an opt-out and enterprise defaults ([Granola security](https://www.granola.ai/security), September 2026). Read each vendor’s privacy page; policies differ.

### Which Granola alternative is best for Swiss or EU teams?

Jamie emphasizes EU residency on its site. tl;dv describes EU hosting and GDPR-oriented controls. Local Mac processing with Vemoir or MacWhisper keeps files on the device unless you export or enable cloud AI. Pick based on your threat model, not marketing labels alone.

### Can I import a Granola export into another tool?

Most alternatives accept common audio formats within plan limits. Vemoir imports MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF, CAF and FLAC on the Mac. Check each vendor’s import caps.

### Do these tools replace legal or compliance review?

No. Read each vendor’s privacy and security documentation, align with your organization’s policies, and decide retention and sharing rules yourself.
