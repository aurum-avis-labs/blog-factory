A **Granola alternative** helps when you like Granola’s no-bot, live note-taking feel, but you want different data handling, pricing, or a Mac-first workflow where meeting audio stays on your device. Granola listens through your computer’s microphone and system audio, builds an AI notepad tied to your calendar, and avoids a visible notetaker in the participant list. The tools below trade some of that cloud convenience for local capture, team bots, or file-based transcription after the call.

This guide compares seven options as of September 2026. Plan limits and prices change, so follow the linked vendor pages before you standardize on a tool.

## What Granola does well

[Granola](https://www.granola.ai/) is an AI meeting notepad for desktop and mobile. You start it manually for a call; it does not auto-join as a meeting guest. On desktop it captures microphone and meeting audio from your computer, transcribes through third-party speech providers, and summarizes with third-party AI models ([Granola security](https://www.granola.ai/security), checked September 2026). Granola states it does **not** store meeting audio long term: it keeps transcripts and your notes in encrypted cloud infrastructure on AWS in the US.

That combination is appealing when client policies forbid a bot in the roster but you still want live notes during Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or similar apps. Granola also ships mobile capture for in-person conversations and syncs notes across devices. Free tiers exist; paid plans expand history and team features ([Granola homepage](https://www.granola.ai/), checked September 2026).

## How to choose a Granola alternative

Start with three practical questions:

1. **Must audio and transcripts stay on your Mac?** Granola processes audio through its cloud stack to produce text. Local Mac apps keep recordings and transcripts on the device by default.
2. **Do you need live notes during the call?** Live capture is different from transcribing a file you export afterward. Vemoir’s free [transcribe recording tool](/tools/transcribe-recording) is an **after-the-call** trial in the browser (no upload); it does not mirror Granola’s live desktop session.
3. **Can a bot join the call?** If the answer is yes, cloud notetakers such as Otter or Fireflies may be simpler for team libraries. If the answer is no, see [meeting notes without a bot](/blog/meeting-notes-without-a-bot) for how that constraint shows up in practice.

For the next live meeting on Apple silicon, the [Vemoir Mac app](/#how) records system audio and your microphone from the menu bar ([how capture works](/#capture)).

## Seven Granola alternatives

### Vemoir (Mac app)

**Best for:** keeping meeting audio, transcripts and generated records on your Mac by default, without a bot in the call.

Vemoir records microphone and system audio from the menu bar. You start and stop each session. Transcription and summaries run locally with on-device models, and you can opt into cloud models with your own API key. The app turns a call into minutes, decisions, action items and a follow-up draft, with speaker separation on the Mac ([how the record works](/#record)).

Pricing is a one-time purchase in the Mac App Store (CHF 20 in Switzerland, €24.99 in Germany, Austria, France and Italy, $22.99 in the US as listed on [pricing](/#pricing)). Requires Apple silicon and macOS Sonoma 14.2 or later.

### Otter.ai

**Best for:** teams that want a calendar-linked notetaker bot, mobile apps and shared workspaces across Zoom, Teams and Meet.

Otter joins meetings from the calendar, transcribes in near real time and sells per-user subscription plans with minute pools on paid tiers ([Otter pricing](https://otter.ai/pricing), checked September 2026). Admin and recording-permission documentation is published in Otter’s help center ([Otter help: recording permissions](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/39339238308503-Recording-Permissions-with-Otter), checked September 2026).

### Fireflies.ai

**Best for:** team conversation intelligence, CRM-oriented workflows and a mature cloud notetaker with admin controls.

Fireflies joins Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and other platforms, stores recordings and transcripts in its cloud workspace and sells per-seat plans ([Fireflies pricing](https://fireflies.ai/pricing), checked September 2026). Security and compliance documentation is published on the same site.

### Fathom

**Best for:** sales and CS teams that want generous free recording, highlight reels and CRM sync on paid tiers.

Fathom records and transcribes meetings, generates summaries and action items, and offers team plans with collaboration features ([Fathom pricing](https://fathom.video/pricing), checked September 2026). Individual plans advertise a choice of bot-based capture or a bot-free beta path on Mac.

### MacWhisper

**Best for:** Mac users who want local transcription and optional cloud AI via their own keys, with flexible export and batch workflows.

MacWhisper transcribes files and can record online meetings without a bot, using local models by default ([MacWhisper site](https://www.macwhisper.com/), checked September 2026). Pro is a one-time license (listed at €64 on the site in September 2026) with lifetime updates on that page. Summaries and chat features can use local or connected AI services you configure.

### tl;dv

**Best for:** teams that want searchable meeting libraries, strong integrations and multilingual transcripts in the cloud.

tl;dv records and transcribes Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, emphasizes team collaboration and CRM connections, and publishes free and paid seat plans ([tl;dv pricing](https://tldv.io/app/pricing/), checked September 2026). Its security pages describe EU hosting options and state that customer recordings are not used to train models ([tl;dv features](https://tldv.io/features/), checked September 2026).

### Jamie

**Best for:** regulated or client-facing teams that want EU-hosted processing without a bot, with workflow automation after the call.

Jamie runs on your device, does not add a virtual participant, and documents EU data residency, deletion of audio after transcription and ISO 27001 certification on its homepage ([meetjamie.ai](https://meetjamie.ai/), checked September 2026). Plans are subscription-based ([Jamie pricing](https://meetjamie.ai/pricing), checked September 2026).

## Where Granola is still the better choice

Granola remains a strong default when you want a lightweight notepad experience, no bot in the participant list, and live transcription on both Mac and Windows with mobile capture for in-person meetings. If you already rely on Granola’s calendar flow, shared note links and cross-device sync, switching purely for privacy may cost more convenience than you gain.

Granola also documents SOC 2 Type 2, GDPR-oriented controls, enterprise model-training defaults and clear statements about third-party AI providers ([Granola security](https://www.granola.ai/security), checked September 2026). Teams that accept US-hosted notes and cloud transcription may prefer Granola’s product polish over rebuilding a workflow on a local app.

## Honest limits to plan for

No tool removes your duty to tell participants you are recording and to handle retention responsibly. Vemoir does not make a recording lawful by itself, and this article is not legal advice.

Cloud notetakers and Granola depend on network access during capture and on vendor policy changes. Even vendors that do not store raw audio still process it to produce transcripts and summaries.

Local-first tools need disk space, model downloads and hardware that meets vendor requirements. Vemoir needs Screen Recording permission for system audio, uses that permission for audio only, and does not support Windows, Intel Macs or mobile ([capture details](/#capture)).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the closest Granola alternative without cloud transcription?

Vemoir and MacWhisper default to on-Mac transcription for live or imported audio. Jamie advertises EU-hosted cloud processing without a bot. Granola itself sends audio to transcription providers to produce text, per its security page.

### Is there a free Granola alternative?

Granola, Otter, Fireflies, Fathom and tl;dv offer free tiers with different limits. MacWhisper has a free Mac tier. Vemoir sells a one-time Mac app for live capture; [/tools/transcribe-recording](/tools/transcribe-recording) is a free after-the-call browser trial, not live desktop capture like Granola.

### Can Vemoir replace Granola’s live notes during a call?

On the Mac, Vemoir records live system audio and your microphone and generates minutes, decisions, action items and a follow-up draft locally ([the record](/#record)). The browser tool only transcribes a file you already have after the call; it does not listen live like Granola on desktop.

### Does Granola store my meeting recordings?

Granola’s security FAQ states it does not store meeting audio long term and keeps transcripts and your notes in encrypted cloud storage ([Granola security](https://www.granola.ai/security), September 2026). Verify current wording before a client audit.

### How does pricing compare to Granola?

Granola publishes free and paid tiers on its site (September 2026). Vemoir is a one-time Mac App Store purchase. Otter, Fireflies, Fathom, tl;dv and Jamie use subscription seat models. MacWhisper Pro is a one-time license on the vendor site.

### Can I use a Granola alternative offline?

Vemoir and MacWhisper support offline workflows once local models are installed. Granola and most cloud notetakers need network access during capture.

### Do these tools train AI on my meetings?

Granola documents anonymized training with an opt-out and enterprise defaults ([Granola security](https://www.granola.ai/security), September 2026). Read each vendor’s privacy page; policies differ.

### Which Granola alternative is best for Swiss or EU teams?

Jamie emphasizes EU residency on its site. tl;dv describes EU hosting and GDPR-oriented controls. Local Mac processing with Vemoir or MacWhisper keeps files on the device unless you export or enable cloud AI. Pick based on your threat model, not marketing labels alone.

### Can I import a Granola export into another tool?

Most alternatives accept common audio formats within plan limits. Vemoir imports MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF, CAF and FLAC on the Mac. Check each vendor’s import caps.

### Do these tools replace legal or compliance review?

No. Read each vendor’s privacy and security documentation, align with your organization’s policies, and decide retention and sharing rules yourself.
