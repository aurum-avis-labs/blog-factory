**Is Fathom private** depends on which risk you mean: where the recording lives, whether Fathom's own models learn from it, who inside the company can see the note, and whether a bot joins the call. Fathom publishes help articles and release notes. This page summarizes those documents as of September 2026. It is not legal advice, and it does not decide whether Fathom fits your contract.

If your bar is "the audio stays on my Mac unless I export it," Fathom's documented model is different: it is a cloud product, with storage in the United States. That can still be an acceptable product when the organization wants shared recordings and has read the same pages.

## What private means before a client call

Most teams mean one of three things: no extra guest in the participant list, no vendor training on the recording, or no copy leaving the laptop. Fathom can address the first on some accounts with bot-free capture, and the second partly with training opt-out settings. It does not address the third on its own pages, because storage stays in the United States until you delete it there. Match the question your client asked to the row you need in the sections below, instead of treating «private» as one switch.

## Is Fathom private on its security page

The help article [Is Fathom secure?](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/296512) (edited 8 September 2025, checked September 2026) states that Fathom is HIPAA, SOC 2 Type II, and GDPR-compliant and has passed security reviews by Zoom. Those are Fathom's own statements. A vendor page is not a certification that your use of Fathom satisfies your client's rules. The same article says Fathom does not have ISO 27001 and points to SOC 2 instead.


Vemoir does not make those compliance claims about itself. If you need a local recording with no bot, that is a different product shape, described later. It is not a claim that local equals certified.

## Where recordings are stored

The same article says all Fathom data is stored in the United States. For users in the EU or UK it says Fathom is GDPR compliant and will sign a data processing agreement. The contact it gives is trust@fathom.video.


US storage is a clear answer to "where does it sit." It is not, by itself, a yes or no for a Swiss or EU company. Your own counsel and your client contract decide whether US hosting is acceptable. This page does not.

Fathom also says it does not sell your data, and that the free app exists because it drives usage for the paid Team Edition. Free is a distribution choice, not a statement that the recording never leaves your laptop.

## Training, subprocessors, and opt-out

On training, the security article says none of Fathom's AI subprocessors (it names Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google) are contractually permitted to use users' data to train their models. In the same answer, Fathom says it uses de-identified customer data to improve the accuracy of its own proprietary models. You can opt out in My Settings. Organizations on Team Edition can opt out all users from Organization Settings.

Read that as two sentences, not one. Subprocessors are not allowed to train on the data. Fathom's own models may use de-identified customer data unless you opt out. If "no training of any kind" is the requirement, the opt-out is the control their page describes, and you should confirm it is on for the account you actually use.

## Who on the team can see the note

Privacy is also an internal sharing question. Fathom's help on [external meeting recording visibility](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/6354945) describes defaults such as users choose, the user's team, or all teams, and admin locks that grey out a user's choice. Team settings can differ by team, so a sales team's external calls and an executive team's calls need not share the same default ([Team Settings](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/6086913), edited 28 October 2025).

[Auto-capture and team visibility](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/13729025) separates two knobs: whether Fathom captures a meeting automatically, and who can see it afterward. Auto-capture off still allows manual capture, according to that article. A meeting can be captured and still private to the user, or captured and visible to a team. Check the lock icon. If an admin locked visibility, your personal setting is not the one in force.

## Bot and bot-free capture

Fathom's release notes describe a version 3.0 bot-free, or "botless," experience, and a recording indicator in the macOS toolbar. Organization admins can block bot-free capture. When that setting is on, meetings use the Fathom Notetaker bot regardless of a user's preference, and bot-free options are greyed out ([Organization Settings](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/3239681), [Release notes](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/6220097)).

So "no bot" is not a permanent property of every Fathom account. It depends on the product experience and on an admin switch that can force the bot. Ask the admin if you are on a team plan, and look at the toolbar indicator when you record. A bot in the participant list is visible to everyone else. A bot-free capture is still Fathom's cloud product unless their pages say otherwise. Their security article still describes US storage.

## Deletion and retention

If you delete your account, the security article says all recording data and metadata are removed, and that after an additional 7 days the data is also removed from backups.

Ongoing retention rules are a separate feature. [Retention policies](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/6057089) (edited 7 August 2026) say an automatic deletion period is for the Enterprise plan, is not self-serve, and is one policy for the team. You request it through help@fathom.video. If you are not on that plan, deletion is the manual path their account article describes, not an automatic clock.

## Where a local Mac app is the better fit

Fathom is the better choice when the team wants a shared cloud library, CRM-style follow-up, admin visibility controls, and is willing to accept US storage plus the training terms above, including the opt-out if you need it. A local Mac app is the better fit when the audio must stay on the machine unless someone exports it.

Vemoir records microphone and system audio on the Mac, with no bot in the call. You start and stop. Transcription and speaker separation stay on the Mac. Optional cloud text is a Keychain key you activate, and it does not receive the audio. For a file you already have, the browser tool [transcribe a recording](/tools/transcribe-recording) runs in the tab, up to 15 minutes, three successful runs a week, with no speaker labels and no upload of the file to Vemoir. It is not a Fathom replacement for a team library.

Fathom remains the better tool when colleagues on Windows need the same login and the same retention admin. Vemoir is Apple silicon, macOS Sonoma 14.2 or later. It is not their team console.

If you only needed the cited pages, stop at the links above and check the dates. Policies move. The September 2026 reading is the one this article used.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Fathom store meeting data in the United States?

Its security article says all Fathom data is stored in the United States, and that EU and UK users can request a DPA.

### Does Fathom train on recordings?

The same article says subprocessors Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google are not permitted to train their models on users' data, and that Fathom uses de-identified customer data for its own models unless you opt out.

### Is bot-free capture always on?

No. Release notes and organization settings describe bot-free capture, and an admin control that forces the Notetaker bot for the whole organization.

### How do I delete recordings?

Account deletion removes recording data and metadata, then backups after 7 days, according to the security article. Automatic retention periods are described as an Enterprise request, not a self-serve switch on every plan.

### Does "GDPR-compliant" on Fathom's page settle a Swiss contract?

No. It is their statement. Your agreement and your client decide. This article is not legal advice.

### Can I try a transcript without sending Fathom the file?

If you already have an audio file, [transcribe it in the browser](/tools/transcribe-recording). The file is not uploaded to Vemoir. The limit is 15 minutes and three successful runs a week. Live team capture is a different product.

### Does Vemoir claim the same certifications?

No. Do not read Fathom's HIPAA or SOC 2 sentences as claims about Vemoir.

### Where do I confirm this before a client call?

Open [Is Fathom secure?](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/296512) and the visibility articles linked above. The copies checked for this page were current as of September 2026.

Read Fathom's pages if a shared cloud library is what you want. If the recording should stay on the Mac, start with [transcribe a recording](/tools/transcribe-recording) for a file you have, or the Mac app when the next call is live.
