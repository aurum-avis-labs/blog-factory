You can **record a Teams meeting on Mac** in three common ways: Teams’ own cloud recording (when your license and role allow it), a macOS screen capture that saves a video file, or a Mac app that records system audio and your microphone without joining a bot to the call. The right choice depends on who must receive the official file, whether you need a transcript inside Teams, and whether the audio should stay on your Mac.

This guide walks through each path with steps, storage locations from Microsoft’s documentation, and honest limits. It is not legal advice. Tell participants you are recording and follow your organization’s rules before you start.

## What Teams cloud recording gives you

Teams can record a scheduled or Meet-now meeting to the cloud when your tenant enables meeting recording and your account has a supported license. As of September 2026, Microsoft lists cloud recording and related features under Teams Premium and several Microsoft 365 and Office 365 plans; your admin can restrict who may start a recording.

Typical roles that can start recording include the meeting organizer and, when policy allows, presenters. Guests and anonymous joiners usually cannot start a cloud recording unless an organizer grants it. Check your tenant’s meeting policy if the Record button is missing.

When recording finishes, Microsoft stores the file in the organizer’s OneDrive for private meetings, or in the channel’s SharePoint folder for channel meetings. The recording also appears in the meeting chat (or channel) with a link. Playback uses the Teams player; download permissions depend on admin settings.

Teams can generate a transcript for cloud recordings when transcription is enabled for your organization and the spoken language is supported. That transcript lives with the recording in Teams and is not the same as a local audio file on your Mac. For Microsoft’s current storage and playback details, see [Play and share a meeting recording in Teams](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/play-and-share-a-meeting-recording-in-teams-7d7e5dc5-9ae4-4b94-858a-0a1820c564a3) and [Teams cloud meeting recording](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/cloud-recording).

### When Teams recording is the better fit

Choose Teams cloud recording when everyone expects the official copy in SharePoint or OneDrive, when you need the built-in Teams transcript and sharing inside your tenant, and when IT has already approved cloud storage for meeting content. You do not install extra software, and participants see the standard recording notification in Teams.

## Why macOS screen recording is a weak default

macOS can record the screen (and often the meeting window) through the Screenshot toolbar or QuickTime Player. You choose **Record Selected Portion** or **Record Entire Screen**, select Teams, and save a `.mov` file to your Mac.

That approach has clear limits for meeting records:

- You capture **video**, not a structured transcript. macOS does not turn the call into searchable text.
- File size grows quickly, especially on long calls or retina displays.
- You must keep the Teams window visible and unobstructed; notifications and window moves show up in the video.
- Remote participants’ video tiles and shared slides are mixed into one screen recording, which is awkward to quote later.
- System audio capture depends on macOS version and settings; mic-only recordings miss other speakers on the call.

Screen recording is reasonable for a short demo you will edit once. It is a poor default when you need minutes, decisions, and action items from a working meeting.

## Tell participants and get consent

Recording a meeting affects everyone on the call, not only the person who clicks Record in Teams or Vemoir.

Practical steps many teams use:

- State at the start that the meeting is being recorded and why (minutes, client file, training).
- Mention recording in the calendar invite or agenda when possible.
- If someone objects, stop recording or leave the call, according to your internal policy.
- Store files only as long as your retention rules require, and limit access to people who need the record.

Vemoir does not make a recording lawful or compliant by itself. Consent and retention remain your responsibility.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can anyone record a Teams meeting on Mac?

Only if policy and license allow it. Organizers and permitted presenters can start cloud recording when the feature is enabled. Other participants may see a notification but cannot start the tenant recording unless roles allow it.

### Where does a Teams cloud recording go?

Microsoft stores it in the organizer’s OneDrive for a private meeting, or in the channel SharePoint library for a channel meeting. The meeting chat or channel posts a link for playback.

### Does Teams always create a transcript?

No. Transcription requires the right license and admin settings, and supported languages. The transcript is tied to the cloud recording, not exported automatically as a local text file on your Mac.

### Is QuickTime or Screenshot enough for meeting minutes?

Usually no. You get a video file without structured minutes, speaker-aware text, or decisions unless you transcribe elsewhere. It can work for short visual demos, not for ongoing meeting records.

### Why does Vemoir need Screen Recording permission?

macOS uses that permission gate for capturing audio from other apps, including Teams. Vemoir records meeting audio, not your screen pixels.

### Will participants see a bot if I use Vemoir?

No. Vemoir does not join the meeting as a participant. You should still tell people you are recording, same as with any other method.

### Can I record Teams on Mac without an organizer role?

You often cannot start **Teams cloud** recording without permission. You can still capture audio locally on your Mac if your policy allows personal records and you have consent from participants.

### What file formats can I import after a Teams call?

If you receive an audio or video export you are allowed to use, Vemoir imports common audio formats (MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF, CAF, FLAC) on the Mac app. Browser tools on vemoir.ch accept shorter files and do not record live meetings.

### Does Vemoir work offline?

After you download the on-device models, capture, transcription, and summary generation can run without internet. Initial setup and model downloads need a connection.

### What hardware does Vemoir require?

Apple silicon Mac (M1 or newer), macOS Sonoma 14.2 or later, 8 GB RAM minimum (16 GB recommended for long meetings), and about 10 GB free storage for models. Intel Macs are not supported.
