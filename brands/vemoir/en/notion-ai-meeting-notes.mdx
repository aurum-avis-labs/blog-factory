**Notion AI meeting notes** are the reasonable default when you already pay for Notion and the meeting note should land as a page your team can open. Notion's help center says the feature is on the Business and Enterprise plans (and on eligible mobile subscriptions that include Notion AI), that you start it with `/meet`, and that the desktop app can hear your microphone and the system audio. The browser, on that same page, hears the mic and misses remote voices if you are on headphones.

If you are not on that plan, or the note should not depend on a Notion page, the feature is the wrong place to start. This is the split, from Notion's own help, checked September 2026: [Take AI Meeting Notes in Notion](https://www.notion.com/help/ai-meeting-notes).

## What Notion AI meeting notes require

Notion's help lists the gates in plain language.

  <div><h3>Local Mac capture fits when</h3><ul><li>Audio should not upload for transcription by default</li></ul></div>

You need a plan that includes the feature: Business or Enterprise on the workspace, or an eligible mobile subscription where Notion AI is included. Confirm the current plan names on [Notion's pricing](https://www.notion.com/pricing) before you assume a Plus seat is enough. The help page is the source for the gate, not a blog recap.

On desktop you need the Notion app at version 4.7.0 or higher. On a Mac, macOS 13 or later. Windows needs a current version, in Notion's wording.

You type `/meet` on a page, or you start from the Upcoming events tile. You can write agenda lines under Notes before the call. Notion says the summary takes those lines into account. You press Start transcribing, which the help text treats as your confirmation that everyone has consented. You press Stop. A summary needs at least one minute of audio.

That is a page in a workspace you already use. You get the page, the permissions, and the database you may already have for meetings. A private tape on one laptop lives somewhere else entirely.

  <li>Accept microphone, system audio, and screen recording prompts if you need remote voices</li>

## Desktop, browser, and phone are not the same

The help page draws this carefully, and it is the part people skip.

**Desktop app.** Best for video calls, because it can use system audio and the microphone, including when you wear headphones. Notion's troubleshooting note says the feature asks for system audio and screen recording permissions so it can transcribe other people on a call. That screen recording permission is Notion's requirement, written on their help page. It is how they reach system audio on the setups they support.

**Browser.** Mic only. System audio is not captured. If you wear headphones, audio from Meet, Zoom, or Teams is not captured. If the room hears the call from speakers and the volume is high enough, some of that sound may reach the mic. Do not plan a headphones call in the browser and expect the other side in the transcript.

**Notion mobile.** The phone mic. Notion says this works best in person, or when meeting audio plays out loud from another device. A call on the phone with earbuds will not include the other participants.

Speaker labels, on desktop, work best for a virtual one-to-one where Notion has both your mic and system audio, and better still if the note is tied to a calendar event so the other person can be named. Notion says labels may be weak in person, in a group on one microphone, in the browser, and on mobile. A Google Meet add-on in Chrome can add speaker labels for Meet on desktop only.

You can upload an existing recording into a meeting notes block: AAC, M4A, MP3, and WAV. Notion's help says MOV, MP4, and Loom files are not supported for that upload. If your file is a video, you need audio extracted first, or another tool.

## Stop, resume, and retry the summary

Notion keeps the transcript block open while you record. When you press Stop, the page holds the raw transcript and can generate a summary. Notion's help says a summary needs at least one minute of audio. Shorter clips may leave you with text only.

You can resume transcribing on the same page when the meeting continues. Notion treats that as one session tied to the block, not a fresh upload. If the summary missed a decision, change custom instructions or the template, then retry the summary on the existing transcript. Retry does not re-record the call.

Custom instructions (sales call, standup, your template) apply to new recordings or when you resume. Changing them does not rewrite an old summary until you retry it.

## Consent, citations, and who can open the page

Before you start, Notion reminds you to tell people. You can paste a consent line into the meeting chat, play a spoken consent message through your computer speakers (headphones off, speakers on, or they will not hear it), or say it yourself. Workspace owners can enforce an audio consent message for everyone under Settings, Notion AI, AI Meeting Notes.

The summary can include citations. Hover shows a snippet. Click jumps to the line in the transcript.

By default, meeting notes you create are private to you. A setting can auto-share notes that were started from a Notion calendar event with participants who are members of the workspace. Auto-share is personal: each member sets it. People outside the workspace do not become editors because they were on the call.

You can pick a default database so new meeting pages land in one place. Sharing still has to be right on each page, or the database is a private pile.

## When staying in Notion is the better choice

Stay if most of these are true.

The workspace is already Business or Enterprise. The team looks for decisions on Notion pages. You want the note next to the project doc you already have. You accept Notion's desktop permissions because the page is the point. A one-to-one on Meet or Zoom with the desktop app matches the setup Notion says labels handle best.

Uploading a WAV or MP3 you already exported into a page the team can comment on is also a Notion-shaped job.

If the call is in the room and someone has the phone, Notion's own help says mobile is the fit, with the limits above. You do not need a second product to hold up a phone.

Want the note on a Notion page without hunting for a file afterward? Start from `/meet` in the desktop app when the plan already includes the feature.

## When the note should not be a Notion page

Leave Notion's recorder for a different path when the page is not the system of record.

You are not on Business or Enterprise, and you would buy the plan only for meeting notes. Price that plan against the notes you actually need. The help page does not claim the feature on every seat.

You do not want a screen recording permission for meeting audio. Notion's help says the desktop path asks for it. A Mac meeting app can use an audio-only system tap and the System Audio Recording permission, and not record the screen. Vemoir is that shape: menu bar, microphone plus system audio, transcription on the Mac, no bot in the call. Notes, decisions, and a follow-up stay on the machine unless you export them or you turn on your own text API key. The Mac app is on the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794693630). It does not write into a Notion database. If the team requires the page, you still paste. Apple silicon and macOS Sonoma 14.2 or later. No Windows, no Intel Mac.

You have a Loom or an MP4. Notion's upload list excludes those. Extract audio, or transcribe the file elsewhere. A short file can go through [transcribe recording](/tools/transcribe-recording): 15 minutes, three runs a week, nothing uploaded, no speaker labels.

You need a checkable line on the Mac without opening Notion. Evidence in a local record points at the transcript stored there. Notion's citations point at the transcript on the page. Both are valid. They are not the same library.

You are in a group in one room and you expected named speakers. Notion says that setup is weak for labels. A single room mic on a Mac is also weak. Do not switch products hoping a group around one laptop becomes a clean speaker list. For how a local mic-in-the-room recording behaves, read the capture description on [the homepage](/#capture). Writing minutes by hand is still honest: the [meeting minutes template](/blog/meeting-minutes-template).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do Notion AI meeting notes work on the free plan?

Notion's help says Business or Enterprise, or an eligible mobile subscription that includes Notion AI. Check your workspace plan before the meeting, not during it.

### Can I resume Notion AI meeting notes after I stop?

Yes, on the same page. Resume transcribing continues the block. Retry the summary if the wording was wrong. You do not need a new `/meet` block for a pause in the same call.

### Can I use Notion in the browser with headphones?

You will get your microphone, not the other people. The help page says the browser does not capture system audio, and headphones keep the call out of the room mic. Use the desktop app for a video call.

### Does Notion record the screen?

The help page says AI Meeting Notes needs system audio and screen recording permissions so it can transcribe other participants on virtual meetings. That is Notion's stated requirement. It is separate from an audio-only Mac recorder that does not ask for screen capture.

### Can I drop a Loom into the meeting block?

Not as a Loom or MP4, according to the help page. Supported uploads are AAC, M4A, MP3, and WAV.

### Are speaker names reliable in a group?

Notion says they work best for a virtual one-to-one on the desktop app. In-person, group meetings on one mic, browser, and mobile are called out as weak. A Meet add-on can help labels inside Google Meet on desktop.

### Will my teammates see the note?

Not by default. Notes start private. Auto-share applies to notes started from Notion calendar, for workspace members, if you turn that setting on.

### What if I only need text from a file I already have?

Use a local transcriber or the browser tool for a short file, then paste into the page if the team still reads there.

If the workspace already pays for the feature and the page is home, start from `/meet` and read Notion's help for the permission prompts. If the recording should stay on the Mac, the app is on the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794693630).
