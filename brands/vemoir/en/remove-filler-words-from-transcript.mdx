Clichés, passive voice and long sentences can stay visible as separate hints. They are left for you to edit. The original paste stays until you change it.

## What counts as a filler here

Hesitations such as "um" and "uh", discourse markers, immediate repeats and intensifiers are on the removal list. The rate is hits divided by total words, times 100. A phrase like "you know" counts as one hit.

Many automatic transcripts already drop hesitations. If the page shows fewer ums than you remember saying, trust the text you pasted. Clean what is there, not what you heard in the room.

Speaker names are read from the paste when they are already present. They are not inferred from audio. Swiss German fillers use the German lists; dialect markers are not split out as a separate language.

## Clean the scaffolding first when the file is a VTT

A Zoom or Teams caption file is often messy before fillers matter. Strip cue numbers and timestamps first with the [transcript cleaner](/tools/transcript-cleaner), then bring the plain text here. How that job works is in [clean a messy meeting transcript](/blog/messy-meeting-transcript). Do not delete real speaker prefixes while you chase ums.

## When removing fillers is the wrong edit

Minutes need decisions, owners and dates. A speech with every "um" deleted can still hide who decided. Put the commitment on the decision line, and leave filler cleanup for drafts you will read aloud or publish as text.

If you only need pace, do not strip the transcript first. Measure words per minute on the text you will actually say, with the [speaking speed test](/tools/speaking-speed-test).

If you need share of the floor by name, count a labeled paste in [who held the floor](/tools/who-held-the-floor). Cleaning fillers does not create names.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is this tool free?

Yes. No account and no upload. Matching and cleaning run in this tab.

### Which filler words are removed?

Hesitations, discourse markers, immediate repeats and intensifiers. Clichés, passive voice and run-on sentences are highlighted and left for you to edit.

### Is my text uploaded?

No. Lists and rules run locally in the browser.

### Can it remove filler words from a recording?

Not from the audio. Transcribe first, then open the filler cleaner from the text. The download is text.

### Does it invent speaker labels?

No. Names stay only if they were already in the paste.

### Does this use a free transcription run?

No. Filler matching does not download Whisper or spend one of the three successful model runs in the rolling 7-week window.

### Does it work in other languages?

Yes. Choose the page language. Lists cover English, German, French and Italian. Swiss German uses the German lists.

### Is the cleaned file a certified transcript?

No. It is an edited text file. Vemoir does not claim HIPAA or GDPR certification for this page.
