The **best meeting notes app for Mac** depends less on marketing labels and more on how you capture audio, where that audio is processed, and what you need after the call. Some tools send a visible notetaker into Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams. Others record microphone and system audio on your Mac so nobody extra appears in the participant list. This guide compares eight Mac-relevant options using the same selection criteria, with features checked on each vendor site as of **September 2026**.

## How we picked meeting notes apps for Mac

We filtered the market to tools that Mac users actually run today: native Mac apps, desktop clients, or services with a documented Mac capture path. We ignored Windows-only recorders and phone-only dictation apps.

For each product we checked five decision points on the vendor’s own site or help center:

1. **Bot or local capture:** Does a named notetaker join the call by default, or does capture happen on the Mac through microphone and system audio?
2. **Where audio and text go:** Cloud processing, optional cloud models, or on-device transcription and storage.
3. **Offline use:** Can you run a full workflow without the network after setup?
4. **Languages and minutes:** Transcription coverage plus whether the product outputs structured minutes, decisions, and action items or mainly a transcript.
5. **Price model:** Subscription, freemium, or one-time purchase (stated as of September 2026 on each pricing page).

We also looked for an honest limit: saved meeting video, speaker labels without a bot, team libraries, CRM sync, or Intel Mac support. Those trade-offs matter as much as feature lists.

## Vemoir

**Best for:** Mac users who want meeting records to stay on the device by default, with no bot in the participant list.

Vemoir is a Mac menu bar app from Aurum Avis Labs. It records your microphone and system audio from any meeting app that plays sound on the Mac (Teams, Zoom, Meet, Slack huddles, and others). **No bot joins** the call; you start and stop recording yourself. Transcription and summaries run **locally on the Mac** with local models unless you opt into a cloud model with your own API key.

After a call, Vemoir produces a structured record: minutes, decisions, action items, and a follow-up draft. You can type notes during the meeting; they fold into the generated sections. **Ask Vemoir** lets you question a meeting with answers linked back to the transcript. Sharing uses native macOS sharing for the parts you choose.

**Offline:** Once models are installed, the full workflow can run offline; internet is needed for setup and model downloads. **Requirements:** Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS Sonoma 14.2+, 8 GB RAM (16 GB recommended for long meetings). Intel Macs are not supported.

**Price (Mac App Store, as of September 2026):** One-time purchase, no subscription: CHF 20 in Switzerland, €24.99 in Germany, Austria, France, and Italy, $22.99 in the US. Every feature is included. See [pricing on the site](/#pricing) or the [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794693630).

**Limits:** No Windows, iPhone, or Android app. No automatic calendar bot that attends when you are absent. For a file you already have, the free browser tools on vemoir.ch transcribe up to 15 minutes without upload, but they do not record live meetings or separate speakers.

## Otter.ai

**Best for:** Teams that want a shared cloud library, calendar auto-join, and integrations, and accept a visible notetaker or Otter’s desktop capture path.

[Otter](https://otter.ai/) is built around an **AI Meeting Agent** that can **join Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet** as a participant, transcribe in real time, and share notes. Otter’s help center describes chat announcements when the notetaker joins and optional pre-meeting permission flows for Teams via an Outlook extension on eligible plans.

Otter also advertises **bot-free recording** from Mac or Windows desktop apps and Chrome, so “Otter” is not only the bot path. Cloud storage and collaboration remain central; pricing is **subscription** based ([Otter pricing](https://otter.ai/pricing), checked September 2026): free Basic, Pro from about **$8.33/user/month** billed annually (or higher monthly), Business around **$19.99–$24/user/month** annually depending on the page tier, Enterprise custom.

**Limits:** Default bot visibility can conflict with client policies that ban extra participants. Advanced admin, SSO, and formal DPAs sit on higher tiers per Otter’s pricing and security pages. Offline transcription on the Mac without cloud is not Otter’s main design.

## Fireflies

**Best for:** Revenue and operations teams that want unlimited transcription on paid tiers, CRM automations, and optional video recording when the bot joins.

[Fireflies](https://fireflies.ai/) is known for **Fred**, the **Fireflies.ai Notetaker** that joins calendar meetings or can be invited via `fred@fireflies.ai` ([join FAQ](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/9554534786-how-fireflies-joins-and-records-your-meetings-faqs)). The **desktop app** also offers **Take Notes** mode: system audio capture **without** a bot, but Fireflies documents that this path **does not save audio/video** and **does not provide speaker labels** ([bot vs no bot](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/7066927951-bot-vs-no-bot-which-capture-am-i-using), September 2026).

Fireflies states that data is **not used to train AI models** on its notetaker marketing page. Plans are **per-seat subscription**; Fireflies’ pricing blog (September 2026) lists **Free**, **Pro** about **$10/seat/month** annually, **Business** about **$19/seat/month** annually, **Enterprise** about **$39/seat/month** annually, with unlimited transcription called out across tiers.

**Limits:** Bot mode is visible to everyone on the call. No-bot mode is lighter but drops saved media and diarization. Processing is cloud-first, not an offline Mac archive.

## Granola

**Best for:** People who want an editable AI notepad on Mac (and other platforms) without a bot, and who accept cloud transcription after local capture.

[Granola](https://www.granola.ai/) markets itself as an **AI notepad**, not a bot. It captures **microphone and system audio on your device** and does **not** join as a meeting participant ([Granola homepage](https://www.granola.ai/), September 2026). Granola’s Zoom guide states it **does not store meeting audio**; transcripts and notes remain in the product while processing uses cloud providers.

Granola syncs with calendars, works across meeting apps, and offers mobile apps. Pricing is subscription-based on [Granola’s site](https://www.granola.ai/) (check the live page for current tiers).

**Limits:** Not a local-only archive like a fully offline Mac transcription stack. You must open a note or accept a notification to start capture; Granola’s docs describe manual starts for ad hoc calls. macOS auto-end detection may require admin rights on managed Macs.

## Jamie

**Best for:** EU-hosted notes, no bot, and structured templates when you attend meetings yourself (online or in person).

[Jamie](https://www.meetjamie.ai/) runs on your device and states **no bot joins** your calls. Marketing emphasizes **EU data residency**, **ISO 27001**, and that Jamie **does not store audio** ([Jamie homepage](https://www.meetjamie.ai/), September 2026). [Pricing](https://www.meetjamie.ai/pricing) (September 2026) includes a **Free** tier (10 meetings/month, 30-minute cap), **Plus** from about **€21/month** billed annually, **Pro** from about **€39/month** billed annually, plus team and enterprise plans with higher duration limits.

**Limits:** Meeting credits and duration caps on lower tiers; notes can lock until you upgrade per [Jamie docs on plans](https://docs.meetjamie.ai/billing-pricing/subscriptions-plans). Cloud processing, not offline Mac-only models.

## tl;dv

**Best for:** Teams that want generous free recording, clip sharing, and optional EU hosting, with either a visible bot or bot-free desktop capture.

[tl;dv](https://tldv.io/) records Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. Default calendar capture uses a visible **tl;dv Notetaker** participant. The **desktop app** supports **bot-free recording** by capturing system audio on Mac or Windows ([help article](https://intercom.help/tldv/en/articles/14433337-recording-without-a-bot), September 2026). [Pricing](https://tldv.io/pricing) (September 2026) includes **Free** with unlimited recordings and transcription but limits on AI notes and Ask AI; **Pro** about **$18/seat/month** annually; **Business** about **$29/seat/month** annually.

**Limits:** Bot-free mode is audio-only on your machine; consent disclosure is your responsibility. Cloud library and AI features dominate; not a local-first Swiss Mac archive.

## Fathom

**Best for:** Individuals who want **unlimited recordings and transcripts on a free plan** and can live with a visible bot, or Mac users testing **bot-free capture (beta)**.

[Fathom](https://www.fathom.ai/pricing) (September 2026) advertises **Free forever** with unlimited recordings and transcriptions, instant summaries, and a choice of **bot-free (beta on Mac)** or bot capture. Paid **Premium** is about **$16–$20/month** depending on billing, adding advanced summaries, action items, and Ask Fathom. Team tiers add collaboration and CRM features.

When the bot joins, participants see a **Fathom Notetaker** in the list, as described in Fathom’s pricing and product pages.

**Limits:** Advanced AI summaries and action items on the free tier are capped (Fathom’s pricing page describes limits on premium-style summaries after the first calls each month). Bot-free remains beta on Mac. Cloud storage, not local-only retention on your Mac.

## MacWhisper

**Best for:** Mac power users who want **local file and meeting transcription**, export control, and optional cloud models, and who will assemble their own minutes template.

[MacWhisper](https://www.macwhisper.com/) focuses on **transcription on your Mac** with local Whisper models by default. Pro adds **meeting recording** from system audio for Zoom, Teams, Webex, Skype, Discord, and more, plus speaker recognition ([MacWhisper site](https://www.macwhisper.com/), September 2026). **MacWhisper Pro** is a **one-time** license (listed at **€64** on the official site in September 2026; App Store SKUs differ).

**Limits:** MacWhisper is a transcription workstation, not an out-of-the-box minutes-and-actions product like Vemoir or Granola. You bring templates and workflow. Optional cloud transcription is opt-in in Pro.

## Three quick tests before you buy

1. **Bot visibility:** Run an internal Teams or Zoom call and check whether a notetaker appears in the participant list. If it does, plan how you will disclose that on client invites.
2. **Network tab:** On cloud tools, watch whether audio bytes upload during a test. Vemoir and MacWhisper keep capture on the Mac by default (cloud only when you opt in).
3. **Export shape:** Ask for minutes, decisions, and action items in an editable format. Some tools stop at a short bullet summary.

With everyone’s consent, record the same internal meeting twice with two finalists and compare editing time and name accuracy.

## Where a cloud bot notetaker is the better fit

A Mac-local app is not always the right answer. Cloud bots and team libraries still win when:

- **You need a shared searchable archive** across sales, success, or product, with permissions managed centrally (Otter, Fireflies, tl;dv, Fathom team tiers).
- **You want the notetaker to join without you** on calendar invites, including back-to-back meetings you cannot attend (Otter auto-join, Fireflies auto-join, tl;dv auto-recording).
- **CRM or task automations** should fire from every call (Fireflies Skills, Fathom Business, Otter integrations).
- **You must record meeting video** with a hosted copy, not just text (Fireflies bot mode, tl;dv bot mode).

In those cases, pick the vendor whose data handling and pricing page matches your organization’s rules, announce recording to participants, and treat consent and retention as your responsibility. Vemoir does not replace a company-wide conversation archive; it reduces exposure by keeping default processing on the Mac you control.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best meeting notes app for Mac without a bot?

Several Mac tools avoid a visible participant: **Vemoir**, **Granola**, **Jamie**, **MacWhisper**, and bot-free modes in **Fireflies**, **tl;dv**, **Fathom**, and **Otter desktop**. They differ on whether audio stays on the Mac (Vemoir, MacWhisper by default) versus cloud transcription after local capture (Granola, Jamie). Check each vendor’s help center for the path you plan to use.

### Can these apps record Zoom, Teams, and Google Meet on a Mac?

Yes, for local capture products, any app that plays meeting audio on the Mac can be recorded from system audio plus your microphone, as Vemoir, Granola, Jamie, and desktop modes of Fireflies and tl;dv document. Bot-based tools join the specific platform link instead.

### Do Mac meeting notes apps work offline?

**Vemoir** and **MacWhisper** support offline workflows once local models are installed. Cloud-first notetakers (Otter, Fireflies, Granola, Jamie, tl;dv, Fathom) need network access for transcription and sync during capture or upload.

### How much do Mac meeting notes apps cost?

Models range from **free tiers** (Fathom, tl;dv, Jamie, Fireflies, Otter Basic) to **subscriptions** from roughly **$10–$30 per user per month** on annual billing for pro tiers, and **one-time** purchases (**Vemoir** on the Mac App Store, **MacWhisper Pro** on macwhisper.com). Verify live pricing before purchase.

### Are AI meeting notes private on a Mac?

Privacy depends on architecture, not the word “AI.” Local-first apps keep recordings and transcripts on your Mac unless you export or opt into cloud models. Cloud notetakers store data in their service; read each vendor’s privacy and security pages and align them with your client contracts.

### Does Vemoir support Intel Macs or Windows?

No. Vemoir requires **Apple silicon** and **macOS Sonoma 14.2+** per the product fact sheet. Windows and Intel Macs are not supported.

### Can I import an existing audio file instead of recording live?

**Vemoir** imports MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF, CAF, and FLAC into a record. **MacWhisper** transcribes dropped files. Cloud tools often include upload features on paid tiers. The free Vemoir browser tools handle shorter files without upload but do not replace live menu bar capture.

### Which app is best for Swiss professionals who mention client confidentiality?

Many Swiss teams start with **where audio is processed** and **whether a bot appears on client calls**. Local Mac processing (Vemoir, MacWhisper) and EU-hosted cloud notes (Jamie) address different parts of that conversation. None of these apps makes a recording lawful by itself; follow your policies and applicable law.

### Do these tools replace a human minute taker?

They reduce typing time but still need review. Use generated minutes as a draft, verify decisions and action owners, and edit names against your project glossary. Vemoir supports speaker profiles and glossaries on Mac to improve names and terms over time.
