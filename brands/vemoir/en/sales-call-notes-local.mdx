<p class="blog-lede">Local sales call notes are the follow-up you write after a conversation, with the recording and transcript on your Mac, not in a revenue intelligence cloud.</p>

**Sales call notes** that stay on your Mac answer a narrow search: you need a discovery recap, objections, and next steps, and the buyer or your policy says the tape should not ride along with your usual call platform. That is different from a forecast tool. If the recording exists so a manager can score talk tracks and push fields into Salesforce or HubSpot, Gong and Avoma are built for that job. This page is the Mac path when you still need the words on the machine you control.

Say you are keeping a copy. No bot in the participant list is not consent. A local file is not a private legal channel, and it is not a certification.

## When local sales call notes are enough

Use a local note when most of this is true.

<ul class="blog-checklist">
  <li>Announce recording when your policy requires it</li>
  <li>Capture objections and next steps in the local minutes</li>
  <li>Export only the summary if CRM should not get raw audio</li>
</ul>

The buyer asked that the call not be processed by your usual call-intelligence vendor, or your own policy says this account stays off that platform. You still need a follow-up the same day: what they asked, what you offered, what is open. The official deal record in the CRM can be a short field you type yourself. Nobody is scoring your talk track from this tape.

<ul class="blog-scenarios">
  <li><h3>If you are a founder on the first twenty calls</h3><p>A menu-bar recorder and a short email recap beat buying a team platform before you know the motion.</p></li>
  <li><h3>If one enterprise account is off the shared library</h3><p>Keep that conversation on the laptop, then paste only the summary fields your policy allows.</p></li>
</ul>

The call is on a Mac you already use for the meeting app. Audio that plays on the machine can be captured as system audio, plus your microphone, without adding a participant. You start and stop. For an in-person pitch with one laptop, see [in-person meeting notes on a Mac](/blog/in-person-meeting-notes-mac). If you need the host's official cloud recording instead, use that product's recorder and then, if you may, download a file.

## What to capture in discovery call notes

A template helps only if each line has a job. Before you open the recorder, decide what you will need in the CRM tomorrow.

| Block | What goes in it |
| --- | --- |
| Context | Company, role, what triggered the call |
| Situation | How they work today, in their words when you quote |
| Pain | What costs time, money, or risk |
| Fit | What you showed, what landed, what did not |
| Objections | Exact wording when you can check the tape |
| Next step | Owner, date, and what "done" looks like |

Your manual line is your inference. Mark it as yours. A quote is what they said on the recording after you checked the highlighted passage.

## Where Gong and Avoma stay the better system

Gong and Avoma win when the recording exists to coach a team against the CRM.

Deal fields that update from the call, a pipeline view built from recordings, scorecards, and a manager listening across reps are their category. A menu-bar app on one laptop does not sync opportunities, does not rank calls, and does not give an admin a team library in a browser. If that is the requirement in the sales kickoff, buy the system that does it.

They also win when reps are on Windows, in a browser, or on a phone between meetings. This Mac path needs Apple silicon and macOS Sonoma 14.2 or later. It is not the device in every bag.

Read their current security and pricing pages before you quote them. This article does not restate their claims as if they were measured here. Local notes still help beside those tools when one account is excluded, or when you want a private first pass before anything is shared. They do not replace the system of record. For client calls under a stricter upload rule, [client call notes without upload](/blog/client-call-notes-without-upload) walks the same constraint from the buyer side.

## From a file you already have

[Recording to notes](/tools/recording-to-notes) takes a file and drafts notes you can send. The audio is not uploaded. Transcription and the notes draft count as one of three successful runs a week. Fifteen minutes. No speaker inference.

Drop the file you downloaded from the meeting tool, after you had the right to keep it. Do not play the call through speakers into a microphone to capture it again. Swiss German usually comes out as Standard German in that public model. Longer calls belong on the Mac app, not in the tab.

Supported shapes in that tool include the usual short meeting file. If you only have video, a short MP4 can go through the sibling [transcribe recording](/tools/transcribe-recording) path for words alone. The notes page is the one that tries to draft what you send. Paste the result into the email yourself. The tool will not log in to the CRM.

Want a draft without installing anything? Start with the browser tool above. For the next live call on the same Mac, Vemoir records from the menu bar with no bot.

## From the call while it is happening

On the Mac, start the menu-bar recording before the useful part, with the project set if this buyer has one. Headphones are fine: system audio is the call. macOS grants other apps' audio through Screen Recording permission. Vemoir uses that permission for meeting audio only, not for screen contents. Transcription runs on the Mac after you stop. The live view is a preview.

Speaker separation is stronger here than in the browser, because your mic and the remote mix are separate tracks. Rename a buyer without creating a voice profile if this is a single call. "Remember this speaker" is optional. For a prospect who was not asked to be recognized next time, leave it off. Unknown speakers stay anonymous.

Decisions are wording, a person, and a date. "They will review the security questionnaire by Friday" is an action only if they said so: description, owner, due date, status. A nod you thought you saw goes in a manual note, marked as yours.

Search later across that project's meetings and notes. Ask can cite a passage. It will not invent a discount you did not offer. Glossary terms you add do not rewrite the original transcript.

Storage: retention is chosen per meeting at creation. You can drop audio after a successful transcript and keep the text. Delete the meeting if this account's recording should not remain. That removes the transcript, the generated record, attached actions and decisions, and the recording folder. It does not delete a note you already copied into the CRM or into Apple Notes. There is no PDF or DOCX export. Copy Markdown or send the Apple Note to the folder you picked.

The app is on the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794693630). Intel Macs are unsupported.

## The note you actually send

A follow-up that earns the meeting is short.

What you heard they need. What you said you would do. What you are waiting on from them, with a date, and only if the call supports it. A link to anything you promised to send. One question that moves the next step.

Do not attach the transcript by default. Do not paste twenty lines of small talk. If a quote matters, check it against playback when you kept audio, or against the highlighted passage. An evidence link that cannot be resolved is not shown.

If the CRM must change, type the field. Local notes do not push it. That gap is the reason Gong or Avoma still win for a coached team.

For a written skeleton when you are not recording at all, the [minutes sheet](/tools/minutes-sheet) is a blank form in the browser. No model, no upload.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will this update Salesforce or HubSpot?

No. You copy text out and you type the CRM yourself. Products built for revenue teams do that sync. This path does not.

### Is a local recording visible to the buyer?

Not as a bot. Nobody new joins the participant list. They also do not get an automatic "this meeting is being recorded" banner from the Mac app. If they should know, you say it.

### Can my manager review the call?

Only if you send them something. There is no team workspace and no coaching score in this app. If review across reps is the requirement, use the platform your team already bought.

### What about a 45-minute discovery call?

The browser tool stops at 15 minutes. Record or import on the Mac. 16 GB of memory is the recommendation for long meetings. 8 GB is the minimum, with 10 GB free.

### Does the audio go to an AI vendor?

Not by default. Transcription stays on the Mac. Optional OpenAI or Anthropic keys, which you supply, take text only, stored in Keychain, with no audio and no silent fallback if you never turn them on.

### Are Gong and Avoma less private?

This page does not score them. Their own docs describe how they store and share calls for a team. That sharing is a feature when coaching is the point, and a mismatch when the buyer or your policy says the tape stays put.

### Can I use this on Windows?

Not this app. A short file can still go through the browser tool from any desktop browser that runs it, within the 15-minute cap and the weekly limit.

Keep the tape on the Mac when the account requires it, and write the follow-up from lines you checked. When the organization needs coaching and CRM sync, use Gong or Avoma for that. A file you already have can start at [recording to notes](/tools/recording-to-notes).
