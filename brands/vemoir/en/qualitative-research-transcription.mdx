**Qualitative research transcription** turns an interview or a focus-group recording into text you can code. The text is not the analysis. Codes, consent forms, and the decision about what “anonymous” means in a small sample are still your protocol. A transcription page will not assign pseudonyms correctly, and it will not know which stretches the participant withdrew.

For a file you already have, start at [transcribe an interview](/tools/transcribe-interview). It does not upload the audio. It stops at **15 minutes** and at **3 successful runs a week**. It does not label speakers.

## Consent and the file, before the model

Your information sheet should already say whether audio is recorded, who transcribes it, whether a vendor’s servers are involved, and when the audio is deleted. If the sheet says “the university keeps the recording,” a surprise upload to a commercial notetaker breaks the sheet even when the transcript looks better. Match the method to the sentence participants signed.

A browser tool that does not upload the file is one way to transcribe without adding a processor. It is not an ethics approval. Committees, the revised Swiss data protection act (nDSG) where the project sits in Switzerland, the GDPR where it sits in the EU, and your data-management plan still govern. This article does not claim the workflow is approved, certified, or compliant. Put the tool name in the amendment if the protocol listed a different method.

Withdrawn segments should be cut from the audio before you transcribe, or marked so you do not code them. A model will happily transcribe the part a participant revoked. It does not read the consent form.

Pseudonyms belong in the transcript you analyze, not only in the paper. If the raw text contains real names, that file is identifiable data. Replace names before the transcript goes to a coder who should not see them, and before any synced folder. A Desktop folder that syncs is a transfer.

## Qualitative research transcription inside the cap

1. Export the recording to MP3, M4A, WAV, or MP4.
2. If the interview is longer than 15 minutes, the browser tool is the wrong place for the whole tape. Trim only if your protocol allows working from an excerpt. Otherwise use a local model on the machine named in your data plan. Burning three weekly runs on three slices of one interview also blocks every other tape that week.
3. Open [transcribe an interview](/tools/transcribe-interview). Check the Network panel once if your lab book requires evidence that the file was not posted.
4. Save the text into the project folder your protocol names, not into a personal chat app.
5. Because there are no speaker labels, a dyadic interview needs you to mark interviewer and participant. A focus group without labels is close to unusable for turn-taking analysis. Do not pretend the browser output is diarized. For focus groups, use a local path that separates speakers, then verify each label against the audio. Unknown speakers stay unknown. A guessed name in a focus group can identify someone in a small community.

The no-upload steps are also written as a general guide in [transcribe an interview without upload](/blog/transcribe-interview-without-upload). Follow that piece for the click path. Follow this one for what the transcript is in a study.

For interviews longer than 15 minutes or focus groups that need speaker separation, import the file into [Vemoir](/#capture) on the Mac named in your data plan.

Public Whisper models usually render Swiss German as Standard German. If your analysis is of dialect, wording, or code-switching, this output is a finding risk, not a convenience. Budget time to correct, or choose a method your protocol already validated. There is no accuracy percentage in this article because quoting one would invent a benchmark.

Store the original audio according to the retention you promised. The transcript does not replace it until your protocol says it does.

## What you still do by hand

**Timestamps.** Add them where a quote will appear in the paper, so a reviewer can ask for the moment. The browser text may not give you the granularity you want. Note the clock from your recorder if you have it.

**Overlapping speech.** Two people talking at once will collapse into a mess or a single line. Mark it as overlap. Do not pick a clean sentence that nobody said.

**Nonverbal and setting.** Laughter, long pauses, and “the participant pointed at the machine” are not in the audio model. If your method includes them, your notes from the session are the source. Fold those notes in deliberately. A polished paragraph that erases a pause can change the analysis.

**Translation.** A transcript in the interview language is not a translation into the language of the article. Translate as its own step, and keep both. Do not let a chat model silently translate identifiable stories onto a vendor server if the protocol forbids that.

**The codebook.** Codes are your theory or your inductive pass. Paste a quote under a code in the software you use for that. The transcription page will not build themes for you. If you draft themes with a model, label them as a draft and check them against the full text, not against a summary that dropped the disconfirming interview.

A second coder needs the same transcript version. Export once, name the file with the interview ID from your protocol, and do not let each coder “clean up” wording in their own copy. Disagreement about codes is useful. Disagreement about what was said is a broken method. Keep the audio available to both people under the access rule you already wrote down.

Pilot interviews are still data if the protocol says they are, and they are still personal data. Do not transcribe the pilot on a random website because it “does not count.” If it does not count, delete it. If it counts, it goes through the same path as interview 12.

## Where a coding package is the better tool

NVivo, MAXQDA, Atlas.ti, or the open-source package your lab standardized on is where coding, memos, and intercoder checks belong. A meeting-notes app produces minutes and action items. Those are the wrong objects for a study. Use transcription to get text. Use the coding package to do the research.

A professional transcription service is the better buy when the protocol demands a verbatim style guide you do not have time to apply, or when the language is outside the model you can run locally. Read their processing terms against the information sheet before you send a tape. “Free and fast” is not a legal basis.

Automatic speaker labels that you do not verify are worse than no labels, because a wrong name looks like data. Verify or do not use the label in the write-up.

A live notetaker bot in a remote interview changes who is in the room. If the information sheet did not name that vendor, do not add them on the day. Record locally under the sheet you already have, or amend the sheet before the next participant.

Label the interview ID in the first line of the transcript file so a later export does not become an orphan in Downloads.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does qualitative research transcription here upload the interview?

No. The interview tool runs in the browser. Files are up to 15 minutes, with three successful runs a week.

### Can I transcribe a 90-minute interview on that page?

Not as one file. Use a local workflow named in your data-management plan, or transcribe only an excerpt if the protocol allows.

### Will focus-group speakers be separated?

Not in the browser tool. Plan on a method that diarizes, then check labels against the audio.

### Does the tool anonymize names?

No. You replace names. The model does not know your pseudonym list.

### Is the output verbatim?

It is a speech-to-text draft. Overlaps, dialect, and false starts need your ear. It is not a certified transcript.

### Can I claim the project is GDPR-compliant because the file stayed local?

No. Local processing can support a data-minimization choice. The claim, if you make one at all, belongs in the protocol and the legal basis, not in a product sentence.

### What happens to Swiss German?

Expect Standard German from public Whisper models. That may be unacceptable for dialect analysis. If the study is about wording, budget a human pass and record that pass in the method. A cleaned transcript nobody checked is not a dialect corpus.

### Where should the text go after?

Into the folder and the coding software your protocol already names. If a coder leaves the project, remove their copy on the same day you remove their other access. A transcript on a departed laptop is still the interview.

### How is this different from journalist interview transcription?

Studies need **pseudonyms, coding, and protocol amendments**. Newsrooms need **quote checks and off-the-record discipline**. The browser tool is the same cap. For the newsroom path, see [journalist interview transcription](/blog/journalist-interview-transcription).
