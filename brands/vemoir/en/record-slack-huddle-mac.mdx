## When Slack already writes the huddle notes

Slack documents **AI huddle notes** on paid plans ([Slack help](https://slack.com/help/articles/31377193680019-Use-AI-to-take-huddle-notes-in-Slack), as of September 2026). Someone in the huddle turns notes on, unless a member has set a channel to start them automatically. Everyone in the huddle is notified when notes start, stop, or are cancelled. After the huddle, Slack shares a canvas in the huddle thread with people, topics, and action items. Transcripts sit in that canvas and, on Slack’s own description, do not show up in search results. Anyone in the channel or DM where the huddle happened can view the notes.

Slack also says it is **not possible** to use AI notes in huddles with external people or guests. Guests cannot use the feature. If your huddle is a customer, a candidate, or anyone outside the workspace, the canvas path is closed even when the plan is paid.

That is the honest fork. Internal huddle, paid Slack, no guests: start with Slack’s notes. External person, free plan, or you need the audio file itself: record on the Mac.

Huddle audio is not the same thing as a Slack clip. Clips are messages you record on purpose. A huddle is the live conversation. Do not expect a Zoom-style folder of MP4 and M4A to appear when the huddle ends.

## Record a Slack huddle on Mac with system audio

Vemoir records the microphone and macOS system audio as two local tracks. Slack counts as “any app that plays audio on the Mac.” No extra participant appears in the huddle. You start and stop. The steps:

  <li data-step="1"><h3>Join the huddle on this Mac</h3><p>Audio must play through the Mac you record.</p></li>
  <li data-step="2"><h3>Allow System Audio Recording</h3><p>Audio only for Vemoir, then relaunch.</p></li>
  <li data-step="3"><h3>Start from the menu bar</h3><p>Record before the important segment ends.</p></li>
</ol>

Use a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS Sonoma 14.2 or later, 8 GB of memory, and 10 GB free for models. Install from the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794693630). Allow **Microphone** and **System Audio Recording** (audio only, not the screen). Details sit under [capture](/#capture). Download a local speech model before the huddle if needed; after that, transcription can run offline.

Join the huddle on the same Mac, start recording from the menu bar, and say you are recording. Slack does not show a banner for a local app. Stop when the huddle ends; the transcript is built from the finished local file, not the live preview.

[Meeting app support](/#platforms) lists Slack huddles with Teams, Zoom, Meet, and Discord. For Teams-specific rules, see [record a Teams meeting on Mac](/blog/record-teams-meeting-mac).

## What you get after you press stop

On the Mac you get a transcript, minutes, decisions, action items, and a follow-up draft. Speaker separation runs locally. You can rename a label without building a voice profile. “Remember this speaker” is a separate, explicit choice and the profile is encrypted and deletable. Unknown speakers stay anonymous.

Ask a question about the huddle afterward and the answer can point at a line in the transcript. Notes you typed during the huddle are folded into the minutes and keep their own origin, so a sentence you wrote is not disguised as something Slack’s audio proved.

Nothing is uploaded unless you export it or you opt into a cloud text model with your own API key. That option is text only. The audio stays on the Mac. There is no silent fallback if a cloud model fails. Leave the option off if the huddle should not leave the machine at all.

You share through the macOS share sheet, and only the parts you choose. The whole channel will not see the note the way a Slack canvas is visible to the channel. That is a feature when the huddle included a client and a flaw when you wanted the team to find the note inside Slack. If the team needs the note in the huddle thread, paste a summary you are allowed to share. Do not assume Vemoir posts back into Slack. It does not.

A short excerpt you already exported as MP3, M4A, WAV, or MP4 can go through the free [transcribe recording](/tools/transcribe-recording) tool: 15 minutes, 3 successful runs a week, no speaker labels. The live huddle itself is the Mac app.

## Where Slack’s canvas is the better record

Use Slack AI notes when all of these hold: paid plan, members only, no guests, and a canvas in the thread is the record the team will actually open. Slack notifies the huddle when notes start. A local recorder does not. If your standard is “everyone saw that notes were on,” Slack’s feature matches that standard and a menu-bar app does not, unless you say so out loud.

Slack’s notes also stay in the workspace where the conversation happened. A local file on one laptop is invisible to a colleague who was in the same huddle and does not have your export. For an internal standup, that shared canvas wins.

Use the Mac recording when an external person is in the huddle, when you are not on a paid plan, when you need the audio file, or when the notes should not live in a channel that the whole workspace can open. [Meeting notes without a bot](/blog/meeting-notes-without-a-bot) is the general version of that choice. Huddles are just the Slack-shaped case.

Consent and retention are yours. Vemoir is not a statement that recording this huddle was allowed. If the workspace rules ban local recorders, Slack’s own notes are the path that stays inside the product, or you take notes by hand.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I record a Slack huddle on Mac if AI notes are off?

Yes, by recording system audio and your microphone locally. Slack will not produce the canvas for you in that case.

### Does a bot join the huddle?

Not with Vemoir. You record the Mac. No extra tile appears because of the app.

### Will Slack tell the others I am recording?

Slack’s help says people are notified when **Slack AI notes** start or stop. A local app is outside that notice. Tell them yourself.

### Why is there no recording file in Slack after the huddle?

Slack’s documented output for AI notes is a canvas, not a Zoom-style audio folder. If notes were never turned on, there may be no transcript at all.

### What does the app cost?

CHF 20 in Switzerland, €24.99 in Germany, Austria, France, and Italy, $22.99 in the US, one time, on the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794693630). No subscription.

### Which permission does macOS ask for?

System Audio Recording for other apps’ sound, and Microphone for your voice. The screen is not recorded.

### Can I transcribe a huddle in the browser?

Only if you already have a file of 15 minutes or less. The browser tools do not join huddles and do not capture live audio.

### What about guests and Connect channels?

Slack says AI notes cannot be used in huddles with external people or guests. That is when a local recording, with consent, is the remaining option if policy allows it.
