To **transcribe a voice memo on Mac**, export the recording as an M4A from Voice Memos, then run speech-to-text on that file. Apple's app stores the audio; it does not give you a portable transcript you can drop into notes, email, or a research folder unless you copy Apple's built-in text (where that feature exists) or convert the file yourself.

This guide covers Apple's official share and drag-to-Finder path, the built-in Voice Memos transcript on supported Macs, and how to turn an M4A into text in the browser without uploading it to Vemoir. It also marks where a longer memo belongs in a local Mac app.

## How to transcribe a voice memo on Mac

The reliable path is: **get an M4A you control**, then choose a transcription method that matches the length and whether the file may leave the device.

1. Open the **Voice Memos** app on your Mac.
2. Select the recording.
3. Export a copy (Share, or drag to a Finder folder). Apple documents both in [Share a recording on Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/voice-memos/share-a-recording-vm05f9fa82d4/mac).
4. Transcribe that M4A: Apple's in-app transcript if it is available on your Mac, the free [voice memo tool](/tools/transcribe-voice-memo) for files up to 15 minutes, or [import the file](/#capture) in the Vemoir Mac app for longer memos and speaker separation.

Do not rename or delete files inside Voice Memos' hidden Library folder. Treat that path as a last-resort backup, not as an editing workspace.

## Export the M4A from Voice Memos

Voice Memos on Mac writes AAC audio. The usual container after export is **M4A**, which the browser tool and the Mac app both accept (along with MP3, WAV, and, in the browser, MP4 when the audio track decodes).

Apple's supported export methods:

1. Go to **Voice Memos** on your Mac.
2. Select the memo.
3. Click the **Share** button and choose an app or service, or **drag the recording into a Finder window** to save a copy in a folder you pick ([Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/guide/voice-memos/share-a-recording-vm05f9fa82d4/mac)).

You can also drag a recording into Mail, Notes, or Messages. For transcription, a folder on disk is cleaner: you keep the original in Voice Memos and work on the copy.

Quality lives under **Voice Memos → Settings**. Apple lets you choose **Compressed** (smaller) or **Lossless** (larger) ([Voice Memos settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/voice-memos/change-voice-memos-settings-vm0ba246ff3e/mac)). Either format can become an M4A you drop on a tool. Lossless files are heavier; if a browser decode fails, export again as Compressed or convert to WAV.

If the memo synced from iPhone, it still appears in Voice Memos on the Mac when you use the same Apple Account ([see recordings on all your devices](https://support.apple.com/guide/voice-memos/see-your-recordings-on-all-your-apple-devices/mac)). Export from the Mac once it has finished downloading. A memo that is still a cloud placeholder will not give you a complete file.

WhatsApp voice notes are a different container. Many land as `.opus` or `.ogg`. The Vemoir browser tool refuses those. Save or share a copy as **M4A or MP3** on the phone first. There is no WhatsApp bot in this workflow.

## When Apple's built-in Voice Memos transcript is enough

On **macOS 15 or later** with **Apple silicon**, Voice Memos can recognize speech and show a transcript in the app. Apple documents this in [View a Voice Memos transcription on Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/voice-memos/view-a-transcription-of-a-recording-vm4a03609f0d/mac). The feature is **not available in all countries or regions**. You can view text while recording or afterward, copy passages, and search titles plus transcripts.

Use that path when:

- The memo is already in Voice Memos on a supported Mac.
- You only need to copy a few paragraphs into Notes or an email.
- You do not need speaker labels, a minutes draft, or a file-based workflow you can rerun later.

Stay off that path when Apple's transcript is missing in your region, the copy-out is awkward, or you need a workflow that does not depend on Voice Memos staying open. Then export the M4A and transcribe it as a file.

Apple's in-app text is still not a certified transcript. Names, numbers, and quotes need a human pass, the same as any speech-to-text output.

## Turn the exported M4A into text in the browser

For a memo you already exported, the free tool at [/tools/transcribe-voice-memo](/tools/transcribe-voice-memo) runs a public Whisper model **in the tab**. The file is **not uploaded** to Vemoir. On first use the model downloads into the browser cache. Later runs can work with the network off once that cache is in place.

Limits to plan for:

- **15 minutes** per file.
- **Three successful runs per week**.
- **No speaker labels**. Names already in the file stay; the tool does not invent who spoke.
- **No live recording** in the browser. This is after-the-file work.

Swiss German speech in public Whisper models usually comes out as **Standard German** text, not dialect spelling. Do not treat the wording as a certified or court-ready record.

Steps:

1. Confirm the file is MP3, M4A, WAV, or MP4, and **15 minutes or shorter**.
2. Open [/tools/transcribe-voice-memo](/tools/transcribe-voice-memo).
3. Drop the file and pick the **spoken language** (the tool does not translate).
4. Copy or download the text. Keep or delete the M4A yourself. Retention stays with you.

The same 15-minute trial exists on the generic [transcribe recording](/tools/transcribe-recording) page if you already have a meeting file rather than a Voice Memo.

## How to check that the memo was not uploaded

1. Open the tool page and start a short run.
2. Open **Developer Tools** (Chrome or Edge: **View → Developer → Developer Tools**, or right-click → **Inspect**).
3. Open the **Network** tab. Filter by **Fetch/XHR** if you have that option.
4. Watch for a multipart upload of your audio. You should see **model or asset downloads** on first use, not your M4A posted to a Vemoir host.

Internet is required for that first model download. After the model is cached, you can disconnect and run again if the browser still has the weights. Closing the tab stops a run in progress.

## When the memo is longer than 15 minutes

The browser page refuses a file over 15 minutes **before** the model runs. Splitting a long interview into clips still spends **one weekly run per successful pass**, and you lose a single continuous transcript.

For long Voice Memos, import the M4A in the [Vemoir Mac app](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794693630). Common local audio files can be imported with a file picker or drag and drop, assigned to a project, and processed with the same local pipeline as a recorded meeting ([capture and models](/#capture)). Transcription and speaker processing stay on the Mac. Summaries and Ask Vemoir run locally by default. A cloud text model is opt-in with your own API key; it does not receive the raw audio.

If the next capture is a live call rather than a memo, record from the menu bar instead of hunting files afterward ([how it works](/#how)). No bot joins Zoom, Teams, Meet, or a Slack huddle.

Want this without hunting Voice Memos folders every time? [Import the next M4A on your Mac](/#capture) or record the call from the menu bar.

## The hidden Library folder (last resort)

On current macOS versions, Voice Memos often keeps recordings under:

`~/Library/Group Containers/group.com.apple.VoiceMemos.shared/Recordings/`

That path is not in Apple's user guide. People find it via Finder's hidden Library (hold **Option** in the **Go** menu) or by inspecting the Voice Memos process. Filenames inside are not always the titles you see in the app. A companion database tracks titles.

Copy a file out if Voice Memos will not export. **Do not rename, move, or delete files in place.** You can break the app's library. Prefer Share or drag-to-Finder whenever those work.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I transcribe a voice memo on Mac without uploading it?

Export an M4A with Share or drag-to-Finder ([Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/guide/voice-memos/share-a-recording-vm05f9fa82d4/mac)), then use the [voice memo tool](/tools/transcribe-voice-memo) in the browser. The file stays in the tab. For longer memos, import the same M4A in the Mac app.

### Does Voice Memos already transcribe on Mac?

On macOS 15 or later with Apple silicon, Voice Memos can show a transcript in the app, not in every country or region ([Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/guide/voice-memos/view-a-transcription-of-a-recording-vm4a03609f0d/mac)). If the feature is missing or you need a file-based workflow, export the M4A.

### Where does Voice Memos store files on Mac?

Work from the app. Apple's documented path is Share or drag a recording into Finder. A hidden Group Containers folder exists on many Macs; treat it as a backup, not as the place to edit files.

### Can I convert M4A to text on Mac for free?

Yes, for files up to 15 minutes and up to three successful browser runs per week on [/tools/transcribe-voice-memo](/tools/transcribe-voice-memo). Apple's built-in transcript, where available, is another free path inside Voice Memos.

### Will the browser tool label speakers?

No. Browser tools do not separate speakers. Speaker profiles and rename-after-the-fact are Mac-app features ([capture](/#capture)).

### What about a WhatsApp voice note?

Export it as M4A or MP3 first. `.opus` and `.ogg` are refused. There is no live WhatsApp capture and no bot.

### Does Swiss German stay Swiss German?

Usually not in the browser. Public Whisper models typically write **Standard German**. There is no dialect model in that download.

### What if my memo is longer than 15 minutes?

Use the Mac app import path, or stay in Voice Memos if Apple's transcript is available and good enough. Splitting clips in the browser burns the weekly quota quickly.

## Next step

Export the memo, then open [/tools/transcribe-voice-memo](/tools/transcribe-voice-memo) if it is 15 minutes or shorter. For a longer file or speaker labels, import the M4A in the [Mac app](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794693630) and keep processing on the device.
