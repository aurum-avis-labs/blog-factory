## What the one-time meeting notes app includes

Vemoir records from the menu bar. It captures your microphone and the Mac’s system audio as separate local tracks, so a call in Teams, Zoom, Meet, Slack, Discord, or another app that plays sound on the Mac can be recorded without a bot joining the participant list. You start and stop the recording. Nobody on the call sees an extra guest.

macOS asks you to allow **System Audio Recording** for that tap. It is audio only. The app does not record the screen. You also allow the microphone if you want your own voice on its own track. Details sit on [capture](/#capture). Which apps this covers is on [platforms](/#platforms).

After you stop, transcription and speaker separation run on the Mac. The app writes minutes, decisions, action items, and a follow-up draft. You can ask questions about the meeting, and the answers point back at the transcript. Notes you type during the call fold into the record. You share only what you choose through macOS sharing.

Local models are the default. A cloud text model (OpenAI or Anthropic) is opt-in, uses your own API key in the Keychain, and applies to text. Audio and speaker separation stay on the Mac. There is no silent fallback to a cloud model if the local one fails.

Once the models you chose are downloaded, the meeting workflow can run offline. Internet is for setup and those downloads, not for every call.

## What the Mac must be

Apple silicon, M1 or newer. macOS Sonoma 14.2 or later. At least 8 GB of memory and 10 GB of free storage for models. 16 GB or more is the recommendation for long meetings. Intel Macs are not supported. There is no Windows, iPhone, iPad, or Android app.

The interface is English, German, French, Italian, and Dutch. Transcription language depends on the speech model you download. Parakeet v3 covers 25 European languages. Whisper Turbo, Medium, and Large-v3 are the other local speech choices. Qwen3 4B writes notes on most Macs. gpt-oss-20b is the local text model for high-memory Macs. Those models do not transcribe the audio. They write from the transcript. A ranking by accuracy would need a published percentage, and there is none to quote.

Swiss German dialect is not a claim. Standard German is. A dialect path has no date yet.

You will spend disk on the models. The 10 GB figure is a floor, not a promise that every model fits in a sliver of free space. The app shows download size before you fetch a model, and downloads are explicit. It does not silently swap a model in the middle of a recording.

## How a live call becomes notes

1. Install Vemoir from the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794693630) and open it once.
2. Allow the microphone and **System Audio Recording** when macOS asks. You can revisit both under System Settings, Privacy & Security.
3. Download the speech model and the local text model you intend to use. Wait until the download finishes. Setup can resume if you quit halfway.
4. Optional: file the next recording under a project before you press record, if the call belongs to a client.
5. Join the meeting in the app you already use. Start recording from the menu bar when you are ready, and tell the other people that you are recording. Consent is your responsibility.
6. Type anything you already know is a decision into Manual Notes if you do not want to rely on the transcript alone.
7. Stop the recording. The app rebuilds the transcript from the finished audio on the Mac. A live preview during the call is not the record.
8. Read the summary, actions, and decisions. Rename a speaker if a label is wrong. Export or share only the note you want someone else to see.

If you already have a file, you can import MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF, CAF, or FLAC through the same local pipeline. A short file can also go through [transcribe a recording](/tools/transcribe-recording) without installing anything, inside the 15-minute and weekly limits.

## Where a subscription is still the better fit

Pay Otter, Fireflies, Granola, or a similar seat product when the company needs one searchable library, an admin console, and a bot or a calendar join that a laptop on one desk will not provide. A one-time app on your Mac does not put the same transcript in a colleague’s account. If that shared inbox is the job, the subscription is the honest buy. Their current seat prices are a separate article. Start from their own pricing pages.

The one-time app is the better fit when you record your own calls, the audio should stay on the machine, and you do not want a renewal in March. It is also the fit when a client will not accept an extra participant. A bot-free capture is the point of the menu-bar recorder, described in [meeting notes without a bot](/blog/meeting-notes-without-a-bot).

Neither option makes a recording lawful. You still tell participants, and you still decide how long to keep the file. Vemoir does not claim HIPAA, a GDPR certificate, or legal privilege.

Before you buy, check the Mac, not the store text: Apple silicon with Sonoma 14.2 or later, 10 GB free before the speech model downloads, and whether the next group will accept a recording. The app does not announce recording in Zoom, Teams, or Slack. You do.

Run the first test on a short call you can reread the same day. Listen to one minute of audio against the text before you share the minutes. A ten-minute file with no live capture can stay on the browser tool. Buy the app when the next meeting is live on the Mac and longer than fifteen minutes.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much is the one-time meeting notes app?

CHF 20 in Switzerland, €24.99 in Germany, Austria, France, and Italy, and $22.99 in the US, on the Mac App Store. No subscription.

### Do I pay per meeting or per seat?

No. One purchase covers the app on your Mac. There is no seat count inside Vemoir.

### Are the free website tools included in the CHF 20?

The website tools are free and limited. The App Store price is the Mac app: live recording, speaker separation, and the local notes workflow.

### Does the price include cloud transcription?

Transcription runs on the Mac. If you turn on your own OpenAI or Anthropic key, that vendor bills you for text generation. Audio still stays local. You can leave the cloud option off.

### Which Macs are left out?

Intel Macs, and anything older than Apple silicon or older than macOS Sonoma 14.2. Phones and Windows PCs are not supported.

### Will it record my screen?

No. System Audio Recording is the permission for other apps’ sound. The screen is not captured.

### Can two people share one purchase?

The App Store license follows Apple’s rules for the buyer’s Apple ID. Vemoir does not sell a team plan or a second seat.

### What if I only have a 12-minute clip?

Use the free [recording transcription](/tools/transcribe-recording) tool. Keep the 15-minute and 3-runs-a-week limits in mind. Do not buy the app for a single short file unless you also want live capture next week.
