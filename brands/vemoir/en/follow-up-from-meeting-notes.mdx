A **follow-up from meeting notes** is the message or the file you send after the call, built from the record rather than from memory. It should say what was chosen, who does the next thing, and what is still open. It should not attach the recording unless the other person asked for the tape.

This is a writing job with a clear boundary. The notes stay as evidence on your Mac. The follow-up is the short document that leaves the room.

## What a follow-up from meeting notes contains

Four blocks are enough for most calls:


<Image src={inline1} alt="A follow-up letter and a closed notebook" width={700} quality={80} class="w-full" />

<p><em>Photo by <a href="https://unsplash.com/@brandomakesbranding?utm_source=blog_factory&utm_medium=referral" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">brandomakesbranding</a> on <a href="https://unsplash.com/?utm_source=blog_factory&utm_medium=referral" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Unsplash</a></em></p>

1. **One sentence on why the call happened.** Not a summary of the small talk.
2. **Decisions.** Wording, person, date. If nothing was decided, say that.
3. **Actions.** Description, owner, due date. Skip an owner and the line is a wish.
4. **Open questions.** What you still need, and from whom.

A client follow-up can close with the next date. An internal follow-up can close with where the full record lives. Neither version needs the transcript pasted underneath.

A skeleton you can paste into Mail or Notes:

```text
Subject: {meeting}, {date}

Thanks for the time on {date}.

Decisions
- 

Actions
- {what}. Owner: {name}. Due: {date}.

Still open
- 

Next
- 
```

Good.

## Write it the same day

The follow-up gets worse overnight because you start smoothing. Write from the notes, not from the mood of the call.

Order that stays reliable:

1. Stop the recording and let the record finish. A live preview during the call is only a preview. The transcript that counts is the one built from the finished audio.
2. Read decisions and actions. Correct a wrong owner before you copy anything.
3. Fold in manual notes you typed during the call (a figure, a name, a constraint). Those notes are yours. They should show up as yours, not as a quote.
4. Copy the short version. Send that. Leave the raw transcript on the Mac.

If you use your own OpenAI or Anthropic key for the draft, that path is text only, and you turn it on yourself. The audio is not part of that request. If the provider fails, the draft stops. You can still copy what is already on the meeting.

## Copy, a document, or Apple Notes

Vemoir gives you three ways to get the follow-up out. All of them are on the Mac until you send them.

**Clipboard.** Summaries, documents, and meeting minutes can be copied. The toolbar follows the section you are looking at: Summary, Actions, Decisions, or Transcript. Copy Decisions and Actions for the follow-up. Copy the transcript only when someone asked for the words.

**A document.** The Documents area writes a Markdown file for a meeting or a project, from a built-in template or one you wrote. It autosaves, keeps a provenance trail, and can be regenerated without throwing away your edits. You can save an Ask answer as a document in that meeting or project. Export can leave out the evidence links, which is what you want in a note the other person will read without opening your library.

**Apple Notes.** Pick a real account and a folder on the project. Export from the meeting, or turn on export after processing succeeds. The note includes the summary, key points, risks, open questions, decisions, actions, participants, and custom documents, plus a provenance line. Vemoir stores the note's identifier so a later export updates that note instead of creating a second one. Preview the combined note before it goes across.

There is no PDF or DOCX export in the app. If a client portal demands one of those, paste the Markdown or the Notes text into the tool they already use. The content is the follow-up. The container is theirs.

Sharing is the same set of sections, through the Mac share sheet, for the part you choose. You do not have to share the recording to share the note.

If you map `ME` to your name in export settings, copied text shows `Name (Me)` so the recipient sees a person, not a placeholder.

## What not to put in the follow-up

- The recording, unless it was requested and you are allowed to send it.
- Speaker-by-speaker transcripts of side comments.
- A voice profile, a glossary, or anything that is a setting rather than an outcome.
- Internal evidence keys. Those are stripped from copied chat and documents. The readable note should not contain them.
- A second, friendlier version of a decision that contradicts the row. Edit the row, then copy.

A follow-up that restates the decision more softly will be the version the client remembers. Make the row and the email say the same thing.

## When the other side needs a different shape

Some recipients want a status note for a whole project, not a recap of one hour. A project document can draw on the meetings filed there. A built-in Project Status Update is for that project-wide document, not for a single call. Pin it on the project if you will reuse it.

Some recipients want only the actions, as a table: description, owner, due date. Copy Actions when that is the whole message.

Some recipients are in the room next time and only need the open questions. Send those three lines. A long follow-up is not more complete. It is harder to answer.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a follow-up from meeting notes the same as the minutes?

The minutes are the record. The follow-up is the part you send. They should not contradict each other. The follow-up is usually shorter.

### Can I send the follow-up without the recording?

Yes. Copy the summary, the decisions, or a document. The audio stays on the Mac unless you export it yourself.

### What if I need a PDF?

The app writes Markdown and can hand a note to Apple Notes. Paste that text into whatever produces the PDF. There is no PDF or DOCX export in Vemoir.

### Will a second export create a second Apple Note?

Not if Vemoir still has the note identifier. A later export updates the same note. If you deleted the note in Apple Notes, the next export cannot update what is gone.

### Do manual notes show up in the follow-up?

They can, because they feed the summary, actions, decisions, and documents. They keep their own mark. They are not presented as a line someone spoke.

### Does the browser draft the email?

The minutes sheet does not. It is a blank you fill and copy. The Mac app can draft the sections from the recording, and you copy the result.

### Who is "me" in the copied note?

Set your export name so `ME` renders as your name with `(Me)` beside it. Otherwise the recipient sees the placeholder.

Copy the decisions and the actions the day of the call. The controls for that handoff are under [exports](/#exports).
