You export a Teams transcript after the meeting from the meeting chat's Recap, if transcription was on and your role is allowed to download it. Microsoft’s support page says organizers and co-organizers can download it as a `.docx` or a `.vtt` by default, and that others may only view it unless an admin policy says otherwise. The file is not a recording of the screen, and it does not exist if nobody turned transcription on.

Menu names move. The steps below follow Microsoft Support as of September 2026: [download a meeting transcript](https://support.microsoft.com/en-au/teams/meetings/start-stop-and-download-live-transcripts-in-microsoft-teams-meetings). If your tenant looks different, trust the labels in your Teams client over this page, and ask the organizer before you hunt through OneDrive.

## Check that a transcript exists

Teams can transcribe a meeting only if transcription ran. Turning on a recording does not always mean a transcript was stored for you to download later. If the button was never available, an admin may have disabled transcription, or the meeting type may not support it. Guests and people outside the organizer’s org often cannot download even when they could see captions on screen.

Live captions during the call are not the same artifact as the file. Captions scroll away. The transcript, when it exists, is kept with the meeting. Microsoft says transcripts for meetings with recording or transcription are stored in the meeting organizer’s OneDrive for Business, and that people reach them through the meeting chat and the Recap tab until the organizer deletes them.

Before you follow a five-step guide, open the past meeting and see whether Recap shows a transcript at all. If it does not, no export procedure will create one from memory.

## Export a Teams transcript from Recap

On desktop Teams, Microsoft documents this path:

<p><em>Photo by <a href="https://unsplash.com/@creatopy?utm_source=blog_factory&utm_medium=referral" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Creatopy</a> on <a href="https://unsplash.com/?utm_source=blog_factory&utm_medium=referral" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Unsplash</a></em></p>

1. Select **Chat**.
2. Open the past meeting chat.
3. Select **Recap**.
4. Select **Transcript**.
5. Open the dropdown next to **Download** and choose the file type, typically `.docx` or `.vtt`.

You can also start from the calendar event for that meeting and look for recordings and transcripts, depending on the build. Town halls and some webinar-style meetings use a different recordings pane. If Download is missing, you are probably not an organizer or co-organizer, or the policy blocks downloads for attendees even inside the tenant.

The Teams mobile app can show a transcript after the meeting from the chat. Microsoft notes that downloading is a desktop task. Do not expect the phone to hand you the VTT.

Save the file somewhere you control. The name Teams chooses is easy to confuse with the video. Keep the transcript next to a note of the meeting date, and do not drop the only copy into a chat with the whole company "so everyone has it" unless that was the point.

## What the download actually contains

A `.vtt` is a caption file. It starts with `WEBVTT`, then cue times, then lines of speech. When speaker attribution is present, a line often looks like `<v Name>what they said</v>`. That name is what Teams stored. It might be a display name, a room, or a phone. It is not a cleaned attendance list.

A `.docx` is the same conversation in a document, easier to forward, worse to parse if you still need the times. Some tenants and some older documentation disagree about Word downloads. If you only see one format, take that format. Do not install a random "Teams transcript converter" to force the other.

Neither file is the meeting video. The recording, if one exists, is a separate download and lives under the organizer’s OneDrive. Sharing the transcript is not the same as sharing the video, and sharing either one may be more than the attendees agreed to. This article is not legal advice.

If you needed a transcript and the meeting was not transcribed, Teams will not reconstruct it from the video inside the Recap button. People sometimes get captions later from Stream on a recording. That is a different Microsoft feature, with its own delay and its own permissions. It is not a promise that yesterday’s untranscribed call now has a VTT.

## Turn the export into readable text

The VTT is the right input for a formatting clean, not for a second transcription. Drop it on the [transcript cleaner](/tools/transcript-cleaner). Cue numbers and timestamps come off. Names stay if they were in the file, including `<v Name>` tags. If there were no names, the page says so and does not invent speakers. That clean does not spend one of the three weekly model runs.

Then:

- Skim numbers, dates, and product names.
- Send the cleaned text to [meeting notes from a transcript](/tools/meeting-notes-from-transcript) only if you want a draft recap. That draft uses a weekly run. Check every owner.
- Copy decisions and actions onto the [minutes sheet](/tools/minutes-sheet) when you are ready to file a protocol. That sheet does not transcribe and does not guess names.

Do not paste the raw VTT into the follow-up mail. The cues are noise. Do not paste a model’s recap without the sentence that supports each decision.

A `.docx` that is already prose may not need the cleaner. Copy out the text, watch for headers Teams inserted, and still do not treat "Conference Room" as a person.

## When you cannot export, and what to do instead

You will fail the Recap path for boring reasons: you joined as a guest, transcription was off, the organizer deleted the file, or the admin allows viewing but not download. None of those are fixed by refreshing the chat.

For the next call, decide before it starts.

- If the organizer will transcribe and you are a co-organizer, use Teams and export afterward. You already know the path.
- If you may not get the file, record on the Mac from the menu bar while you are in the meeting, with people told. Vemoir captures microphone and system audio. No bot joins the Teams roster. macOS permission for other apps’ audio is **System Audio Recording**, audio only, not a screen recording. The how-to for the live side is [record a Teams meeting on Mac](/blog/record-teams-meeting-mac).
- If someone already handed you an audio file of 15 minutes or less, [transcribe a recording](/tools/transcribe-recording) runs in the browser and does not upload the file. It will not label speakers. Longer files belong on the Mac: import MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF, CAF, or FLAC.

A local recording is your file. A Teams transcript is the organizer’s file in their OneDrive until they delete it. Pick the one you are actually allowed to keep.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who can export a Teams transcript?

By default, the organizer and co-organizers, according to Microsoft Support. Other people in the org can often view it in Recap and still lack Download. Guests may see less. Admin policies override the default.

### Which file should I choose, VTT or Word?

Choose `.vtt` if you want to strip timestamps and keep speaker tags in a predictable text form. Choose `.docx` if a person needs to read it in Word immediately. You can clean a VTT without spending a transcription run.

### The Download button is missing. Did the transcript fail?

Not always. Transcription may have run, and your account may only be allowed to view. Ask the organizer to download it or to change who can. If Recap has no transcript tab, transcription was likely off or the file was deleted.

### Can I export a transcript from the Teams mobile app?

You can view it there after the meeting. Plan on desktop for the file. Microsoft documents download from Chat, Recap, and the download control on desktop.

### Does the export include speaker names?

Often, as voice tags in the VTT, when the tenant still allows speaker attribution. If the tags are absent, no cleaner will put them back. The browser does not separate speakers from audio, and this download is already text.

### Where is the file stored?

Microsoft says it is stored in the organizer’s OneDrive for Business and opened through the meeting chat and Recap until the organizer deletes it. Do not assume a copy remains on your laptop unless you downloaded it.

### Can I get a transcript if we only recorded video?

Not from the Recap transcript control, if transcription never ran. A recording can be a different object from a transcript. Check Stream only if your tenant actually generates captions there, and do not promise a file you have not seen.

### Is a Teams transcript the same as minutes?

No. It is what was said, plus timestamps and whatever names Teams stored. Minutes are the decisions and the actions. Clean the export, then write the short page.
