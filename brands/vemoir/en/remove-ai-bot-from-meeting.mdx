To **remove an AI bot from a meeting**, treat it like a guest: the host or co-host removes it from the call, and whoever connected the notetaker to a calendar turns off auto-join. A bot is not a microphone setting. It is an account on the participant list. If you did not invite it, you need the host or the person who owns the calendar connection.

This is the in-call fix. Why bots show up, and what a meeting without one looks like, is in [meeting notes without a bot](/blog/meeting-notes-without-a-bot). The steps below assume the bot is already in the room.

## Remove the bot from a meeting that is running

Read the participant list first. The bot is usually named Otter, Fireflies, Fathom, tl;dv, a company "notetaker," or a person's name plus "AI." Say that name once so everyone means the same row.

On Zoom, the host or co-host removes the entry under Participants. On Google Meet, the host removes it from the same panel. On Microsoft Teams, an organizer or presenter who may manage participants can remove someone ("Remove" or "Remove from meeting"). If you are a guest without that control, ask the host. You cannot remove a bot from a meeting you do not control.

**Zoom.** Open Participants, hover the bot name, choose Remove. Co-hosts see the same controls when the host allowed them. The bot cannot re-enter unless someone invites it again.

**Google Meet.** In the participant panel, Remove person or the three-dot menu beside the name. External guests without host rights see the bot but cannot remove it themselves.

**Microsoft Teams.** Under Participants, Remove from meeting. Some tenants label it Remove. Listeners need the organizer. Tenant policies that block certain apps are an admin topic, not a substitute for removing the bot in this call.

Remove stops this call only. Audio the bot already uploaded stays in the product account until the owner deletes it there. Otter describes recording permissions and the joining notetaker in [Recording Permissions with Otter](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/39339238308503-Recording-Permissions-with-Otter) (September 2026). If your rules require it: turn recording off in the product and delete the meeting there.

Do not invent a tenant-wide block for "all bots" in this moment. Whether an admin disabled apps is that company's policy, not a button on the tile.

## Stop the same bot from joining the next call

It returns because a calendar still allows "join every meeting." Removing it once does not clear that rule.

Find the account that owns the bot:

- Otter, Fireflies, Fathom, and tl;dv document a bot that enters when the connected calendar has a link. Turn auto-join off and disconnect the calendar if the next invite should stay bot-free.
- Fathom 3.0 describes bot-free modes on a Mac for English meetings ([Fathom 3.0](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/11577345), September 2026). If your team uses that mode, the participant list is the wrong place to look. If a Fathom bot still appears, auto-join is still on for that user.
- A teammate's personal account can admit a bot to your external meeting. You see it; they turn it off. You cannot change their account from the guest view.

Run a short test meeting with the same account afterward and confirm no extra participant appears. That beats support chat saying "off" while the calendar link is still live.

In Outlook or Google Calendar you sometimes see add-ins or connected apps beside the event. Whoever enabled a notetaker there should clear it before the next external call. Hosts who cannot see someone else's calendar need to ask that account owner directly.

## Local recording instead of a bot

Something that never joins has no tile to remove. Vemoir captures microphone and system audio from the Mac menu bar. You start and stop; Zoom, Meet, and Teams see no extra guest. macOS asks for Microphone and System Audio Recording. The capture is speaker and mic audio, not screen content, and not a bot.

For the next call without a name on the list, [Vemoir](/#how) records locally when you start it.

Zoom's recording banner belongs to Zoom's recorder. A menu-bar app that taps system audio does not flip that banner. You still tell the room you are recording. The app does not post in chat.

After the call, a short clip can go through [transcribe a recording](/tools/transcribe-recording): file stays in the tab, no upload, 15 minutes, 3 successful runs a week, MP3/M4A/WAV/MP4, no speaker labels. Check the Network tab if you want proof nothing is posted. A full meeting belongs in the Mac app, with transcription and speaker separation on the machine.

That path does not delete a bot someone else already sent. It is how you take notes on the next call without adding a participant.

Privacy law is not just the absence of a bot. Telling people you record is still on you. This is not legal advice.

## What you tell the room

Name the bot, say you are removing it, and say whether anyone is still recording locally. "No bots" is not the same as "nobody is writing this down." Local capture is invisible on the participant list. The honest line: the bot is gone, and the host is recording on the Mac.

If a customer forbade recording, stop local capture too. Removing the bot does not make a personal recorder allowed. Consent is your job; the app does not ask the other side.

If you only need text from a file you already have, the browser tool is enough for clips under 15 minutes. For the next live call, start capture before people join and name it out loud when they arrive.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who can remove an AI bot from a meeting?

The host or co-host on Zoom, the host on Google Meet, and on Teams an organizer or presenter who may remove participants. A guest sees the bot and asks the host.

### Does Remove delete the recording the bot already made?

No. It disconnects the participant from this call. The copy in the product account remains until the owner deletes it there ([Otter recording permissions](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/39339238308503-Recording-Permissions-with-Otter), September 2026).

### Why did the bot come back five minutes later?

Auto-join or a calendar connection is still on. The person who connected the tool must turn that off. A second Remove does not change their account.

### Will Zoom show a recording banner for a Mac app?

Zoom's banner tracks Zoom's own recording. System audio from the menu bar does not add a participant and does not trigger that banner. You tell the room yourself.

### Can I block every AI bot for my company?

Only an admin of the tenant or product can set that, and only for tools they control. This article does not describe a Microsoft switch that removes every vendor's bot. Remove the one you see, then turn off the account that invited it.

### Is there text without inviting a bot?

For files under 15 minutes, [transcribe a recording](/tools/transcribe-recording) runs in the tab with no upload. No speaker labels, 3 successful runs a week. Live capture of the whole meeting is the Mac app.

Next time a bot appears, read the name, have the host remove it, turn off auto-join, then glance at the participant list again before you continue the agenda.
