A **Fireflies.ai alternative** helps when you want AI meeting notes, search and conversation intelligence, but a cloud notetaker bot in the participant list is a problem, or when you need tighter control over where audio is processed and stored. Fireflies is strong at team-wide capture, CRM-oriented workflows and deep analytics across Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams. The options below trade some of that cloud convenience for local capture, different pricing or narrower platforms.

This guide compares seven alternatives as of September 2026. Plan limits and prices change, so follow the linked vendor pages before you standardize on a tool.

## What Fireflies.ai does well

[Fireflies.ai](https://fireflies.ai/) is built around an AI notetaker that can auto-join calendar meetings, record, transcribe and summarize in the cloud. The product advertises speaker recognition, action items, AskFred search across past calls, conversation intelligence and a large integration catalog for CRM and project tools ([Fireflies homepage](https://fireflies.ai/), checked September 2026). Paid tiers add team workspaces, analytics, API access and admin controls ([Fireflies pricing](https://fireflies.ai/pricing), checked September 2026).

For revenue and customer-facing teams that want one searchable library of calls, Fireflies' bot-first workflow and per-seat plans are the point. The vendor also documents a Chrome extension path to record some Google Meet calls without the Fireflies bot joining as a participant, plus mobile and desktop apps for in-person capture ([Fireflies pricing](https://fireflies.ai/pricing), checked September 2026). Security and compliance pages describe SOC 2 Type II, GDPR and HIPAA offerings, private storage on enterprise tiers and a stated policy that customer data is not used for AI training by default ([Fireflies security](https://fireflies.ai/security), checked September 2026).

## How to choose a Fireflies.ai alternative

Start with three practical questions:

1. **Can a bot join the call?** Many clients and IT policies block third-party notetakers in the participant list. See [meeting notes without a bot](/blog/meeting-notes-without-a-bot) for how that constraint shows up in practice.
2. **Where does audio go?** Cloud services store recordings and transcripts on their infrastructure. Local Mac apps keep processing on your device unless you opt into a cloud model or export.
3. **What do you need after the call?** CRM sync, team search, offline use and one-time pricing all point to different tools.

If you only need text from a file you already have, Vemoir offers free browser tools on [vemoir.ch](/tools) that transcribe in the tab without uploading the file (see [how capture works](/#capture) for the Mac app).

## Seven Fireflies.ai alternatives

### Vemoir (Mac app)

**Best for:** keeping meeting audio, transcripts and generated records on your Mac by default, without a bot in the call.

Vemoir records microphone and system audio from the menu bar. You start and stop each session. Transcription and summaries run locally with on-device models, and you can opt into cloud models with your own API key. The app turns a call into minutes, decisions, action items and a follow-up draft, with speaker separation on the Mac ([how the record works](/#record)).

Pricing is a one-time purchase in the Mac App Store (CHF 20 in Switzerland, €24.99 in Germany, Austria, France and Italy, $22.99 in the US as listed on [pricing](/#pricing)). Requires Apple silicon and macOS Sonoma 14.2 or later.

### Otter.ai

**Best for:** teams that already rely on Otter's calendar notetaker, mobile apps and shared workspaces across Zoom, Teams and Meet.

Otter joins meetings from the calendar, transcribes in near real time and sells per-user subscription plans with minute pools on paid tiers ([Otter pricing](https://otter.ai/pricing), checked September 2026). Admin and recording-permission documentation is published in Otter's help center ([Otter help: recording permissions](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/39339238308503-Recording-Permissions-with-Otter), checked September 2026).

### Fathom

**Best for:** sales and CS teams that want generous free recording, highlight reels and CRM sync on paid tiers.

Fathom records and transcribes meetings, generates summaries and action items, and offers team plans with collaboration features ([Fathom pricing](https://fathom.video/pricing), checked September 2026). Individual plans advertise a choice of bot-based capture or a bot-free beta path on Mac.

### Granola

**Best for:** a lightweight AI notepad that listens through your computer audio without adding a meeting participant.

Granola runs on desktop and mobile, uses your calendar, and relies on third-party transcription and AI providers to produce notes ([Granola homepage](https://www.granola.ai/), checked September 2026). Its [security page](https://www.granola.ai/security) states that it does not store meeting audio long term, that notes live in encrypted cloud infrastructure, and that enterprise customers can disable model training by default.

### MacWhisper

**Best for:** Mac users who want local transcription and optional cloud AI via their own keys, with flexible export and batch workflows.

MacWhisper transcribes files and can record online meetings without a bot, using local models by default ([MacWhisper site](https://www.macwhisper.com/), checked September 2026). Pro is a one-time license (listed at €64 on the site in September 2026) with lifetime updates on that page. Summaries and chat features can use local or connected AI services you configure.

### tl;dv

**Best for:** teams that want searchable meeting libraries, strong integrations and multilingual transcripts in the cloud.

tl;dv records and transcribes Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, emphasizes team collaboration and CRM connections, and publishes free and paid seat plans ([tl;dv pricing](https://tldv.io/app/pricing/), checked September 2026). Its security pages describe EU hosting options and state that customer recordings are not used to train models ([tl;dv features](https://tldv.io/features/), checked September 2026).

### Jamie

**Best for:** regulated or client-facing teams that want EU-hosted processing without a bot, with workflow automation after the call.

Jamie runs on your device, does not add a virtual participant, and documents EU data residency, deletion of audio after transcription and ISO 27001 certification on its homepage ([meetjamie.ai](https://meetjamie.ai/), checked September 2026). Plans are subscription-based ([Jamie pricing](https://meetjamie.ai/pricing), checked September 2026).

## Where Fireflies.ai is still the better choice

Fireflies remains a sensible default when everyone accepts a visible notetaker, you want conversation intelligence across a large call library, and you rely on CRM and workflow integrations out of the box. Teams that already built playbooks around AskFred, topic trackers and team analytics may lose more than they gain by switching for privacy alone.

Fireflies also publishes enterprise admin tooling, custom retention, private storage options and compliance documentation aimed at regulated buyers ([Fireflies security](https://fireflies.ai/security), checked September 2026). If your organization standardized on Fireflies seats and integrations, weigh migration cost against data-location requirements.

## Honest limits to plan for

No tool removes your duty to tell participants you are recording and to handle retention responsibly. Vemoir does not make a recording lawful by itself, and this article is not legal advice.

Local-first tools still need disk space, model downloads and a Mac that meets vendor requirements. Vemoir needs Screen Recording permission for system audio, uses that permission for audio only, and does not support Windows or Intel Macs ([capture details](/#capture)).

Cloud notetakers depend on uptime, plan limits and vendor policy changes. Even vendors that do not train on your data still process it on their servers for transcription and summaries.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the closest Fireflies.ai alternative without a bot?

Granola, Jamie, MacWhisper and Vemoir all advertise capture without a bot joining the call. Only Vemoir and MacWhisper default to on-Mac transcription; Granola and Jamie process audio through their cloud stacks per their security pages.

### Is there a free Fireflies.ai alternative?

Fathom, tl;dv, Otter, Granola and Fireflies itself offer free tiers with different limits. MacWhisper has a free Mac tier. Vemoir sells a one-time Mac app, while [browser tools on vemoir.ch](/tools) transcribe short files locally without upload.

### Can I use a Fireflies.ai alternative offline?

Vemoir and MacWhisper support offline workflows once local models are installed. Cloud notetakers generally need network access during the meeting.

### Does Fireflies always send a bot to my meeting?

Fireflies documents a calendar notetaker that joins live meetings, and paid plans describe recording through a Chrome extension for some Google Meet calls without the bot ([Fireflies pricing](https://fireflies.ai/pricing), September 2026). Check the current product pages before you assume bot-free capture.

### How does pricing compare to Fireflies?

Fireflies charges per seat on paid plans ([pricing](https://fireflies.ai/pricing), September 2026). Vemoir is a one-time purchase. Otter, Fathom, tl;dv and Jamie use subscription seat models. MacWhisper Pro is a one-time license on the vendor site.

### Can I import an old Fireflies recording into another tool?

Most alternatives accept audio or video files within plan limits. Vemoir imports common formats such as MP3, M4A and WAV on the Mac. Check each vendor's import caps.

### Do these tools replace legal or compliance review?

No. Read each vendor's privacy and security documentation, align with your organization's policies, and decide retention and sharing rules yourself.

### Which Fireflies.ai alternative is best for Swiss or EU teams?

Jamie emphasizes EU residency on its site. tl;dv describes EU hosting and GDPR-oriented controls. Local Mac processing with Vemoir or MacWhisper keeps files on the device unless you export or enable cloud AI. Pick based on your threat model, not marketing labels alone.

### Will switching tools delete my Fireflies history?

Fireflies describes data ownership and deletion controls on its security pages. Export what you need before you cancel, and verify deletion steps on Fireflies' site.

### Can Vemoir summarize meetings like Fireflies?

Yes on the Mac. Vemoir generates minutes, decisions, action items and a follow-up draft locally, and you can ask questions against the transcript ([Ask Vemoir](/#chat)).
