**Treuhand meeting notes** are the record of a client conversation: mandate, period, missing documents, decisions taken, and who sends the next file. They do not replace Bexio or Abacus. The ledger stays the ledger. The note is how you remember what the client said on the phone and what you promised to file.

A blank structure for that note is the [minutes sheet](/tools/minutes-sheet). It does not connect to the accounting system and it does not upload a recording.

## What Treuhand meeting notes have to capture

Swiss fiduciaries sit between the client and a pile of deadlines: VAT, salary statements, annual accounts, a board that wants a sentence it can sign. The note fails when it says “discussed the year-end” and never says which year or which entity.

<ul class="blog-scenarios">
  <li><h3>If the client brings figures</h3><p>Capture amounts in the decisions list, not only in free text.</p></li>
  <li><h3>If counsel joins by phone</h3><p>System audio on the Mac still needs consent from everyone.</p></li>
  <li><h3>If the file is bilingual</h3><p>Pick the dominant language for the model, then fix names in the glossary.</p></li>
</ul>


Use a short header every time:

- Client and entity, including the legal form if one group has several.
- Mandate type, in your own words: bookkeeping, payroll, tax, audit support, formation. Not a slogan.
- Period the conversation was about.
- People on the call, on both sides.
- Date.

Then three lists. Documents the client still owes you. Decisions, such as “depreciate the vehicle over five years, client confirmed on this call.” Open questions you will not guess. A decision the client did not actually make should stay in open questions. Speech-to-text is happy to turn a maybe into a yes.

Professional secrecy for fiduciaries is your obligation, not a feature toggle. This article does not say a workflow is compliant with the nLPD or with the rules of your association. It says: do not send the audio to a tool the client did not expect, and do not leave the only copy of a figure in a chat window.

## During the call, and just after

You can type the header into the minutes sheet while the client is still talking. You can also record the call on the Mac and fill the sheet from the transcript afterward. Recording needs consent. Say it. A local recorder does not announce itself inside the phone app or Teams.

If you record, keep the audio on the machine unless you have a reason and a right to put it somewhere else. Browser transcription fits a short excerpt: 15 minutes, 3 successful runs a week, file not uploaded, no speaker labels. A 40-minute year-end review does not fit that cap. Trim to the part where the client confirms a treatment, or use a local app for the full call. For a full client call on your Mac without sending audio to the browser tool, see [capture](/#capture).

Standard German in the transcript is what you should expect even when the client spoke Swiss German. Read numbers out loud against the audio or against the document. Dialect coming back as standard wording is normal for the public Whisper models in the browser tools. It is not a dialect transcript.

After the call, the useful order is:

1. Header, while you still remember who joined late.
2. Documents missing. Name the file the client uses, not “the PDF.”
3. Decisions with the period and the entity.
4. What you will do by which date, and what the client will do.
5. Anything that must not go into an email to the whole company, such as a shareholder dispute that came up beside the accounts.

[Meeting minutes template](/blog/meeting-minutes-template) is a general structure if your firm does not already have a Treuhand memo format. Prefer the firm’s format when it exists. The sheet is a draft surface, not the archive.

If you want the cost of the hour on an internal copy, the [meeting cost calculator](/tools/meeting-cost) multiplies people and hourly rates for a shared duration and prints one sentence in francs. It does not see the audio. It also does not tell you the meeting was unnecessary. Some mandates bill the hour from this kind of sentence. Some do not put money in the client-facing note. Choose deliberately.

## Figures, deadlines, and the words around them

Write figures as digits in the note even if the transcript spelled them. “About forty” is not a posting. If the client was approximate, write that they were approximate.

Deadlines need a calendar date. “End of next week” dies on the following Monday when nobody agrees what next week was. Convert it before you close the note.

Related parties, loans to shareholders, and salary versus dividend are the usual places a loose sentence becomes a wrong filing. Quote the client’s instruction and the document you will follow. If you gave advice, label it as your recommendation versus their decision. Your future self, and a colleague covering your holiday, cannot reconstruct tone from a smoothed paragraph.

Store the note with the mandate, in the system you already use for client files. A transcript in Downloads is not a file plan. Export the finished note and put it where the engagement letter says working papers live. Delete drafts you do not need. Retention for client files is a professional question. Do not take a blog’s number as the retention period.

Language of the note can follow the client. The app interface can run in German, French, Italian, English, or Dutch. The minutes you file should be in the language the client and the file expect, which you can edit by hand if the draft came out in the wrong one.

## Where the ledger is the better system

Postings, VAT codes, and payroll belong in the accounting software. A meeting note that tries to be a journal entry will be wrong in both places. Write the decision in the note. Enter the posting in the ledger. If they disagree a month later, the ledger and the source document win, and the note is how you explain the disagreement.

A client who runs three entities will mention the wrong one in the first five minutes and the right one after you ask. The note should say when that correction happened. Otherwise next quarter’s file follows the first name they said. Write “corrected to Holding AG after the payroll item” in the header area, not only in a parenthesis that nobody sees.

Payroll side questions (a bonus, a leaver, a withholding change) need their own line even when the meeting was booked as “annual accounts.” Those items have different deadlines from the financial statements. Mixing them into one paragraph is how a salary change waits until the accounts are signed.

A client portal your firm already pays for is a better place to request missing documents than a personal transcript app. Use the portal’s task list. Use the note to remember the phone call that created the task.

A shared bot is a poor fit when the client is a small company that did not agree to an extra participant, which is most of a Treuhand book. Local notes or typed minutes match that room better. A bot can still be right for an internal training call where everyone on staff expects it. Do not use the same setting for both.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are Treuhand meeting notes the same as the annual accounts?

No. The note records the conversation. The accounts are the statements you prepare from the books.

### Can I transcribe a client call without uploading it?

A file of 15 minutes or less can run in the browser tools without upload. Longer calls need a local workflow. Speaker names are not added by the browser page.

### Will Swiss German come out as dialect?

No. Expect Standard German text. Check the figures yourself.

### Does the minutes sheet file into Abacus or Bexio?

No. You copy the finished note into the system you use for the mandate.

### Should the note include the fee for the hour?

Only if that is how you work. The cost calculator can draft a franc sentence for internal use. Leave it off the client copy if your engagement letter handles fees separately.

### Is this compliant with fiduciary secrecy?

This article does not claim that. Secrecy is your conduct: who hears the audio, where the file goes, how long you keep it.

### What if the client says “book it somehow”?

Write the open question. Do not convert it into a decision in the minutes. Call back or send the options.

### Can several staff share one laptop’s recording?

Only if your firm allows that archive. A personal Mac is a weak file plan for a mandate another person might have to pick up. Export the note into the client file. Put the engagement name in the filename so a substitute can find it without opening five recordings.
