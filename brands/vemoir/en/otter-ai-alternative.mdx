An Otter.ai alternative is the right search when you want meeting notes and you do not want a bot in the participant list, or you do not want the audio uploaded. Otter's desktop app can record without a bot. The transcript still goes to Otter. If the recording has to stay on the Mac, Otter is the wrong tool, desktop app included.

The rows below come from each vendor's own pages, checked on 26 October 2026. Prices and caps move. Open the linked page before you buy. For a head-to-head on the product side, see [Vemoir vs Otter.ai](/compare/otter-ai).

## Why people leave Otter

People leave for two different reasons, and they need two different answers. One is the bot. The other is the cloud upload.

Otter can join Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet and write the notes from inside the call. The [Otter desktop app](https://otter.ai/desktop) is the other path. On 26 October 2026 that page said you can record without a bot in the meeting, including Slack Huddles, Zoom, Google Meet and an in-person conversation, on Mac or Windows. You press record. No extra name has to appear in the participant list.

That does not keep the audio on your computer. Otter's [MP4-to-text page](https://otter.ai/mp4-to-text) says transcription data uses AWS S3 storage with AES-256 server-side encryption, and it describes Otter as SOC 2 Type 2. The [pricing table](https://otter.ai/pricing) lists AES-256 encryption for real-time content. A quieter participant list and a file that never left the Mac are different tests. The desktop app passes the first and does not claim the second.

List prices on [otter.ai/pricing](https://otter.ai/pricing), read on 26 October 2026:

**Basic** is free. The comparison table lists 300 transcription minutes per user a month, 30 minutes per conversation, 3 audio or video imports for the life of the account, and the 25 most recent conversations. Export is mp3 and txt.

**Pro** is $16.99 per user per month, or $8.33 per user per month on annual billing. The table lists 1,200 minutes per user a month, 90 minutes per conversation, and 10 imports a month. Export adds pdf, docx and srt. The plan cards also show a lower rate marked for an India-issued credit card. That is not the list price.

**Business** is $30 per user per month, or $19.99 per user per month on annual billing. The table lists unlimited meetings, a separate cap of 6,000 minutes per user a month on imported files, and meetings up to 4 hours. The monthly card also shows $24 per user per month as 20 percent off for 3 months. Read the label before you quote a number.

**Enterprise** is custom. The page lists SSO and SCIM with a minimum of 100 users, and HIPAA as an add-on. HIPAA is not part of Basic, Pro or Business.

The same table lists transcription in English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese and Chinese.

Three of the tools above can do the work on the machine. They are not the same product.

### Vemoir

**Best for:** keeping the recording, the transcript and the minutes on the Mac, with no bot in the call.

Vemoir records the microphone and system audio from the menu bar. You start and stop each session. Transcription and summaries run on the Mac with local models. A cloud text model is opt-in, with your own API key, and there is no silent fallback if it fails. The record is minutes, decisions, action items and a follow-up draft. Speaker separation runs on the Mac. You can ask questions about a meeting, and the answers link back to the transcript ([the record](/#record), [Ask Vemoir](/#chat)).

It works with any app that plays audio on the Mac, including Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Slack huddles and Discord ([platforms](/#platforms)). macOS asks for Microphone and System Audio Recording. That permission is audio only. Vemoir does not record the screen.

Price, as of October 2026: a one-time purchase in the Mac App Store, with no subscription and no charge per meeting. CHF 20 in Switzerland, €24.99 in Germany, Austria, France and Italy, $22.99 in the US ([pricing](/#pricing)). Every feature is in that purchase. Requirements: Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS Sonoma 14.2 or later, at least 8 GB of memory and 10 GB of free storage. 16 GB or more is the recommendation for long meetings. Intel Macs are not supported, and neither are Windows, iPhone or iPad.

Import of a file you already have: MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF, CAF and FLAC. Notes you type during the meeting fold into the minutes. The interface is English, German, French, Italian and Dutch. Parakeet v3 transcribes 25 European languages. Other models differ. There is no Swiss German dialect output to plan on. You share the notes you choose, including an Apple Notes folder. The app does not export PDF or DOCX.

**Skip Vemoir** if you work on Windows or an Intel Mac, if someone who is not in the room must record the call with a bot, or if the team needs one shared cloud library with CRM fields.

If you already have a file and you only need text, the free [transcribe recording](/tools/transcribe-recording) tool runs in the browser tab. The file is not uploaded. The cap is 15 minutes and three successful runs a week. It does not record a live meeting and it does not name speakers. For the next live call, the app records from the menu bar ([how capture works](/#capture)).

### MacWhisper

**Best for:** a Mac user who wants local transcription of files and meetings, and will add an AI key only if they want summaries.

[MacWhisper](https://www.macwhisper.com/) transcribes with local models, records online meetings without a bot, and lists Zoom, Teams, Webex, Skype, Chime, Discord and other calls. The site says offline processing and over 100 languages. That language count is their claim. Pro is listed at €64 once, with lifetime updates, on the direct download. The free tier is €0. Summaries can use a cloud model you connect or a local model through Ollama or LM Studio.

**Skip MacWhisper** if you need Windows, or a shared team library with CRM fields. It is a transcription app on one Mac.

### Meetily

**Best for:** someone who wants the MIT-licensed Community Edition, a Windows installer, or a Linux build from source, and will run the app on their own machine.

The [Meetily README](https://github.com/Zackriya-Solutions/meetily/blob/main/README.md) says the app runs on the local machine, works offline, and supports macOS, Windows and Linux. Linux is a build from source. The macOS download named on that page is an Apple silicon disk image (`meetily_0.4.1_aarch64.dmg`). The [pricing page](https://meetily.ai/pricing) lists Community Edition as free. Pro is billed yearly at $120 per user. The same card also shows $10 and $25 per user per month, so read the toggle before you budget. Enterprise is custom. There is a 14-day Pro trial. The pricing page says transcription runs locally and that recordings do not leave the device unless you choose another provider. Pro lists cloud transcription providers, speaker diarization, and PDF, DOCX and Markdown export.

**Skip Meetily** if you need an Intel Mac. Vemoir does not run on Intel, and the macOS disk image on the README is Apple silicon. Skip it if you need a managed team workspace with CRM fields rather than an app you run yourself.

## Best for teams

A team library is a different job from a private recording on one Mac. Stay with a cloud notetaker when the point is search across everyone's calls, admin controls, and a CRM field.

**Otter Business** fits when the company has already accepted Otter's cloud, wants the notetaker or the desktop app, and needs admin tools. HIPAA, SSO and SCIM sit on Enterprise, not on Business. The minimum for SSO and SCIM on the pricing page is 100 users.

**Fireflies** fits a team that wants a cloud notetaker, search, and admin controls, and has accepted that the audio lives in Fireflies. The [Fireflies alternative](/blog/fireflies-alternative) guide is the longer comparison. The pricing page lists SOC 2 Type II, GDPR and HIPAA among its security rows, and says customer data is not used for AI training. None of that puts the audio on your disk.

**Fathom** fits an individual or a small sales team that wants a free plan with a lot of recording, clips, and the option of a bot. Bot-free capture is marked beta, and only for Mac. Team and Business have a two-user minimum. Business adds CRM field sync. A bot-free Mac recording still ends in your Fathom account. There is no offline mode on the pricing page.

**Jamie Team** fits a group that wants no bot, processing in the EEA, Switzerland and the UK, and will pay a subscription. On the yearly view the Team row was $37 per seat per month, billed annually. The homepage does not describe an offline mode. "Runs on your device" and "audio is never stored" still mean Jamie processed the audio. It is not a file that never left the laptop.

**Granola Business** fits people who already take notes and want them rewritten from the computer's audio, without a participant joining. [Granola's pricing page](https://www.granola.ai/pricing) lists Basic at $0, Business at $14, and Enterprise at $35 per user per month. It did not show a second annual figure on the plan cards. Basic meeting history is 30 days. Business and Enterprise are unlimited. Bot-free device-audio capture is listed on every plan. The [security page](https://www.granola.ai/security) says you start Granola yourself, it does not add a bot, and it does not keep the audio. Granola says those providers are not allowed to train on your data. Granola itself trains on anonymized data unless you opt out. Enterprise has that off by default. Notes sit in US-hosted AWS.

**tl;dv** fits a team that wants a cloud library and EU hosting, and does not need the file on the laptop. The homepage says a bot is not required. "No bot" does not mean the audio stayed on your machine.

**Skip all of these** if a fiduciary, a journalist or a recruiter cannot put that client's voice on a vendor server. Use a local Mac app instead. Vemoir does not add a shared team workspace. If several people must co-edit one living document with comments, a cloud library is the better fit, and you should say so before you switch.

No tool on this page makes a recording lawful. You tell people, you keep what you can justify, and you delete the rest. This is not legal advice.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the closest Otter.ai alternative without a bot?

Otter's desktop app records without a bot, and the transcript still goes to Otter. Granola, Jamie, MacWhisper, Meetily and Vemoir also skip the participant list. Vemoir, MacWhisper and Meetily keep transcription on the computer by default. Granola and Jamie do not.

### Does the Otter desktop app keep audio on my computer?

No. It avoids the bot. Otter's MP4-to-text page still describes AWS S3 storage, and the pricing table lists AES-256 for real-time content. If the file must not leave the Mac, the desktop app fails that test.

### Is there a free Otter.ai alternative?

Otter Basic, Fathom's free plan, Fireflies' free tier, Granola Basic ($0, 30 days of history), MacWhisper's free tier and Meetily Community all exist, with the limits above. tl;dv's homepage describes a free plan with unlimited recordings. Vemoir's live capture is a one-time purchase. The [browser tool](/tools/transcribe-recording) is free for a file you already have: 15 minutes, three successful runs a week.

### Can I use an Otter.ai alternative offline on a Mac?

Vemoir, MacWhisper and Meetily can, once local models are installed. The first download still needs a network. Otter, Granola, Fathom, Fireflies and tl;dv do not describe an offline mode. Jamie's homepage does not either.

### Which Otter.ai alternative is the best fit on a Mac?

If the audio must stay on that Mac, use Vemoir, MacWhisper or Meetily, and check the chip. Vemoir and the Meetily macOS disk image named in the README are Apple silicon. MacWhisper is the one to open first when the job is a file you already have and you do not need minutes written for you. Vemoir is the one to open when you want minutes, decisions and a follow-up from a live call, as a one-time purchase.

### Which option fits a team?

Otter Business, Fireflies, Fathom Team or Business, Jamie Team, Granola Business and tl;dv are built as shared cloud libraries. Pick from where the audio is allowed to go, not from the feature grid alone. A single Mac app is the wrong buy when the requirement is one workspace for the whole company.

### What can I export?

Otter Basic exports mp3 and txt. Pro and above add pdf, docx and srt. MacWhisper's free tier exports srt, vtt and txt. Pro adds md, pdf, html and docx on the direct download. Meetily Community is copy. Meetily Pro lists PDF, DOCX and Markdown. Vemoir shares the notes you choose, including Apple Notes, and does not export PDF or DOCX. Comments and shared channels in Otter stay there until you copy them. Export the audio before you cancel. Vemoir can import MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF, CAF or FLAC.

### What does Otter cost?

Basic is free, with the minute and import limits on the pricing page. Pro is $16.99 per user per month, or $8.33 on annual billing. Business is $30, or $19.99 on annual billing. Enterprise is custom. The page also shows shorter promotional rates. Use the unlabeled list price unless the offer applies to you. Figures are from [otter.ai/pricing](https://otter.ai/pricing) on 26 October 2026.

### Does Otter transcribe German?

The pricing table lists German, along with English, Spanish, French, Japanese and Chinese. That is transcription inside Otter, not a promise that every screen is in German. Read the row on the pricing page the week you decide.
