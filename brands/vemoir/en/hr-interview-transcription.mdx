**HR interview transcription** is a text copy of a conversation you were allowed to record, or that the candidate already knows you hold. It is not a hiring score or a ranking. You still say the consent line at the start. Your ATS stays the system of record. The transcript is a draft you read before anyone treats a sentence as fact.

For a file you have, use [transcribe an interview](/tools/transcribe-interview). The file is not uploaded. The cap is **15 minutes** and **3 successful runs a week**. Speakers are not labeled.

## Say what you are recording, then record

Tell the candidate the interview will be recorded or transcribed, why, who can see the text, and how long you keep it. Do this before the substantive questions, not in a follow-up email after you already have the audio. If they refuse, continue without a recording and take notes. A tool does not override a refusal.

<ul class="blog-checklist">
  <li>State that you record at the start of the interview.</li>
  <li>Store the file where HR policy allows.</li>
  <li>Redact third-party names before you share excerpts.</li>
</ul>


In Switzerland, the law people mean when they say “we need a basis and a purpose” is the revised Data Protection Act. This article is not that basis and it is not legal advice. Works councils, US state consent rules, and a group policy can all be stricter than a product page. Follow the stricter one.

A bot that joins the video call announces itself by sitting in the participant list. Some candidates find that clearer. Some candidates and some roles should not have an outside vendor in the room at all. [Transcribe an interview without upload](/blog/transcribe-interview-without-upload) covers the same idea after the fact, with a file you already hold. [Meeting notes without a bot](/blog/meeting-notes-without-a-bot) is the live-call version.

Do not transcribe a conversation the candidate thought was a private aside. If you stayed on the recording during small talk about health, family, or anything you should not factor into hiring, do not paste that stretch into the scorecard. Deleting a paragraph from the notes is not the same as deleting the audio. Decide about the audio under your retention rule.

## HR interview transcription for a file you hold

1. Export or save the interview audio as MP3, M4A, WAV, or MP4.
2. If the interview is longer than 15 minutes, trim to the section you need to check, or use a local transcription path that is not the weekly browser cap. Do not treat three 15-minute chunks as one free workflow. Three successful runs is the whole week.
3. Open [transcribe an interview](/tools/transcribe-interview). The model downloads in the browser. Later runs can be offline. The audio file is not sent to Vemoir. The Network panel is there if you want to verify that.
4. Read the text. There are no speaker labels. You will see one stream. Mark the interviewer and the candidate yourself if the quote matters. Guessing wrong in a hiring note is worse than leaving a quote out.
5. Correct names, product names, and any figure. Public Whisper models write Swiss German toward Standard German. A candidate who spoke dialect did not suddenly speak textbook Standard German. Do not treat the wording as a style sample unless you have checked the audio.

A panel interview with four people is a bad fit for an unlabeled transcript. Either take structured notes live, or use a local tool that separates speakers and then still have a human assign the names. Unknown speakers should stay unknown. Do not “helpfully” attach a name the audio did not establish.

The Mac app can record the call locally from system audio and the microphone, then separate speakers on the machine. That is the live path when the interview runs on the Mac. This page's tool is the after-the-file path. For the next interview on your Mac without a bot on the roster, see [capture](/#capture).

## What to extract, and what to leave out

Keep: examples of work the candidate described, questions you still need to ask a reference, and practical constraints they stated (notice period, location, permit) that they expect you to use.

Leave out of the shared note: health, pregnancy, religion, union activity, and anything else your process says is out of bounds, even if it was said. A complete transcript in a shared drive is a complete copy of the out-of-bounds material. Share a short evaluation. Restrict the raw audio.

Write the evaluation in sentences a second interviewer can check against the text. “Great culture fit” is not a sentence they can check. “Described migrating the payroll calendar in Q3 and named the two systems” is.

Store the note on the candidate in the ATS, with the access your HR policy already defines. A transcript in a personal Downloads folder is invisible to the hiring manager and still subject to a retention clock. Put it where the policy says, or do not keep it.

If you need minutes-style actions (“send the case study by Friday”), [meeting notes from a transcript](/tools/meeting-notes-from-transcript) can draft that list. It is not an ATS.

A panel that interviewed the same candidate on different days should not merge two transcripts into one voice. Label the date on every quote you keep. A sentence from the hiring manager’s screen is not a sentence from the case-study interview. Mix them and the scorecard argues with itself.

Recordings of rejected candidates are the ones retention rules are for. Do not leave them in Downloads because nobody hired the person and nobody felt responsible for the file. The policy either keeps them for the stated period or deletes them. “We might reopen the role” is not a retention schedule.

## Where the ATS is the better system

Greenhouse, Personio, or the spreadsheet your company actually uses is the system of record for stage, scorecard, and who may view the candidate. Transcription does not replace it. Paste a checked summary into the scorecard. Do not ask the transcript to compute a hire recommendation. There is no model score in this workflow and there should not be one you invent.

Live coding tests, work samples, and a structured scorecard beat a verbatim transcript when the role is senior and the risk is a charming monologue. Use the transcript to quote accurately. Use the scorecard to judge.

A vendor that stores every interview in a shared recruiting workspace can be the right buy when many recruiters must search old loops and your DPA covers it. It is the wrong buy when the candidate was told the recording stays inside the company and the vendor was never named. Read what you promised in the invitation.

Panel debriefs belong in the ATS the same day, while the interview is still distinct from the one you ran at 4 p.m. A transcript does not sort itself into the right candidate. Paste the checked lines under the right requisition, then close the audio file so it is not the thing still open on a shared screen.

A scorecard without a date is hard to defend later. Date it when you paste, on the scorecard itself, not only in the calendar invite.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does HR interview transcription upload the audio?

Not on the vemoir.ch interview tool. The file stays in the browser, up to 15 minutes, three successful runs a week.

### Will it show who asked the question?

No. The browser tools do not label speakers. Add the names yourself if you quote.

### What if the interview is 45 minutes?

Trim a section, or transcribe locally outside the browser cap. Three slices would use the whole weekly allowance.

### Can I record without telling the candidate?

This guide will not help you do that. Tell them first. If they say no, take notes.

### Is the transcript a hiring score?

No. It is text. The evaluation is a person’s job, filed in the ATS.

### Does Swiss German stay in dialect?

Expect Standard German from the public Whisper model. Check idioms against the audio before you quote them.

### Where do I put the file afterward?

In the candidate record your policy names, for as long as that policy allows. Then delete the copies, including downloads. If two recruiters saved the same file, the deletion has to cover both laptops, not only the shared drive.

### Is this GDPR-certified?

No. Do not claim that. The data protection duties sit with you: purpose, access, and retention. Write the retention date on the candidate file when you save the transcript, so a later cleanup does not depend on someone remembering the interview.
