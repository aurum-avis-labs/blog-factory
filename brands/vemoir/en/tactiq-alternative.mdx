A **Tactiq alternative** makes sense when you want meeting notes and you do not want to depend on the live captions of Google Meet, Zoom, or Microsoft Teams. Tactiq is a Chrome extension for those three. Its own FAQ says it does not record or store the audio: the transcript happens in real time and is saved as text. That is a real product, and it is not the same job as capturing microphone and system audio on a Mac.

This comparison stays with what Tactiq publishes, checked September 2026, and with what a local Mac app can and cannot do. It does not treat a browser extension as live-capture parity.

## What Tactiq documents

[Tactiq](https://tactiq.io/) describes an AI note taker for Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams, delivered as a Chrome extension. The homepage says the transcript is shown to you, that you can notify others you are transcribing, and that the extension does not record or store meeting audio. The transcript is real-time text.


<div class="blog-pair">
  <div><h3>Who this is for</h3><ul><li>People in Chrome on Meet, Zoom, or Teams who want live captions saved as text without storing audio</li></ul></div>
  <div><h3>Who should skip it</h3><ul><li>Calls where you need replayable audio or apps outside that set</li></ul></div>
</div>


Their speech-to-text page says the same thing in shorter form: install the extension, turn on transcription in the meeting, save transcriptions from Meet, Zoom, and Teams ([Tactiq speech to text](https://tactiq.io/speech-to-text), checked September 2026). The product does not join the call as a participant. Nobody sees a "Tactiq bot" in the list unless you choose to tell people.

The homepage also states ISO 27001, a SOC 2 Type II attestation, and that the product is GDPR and HIPAA compliant. Those are Tactiq's words. A badge on a marketing page is not a decision about your call. Consent is still yours. This article does not grade those claims, and it does not say any notetaker is appropriate for a privileged or regulated conversation.

On AI, Tactiq's pricing FAQ says they use OpenAI's enterprise API, not ChatGPT, and points at OpenAI's API data policy for the statement that API data is not used to train OpenAI's models ([Tactiq pricing](https://tactiq.io/buy), checked September 2026). Read that policy yourself if the training question is why you are here. Policies change.

## Captions are not a recording

The extension reads the meeting's live text. That has consequences a recording app does not share.


<aside class="blog-callout blog-callout--limit">
  <span class="blog-kicker">Limit</span>
  <p>Verify Tactiq pricing and data handling on the vendor site before you buy. Figures in this article are dated in the text.</p>
</aside>


**The platform has to be producing captions.** If captions are off, wrong, or unavailable in that tenant, Tactiq has nothing reliable to save. A system-audio recorder does not ask the meeting app for a caption track. It takes the sound leaving the Mac.

**Three meeting apps, in Chrome.** Meet, Zoom, and Teams are the set Tactiq's pages name. A Webex call, a Slack huddle, a FaceTime, a Loom you already downloaded, or two people in a room are outside that set. An extension is not a microphone.

**No tape afterward.** If the text is wrong, you cannot relisten inside Tactiq, because Tactiq says it does not store the audio. A local recording lets you open the passage, fix a name, and keep the line as evidence. You trade that away when the design is "text only."

**Speaker labels are the platform's labels, when they exist.** Tactiq is not separating voices from a two-track audio file. If Meet's captions attribute a line, you see that attribution. If they collapse a room into one stream, you do not get a diarization pass on raw audio.

**The extension has browser permissions.** Installing it is a Chrome (or compatible browser) decision, including whatever host access the extension requests. That is a different risk from a Mac app that asks for the microphone and for System Audio Recording. Neither risk is zero. They are not the same risk.

This is why a Tactiq alternative that records audio is not a drop-in clone. It keeps a tape. It also stores something Tactiq deliberately does not.

## What Tactiq charges

Checked September 2026 on [tactiq.io/buy](https://tactiq.io/buy):

- **Free:** $0. The page describes a free Chrome extension and, in the credit explanation, 5 AI credits a month on Free. Marketing lines around the product refer to a first batch of transcripts without a card (the speech-to-text page says the first 10 meeting transcriptions). Confirm the current free cap on the pricing page. It is a trial shape, not an unlimited archive.
- **Pro:** $8 USD per user a month, annual billing. Unlimited transcripts, 10 AI credits a month, the ability to disable the transcript notification and to pause automatic transcription, on top of Free.
- **A team tier:** $16.67 USD per user a month, annual, described for 1 to 20 users.
- **A higher tier:** $29.17 USD per user a month, annual, with unlimited AI credits.

One AI credit, on that page, covers one AI action: a summary, action items, or a question to Tactiq AI. Transcripts and AI credits are different meters. You can have the text and still be out of credits for the summary.

Cancel from billing. The page says upgrades are available and that downgrades and cancellations take effect on the renewal boundary.

## How to choose a Tactiq alternative

1. **Are you always in Chrome, on Meet, Zoom, or Teams, with captions available?** Tactiq is built for that rectangle. If the call leaves it, look elsewhere.
2. **Do you need the audio later?** If a wrong line must be checkable against sound, text-only is the wrong default.
3. **Do you need the note shared in a Tactiq workspace, or on your Mac?** The extension is a team product with per-user pricing. A one-time Mac app is a single machine.

A file you already exported is a third path. The free [transcribe recording](/tools/transcribe-recording) tool runs in the browser tab, does not upload the file, and allows 3 runs a week, up to 15 minutes, MP3, M4A, WAV, or MP4. It does not read captions. It does not label speakers. It does not sit in the live meeting.

## Tactiq alternatives by the job

### Vemoir, when you want the tape on the Mac

Vemoir records microphone and system audio from the menu bar. You start and stop. No extension, no bot. The macOS permission is System Audio Recording. The capture is audio-only. It does not record the screen, and it does not ask Screen Recording.

Transcription and speaker separation run on the Mac after you stop. The live view during the call is a preview. The transcript that counts is the one from the finished audio. Summaries, decisions, actions, documents, and Ask Vemoir stay on the Mac unless you export them or you explicitly turn on your own OpenAI or Anthropic key for text. That key does not receive audio. If the provider fails, the request stops.

You can rename speakers without saving a voice profile. Remembering a speaker is opt-in, encrypted, and deletable. Unknown speakers stay anonymous. Notes you type during the call fold into the minutes and keep their own mark. They are not shown as verbatim lines.

App Store price, from [pricing](/#pricing): CHF 20 in Switzerland, €24.99 in Germany, Austria, France, and Italy, $22.99 in the US. One-time, no per-meeting fee, no seats. Apple silicon, macOS Sonoma 14.2 or later, 8 GB memory, 10 GB free. 16 GB is the recommendation for long meetings. No Intel Mac, no Windows, no iPhone. Store link: [Vemoir](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794693630).

Interface languages: English, German, French, Italian, Dutch. Parakeet v3 transcribes 25 European languages. That is not a Swiss German dialect mode.

### Stay on captions if the tape is the problem

If the reason you picked Tactiq is that you do not want an audio file anywhere, replacing it with a recorder moves you backward. Keep the extension. Tell people you are transcribing. Use the notification controls on the paid plan if the duplicated consent line is the annoyance, not the existence of a transcript.

### Otter, Granola, and a bot, when captions are not the point

Otter and similar cloud notetakers record meetings and store transcripts in their cloud. Some add a visible bot. Granola avoids a participant and still keeps notes off your disk. Those are different from Tactiq (they have audio or a cloud note store) and different from a local Mac app. Read [Otter.ai alternative](/blog/otter-ai-alternative) and [Granola alternative](/blog/granola-alternative) if that is the comparison you actually need. A longer note on Otter's own privacy pages is [Is Otter.ai safe?](/blog/is-otter-ai-safe).

### A short file, after the call

Tactiq's published path is the live meeting in the browser. If the meeting already ended and you have a file, the extension is the wrong tool. Use the browser transcriber for a short file, or import MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF, CAF, or FLAC into the Mac app for a longer one.

## Where Tactiq is still the better choice

Tactiq wins in places a Mac recorder does not.

**You refuse an audio file.** Their FAQ is unusually clear: no recording, no stored audio, text in real time. If that sentence is your policy, do not "upgrade" to an app that keeps a tape until you delete it. The honest alternative is another text path, or notes you type yourself. The [minutes sheet](/tools/minutes-sheet) is a blank form with no model and no upload.

**You live in Chrome on three platforms.** Meet, Zoom, and Teams, captions on, extension allowed by the company. You get a transcript without a menu-bar app, without an Apple-silicon requirement, and without a one-time Mac purchase. A Windows laptop in the same Chrome profile is in scope for Tactiq and out of scope for Vemoir.

**You want per-user admin on a small team.** The pricing page is built around seats, AI credits, and the option to disable the in-meeting transcript notice. A local app has no org console. Sharing is an export you choose: copy, Markdown, or Apple Notes.

**AI credits match how you work.** If you only need the caption text and you summarize by hand, the free transcript allowance may be enough. Paying for a full recorder to get a paragraph you will rewrite is a bad trade.

**You need the transcript during the call, as text, without waiting for a file to finish.** Tactiq's pitch is real-time text. A local app's authoritative transcript is built after you stop. The during-call view is a preview, not the record. If you must paste a quote into chat before you hang up, the extension fits that minute better.

## A week of mixed calls

Write down the actual week before you switch tools. Many people have four Meet calls, one Teams call, and one conversation that is just a phone on speaker in the room.

The four browser calls can stay on captions if the policy is text-only and the captions in that tenant are good enough to send. The Teams call is still inside Tactiq's named set. The phone on the table is not. That one needs a microphone recording, or handwritten notes. Forcing the extension to cover it produces an empty transcript and a false sense that the meeting was captured.

The other failure in the same week is a file. Someone forwards a recording after the fact and asks for the same notes. The extension was never in that meeting. Drop the file on a transcriber, or import it on the Mac. Do not reopen the calendar event and hope captions still exist.

If only one call in the week needs an audio archive, you do not have to move the other four. Use the extension where captions are the record you want. Use a local recorder where you may need to replay a sentence. Write that split in the invite, in one line, so the other person hears it before either tool starts.

## Limits to plan for

Vemoir will not read Meet's caption track, and Tactiq will not become a local two-track recorder. Mixing the mental models causes the worst rollouts: people install the extension, assume they have audio, and discover a wrong line with nothing to replay.

Browser tools on vemoir.ch are also not Tactiq. They transcribe a file. They do not join a tab's meeting. They do not label speakers. Swiss German tends to come out as Standard German. The Mac app does not add a dialect claim on top of that.

There is no PDF or DOCX export from Vemoir. There is no bot, no Windows build, and no phone app. Offline work waits until the speech model and the language model you chose have finished downloading. Internet is for setup and those downloads.

Do not treat SOC 2, GDPR, or HIPAA lines on any vendor homepage as permission. Tactiq states those on its homepage. Your obligation to tell people, and your retention rules, are not transferred to the vendor by the statement.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a Tactiq alternative a better caption tool?

Only if you still want captions. If you want audio, you are leaving Tactiq's design. Say that to the team before you switch, or people will assume the tape exists when it does not, or the reverse.

### Does Tactiq store the recording?

Tactiq's homepage FAQ says no. It does not record or store the audio. The transcript is real-time text. If a vendor later changes that, the page is the source, not this article.

### Does the extension work in Webex or a Slack huddle?

Tactiq's pages name Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams. Do not plan on it for other apps. A Mac recorder follows the audio device, not the meeting brand.

### Can I get speaker names without captions?

Not from Tactiq, if the captions do not provide them. A local diarization pass needs the audio. It still will not invent a name for someone it has never been asked to remember.

### What does the free plan include?

Checked September 2026: a free extension, a small AI credit allowance (the pricing page explains 5 credits a month on Free), and a limited number of transcripts (the speech-to-text page says the first 10). Pro at $8 per user per month annually lists unlimited transcripts and 10 AI credits. Recheck before you rely on the cap.

### Is the Mac app a Chrome extension?

No. It is a native Mac app. It does not read caption DOM from a tab. It records sound.

### What if I only have a 10-minute file?

Skip both live tools. Use [transcribe recording](/tools/transcribe-recording). Three runs a week, 15 minutes, no upload.

### Should I run Tactiq and a local recorder together?

Only with a clear consent line for both, and a reason. You will get two transcripts that drift. Most people should pick the one that matches the policy: text without audio, or audio on the Mac.

If captions are enough, stay with Tactiq and read their pricing page before the renewal. If you need the sound on the Mac, the limits of a short file are on [transcribe recording](/tools/transcribe-recording), and live capture is described under [capture](/#capture).
