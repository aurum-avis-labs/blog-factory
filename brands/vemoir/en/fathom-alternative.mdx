You search for a **Fathom alternative** when the summaries help, yet a notetaker in the participant list is a dealbreaker, or when the recording should stay on your Mac. Fathom covers Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. It also documents bot-free capture on Mac for some English meetings. The tools below give up part of that convenience in exchange for local processing, a different price model, or a narrower job.

## What Fathom does well

[Fathom](https://fathom.video/) is built for people who want a transcript and a summary without writing during the call. It covers Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. The product story on its own pages is a generous free recording tier, highlight clips, and paid plans that add team libraries and CRM field sync. Treat any dollar figure as stale until you read [Fathom pricing](https://fathom.video/pricing).


Capture is no longer “bot only.” Fathom’s help article on the 3.0 desktop experience describes four modes on Mac, for English-language meetings, after you upgrade inside the desktop app ([How to upgrade to the new Fathom experience](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/11577345), checked September 2026):

- Capture off.
- Transcript only, bot-free: no recording saved, transcript and summary only.
- Audio only, bot-free: audio captured locally on the device, no video.
- Audio and video with a bot: a bot joins and records full audio and video.

The same article says the 3.0 upgrade path it describes is for Mac and English meetings, and that Windows users should not expect that banner yet. It also describes a recording notice you can turn on so Fathom posts in the meeting chat, and a consent confirmation before you view a summary.

That split matters. If your clients already accept a visible notetaker, the bot path is automatic and shared. If they do not, the documented Mac bot-free modes are the feature you are actually buying, and they are narrower than “any meeting, any language, any computer.”

Fathom’s Chrome listing for Google Meet also advertises bot-free capture, unlimited recording and transcription on a free plan, and compliance language (SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, HIPAA) in that store text. Those are Fathom’s claims about Fathom, not a certificate you can copy onto another product. Read the current listing and the trust pages if procurement asks.

Start with the constraint that actually blocks you, not with a feature list.

Some clients and Teams admins treat a notetaker as an uninvited guest. A cloud library is the product when six people must search last month’s calls. A local Mac app is the product when the file should not leave the machine unless you export it or turn on a cloud text model yourself.

If the meeting is already finished and you only have a file, you do not need a live notetaker at all. Vemoir’s free [transcribe a recording](/tools/transcribe-recording) tool runs in the browser tab after the call. The file is not uploaded. Limits are real: up to 15 minutes, 3 successful runs a week, MP3, M4A, WAV, or MP4, no speaker labels. Live capture, speaker separation, and longer meetings are in the Mac app.

Want the next call on the Mac without a participant named Fathom? Vemoir records from the menu bar.

## Seven Fathom alternatives

### Vemoir (Mac app)

**Best for:** keeping meeting audio, the transcript, and the generated record on your Mac by default, with no bot in the call.

Vemoir is a native Mac app from Aurum Avis Labs GmbH in Oberägeri, Switzerland. You start and stop each recording from the menu bar. It captures the microphone and system audio as separate local tracks, so it follows whatever meeting app is playing sound: Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, Slack huddles, Discord, and others. Nobody sees an extra participant. You tell people you are recording. The app does not announce itself inside the meeting.

Transcription and speaker separation run on the Mac. Summaries, action items, decisions, Ask Vemoir, and documents run on the Mac by default. You can route text work to OpenAI or Anthropic with your own API key. That choice is explicit. There is no silent fallback to a cloud model. Audio stays on the device even when a text model is external. Once the models you chose are installed, the meeting workflow can run offline. Internet is for setup and model downloads.

The app turns a call into minutes, decisions, action items, and a follow-up draft. Notes you type during the call fold into that record. You share only the parts you choose, through macOS sharing ([how the record works](/#record)).

Price, as listed for the Mac App Store: CHF 20 in Switzerland, €24.99 in Germany, Austria, France, and Italy, $22.99 in the US. One payment, no per-meeting fee, no seat count ([pricing](/#pricing)). Requires Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS Sonoma 14.2 or later, at least 8 GB of memory, and 10 GB of free storage. 16 GB is recommended for long meetings. Intel Macs are not supported. macOS asks for Microphone and System Audio Recording. Vemoir uses those permissions for audio only. It does not record the screen.

### Otter.ai

**Best for:** a calendar-connected notetaker with mobile apps and shared workspaces, when a visible bot is acceptable.

Otter joins Zoom, Teams, and Meet, transcribes, and summarizes. Paid plans add longer meetings and team features ([Otter pricing](https://otter.ai/pricing), checked September 2026). Otter documents how the notetaker appears and how admins configure recording ([Otter help: recording permissions](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/39339238308503-Recording-Permissions-with-Otter)). If your team already lives in Otter channels, replacing that archive with a Mac app is a migration, not a toggle.

### Fireflies.ai

**Best for:** a team conversation library with CRM-oriented workflows and admin controls.

Fireflies joins listed meeting platforms, stores recordings and transcripts in its cloud workspace, and sells per-seat plans ([Fireflies pricing](https://fireflies.ai/pricing), checked September 2026). Its privacy policy states that meeting content is not used to train its AI models and that vendors are contractually barred from training on that content ([Fireflies privacy policy](https://fireflies.ai/privacy-policy), checked September 2026). The terms still allow Fireflies to derive usage and statistical data from user content for internal purposes. Read both pages if “no training” is the sentence procurement will quote.

### Granola

**Best for:** a lightweight notepad that listens on your computer without adding a participant, while accepting cloud notes.

Granola captures microphone and system audio on the desktop, does not store meeting audio long term, and keeps transcripts and notes in encrypted cloud infrastructure ([Granola security](https://www.granola.ai/security), checked September 2026). Its privacy FAQ says anonymized data may be used for Granola’s own model improvements on Free and Business plans, with an opt-out, and that third parties such as OpenAI and Anthropic are not allowed to train on your data ([Granola privacy FAQ](https://docs.granola.ai/help-center/consent-security-privacy/security-privacy-data-faqs)). That is a different promise from “the audio never left this Mac.”

### MacWhisper

**Best for:** dropping a file in from Finder and transcribing it locally, with optional meeting recording.

MacWhisper transcribes with local models by default. Drag a file onto the app and the audio stays on the Mac unless you turn on a cloud transcription provider ([Keeping transcriptions private](https://docs.macwhisper.com/article/52-keeping-transcriptions-private), updated July 27, 2025). The site lists MacWhisper Pro as a one-time license at €64 with lifetime updates on that page (checked September 2026). Meeting recording exists, including a beta automatic-detection flow that the docs say needs Screen Recording and Microphone, and that is not available in the Mac App Store build ([Record meetings](https://docs.macwhisper.com/article/30-record-meetings)). If your job is “this M4A is already on disk,” MacWhisper is the stronger fit. See the honest section below.

### tl;dv

**Best for:** a searchable team library of Zoom, Meet, and Teams calls, with EU hosting options described by the vendor.

tl;dv’s security commitment says no customer data is used to train its AI, that you can choose Europe or the US for AI hosting, and that its data centers are in Europe ([tl;dv security commitment](https://tldv.io/features/security-commitment/), checked September 2026). Capture for the main platforms is a cloud meeting product, typically with a bot in the call. Confirm the current plan names on [tl;dv pricing](https://tldv.io/app/pricing/).

### Jamie

**Best for:** bot-free capture with processing that Jamie says stays in Europe, on a subscription.

Jamie’s own materials describe a desktop app that does not add a participant, EU storage and processing, and deletion of audio after transcription ([Jamie on data handling](https://www.meetjamie.ai/blog/llm-info), checked September 2026). It is not a one-time Mac purchase, and the notes live in Jamie’s cloud, not only on your disk. Plan limits are on [Jamie pricing](https://meetjamie.ai/pricing).

If you only need text from a short file you already exported, use the [browser transcribe tool](/tools/transcribe-recording). It does not replace Fathom’s live desktop capture.

## Where Fathom is still the better choice

Fathom is the better default when the team agrees to a shared cloud library, sales or success wants clips and CRM updates, and someone will keep the admin settings current. A one-time Mac app does not give you a company-wide search box across every colleague’s calls, and it does not push fields into Salesforce for you.

Fathom is also easier when people jump between Mac and Windows, or when a large share of calls are in a language that the documented 3.0 bot-free upgrade does not cover yet. In that case the bot is the path Fathom actually supports broadly, and the free recording tier is the reason many teams try it before they pay.

If you want mobile capture of a conversation that never happens on a computer, Fathom’s mobile story (and hardware recorders, and phone apps) beat a Mac menu bar. Vemoir does not run on iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows, or Intel Macs.

## Honest limits to plan for

Local processing has a cost you can see. Models take disk space. The first run needs a download. A meeting longer than your patience still has to finish on that Mac. Vemoir does not invent a shared team workspace. If two people must edit the same live notes, you will export a document and use whatever sharing tool you already have ([exports](/#exports)).

Do not expect the browser tool to behave like Fathom. It will not join a call, it will not label speakers, and it will not take a file longer than 15 minutes. Swiss German speech in the public Whisper models used by the browser tools usually comes out as Standard German. The Mac app does not claim Swiss German dialect transcription.

None of these products make a recording lawful. You still tell participants, and you still decide how long you keep the file. Vemoir does not claim to be compliant, HIPAA-ready, or GDPR-certified.

Fathom’s own limits belong in the same list. The bot-free modes in the help article are tied to Mac and English meetings on the desktop app version they describe. A transcript-only mode that does not save audio is a real product choice, and it is a poor fit if you need to replay the conversation. Pricing and summary limits on the free plan should be read on Fathom’s site the week you roll it out, not from a blog table.

## A practical way to decide

Pick one real meeting from last week and write down three facts: who would object to a bot, whether anyone else must search the transcript next month, and whether the audio may leave your laptop.

If someone else must search it, and the call is in English on a Mac, try Fathom’s documented bot-free mode before you buy a different app. If the objection is “no extra name in the roster, and the file stays here,” try Vemoir on the next call you host ([how it works](/#how), [platforms](/#platforms)). If the artifact is already a file in Finder, open MacWhisper or, for a short file, the [browser tool](/tools/transcribe-recording).

A second filter is language and machine. A Zurich team that mixes English and German on Apple silicon can record with Vemoir and pick a local model. Parakeet v3 covers 25 European languages. Other models have their own coverage. The interface is available in English, German, French, Italian, and Dutch, separate from the language of the transcript ([languages](/#languages)). A Windows-heavy team that already pays for Fathom should not switch to a Mac-only app to satisfy one person’s preference.

Keep the pilot small. Record two calls you would be willing to delete, export the minutes, and see whether anyone opens them. A tool that produces a library nobody reads is a storage decision, not a productivity decision.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a Fathom alternative that does not add a bot?

Yes. Fathom itself documents bot-free capture modes on Mac. Vemoir, Granola, Jamie, and MacWhisper also capture from the computer without joining as a participant. Read each vendor’s page for where the transcript is stored after that.

### Does Fathom keep a free plan?

Fathom advertises free recording and transcription, with paid plans for advanced team and CRM features. The exact monthly limits are on [Fathom pricing](https://fathom.video/pricing). Check them before you promise “unlimited” to a team.

### Can I use Fathom’s bot-free mode in German?

The help article checked in September 2026 describes the 3.0 upgrade for English-language meetings on Mac. Do not assume other languages follow the same path until Fathom’s own docs say so.

### Where does Vemoir send the audio?

By default it does not send it. Transcription runs on the Mac. A cloud model, if you turn one on, receives text for the features you assign, not the raw audio. Exports are something you choose.

### What if I already have a recording file?

Use the [transcribe recording tool](/tools/transcribe-recording) for a file up to 15 minutes, three successful runs a week, with no upload and no speaker labels. Longer files and live meetings belong in the Mac app, or in MacWhisper if you only need a file transcript.

### Does Vemoir work with Zoom, Meet, and Teams?

Yes, because it records system audio and the microphone, not because it integrates with one vendor’s cloud. Any app that plays the call on the Mac is in scope ([platform support](/#platforms)).

### How much does Vemoir cost?

A one-time Mac App Store purchase: CHF 20 in Switzerland, €24.99 in Germany, Austria, France, and Italy, $22.99 in the US. No subscription and no per-seat fee.

### Who should stay on Fathom?

Teams that want a shared cloud archive, clips, and CRM updates, and that accept either a bot or Fathom’s documented Mac limits. Also anyone who must record on Windows.

For the next meeting on a Mac, start with [how capture works](/#capture) or install Vemoir from the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794693630). If you only need to see a short file turned into text first, open [transcribe a recording](/tools/transcribe-recording).
