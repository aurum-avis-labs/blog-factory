## What Fathom vs Otter is really choosing

Both products record meetings so a person or a team can search them later. Both summarize. Both are built around an account, not around a file that never leaves a laptop. The useful difference is not a slogan about AI notes. It is who the library is for, what the free account still withholds, and whether a bot has to sit in the participant list.

Fathom's page speaks to individuals and to teams. The individual free plan is generous on volume. Team and Business add search across calls, comments, folders, CRM field sync, coaching metrics, and scorecards. Public API and MCP appear in their navigation as Fathom features. They are not a reason to assume a Mac app has the same connectors.

Otter is the product many teams already know for a transcript that people read while the meeting is happening, with a shared workspace and mobile apps. If your coworkers already live there, the switching cost is the library, not the record button. Read the two linked posts before you restate Otter's security page from memory.

A third path exists when the account itself is the problem. A Mac menu-bar app can take microphone and system audio without joining the call, and keep the transcript on the machine. That is not Fathom and it is not Otter. It also will not update a CRM or score a rep. Hold that until the last sections so the comparison stays about the two products you asked about.

## The free plans

Fathom's individual free plan, on the pricing page, lists unlimited recordings and transcriptions, a choice of bot-free capture (in beta) or bot capture, instant AI call summaries, and clips, playlists, and search across calls. "Free forever" is their wording. There is no minute counter in that free row.

That is a different free story from a plan that meters transcription time. If you are comparing Fathom vs Otter on cost for a heavy week of external calls, start by writing down how many minutes Otter's current free plan actually includes. Their page has advertised more than one annual figure depending on the toggle. Treat the number you see today as the number. A blog that locks $8.33 or $16.99 will be wrong for someone whose screen shows the other. The basic free tier people still cite is on the order of a few hundred transcription minutes a month, not unlimited. Confirm it before you tell a team they will not hit a wall.

What Fathom's free row does not include, by reading the paid rows underneath, is the advanced summaries, AI action items, and the conversational assistant that sit on Premium. Team search, comments, folders, and keyword alerts sit higher still. "Unlimited" is the capture and the basic summary, not every control a manager wants.

Otter's free plan is the place you learn the product with a cap. When the cap binds, you pay or you stop recording. Fathom's free plan is the place you can keep recording and then notice which buttons are on the paid individual plan. Those are both honest designs. They fail different people.

The Team annual price is below the Premium annual price on that page. That is their display, not a calculation you should assume will stay. Two users is the minimum they print on Team and Business. A solo user looking at Team is looking at the wrong row.

Business is the row that matters if the recording exists to change a deal record and to coach. CRM field sync, a deal view, coaching metrics, and AI scorecards are listed there, not on Free and not on Premium. If those words are why you started the comparison, the rest of the table is secondary.

Otter's paid plans are per user as well, and the annual discount on their page is where the conflicting figures show up. Do not publish one Otter Pro price in a deck without a screenshot from the day you buy. Our [Otter alternative](/blog/otter-ai-alternative) is the longer look at what that subscription buys compared with staying off the account.

If the question is only whether audio must stay on the laptop, neither table answers it. [How Vemoir records](/#capture) from the menu bar is the third path in the sections below.

A Mac app that is a one-time purchase does not appear in either table. Vemoir is CHF 20 in Switzerland, €24.99 in Germany, Austria, France, and Italy, and $22.99 in the US, on the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794693630). That price does not include Fathom Business, Otter's workspace, or a CRM. It includes local capture and the record on that Mac. [Pricing](/#pricing) is the same line on the site.

## Bot, bot-free, and who can see the call

Fathom's free row says "choice of bot-free (in beta) or bot capture." Bot-free, read narrowly, means you can try a capture that does not add their participant. The word beta is theirs. A beta control can be missing on a version, limited to Mac, or withdrawn. Do not promise a guest that the bot will stay absent if you have not checked the client you will use that day.

Bot capture is the other choice they still list. A bot is visible. People know a recorder joined. That visibility is a feature when your policy is that recording must be obvious, and a problem when the client has banned notetakers. Fathom's page does not say the bot-free beta stores audio only on the Mac. Summaries are still "instant AI call summaries" inside Fathom. Assume the library is theirs until their docs say a given mode never uploads. This article will not invent an on-device claim they did not print on the pricing page.

Otter's familiar path is a meeting bot and a transcript other people can sit in. The safety post covers where that vendor says data lives and how they talk about training. If your objection to Otter was the bot, Fathom's beta toggle is the closer comparison, not a local app. If your objection was the account, the toggle does not answer it.

Either cloud product can share the meeting with teammates once you are on a team plan. That sharing is the product. Turn it on when the team is supposed to hear the call. Leave the calendar disconnected when a particular account must not be recorded into a shared library.

## Where Fathom is the better system

Pick Fathom when the free volume matters and the team features on Business are the ones you will actually use.

You record more than a metered free tier will allow, and you are not ready to pay Otter for headroom. Fathom's free row is built for that. You want a bot-free attempt on a Mac without buying the paid individual plan first. Their page puts that choice on Free, in beta. You will move to Business because CRM field sync and coaching scorecards are the job, with at least two users. You want clips and playlists as a way to hand a moment to someone who will not watch the hour.

Fathom is also the better system when you want one vendor's story from a generous individual plan up to an enterprise row that names SSO, SCIM, and custom retention. You still have to read the enterprise terms. The row existing on a pricing page is not the contract.

Do not pick Fathom because a navigation label mentions MCP or an API. Those are their integrations. They do not make the recording local, and they do not exist on a Mac app that does not offer them.

## Where Otter is the better system

Pick Otter when the people in the meeting already read Otter, or when the thing you need is the collaborative transcript rather than a coaching scorecard.

A team that has folders, comments, and a habit of opening the live transcript will not become faster because a spreadsheet says another vendor has unlimited free capture. Migration is re-teaching everyone where the notes are. If Otter's current paid price, on the toggle you actually see, is lower than Fathom Premium or Team for the seats you have, and the minutes fit, Otter can be the cheaper renewal. Check the page. Do not take that sentence as a quote of a number.

Otter is the better system when mobile apps and a shared inbox of meetings are the workflow, and Fathom's Business features are irrelevant because you are not a revenue team. A researcher, a student group, or a small staff who already standardized on Otter should not rip it out for a beta toggle.

The limits stay the ones in the safety post: you are trusting their storage description, their bot is visible when they join, and a free minute cap is a cap. If those were already disqualifying, Fathom's free unlimited row is the fair thing to test, still inside their account.

## When neither cloud library is the point

Some calls should not be in Fathom or Otter.

The buyer asked for no notetaker and no third-party library. Your policy says this client stays off call-intelligence tools. You want the transcript on the Mac that joined the meeting, with a delete that removes the file from that machine. You do not need CRM field sync, and you do not need a colleague to replay the call from a browser.

Then Fathom vs Otter is the wrong pairing. Record from the menu bar: selected microphone, system audio, no extra participant. macOS asks for the Screen Recording permission because that is the only route to other apps' audio. The screen itself is never captured. Transcription stays on the Mac. You can turn on your own OpenAI or Anthropic key for text only, stored in Keychain, with no audio and no silent fallback. The default is off. Apple silicon, macOS Sonoma 14.2 or later. No Windows, no Intel Mac.

A file you already downloaded from either vendor, if you are allowed to keep it, can be re-transcribed locally for a passage you want off their editor. Fifteen minutes in the browser at [transcribe recording](/tools/transcribe-recording), three runs a week, no upload, no speaker names. Longer audio imports on the Mac as MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF, CAF, or FLAC.

That path will not score a sales call or push a field to Salesforce. Gong-style coaching was never the claim. Fathom Business still wins for a revenue team that wants the scorecard. Otter still wins for the live shared transcript your team already opens. The local app wins when the tape is not supposed to have an account.

## A week of calls

Monday is an internal standup already in Otter. Leave it there. The notes have a home.

Tuesday is a customer who forbids notetakers. Do not send Fathom's bot and do not send Otter's. If you are allowed to keep a personal copy, the Mac capture is the one that adds no participant. Say that you are recording. A beta bot-free mode is worth testing only if you have confirmed it on that Mac and you accept that the summary still lands in Fathom.

Wednesday is a pipeline review. The manager wants every call in one place and the deal fields touched after the meeting. That is Fathom Business if you have already chosen Fathom, or the CRM workflow you already pay for inside Otter if that is what the team bought. A laptop app with no team library will make Wednesday worse.

Thursday is a 40-minute file someone exported. You need the words, not another seat. Use the local import. Do not upload it into the vendor you were trying to stay out of.

Friday is the decision. If the only complaint about Otter was the free minutes, trial Fathom's free plan on calls you are allowed to put there, and keep Otter for the meetings that already live in it until the trial proves the team will switch. If the complaint was that both libraries are too shared, stop connecting calendars and keep the audio on the Mac.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Fathom's free plan actually unlimited?

Their pricing page says unlimited recordings and transcriptions on the individual free plan, with instant summaries, clips, playlists, and search. Advanced summaries and AI action items are on Premium. Team features start at the Team row.

### Is bot-free the same as on-device?

No. Fathom marks bot-free as beta and still describes the product as their summaries and search. The absence of a bot is not a statement that audio stayed on the Mac. Read their security pages before you treat beta capture as local.

### What does Otter cost?

The public pricing page has shown different annual figures depending on the billing control. Use the figure on the screen when you buy. This comparison will not lock one. The longer Otter write-ups are [is Otter safe](/blog/is-otter-ai-safe) and [Otter alternative](/blog/otter-ai-alternative).

### Which one coaches a sales team?

Fathom lists coaching metrics, AI scorecards, Deal View, and CRM field sync on Business, at the prices above, with a two-user minimum. If that is the requirement, Fathom is the nearer of these two. A local recorder does not do it.

### Can I use either on Windows?

Fathom and Otter are not limited to Apple silicon. The local alternative in this article is. If the team is on Windows, stay with the cloud product that matches the job, or use a short file in the browser tool from whatever machine downloaded it.

### Does a one-time Mac purchase replace Premium?

It replaces the need for an account when the account is what you are avoiding. It does not include Fathom's team workspace or Otter's shared transcript. CHF 20 in Switzerland, €24.99 in Germany, Austria, France, and Italy, $22.99 in the US.

### What should I record this afternoon?

If the calendar is already flowing into Otter or Fathom and the meeting is allowed to be there, leave the connection. If this meeting is excluded, do not add a bot. Capture on the Mac or take notes by hand.

Fathom vs Otter ends with the library you are willing to keep. Fathom's free plan is the wider net. Otter is the transcript a team may already share. The call that should stay on the machine is a third choice, on the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794693630), with capture described under [how it records](/#capture).
