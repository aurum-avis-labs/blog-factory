**ChatGPT to scheduled social posts** starts with a simple thesis: ChatGPT outputs text; scheduling still needs structured handoff. No pretend plugin unless documented.

Most creators do not fail on ideas. They fail on repeated handoffs that no single app owns.

## What good looks like in 2026

You can explain your pipeline in one sentence: where briefs live, where visuals are approved, where queues run, and where you read results. If that sentence mentions six logins, efficiency work belongs before another subscription.

## Practical steps this week

1. Time one post end to end with a stopwatch
2. Circle steps that repeat for every post
3. Remove one duplicate upload or export
4. Write platform-specific caption rules once
5. Re-test the same post type next week
