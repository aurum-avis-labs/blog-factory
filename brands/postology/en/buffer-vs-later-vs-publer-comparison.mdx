## Buffer vs Later vs Publer is a job match, not a ranking

Buffer vs Later vs Publer comes up when a solo creator or a small team wants one queue and is tired of guessing from feature pages. None of the three wins every job. Buffer is the straightforward calendar for posts you already finished. Later is the visual planner when an Instagram grid drives the week. Publer is the budget queue across several accounts, with a free plan that leaves X out.

This comparison uses the same jobs for all three: a lasting free option, the paid entry, caption help, and who should skip it. Figures come from buffer.com/pricing, later.com/pricing, and Publer's help center, checked in September 2026. Re-check before you pay. Plans move, and an AI label on a pricing page is not the same as a writer that knows your brand.

Read the rows across, not down. The cheap cell is not the best cell if the free plan excludes a network you post on every week.

## Where Buffer is still the better buy

Buffer fits the creator who shows up with files. The free plan is enough to learn the queue: one user, 10 scheduled posts per channel. Past that, Essentials bills per channel, so the honest price is the number of channels times the plan, not the $5 headline.

Stay if a teammate only needs that calendar and your visuals are produced elsewhere. Leave the "which is best" search if this is you. An AI assistant on the plan can shorten a caption edit. It is not a reason to expect Buffer to design the week. Teams that already like this split, queue here and create somewhere else, usually regret a switch made for a feature they will open twice.

Skip Buffer if the dominant pain is starting from a blank brief several times a week. You will still have that blank brief after you subscribe. Also watch the free cap. A channel with daily posts plus spares can outgrow 10 scheduled posts before you have learned the product.

## Where Later is still the better buy

Later earns the higher entry when you plan Instagram as a grid and you want to see the sequence before it publishes. The September 2026 check showed a trial on paid plans rather than a free forever queue, and Starter near $18.75 a month on yearly USD billing. AI help is credit-based, and lower tiers have a credit cap. Team features sit on upper tiers, so a two-person shop should read that line before assuming both people are included on Starter.

Stay if the grid preview is part of how you say yes to a post. A cheaper queue that hides the grid will send you back to Instagram's own app, which defeats the point of the subscription.

Skip Later if you barely use Instagram, if you want the lowest bill for a plain queue, or if you needed a lasting free plan to prove the habit. A 14-day trial is enough to drag a grid around. It is a short window for judging a month of real files.

## Where Publer is still the better buy

Publer fits price-sensitive posting across a handful of accounts. The free plan we checked covered 3 accounts and 10 scheduled posts per account, and it excluded X. Professional started at $5 a month per account. Business is the tier tied to AI Assist. That last point is the one to verify on publer.com, because plan names and add-ons move.

Stay if the budget is the constraint and the free-plan limits match the accounts you actually run. Skip if X is central and you hoped the free plan would cover it. Skip if you wanted Instagram-grid planning as the main screen. Publer is a queue with a low entry, not a Later clone at Buffer's price.

## What none of them replace

All three assume a post exists. Caption tools and credits help at the edge of that assumption. They do not hold your brand rules, your image folder, and your calendar in one motion. Creators who paste from a separate writer into any of these tools have a handoff problem. Switching among Buffer, Later, and Publer does not remove it. It changes the destination of the paste.

Stacking two of them is usually a duplicate subscription. Keep two only if each one does a weekly job the other lacks, and you can name that job. "I might need it" is how the bills multiply.

## How to choose without a spreadsheet

Write three lines: networks you will post on this month, whether the Instagram grid changes what you publish, and whether the posts are finished before you open a scheduler.

Grid matters and the posts are visual: trial Later, and accept that the paid entry is higher. Posts are finished and you want a simple queue: Buffer, and multiply $5 by the channels that will leave the free plan. Several accounts and a small budget, and X is optional on day one: Publer, then confirm the free-plan exclusions. If you cannot fill in "the posts are finished," pause. You are about to rent a calendar for work you have not made. The line between a scheduler and a creation flow is in [content automation vs scheduling tools](/blog/content-automation-vs-scheduling-tools).

## Limits that change the bill

The headline price is not the bill. Three cases, using only the September 2026 figures:

A solo creator on Buffer's free plan, one channel, under 10 scheduled posts, pays nothing until the queue outgrows that cap. Later's Starter near $18.75 a month on yearly billing is the price of the grid preview, not a luxury tier. Publer at $5 per account looks like Buffer until you count accounts and notice X missing on the free plan. If a collaborator must log in this month, price that seat on Buffer or Later before you trust the entry number.
