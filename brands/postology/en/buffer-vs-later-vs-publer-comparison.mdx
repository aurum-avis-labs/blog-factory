**Buffer vs Later vs Publer** is a common stack question for solo creators and small teams. None is perfect; each optimizes different pains.

## Quick fit guide

- **Buffer:** Strong queue and AI assistant on free tier; pay per channel on Essentials.
- **Later:** Visual Instagram planning; paid entry higher; team features on upper tiers.
- **Publer:** Aggressive free tier for a few accounts; Business tier adds AI Assist (verify on publer.com).

| | Buffer | Later | Publer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free forever | Yes, with limits | Trial on paid | Yes, with limits |
| Starting paid | From $5/mo per channel | From ~$18.75/mo yearly USD | From $5/mo per account |
| AI captions | AI Assistant on plans | Plan credits | Business AI Assist (verify) |
| Best when | You have assets ready | Instagram visual grid matters | Tight budget multi-network |

Sources checked September 2026: buffer.com/pricing, later.com/pricing, publer.com help center.

Teams that only need reliable queues and already produce visuals elsewhere may not need to switch.

## Where Later is still better

Grid preview and creator-native Instagram workflows matter more than cheapest queue price.

## Where Publer is still better

Price-sensitive multi-account posting with acceptable limits.
