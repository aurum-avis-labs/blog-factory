A **social media workflow for creators** should reduce decisions, not add dashboards. One-flow posting means one brief, one approval, one queue path.

## Start from channels you will actually maintain

Pick two primary networks plus one experimental slot instead of five dormant profiles.

{
<Image src={inline1} alt="Creator working on social content at a laptop" width={700} quality={80} class="w-full" />
}

## Define three repeatable formats

Formats cut creative fatigue: teach, proof, offer, or story. Rotate instead of reinventing.

## Connect draft, visual, and queue

The flow breaks when any step lives in a different app without metadata.
