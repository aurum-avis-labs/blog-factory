# Search rules that bind this repo

Checked against Google Search Central in September 2026. Third-party "ranking factor" percentages are not rules here. They disagree with each other, and Google does not publish a word-count target.

## What Google actually says

- Helpful, reliable pages are written for people. Pages made mainly to gain rankings are what the ranking systems are built to demote. Source: [Creating helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).
- **Scaled content abuse** is many pages produced to manipulate rankings rather than help users. The policy names generative AI used to mass-produce pages without added value, and it applies whether a human, a model, or both produced them. Source: [Spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).
- Using AI to make content is fine when the content is useful. Using it primarily to manipulate rankings is spam. Source: [Google's guidance on AI content](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/02/google-search-and-ai-content).
- Word count is not a ranking factor. A short page that finishes the job outranks a long page that repeats itself. John Mueller has said this directly; the helpful-content guide warns against writing to a supposed preferred length.
- The helpful-content signal is site-wide. A block of thin URLs can make the rest of the domain less likely to show.
- People-first checks that matter while drafting: original information, a clear who-it's-for, evidence for claims, and a reason to exist beyond "this keyword had volume".
- Search Essentials still wants the words people search for in the title, headings, anchor text, and alt text. That is placement, not density.

## How to apply that to a batch

Allowed: a cluster of URLs with different jobs (define, compare, choose, implement), each with its own examples, limits, and internal links.

Not allowed:

- the same outline with the tool name, city, or year swapped
- translations that exist only so another language code is in the URL
- listicles that summarize other blogs and add no decision
- dates changed so a page looks fresh
- pages that target a query the site cannot honestly answer

## Length and citations

Choose length from the query type in [query-map.md](query-map.md).

For queries where an answer box or an AI summary may quote the page, put a direct answer in the first paragraph and again under the H2 that asks the question. Comparisons, limits, and sourced numbers are easier to quote than slogans. This is a writing practice, not a published ranking weight.

Cover the adjacent questions on the same URL when they serve the same intent (price, limits, who should skip). Split them onto a new URL only when the intent changes.

## Experience

Prefer a concrete walkthrough from the product or the brand's own process over a generic explainer. If the brand has no first-hand detail for a claim, narrow the claim or cite a source. Do not fabricate experience to sound like E-E-A-T.
