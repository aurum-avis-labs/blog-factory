# phishing-simulations-allowed-switzerland

## Verified
- Art. 26 ArGV 3 forbids surveillance systems intended to control employee behaviour: https://www.seco.admin.ch/dam/seco/de/dokumente/Arbeit/Arbeitsbedingungen/Arbeitsgesetz%20und%20Verordnungen/Wegleitungen/Wegleitungen%203/ArGV3_art26.pdf.download.pdf/ArGV3_art26_de.pdf
- Art. 328b OR limits processing of employee personal data to what is job-related: https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de (OR via fedlex)
- EDÖB guidance on technical monitoring at work: https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/de/technische-mittel-zur-uberwachung-am-arbeitsplatz
- BACS H2 2025: 29'006 voluntary reports plus 145 from reporting duty; 52 percent fraud: https://www.bacs.admin.ch/de/hjb-25-2-de
- Aurum security awareness: live session 1–2 h, prep, follow-up; remote or on site in Switzerland (service pages, checked 2026-09-30)
