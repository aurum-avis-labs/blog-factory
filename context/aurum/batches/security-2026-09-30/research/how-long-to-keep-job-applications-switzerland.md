# how-long-to-keep-job-applications-switzerland

## Verified
- Art. 6 DSG: personal data only as long as necessary for the purpose: https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de
- Art. 328b OR limits employee data to job-related processing (context for hired candidates): https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de
- EDÖB guidance on employee monitoring (adjacent, not retention table): https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/de/technische-mittel-zur-uberwachung-am-arbeitsplatz

## Not verified
- A single nationwide retention period in months for rejected applications in statute (practice varies; talent pool needs consent)

## Do not claim
- KI screening of applications (bewerbungen-vorsortieren-ki)
- Fixed «3 months for everyone» as law without noting purpose and consent
