## 10. Lessons from waves 1–2 (so they do not happen again)

- **Usage limits:** 10 parallel agents stopped halfway, twice. Max 4 at once, one topic at a time, write files to disk and commit after each topic.
- **Forward links:** wave 1 had 15 body links to posts that were not live yet (404). The seq rule and the validator now catch this.
- **Price drift:** posts said «CHF 49 launch» after the site moved to CHF 99. Take prices only from WRITER-BRIEF section 8.
- **du/ihr:** the German posts used ihr/euch while the landing page uses Sie. Sie everywhere.
- **Honest findings beat the brief:** the skills-shortage post found that office roles show a surplus, not a shortage, and said so. If research contradicts a brief, write what the source says and flag it to Q.
