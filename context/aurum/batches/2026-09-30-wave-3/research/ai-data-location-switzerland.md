# ai-data-location-switzerland
Primary query DE / EN: Muss KI in der Schweiz laufen? Datenstandort / does AI have to run in Switzerland? data location
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Official pages cover the cross-border rule (EDÖB, Fedlex DSG Art. 16/17, DSV Annex 1) and vendor residency options (Microsoft 365 at rest, OpenAI API region settings). Vendor blogs and law-firm notes mix "stored in Europe" with "the model runs in Switzerland". Gap: a short office answer that the DSG does not require Swiss hosting, what the country list and the Swiss-US framework actually cover, and how a mailbox region differs from model processing. Not a repeat of the free-ChatGPT traffic light.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- DSG Art. 16 para. 1: personal data may be disclosed abroad if the Federal Council has determined that the destination state's legislation, or the international body, ensures adequate protection. Art. 16 para. 2 lists safeguards when there is no such decision (treaty, contractual clauses notified to the FDPIC, specific guarantees, standard clauses the FDPIC has approved, issued or recognised, binding corporate rules). Art. 17 lists exceptions, including express consent. Consolidated text read from the official PDF (version date in the URL 20250707), access 2026-09-30: https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/filestore/fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/20250707/de/pdf-a/fedlex-data-admin-ch-eli-cc-2022-491-20250707-de-pdf-a.pdf (permalink used in the post: https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de)
- DSV Art. 8 para. 1: states, territories, specific sectors and international bodies with adequate protection are listed in Annex 1. Annex 1 (cleaned up by the ordinance of 14 August 2024, in force 15 September 2024, AS 2024 435) lists among others Germany, Austria, Belgium, France, Italy and the United States with a footnote. The US line is limited to personal data processed by organisations certified under the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework, and cites Switzerland's designation on 7 June 2024. Access 2026-09-30: https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/568/de
- FDPIC: if adequate protection exists, personal data may be transferred freely to that state by private companies and federal bodies. Without it, contractual clauses, standard clauses (FDPIC has recognised the EU SCCs) or binding corporate rules may apply; otherwise Art. 17 exceptions. The data subject must be informed of disclosure abroad (Art. 19 para. 4 DSG). Disclosure that misses Art. 16 and 17 can be an offence (Art. 61 let. a DSG). Countries and safeguards are mandatory fields in the register of processing activities (Art. 12 DSG). The US addition to Annex 1 entered into force on 15 September 2024. Access 2026-09-30: https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/de/bekanntgabe-von-personendaten-ins-ausland
- Microsoft Learn: data residency means where customer data is stored at rest. Switzerland is a Local Region Geography. Macro Region Geography 4 (European Union/EFTA) lists data centres including Switzerland. Core services named on the page include Exchange Online, SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams, Microsoft 365 Copilot and Copilot Chat. The Data Location card in the admin centre shows the primary provisioned geography for Exchange, SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams and Copilot. Default geography is set by the country chosen when the tenant is created and cannot be changed. Access 2026-09-30: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-overview
- OpenAI API docs: data residency is a project setting for eligible customers. The Europe option is labelled "Europe (EEA + Switzerland)" with domain eu.api.openai.com, and the region table marks storage and processing as yes for supported endpoints. Customer content is stored at rest in the selected region to the extent the endpoint needs persistence. System data may be processed and stored outside the selected region. A region other than the United States requires approval for abuse-monitoring controls and a Modified Retention amendment. The docs do not name a data centre inside Switzerland. Access 2026-09-30: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data
