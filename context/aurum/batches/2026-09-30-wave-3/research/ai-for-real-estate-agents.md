Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- SMG insertion rules for ImmoScout24, fetched 2026-09-30: https://www.immoscout24.ch/c/de/ueber-uns/insertionsregeln
  - Published information must be true and refer to the object, including price, location (street, postcode, town, canton), size and position, the contact person, condition, availability, images and plans.
  - The price field must contain the effective gross rent or the final sale price, or the label "Preis auf Anfrage". Listings without a concrete price are placed last in the results.
  - A valid phone number or email must be on the listing for contact.
  - Images must not show an email, a postal address, a phone number or a licence plate. Faces only with that person's consent.
  - A third party may publish only with the owner's or the property manager's consent.
  - Each new listing is reviewed before it goes live. Repeated deletion and republication by automated means to dodge a listing cap is named as abusive content.
- ImmoScout24 seeker help, search extract of the official article (page did not render on direct fetch, 2026-09-30): interested people are told to use the contact form on the listing; personal application dossiers are deleted immediately by the portal.
