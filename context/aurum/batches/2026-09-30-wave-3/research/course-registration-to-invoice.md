# course-registration-to-invoice
Primary query DE / EN: Kursanmeldung bis Rechnung automatisieren / from course registration to invoice
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Generic form-to-CRM guides and QR-bill software pages. Gap: one course-office chain: form, seat, waiting list, a payer who is not the participant, a QR-bill that follows SIX, a reminder, an attendance list and a certificate. Not a CRM essay (that is web-form-to-crm, linked in relatedPosts only because it publishes later).
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- SIX: the QR-bill has been in circulation since June 2020 and replaced the Swiss payment slips on 1 October 2022. It can be printed or issued digitally. It consists of a payment section and a receipt. The Swiss QR code contains the payment information in digital form, and the same information appears in plain text so the recipient can check it before paying. Access 2026-09-30: https://www.six-group.com/en/products-services/banking-services/payment-standardization/standards/qr-bill.html
- SIX, same page, access 2026-09-30: version 2.3 entered into force on 22 November 2025, and a later section says that from then only the structured address is permitted. The same page's top notice says the introduction of the structured address (type S) remains scheduled for November 2026. FLAG: the page contradicts itself on the date. The posts state both sentences and do not pick one. SIX lists free public generators for people without software and states it does not warrant them.
