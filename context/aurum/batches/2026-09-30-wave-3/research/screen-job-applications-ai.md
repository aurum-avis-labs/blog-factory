# screen-job-applications-ai
Primary query DE / EN: Bewerbungen vorsortieren mit KI / screening job applications with AI: what is allowed
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Vendor ATS pages dominate commercial searches. Official material that fits a Swiss SME is the EDÖB guidance on employer processing and on AI in hiring, the retention note in the EDÖB FAQ (up to three months after a rejection to defend a possible claim under the Gender Equality Act), and DSG Art. 21 on exclusively automated decisions. The gap is a practical split for an ordinary employer: structuring a dossier, an acknowledgement and interview times are fine; ranking, auto-rejection and profile analysis need care; the hiring decision stays with a person. Not a legal opinion, and not the recruitment-agency industry post.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- EDÖB, AI in hiring: the data-protection frame is the same as a conventional process. The employer may collect only what is needed to judge suitability for the role. Recognisability and proportionality are central, because AI analysis can interfere with personality more seriously than a conventional interview. Video and voice analysis that builds profiles raises the bar. https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/de/datenbearbeitung-durch-den-arbeitgeber accessed 2026-09-30.
- Same page, via OR Art. 328b: only data about suitability for the employment, or required to perform the contract. Employee consent is rarely free because of the subordinate position. The page applies the idea to hiring data as well in the AI section (suitability only).
- EDÖB, selection phase: ask only what shows the person can do the job. Origin, union membership, religion and political views only if the organisation's ideology makes them necessary. Health questions are tightly limited. References from third parties need prior consent. Dossiers are seen by HR or the direct manager. After rejection, documents are returned and copies destroyed unless a justification applies. With consent, documents may be kept until a date fixed in advance for a likely reuse. An overriding interest can be argued if the person is informed. https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/de/verschiedene-phasen-des-arbeitsverhaeltnisses accessed 2026-09-30.
- EDÖB FAQ: if there is no hire, application data may be kept for up to three months after the rejection is communicated, so the employer can defend a claim of discriminatory refusal to hire under the Gender Equality Act (Art. 8 para. 2 in connection with Art. 5 para. 2 GlG). A short further extension may be justified if service of a claim is delayed. https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/de/faq-datenschutz accessed 2026-09-30.
- DSG Art. 21: a controller informs the person about a decision based exclusively on automated processing that has a legal consequence for them or significantly affects them. On request, the person may state their view and may demand that a natural person reviews the decision. Exceptions include a decision tied directly to a contract that grants what the person asked, or explicit consent. https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de accessed 2026-09-30.
- EDÖB: the FADP applies directly to AI. https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/de/update-geltendes-datenschutzgesetz-ist-auf-ki-direkt-anwendbar accessed 2026-09-30.
