# ai-for-architects-engineers
Primary query DE / EN: KI im Architektur- und Ingenieurbüro / AI for architecture and engineering offices
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): SIA shop pages identify the 2020 orders for services and fees. Design-tool pages sell CAD and image generation. The gap is the office around the project: hours, the fee invoice, minutes and tasks, plan distribution, tender papers. Not design. The step-by-step from hours to the fee invoice is the later post project-hours-fee-invoice.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- SIA shop: SIA 102 / 2020, title "Ordnung für Leistungen und Honorare der Architektinnen und Architekten", valid from 01.01.2020, SN 508102, 44 pages. Predecessors listed on the page include the 2014 order and the 2018 Kalkulationshilfe. https://shop.sia.ch/normenwerk/architekt/102_2020_d/D/Product access 2026-09-30.
- SIA shop: SIA 103 / 2020, title "Ordnung für Leistungen und Honorare der Bauingenieurinnen und Bauingenieure", valid from 01.01.2020, SN 508103, 68 pages. Predecessors listed include 2014 and 2018 documents. https://shop.sia.ch/normenwerk/ingenieur/103_2020_d/D/Product access 2026-09-30.
- Contents downloads linked from those shop pages (seen in search extracts, access 2026-09-30) list services by phase and by sub-phase, roles such as Gesamtleitung and Fachplanung, and for SIA 103 also Bauleitung, plus fee calculation by time actually spent (Art. 6). The post does not restate those articles. It only says the contract, not a chat, decides how the fee is set. Contents files were served on http links; not placed in the post.
Vendor / product features used (URL each): none. No CAD vendor named.
Not verifiable, therefore left out: a fixed percentage per SIA phase, any recommended hourly rate, WEKO history from older calculation-aid PDFs (the 2020 product pages supersede that story and the post does not use it). No claim that plans can be issued by a model.
