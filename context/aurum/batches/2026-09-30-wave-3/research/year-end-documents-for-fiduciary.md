# year-end-documents-for-fiduciary
Primary query DE / EN: Jahresabschluss Unterlagen für den Treuhänder / year-end closing: documents your fiduciary needs
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Retention articles explain the ten-year duty. Fiduciary marketing lists documents from the adviser's side. The gap is the client's office: one folder, the usual pack after 31 December, and a chase for what is missing, instead of email ping-pong. Valuation and the return stay with the fiduciary.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- Art. 958f OR: books, accounting records, the annual report and the audit report are kept for ten years. The period starts at the end of the financial year. A printed, signed copy of the annual report and of the audit report must be kept. Books and records may be on paper, an electronic carrier or an equivalent form if they still match the underlying transactions and can be made readable again. The Federal Council regulates this in the GeBüV. KMU portal, access 2026-09-30. https://www.kmu.admin.ch/de/elektronische-aufbewahrung-der-geschaeftsbuecher
- Same ten-year duty (Art. 958f OR), including electronic storage (Art. 958f para. 3), on the KMU accounting-duty page. Access 2026-09-30. https://www.kmu.admin.ch/de/buchhaltungspflicht-fuer-handelsgesellschaften
- GeBüV Art. 9: unchangeable carriers are allowed. Changeable carriers are allowed if a technical procedure protects integrity (digital signature procedures are named as an example), the time of storage can be proved (a time stamp is the example), the rules for that procedure are followed, and the procedures plus logs are documented and kept. Fedlex text as returned in search of the consolidated ordinance. Access 2026-09-30. https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2002/216/de
- The KMU portal says changeable carriers need such technical procedures and that logs must be kept, and that anyone entitled must be able to inspect the books within a reasonable time until the retention period ends. Access 2026-09-30. https://www.kmu.admin.ch/de/elektronische-aufbewahrung-der-geschaeftsbuecher
