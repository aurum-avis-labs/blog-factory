# website-chatbot-sme
Primary query DE / EN: Chatbot für die Website: ja oder nein / website chatbot for a small business: yes or no
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Vendor pages and agency listicles sell widgets and quote conversion lifts. Legal notes cover transparency in general. Gap: a decision between a maintained FAQ plus contact form (or the existing inbox) and a chatbot, for a Swiss SME, including wrong promises, handover, maintenance and the FDPIC point that people must be told when they are writing to a machine. Not a phone-assistant comparison (that is W3-D9) and not an agent-versus-workflow essay.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- FDPIC, page dated 24 September 2025: the DSG is technology-neutral and applies directly to AI processing. Manufacturers, providers and users must state the purpose, how the system works, and the data sources. Where intelligent language models communicate directly with users, those users have the right to know whether and to what extent they are corresponding with a machine, and whether the data they enter will be reused to improve self-learning programs or for other purposes. Access 2026-09-30: https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/de/ki-und-datenschutz (EN twin used in the English post: https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/en/ai-and-data-protection)
- DSG Art. 19: when personal data are collected, the controller informs the person, at least of identity and contact details, the purpose, and where applicable the recipients or categories of recipients. If data are disclosed abroad, Art. 19 para. 4 also requires the state or international body and, where applicable, the Art. 16 para. 2 safeguards or the Art. 17 exception. Official PDF, access 2026-09-30: https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/filestore/fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/20250707/de/pdf-a/fedlex-data-admin-ch-eli-cc-2022-491-20250707-de-pdf-a.pdf
- DSG Art. 21: the duty to inform about an automated individual decision, and the right to have a natural person review it, applies when a decision is based solely on automated processing and has a legal consequence or significantly affects the person. A chat answer about opening hours is not that kind of decision. Same PDF, access 2026-09-30.
