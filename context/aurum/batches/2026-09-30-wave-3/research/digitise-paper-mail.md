# digitise-paper-mail
Primary query DE / EN: Papierpost digitalisieren / digitise incoming paper mail
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Swiss Post's own pages describe inbound options and the ePost ScanningService (PDF of incoming business mail, example of an invoice or a contract moved into ERP). kmu.admin.ch explains electronic retention of business books under OR 958f and GeBüV, not the daily sorting of mixed office mail. The gap is the office sequence: open or outsource the scan, read the type, assign a person or a case, and decide envelope by envelope what happens to the paper. Filing names are a later topic.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- Swiss Post, business inbound: the ePost ScanningService scans business mail and provides it as PDF for further handling. The page gives the example of a digitised invoice or contract transferred into ERP or accounting software so a workflow can start. https://www.post.ch/de/geschaeftsloesungen/posteingang accessed 2026-09-30.
- OR Art. 958f para. 1: business books, accounting vouchers, the annual report and the audit report are kept for ten years. The period starts at the end of the financial year. Para. 2: the annual report and the audit report are kept in writing and signed. Para. 3: books and vouchers may be kept on paper, electronically or in a comparable way, if conformity with the underlying transactions is assured and they can be made readable again at any time. https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/27/317_321_377/de accessed 2026-09-30.
- kmu.admin.ch: restates Art. 958f, points to the GeBüV, and states that Art. 9 allows unmodifiable carriers without extra requirements, while modifiable carriers need technical procedures (for example an electronic signature), a trustworthy timestamp, and logs. Authorised persons must be able to inspect the records within a reasonable time until the end of the retention period. https://www.kmu.admin.ch/de/elektronische-aufbewahrung-der-geschaeftsbuecher accessed 2026-09-30.
Vendor / product features used (URL each): Swiss Post ScanningService, URL above. No price from Post is stated, because the inbound page fetched does not list a price for ScanningService. (A separate Post page on collection scanning of barcodes is a different service and is not used.)
Not verifiable, therefore left out: whether Swiss Post returns or destroys the paper original (not stated on the page fetched). A claim that every letter may be shredded after a scan. OCR accuracy rates.
