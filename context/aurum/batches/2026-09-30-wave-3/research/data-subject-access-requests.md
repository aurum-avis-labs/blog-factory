# data-subject-access-requests
Primary query DE / EN: Auskunftsbegehren DSG beantworten / answer a data subject access request under the Swiss FADP
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Law-firm explainers and the EDÖB page on the right of access. The official core is DSG Art. 25 (what the answer contains, free of charge, as a rule within 30 days) and DSV Art. 16 to 19 (form, the 30-day clock from receipt, notice if you miss it, and the cost exception). The gap for an SME office is the search itself: mailbox, CRM, accounting and file shares, an identity check that does not become a new dossier, a template of the statutory points, and a log. Not legal advice, and not the article about which AI tools may see customer data.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- DSG Art. 25: any person may ask whether personal data about them are processed. The answer includes at least the controller's identity and contact details, the personal data, the purpose, the retention period or the criteria for setting it, available information on the origin if the data were not collected from the person, the existence of an automated individual decision and the logic if any, and the recipients or categories of recipients. The controller stays responsible if a processor handles the data. The right cannot be waived in advance. Information is free of charge; the Federal Council may provide exceptions, in particular if the effort is disproportionate. As a rule, information is provided within 30 days. https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de accessed 2026-09-30.
- DSV Art. 16: the request is in writing; it may be oral if the controller agrees. Information is given in writing or in the form in which the data exist, or orally if the person agrees. Electronic is allowed. https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/568/de accessed 2026-09-30.
- DSV Art. 18: information within 30 days of receipt. If that is impossible, the controller informs the person and states the later deadline. A refusal, restriction or deferral is communicated within the same period. Same URL and date.
- DSV Art. 19: where providing information involves disproportionate effort, a contribution may be required. The controller states the amount before answering. If the person does not confirm within ten days, the request counts as withdrawn without cost, and the Art. 18 period starts after that ten-day reflection time. Same URL and date.
- DSG Art. 26: the controller may refuse, restrict or defer information in cases the statute lists, including where a formal law provides for it to protect a professional secret. Same fedlex DSG URL and date. The post does not recast the full list.
