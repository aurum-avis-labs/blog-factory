# complaints-warranty-claims
Primary query DE / EN: Reklamationen bearbeiten und automatisieren / handle customer complaints and warranty claims
SERP: pages on this query mix consumer-rights summaries with helpdesk tools. The gap is the office flow for a Swiss SME: intake with photo and order number, classification, a clock that depends on sale versus work contract, a notice to the supplier, customer updates that do not promise a remedy, and a list of repeated defects. Shop returns ("where is my order", labels) are a sibling post, not this one.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- OR Art. 197: the seller is liable for warranted qualities and for physical or legal defects that remove or substantially reduce the value or fitness for the assumed use, even if the seller did not know the defects. SR 220, consolidated text, https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/27/317_321_377/de#art_197 accessed 2026-09-30.
- OR Art. 201 paras 1–3: the buyer must examine the thing as soon as it is feasible in the ordinary course of business and notify the seller immediately of defects. Failure to notify means the thing is deemed approved, except for defects not recognisable in a customary examination. Those must be notified immediately after discovery. Same URL #art_201, accessed 2026-09-30.
- OR Art. 201 para 4, in force 1 January 2026 (AS 2025 270): defects of a thing integrated as intended into an immovable work, which cause the work to be defective, must be notified within 60 days. Hidden defects within 60 days of discovery. Agreements on shorter periods are void. Same source.
- OR Art. 210 para 1: claims for defects prescribe two years after delivery to the buyer, even if discovered later, unless the seller assumed liability for a longer period. Para 2: five years where a defect of a thing integrated into an immovable work causes the work to be defective. Para 4: an agreement shortening the period is void only if it shortens the period to less than two years (less than one year for used things), the thing is for the buyer's personal or family use, and the seller acts in a professional capacity. All three conditions. URL #art_210, accessed 2026-09-30.
- OR Art. 367 and Art. 371 (work contract): after delivery the customer examines as soon as feasible and notifies defects. For an immovable work the notice period is 60 days, and a shorter agreement is void. The same 60 days applies to defects of a movable work integrated into the immovable work. Claims prescribe two years after acceptance, five years in the integrated-movable and immovable cases. The five-year period cannot be altered to the customer's disadvantage. URL #art_367 and #art_371, accessed 2026-09-30. Art. 370 (approval) supports the "notify or it is accepted" point.
- ch.ch, Returning or exchanging goods, accessed 2026-09-30, https://www.ch.ch/en/safety-and-justice/returning-or-exchanging-goods : no statutory right to return or exchange goods bought in a shop or online. Door-to-door and telephone contracts can be cancelled within 14 days if the value is more than CHF 100. Warranty section: "new products are covered by a two-year warranty... A period of one year applies to used products. Sellers are not allowed to shorten these periods, but may exclude the warranty altogether." Notify as soon as possible. Seller options listed: refund, discount, replacement, or GTC may specify repair only.
Contradiction flagged: ch.ch states a one-year period for used goods and a general ban on shortening. OR Art. 210 para 1 is two years from delivery for defect claims in a sale. The one-year figure in para 4 is the floor for a contractual shortening of used goods, and only when the buyer is a consumer and the seller acts professionally. B2B shortening is not automatically void. The post states the statute, notes the portal's consumer summary, and says this is not legal advice.
Not verifiable, therefore left out: a fixed number of days that "sofort" always means. Industry-standard complaint response times. Any success rate for automation. EU 14-day withdrawal (not Swiss law; ch.ch says online purchases follow the shop rule).
