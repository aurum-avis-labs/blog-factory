# temp-staff-timesheets
Primary query DE / EN: Einsatzrapporte von Temporärmitarbeitenden verarbeiten / process timesheets of temporary staff
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): SECO covers the employer's duty to record working time. Staffing-software pages sell an app. The gap is the office route in a Swiss Verleihbetrieb: many client sites, a client signature as a gate, then payroll and the client invoice from the same hours, with deviations to a person. Distinct from a trades Regierapport (daywork-report-to-invoice) and from monthly payroll preparation.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- SECO, Arbeitszeiterfassung: Article 46 of the Employment Act (ArG) requires employers to keep the records the enforcement bodies need. In particular, duration and the start and end of daily and weekly working time, including compensatory time and overtime, and breaks of half an hour or more, must be visible (Article 73 of Ordinance 1 to the Employment Act, ArGV 1). https://www.seco.admin.ch/de/arbeitszeiterfassung access 2026-09-30.
- Same page: since 1 January 2016, Articles 73a and 73b ArGV 1 allow agreed deviations from systematic recording, under defined conditions. In firms with fewer than 50 employees, simplified recording under Article 73b can also be agreed individually in writing. The page does not present that as the default. Access 2026-09-30.
- Same page: a weekly maximum of 45 hours applies to workers in industrial firms, office staff, technical and other employees, and large retail businesses. A weekly maximum of 50 hours applies to all other workers (Article 9 paragraph 1 ArG). Access 2026-09-30.
- AVG Article 19 paragraph 2: the contract with the hired-out worker must set, among other points, the type of work, place and start, duration or notice, working hours, wage, expenses and allowances, and social-insurance deductions. https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/392_392_392/de access 2026-09-30 (text stand 1 January 2026). Used only to say the timesheet has to feed a contract you already have, not as a template.
- AVG Article 20 paragraph 1: if the client company is covered by a universally binding collective employment contract, the hiring-out firm must apply that contract's wage and working-time provisions. Same URL, access 2026-09-30.
- AVG Article 22 paragraph 1: the contract with the client company must be in writing and must state, among other points, the worker's qualifications, the place and start, the duration or notice periods, and the working hours that apply. Same URL, access 2026-09-30. Not copied in full.
