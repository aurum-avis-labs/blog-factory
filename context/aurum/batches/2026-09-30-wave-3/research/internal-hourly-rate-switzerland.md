# internal-hourly-rate-switzerland
Primary query DE / EN: Stundensatz berechnen Schweiz (Arbeitgeberkosten) / calculate the internal hourly cost of an employee in Switzerland
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Treuhand and payroll sites publish 2026 contribution tables and a lump "Lohnnebenkosten" percentage. They rarely divide the employer total by productive hours, and they often mix statistical averages for pension, accident and family allowances into one rate. The gap is a sheet an SME can fill from its own invoices, with the national 2026 percentages separated from the lines that vary, so the hour can later sit in a calculator. The benefit post uses the hour. This post derives it.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- Employer AHV/IV/EO is 5.3% of the wage (AHV 4.35%, IV 0.7%, EO 0.25%; total 10.6%, half each). Compensation offices also levy an administrative contribution at the employer's expense, with no national rate in the leaflet. https://www.ahv-iv.ch/p/2.01.d (Stand 1. Januar 2026), accessed 2026-09-30.
- ALV is 2.2% (1.1% employer) up to CHF 148'200 per employment. Since 1 January 2023 no ALV is due on the part above that ceiling. The ceiling applies per employment. Leaflet status is 1 January 2025; it was still the current sheet on ahv-iv.ch on the access date, and leaflet 2.01 (status 1 January 2026) points to it. https://www.ahv-iv.ch/p/2.08.d, accessed 2026-09-30.
- BVG thresholds unchanged from 1 January 2026: Mindestjahreslohn CHF 22'680, minimaler koordinierter Lohn CHF 3'780, Koordinationsabzug CHF 26'460, obere Limite des Jahreslohns CHF 90'720. The PDF states no change of these amounts versus 1 January 2025 despite the 13th AHV pension. https://www.bsv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/sAgdISSXenMT/d_Beträge%202026.pdf (dated 06.11.2025), accessed 2026-09-30.
- Compulsory BVG when the annual wage with the same employer is more than CHF 22'680; risk cover after the 17th year of age, old-age savings from the 25th. Obligatory old-age credits of the coordinated wage: 7% (25–34), 10% (35–44), 15% (45–54), 18% (55–65). Employer pays at least half of the contributions. Funds may set other credits if mandatory benefits remain. https://www.bsv.admin.ch/de/altersvorsorge (published 17 July 2025; threshold amount matches the 2026 PDF), accessed 2026-09-30.
- BSV states that current-year rates cannot be given for occupational pension, family allowances, and occupational and non-occupational accident insurance; only prior-year statistics exist. Employer owes BUV premiums; the employee pays NBUV. Family-allowance contributions depend on the canton of the employer's seat and on the fund. https://www.bsv.admin.ch/de/beitraege-im-ueberblick (published 17 July 2025), accessed 2026-09-30.
- Minimum holiday is 4 weeks per service year, 5 weeks until the 20th birthday. Contract or collective agreement can give more. https://www.seco.admin.ch/de/faq-ferien (Art. 329a OR), accessed 2026-09-30. Same minimum: https://www.kmu.admin.ch/de/gutes-management-der-ferienplanung, accessed 2026-09-30.
