# measure-automation-success
Primary query DE / EN: Erfolg einer Automatisierung messen / measure whether an automation works
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Tool vendors count logins and "time saved" estimates. The adesso Switzerland 2026 report says usage rate is almost as common a KPI as process efficiency, and then says usage does not show effect. The benefit post is the sheet before you buy. This post is the same kinds of counts after go-live, against a baseline.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- adesso GenAI Impact Report Switzerland 2026, company chapter: 47% of Swiss companies measure GenAI success by process efficiency, 45% by usage rate, 40% by usage frequency, then cost 32%, customer satisfaction 28%, revenue growth 27%, fewer complaints 18%, and 5% do not measure. adesso's own line: the most-used KPIs show whether employees use GenAI, not whether the technology works. The shares sum above 100%, so respondents could name more than one measure. Method line in the same PDF: HEUTE UND MORGEN surveyed 100 Swiss executives and 750 end customers in February 2026; the KPI paragraph sits in the company section (page marked Unternehmen), not the customer chapter. https://www.adesso.ch/adesso-ch/whitepaper/genai-impact-report-2026-ch.pdf accessed 2026-09-30.
Vendor / product features used (URL each): none. No Aurum result metrics exist (WRITER-BRIEF section 8).
