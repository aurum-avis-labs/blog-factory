# ai-for-insurance-brokers
Primary query DE / EN: KI für Versicherungsbroker / AI for Swiss insurance brokers
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Results are either FINMA registration guides or software adverts for comparison tools. They do not tell a small Swiss broker which repetitive office jobs sit beside the advice (multi-insurer quote requests, a comparison draft, renewal chasing, a claims file) and which duties FINMA says stay with a person and on the record. This post is that split. It is not regulatory advice. The quote-request method itself is a later article.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- FINMA: to act as an untied intermediary you must be entered in FINMA's public register. Tied intermediaries are generally no longer carried in that register. Information must be given to customers before the contract. Untied intermediaries must in particular disclose remuneration from insurers or other third parties. Untied intermediaries report key figures to FINMA each year. https://www.finma.ch/de/bewilligung/versicherungsvermittlung/ accessed 2026-09-30.
- FINMA duties: information in an understandable form before the contract (Art. 45 VAG), for natural and legal persons. Untied intermediaries inform customers of all types of remuneration received from insurers or third parties in connection with the service (Art. 45b VAG). Organisational measures so conflicts are avoided or, if not, disclosed before the contract (Art. 45a VAG), stated for legal persons, sole proprietorships and partnerships. A person may not be tied and untied at the same time (Art. 44 para. 1 let. b VAG). Staff who intermediate under Art. 40 VAG must themselves be in the register; unregistered activity is unauthorised (Art. 41 para. 1 VAG). Internal rules and an appropriate organisation must ensure VAG duties are met (Art. 188 para. 1 AVO). Qualified life insurance has further duties (Art. 39h, 39j to 39k VAG). https://www.finma.ch/de/ueberwachung/versicherungsvermittler/pflichten-fuer-versicherungsvermittlerinnen-und--vermittler/ accessed 2026-09-30.
- The same duties page: untied intermediaries report a cyber incident of material importance within 24 hours as advance notice to vermittler.cyber@finma.ch, and the actual report within 72 hours via the EHP, referring to FINMA supervisory notices 05/2020 and 03/2024. Used only as a limit: a workflow must not hide that notice.
