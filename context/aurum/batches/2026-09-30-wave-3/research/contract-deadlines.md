SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): German-language results on 2026-09-30 are mostly software comparisons (Yupana, top.legal, drweb.de, finban, trusted.de). They list contract-lifecycle products, OCR extraction and reminder features, often under German consumer law (BGB § 309 and § 312k). That law does not govern Swiss B2B contracts. Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- OR Art. 266: a fixed-term lease ends without notice when the agreed duration ends. If the parties continue it tacitly, it becomes an open-ended lease. https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/27/317_321_377/de accessed 2026-09-30 (consolidated text checked in the Fedlex PDF extract of SR 220).
- OR Art. 266a para. 1: an open-ended lease can be ended with the statutory notice period and dates, unless the parties agreed a longer period or a different date. https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/27/317_321_377/de accessed 2026-09-30.
- OR Art. 266d: lease of business premises (Geschäftsräume): the parties may terminate with six months' notice on a locally customary date or, if there is no local custom, at the end of a three-month rental period. https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/27/317_321_377/de accessed 2026-09-30.
- OR Art. 266c: lease of a dwelling: three months' notice on a locally customary date or, if none, at the end of a three-month rental period. https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/27/317_321_377/de accessed 2026-09-30.
- OR Art. 266b covers immovable property and movable structures more generally (three months). 266c and 266d are the specific rules for dwellings and business premises. Same URL and access date.
- SharePoint or Microsoft Lists: any list or library with a date column can be shown as a calendar view, with start and end date columns. https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/lists/create-a-calendar-view-from-a-list accessed 2026-09-30.
