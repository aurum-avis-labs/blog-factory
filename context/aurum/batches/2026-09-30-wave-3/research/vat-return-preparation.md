# vat-return-preparation
Primary query DE / EN: MWST-Abrechnung vorbereiten / prepare the Swiss VAT return (checklist)
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): ESTV pages explain the portal, the 60-day duty and how a fiduciary gets access. Software blogs explain posting VAT in the ledger. The gap is the SME checklist before the fiduciary or the portal: receipts in, reconciliations, input-tax questions, and which of those a workflow can line up. The firm still submits, or explicitly authorises the submitter. Not tax advice. Not the fiduciary's internal production of the return.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- The return must be filed unsolicited within 60 days after the settlement period. Extensions are free and online only, in the ESTV portal service for VAT filing, as part of filing under Art. 123 MWSTV. Requests outside that service are not granted. Firms on MWST-Abrechnung pro extend from the overview. If errors are found while preparing the annual accounts, they are corrected with a Berichtigungsabrechnung under Art. 72 para. 1 MWSTG, at the latest in the return for the period that contains the 180th day after the end of that financial year. Page updated 22 September 2026. Access 2026-09-30. https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/mwst-fristverlaengerung-einreichung-abrechnung
- Filing and payment are both due within 60 days after the period (Art. 71 para. 1 and Art. 86 para. 1 MWSTG). The same page's refund example calls the first quarter due on 31 May. VAT is a self-declaration: no invoice is sent, and payment is unsolicited. Late-payment interest runs under Art. 87 para. 1 MWSTG from the due date; the rate is set in the EFD ordinance SR 631.014; interest is in principle only charged or paid from CHF 100. Page updated 22 September 2026. Access 2026-09-30. https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/mwst-bezahlen
- Since 1 January 2025, VAT-registered firms file online via the ePortal. MWST-Abrechnung easy with a filing code worked until 8 May 2026. From 11 May 2026 only MWST-Abrechnung pro in the ESTV portal accepts returns. Login is AGOV via the myESTV service. A program can export the period as an eCH-0217 XML file and that file can be imported into MWST-Abrechnung pro; a person then checks and completes it. The firm can give staff the roles Ausfüller or Einreicher and invite a fiduciary; ESTV recommends the firm itself also keeps access. Access 2026-09-30. https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/mwst-online-abrechnen
