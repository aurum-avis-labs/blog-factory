# service-appointment-requests-garage
Primary query DE / EN: Serviceanfragen in der Garage automatisieren / handle service appointment requests in a garage
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Pages describe online booking widgets or workshop calendars. They rarely say what a small Swiss garage does when the tyre season hits and most requests are still a phone call or an email: which fields to capture, how a slot is proposed from the ramp calendar, when a courtesy car is actually free, and the confirmation the day before. This post is that sequence. The wider garage office (quotes, parts, MFK, invoices) stays in the overview.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- WERBAS//KSR NET: customer appointments and service and wheel scheduling in the workshop calendar; appointments can be entered before a job exists and later converted; courtesy-car availability and a prefilled agreement. https://werbas-ksr.com/net-planung/ accessed 2026-09-30.
- Aurum's own paid-booking flow is an internal case (booking-with-payment-case). Not retold. No garage metrics.
Vendor / product features used (URL each): WERBAS//KSR planning module, as an example of software the reader may already have. Aurum has not built a garage-software integration.
Not verifiable, therefore left out: how many tyre changes a "typical" Swiss garage does in March or October; whether customers pay to hold a service slot; SMS reminder features of unnamed DMS products.
