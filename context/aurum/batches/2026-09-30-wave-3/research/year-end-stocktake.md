# year-end-stocktake
Primary query DE / EN: Inventur vorbereiten / prepare the year-end stocktake
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Generic accounting explainers treat Inventur as a full accounting lecture (valuation methods, year-end close). The gap is the office checklist for a small firm: count list from the system already in use, one count, quantity differences, handover of the stock list. Valuation stays with the fiduciary.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- Companies that keep accounts under Art. 957 ff. OR must prove each balance-sheet and notes position by an Inventar or another method (Art. 958c para. 2 OR). KMU portal states this explicitly and names legal persons plus sole traders and partnerships with turnover of at least CHF 500'000 in the last year; below that, simplified records of income, expense and assets. Access 2026-09-30. https://www.kmu.admin.ch/de/buchhaltungspflicht-fuer-handelsgesellschaften
- Same wording of Art. 958c para. 2, stand 1 January 2026, on Lawbrary (mirror of SR 220). Fedlex HTML for SR 220 did not render without JavaScript on 2026-09-30; the 2012 amending act on Fedlex contains the same sentence. Access 2026-09-30. https://lawbrary.ch/law/art/OR-v2026.01-de-art-958c/ and https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/filestore/fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/oc/2012/810/de/pdf-a/fedlex-data-admin-ch-eli-oc-2012-810-de-pdf-a.pdf and https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/27/317_321_377/de
- Art. 960c OR: if, at the balance-sheet date, the realisable value of inventories and unbilled services, after costs still to be incurred, is below acquisition or production cost, that lower value must be used. Inventories are raw materials, work in progress, finished goods and merchandise. Confirmed in the 2012 Fedlex act and in the Federal Office for Housing compilation of the accounting rules. Access 2026-09-30. https://www.bwo.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/69aZDWF8Hptm/gesetzliche_bestimmungenzurkaufmaennischenbuchfuehrung.pdf
- Vorräte sit in current assets on the KMU sample balance sheet; the most common balance-sheet date named there is 31 December, and the sheet is a snapshot. Access 2026-09-30. https://www.kmu.admin.ch/de/musterbilanz-einer-firma-bilanz-aufbauen-und-lesen
