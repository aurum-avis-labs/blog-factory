# dunning-payment-reminders
Primary query DE / EN: Mahnwesen automatisieren / automate payment reminders (dunning) in a Swiss SME
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Vendor pages (bexio, Abacus) explain their own dunning levels. Legal pages explain Betreibung. The gap is the office decision: when the program's levels are enough, and what still sits around them (a note before the due date, key accounts, disputed invoices, a call list). Betreibung stays a separate legal step.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- bexio can send dunning emails from defined levels. Under Einstellungen, Funktionen & Module, Mahnstufen you set when a level is emailed. The first automatic email goes out on the due date plus the days set for the payment reminder. A level's payment term is the gap until the next level. Automation can also be set on a single invoice in the Mahnungen tab. Access 2026-09-30. https://help.bexio.com/s/article/000001705?language=de
- bexio Mahnlauf (Verkauf, Weitere, Mahnläufe) lists invoices that have passed the current payment term and lets you move selected ones to the next level, then preview, email or export them. Access 2026-09-30. https://help.bexio.com/s/article/000001718?language=de
- Abacus debtor dunning: statuses offen, versendet and nicht mahnen; a full run or single documents; collective reminders inside a run; release via freigegeben / nicht freigegeben; email sending; Business Process Engine can automate the sending so it does not have to be done by hand. Access 2026-09-30. https://www.abacus.ch/mahnwesen
- Abacus debtor accounting overview also lists multi-level dunning, release, payment agreements and partial payments. Access 2026-09-30. https://www.abacus.ch/produkte/finanzen/debitorenbuchhaltung/uebersicht
- Monetary claims are enforced by Betreibung under the SchKG. Access 2026-09-30. https://www.bj.admin.ch/de/schuldbetreibung-und-konkurs
- The creditor's Betreibungsbegehren form is optional. A request can be filled at betreibungsschalter.ch and filed with the competent Betreibungsamt, which that site also finds. Access 2026-09-30. https://www.bj.admin.ch/de/musterformulare-schkg
Vendor / product features used (URL each): bexio help 000001705 and 000001718; Abacus mahnwesen page and debtor overview, as above. Aurum has not built these integrations. The post names them as the reader's systems.
