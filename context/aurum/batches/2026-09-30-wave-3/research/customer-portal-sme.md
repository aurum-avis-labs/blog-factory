# customer-portal-sme
Primary query DE / EN: Kundenportal KMU / customer portal for an SME
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Product pages sell a portal as a given. The EDÖB explains the right of access, which is not the same thing as a login. A separate cluster post already walks through answering an access request. This page is the decision: repeated status questions and clean data, versus email, and the support you take on.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- Under the Data Protection Act, any person may ask the controller whether personal data about them are processed, and if needed require deletion or correction. The right lets the person check the data held about them. The person has to exercise it themselves. The EDÖB lists what an answer includes (controller identity and contact, the data, the purpose, retention or the criteria, origin where relevant, automated individual decisions, recipients). https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/de/auskunftsrecht accessed 2026-09-30. The same right, without having to show an interest, is on https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/de/meine-rechte-kennen-und-durchsetzen accessed 2026-09-30.
- The how-to for answering a request, including the 30-day rule taken from fedlex, is already the post data-subject-access-requests (seq 81). This article does not restate that procedure.
