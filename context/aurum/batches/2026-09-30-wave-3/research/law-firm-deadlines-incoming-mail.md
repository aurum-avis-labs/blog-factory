# law-firm-deadlines-incoming-mail
Primary query DE / EN: Fristen und Posteingang in der Kanzlei / deadlines and incoming mail in a law firm
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Deadline software pages sell a register. They rarely describe the office sequence in a small Swiss firm: court or authority mail assigned to the file, a date proposed with the page attached, a lawyer confirming before it is entered, reminders the firm itself defines. This post is that sequence. It does not state how a procedural time limit is calculated.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- BGFA Art. 12 let. a: lawyers practise carefully and conscientiously. https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2002/153/de#art_12 accessed 2026-09-30. Used only as the reason a person confirms a date, not as a theory of time limits.
- BGFA Art. 13 para. 2: lawyers ensure assistants keep professional secrecy. https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2002/153/de#art_13 accessed 2026-09-30. Used only to say the secretariat's workflow still sits inside secrecy. The fuller secrecy frame is the law-firm overview, which this post links.
Vendor / product features used (URL each): none. No practice-management product named.
Not verifiable, therefore left out: ZPO or cantonal day-counts, court-vacation rules, whether a missed deadline perishes a remedy, any claim that double control is legally required. Those stay with the lawyer.
