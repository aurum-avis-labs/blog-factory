# ai-for-law-firms
Primary query DE / EN: KI in der Anwaltskanzlei / AI for Swiss law firms: what professional secrecy allows
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Commentary either celebrates generative AI for research and drafting, or warns in general terms about secrecy. Few pages tell a small Swiss firm which office jobs (intake, filing, a deadline proposal, a draft bill) can move, and which setup the Swiss Bar Association actually describes before client material is used. This post is that split. It is practical orientation, not legal advice, and it does not calculate court deadlines.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- BGFA Art. 13: lawyers are bound without time limit and towards everyone by professional secrecy for everything clients entrusted to them in the course of the profession. Release from secrecy does not oblige them to disclose what was entrusted. They must ensure assistants keep the secrecy. https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2002/153/de#art_13 accessed 2026-09-30.
- BGFA Art. 12 let. a: lawyers practise carefully and conscientiously (sorgfältig und gewissenhaft). https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2002/153/de#art_12 accessed 2026-09-30.
- StGB Art. 321, as quoted in the Federal Gazette draft BBl 2025 2004 (which reprints the offence): lawyers, defence counsel and their assistants, among other professions, who reveal a secret entrusted in the course of the profession or perceived while practising it, are punishable on complaint with a custodial sentence of up to three years or a monetary penalty. https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/fga/2025/2004/de accessed 2026-09-30. Consolidated code: https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/54/757_781_799/de#art_321 accessed 2026-09-30. The consolidated HTML did not render article text in a non-JavaScript fetch; the wording used is the reprint in BBl 2025 2004, flagged as a reprint of Art. 321, not as a new offence.
- SAV news: the board adopted a guide on AI on 14 June 2024. https://www.sav-fsa.ch/news/-/asset_publisher/97aPCN6lT9LD/content/id/3812477 accessed 2026-09-30.
- SAV guide (PDF on sav-fsa.ch): before using any software, including AI, clarify who can access input data and where it is stored; professional secrecy, the DSG, and for SAV members the professional rules apply. Routes described: on-premise with no data leaving the firm's network; compliance with the SAV outsourcing and cloud guide; or informed client consent and waiver. Otherwise confidential information must not be entered. AI output can be wrong or incomplete and must be checked. Firms should issue an internal instruction. https://www.sav-fsa.ch/documents/672183/2025869/SAV_KI+Guidelines+2024.pdf/68d5eab4-c2ad-50bd-506f-7ba6af15a839 accessed 2026-09-30. Cloud guide referenced by the SAV, not separately summarised here: https://digital.sav-fsa.ch/digitale-kanzlei-nutzung-von-clouddiensten
