# analyse-survey-free-text
Primary query DE / EN: Freitext-Antworten mit KI auswerten / analyse free-text survey answers with AI
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): On 2026-09-30 German results are vendor or consultant pages (evasys, KI-Syndikat, New Work Institut, Safe Mind, AZ Datenschutz). They discuss GDPR, anonymity thresholds such as 5, 7 or 12 people, and sentiment tools. Those group sizes are not a figure in the Swiss DSG. The gap is a practical Swiss office method: strip identifiers, cluster themes, a person checks a sample, employee surveys and customer comments kept apart, no legal advice.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- DSG Art. 5: personal data are any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person. Sensitive data include religious, philosophical, political or trade-union views, health, intimate sphere, racial or ethnic origin, genetic and certain biometric data, and criminal or administrative proceedings. https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de accessed 2026-09-30 (text checked in the Fedlex PDF of SR 235.1, version 2025-07-07).
- DSG Art. 6 para. 2: processing must be in good faith and proportionate. Para. 3: collection for a specific purpose the person can recognise, and further use must be compatible. Para. 4: data are destroyed or anonymised once no longer required for the purpose. Same URL and date.
- EDÖB: the FADP is technology-neutral and applies directly to AI processing. Controllers must make purpose, functioning and data sources transparent. https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/de/update-geltendes-datenschutzgesetz-ist-auf-ki-direkt-anwendbar accessed 2026-09-30.
- EDÖB on employment: only data about suitability for the job or necessary to perform the contract may be processed (OR Art. 328b). Consent from an employee in a subordinate position is rarely free. https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/de/datenbearbeitung-durch-den-arbeitgeber accessed 2026-09-30.
- EDÖB, AI in hiring section of the same page: the frame is the same as a conventional hiring process; recognisability and proportionality are central when AI creates profiles. Same URL and date. Used only as a caution that AI does not create a lighter regime, not as a survey rule.
