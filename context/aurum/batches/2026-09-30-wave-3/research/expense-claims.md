# expense-claims
Primary query DE / EN: Spesenabrechnung automatisieren / automate employee expense claims (Switzerland)
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Vendor pages sell receipt-scanning apps. The ESTV guide explains the salary certificate. The gap is the office flow: photo, card line, manager approval, one rate, a closed batch for payroll or the books, and what the certificate still has to show. Not supplier-invoice capture.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- ESTV Wegleitung 2026 (Formular 11): all effective expense reimbursements must be declared, including amounts paid with a company credit card. Declaration can be omitted only if every listed condition is met, including: overnight costs against a receipt; effective meal reimbursement generally at most CHF 35, or a flat main meal of at most CHF 30; client invitations against the original receipt; public transport against a receipt; private car for business at most 75 rappen per kilometre; minor expenses against a receipt or a daily flat of at most CHF 20. Scaling those per-case flats up to working days is not allowed. Actual travel is required. If all of that holds, a cross in field 13.1.1 is enough and the amount need not be shown. Access 2026-09-30. https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/afP1GDFr8gE3/dbst-form-lohna-wegleitung-2026-de.pdf
- If those conditions are not met, or there is no approved expense regulation, effective travel, meal and overnight expenses reimbursed against receipts must be stated as amounts. Access 2026-09-30. Same PDF, margin number 53 area.
- Employers who pay more than the effective pattern in margin number 52 can ask the tax authority of the canton of the seat to approve an expense regulation, preferably built on the Swiss Tax Conference templates. Approval covers both effective and flat amounts. With an approved regulation, only flat expenses (field 13.2) are shown as amounts. Other cantons recognise a seat-canton approval if the templates of 1 February 2024, in force from 1 May 2024, are followed. Field 15 carries the remark that the regulation was approved by canton XY on a date. In that case field 13.1.1 is not crossed. Access 2026-09-30. Same PDF. Index of the templates: https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/lohnausweis-rentenbescheinigung
- Flat reimbursements must roughly match actual outlay, including when a regulation exists, and the franc amount of flats still goes on field 13.2. Access 2026-09-30. Same PDF.
