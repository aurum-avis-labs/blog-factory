# ebill-for-businesses
Primary query DE / EN: eBill für Unternehmen / eBill for Swiss businesses (receiving and sending)
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): SIX explains registration, banks and network partners. The gap for an office is what changes compared with a PDF invoice in the mailbox, which approval remains, and that eBill does not replace a match of order and delivery note. No accounting-vendor support matrix, because none was verified.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- eBill is a SIX service. SIX runs the infrastructure for the Swiss banks. Access 2026-09-30. https://www.ebill.ch/de/home/business/faq.html
- Sending requires a network partner. Pricing is set by that partner. eBill users pay no extra transaction fees. Use is free for private end customers. Receiving invoices via eBill in online banking is free. Access 2026-09-30. Same FAQ.
- Difference from an email invoice, in SIX's words: eBill arrives in online banking, where it can be checked and released for payment. An email invoice arrives in the mailbox and the document or its details must be transferred into online banking. Access 2026-09-30. Same FAQ.
- Business documents that can be sent: single and instalment invoices, donation requests, reminders, notifications (Avisierungen) and credit notes. An Avisierung can be an invoice without a pay button, or a credit-note notice. Access 2026-09-30. Same FAQ.
- A business can receive in online banking or, without a media break, in its invoicing software. Several employees can be authorised to view, release or reject invoices for the firm in online banking. The integrated path delivers the data and the PDF, and booking and payment follow the firm's existing processes. Not every bank offers business receipt. Ask the bank. Access 2026-09-30. FAQ and https://www.ebill.ch/de/home/business/receive-invoices.html
- Functions when receiving: authorised staff can view, check, release for payment on the chosen account or reject, register further billers, and adjust settings such as standing approval (Dauerfreigabe). Access 2026-09-30. FAQ.
- Multi-bank receipt needs the same UID at each bank. Without a UID the bank can still set the firm up, with an identifier of the bank, but without multi-banking and without automatically adding billers. Access 2026-09-30. FAQ.
- Invoices the customers have already released for payment stay visible in the platform archive for 730 days after the due date, then are deleted. The sentence is written from the biller's side ("Ihre Kundschaft"). It is not used in the post as a retention rule for the receiver. Access 2026-09-30. FAQ.
- eBill Direct Debit was introduced by SIX in June 2025. Standing approval (Dauerfreigabe) can be a maximum amount or an exact amount. The direct-debit mandate is not limited by amount. Access 2026-09-30. FAQ section eBill Direct Debit.
Vendor / product features used (URL each): ebill.ch business FAQ and the receive page, as above. No accounting-software vendor is named. Aurum has not built an eBill connection.
