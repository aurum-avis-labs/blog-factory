# maintenance-service-reminders
Primary query DE / EN: Wartungstermine automatisch planen / schedule recurring maintenance and service appointments
SERP: service-software vendors and heating-contractor blogs describe their own calendars. The gap is the office job around a contract register: next due date, a reminder early enough to agree a visit, a proposal the customer confirms, then the report and the invoice. Daily technician routing is a different article.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- VKF Brandschutznorm 1-15, Art. 20 Unterhaltspflicht: owners and users of buildings and installations are responsible for keeping structural, technical and defensive fire-protection installations, and building-services systems, maintained as intended and ready for operation at all times. PDF: https://services2.vkf.ch/rest/public/georg/bs/publikation/documents/BSPUB-1394520214-57.pdf/content accessed 2026-09-30. The same reading did not state one national interval for every heating contract. Intervals in the post are therefore left to the contract and the manufacturer instruction.
Not verifiable, therefore left out: a legal duty to service a heating system once a year. Cantonal adoption details of the VKF standard beyond the wording of Art. 20. Microsoft Bookings feature claims. Any show-up rate or time saved.
