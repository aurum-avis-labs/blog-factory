# ai-in-hospitality
Primary query DE / EN: KI in der Gastronomie und Hotellerie: die Büroarbeit / AI in restaurants and hotels: the office work
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Vendor and magazine pages talk about chatbots, menu texts, dynamic pricing and "AI in gastronomy" as a technology trend. They rarely separate what a till, a reservation book or a hotel system already does from the office jobs that still sit in mail and on paper: supplier orders, group offers, stray reservations, supplier invoices, hours. This post fills that gap for a Swiss restaurant or small hotel and points to the KitchenCrew case instead of retelling it.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- Lightspeed describes restaurant inventory tools that deduct stock in real time as menu items are sold, show stock on hand, send low-stock alerts and automate recurring supplier orders. https://www.lightspeedhq.com/pos/restaurant/inventory/ accessed 2026-09-30.
- resmio states that guests can check table availability in real time and book online, including on the restaurant website and on Google, and that reservations land in the reservation software. https://www.resmio.com/reservierungen/ accessed 2026-09-30.
- resmio states that its graphical table plan shows which tables are free, reserved or occupied. https://www.resmio.com/planung/tischplan/ accessed 2026-09-30.
- Mews states that its channel manager, inside the property system, connects to 400+ channels including Booking.com, Expedia and Airbnb for Hotels, with real-time two-way sync of rates and availability so one room is not sold twice. https://www.mews.com/en/products/mews-channel-manager accessed 2026-09-30.
Vendor / product features used (URL each): Lightspeed Restaurant inventory (lightspeedhq.com), resmio reservations and table plan (resmio.com), Mews channel manager (mews.com). Named as examples of what the reader's systems already do. Aurum has not built integrations to them.
Not verifiable, therefore left out: adoption rates of any POS or PMS in Switzerland; vendor marketing figures (resmio "up to 30% more bookings", Mews time-saved and booking-uplift numbers); Gastro-GAV working-time rules; whether a named Swiss hotel product has an API; any claim that Aurum connects to a till or PMS.
