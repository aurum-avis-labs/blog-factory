# ai-for-recruitment-agencies
Primary query DE / EN: KI in der Personalvermittlung / AI for recruitment and staffing agencies
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Official pages cover the licence (AVG), not the office work. Vendor and magazine pages sell matching tools or chat prompts. Gap: which recurring jobs in a small Swiss Vermittlungs- or Verleihbüro sit beside the software (dossier, Kurzprofil, shortlist, timesheet to invoice), and which duties the Act already puts on that data. In-house screening is a different post (screen-job-applications-ai, seq 74).
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- AVG Art. 2 para. 1: whoever regularly and for a fee brings jobseekers and employers together in Switzerland to conclude employment contracts needs a cantonal operating licence. Cross-border placement also needs a SECO licence (Art. 2 para. 3). A branch in another canton needs its own licence (Art. 2 para. 5). https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/392_392_392/de access 2026-09-30. Text stand 1 January 2026.
- AVG Art. 4 para. 1: the placement licence is unlimited in time and covers the whole of Switzerland. Same URL, access 2026-09-30.
- AVG Art. 7 para. 2: the licensing authority may require anonymised statistical information. Art. 7 para. 3: a placement firm may process data on jobseekers and vacancies only as far and as long as required for the placement, and must keep that data confidential. Same URL, access 2026-09-30. This anonymisation duty is toward the authority's statistics, not a rule that client profiles must be nameless.
- AVG Art. 12 para. 1: employers who commercially lend workers to client companies (Einsatzbetriebe) need a cantonal operating licence. Art. 12 para. 2: hiring-out abroad also needs a SECO licence; hiring-out from abroad into Switzerland is not permitted. Same URL, access 2026-09-30.
- AVG Art. 18 para. 1: a hiring-out firm must name itself and its address in public job ads and must state clearly that the worker is employed for hiring-out. Art. 18 para. 3: employee data may be processed and passed to client companies only as far and as long as required for the assignment; anything beyond that needs the employee's express consent. Same URL, access 2026-09-30.
- AVG Art. 20 para. 1: if the client company is covered by a universally binding collective employment contract, the hiring-out firm must apply that contract's wage and working-time provisions. Same URL, access 2026-09-30. Mentioned only as a limit; the timesheet steps are the later post.
- SECO: the Act aims to protect jobseekers and hired-out workers; placement and hiring-out inside Switzerland need a cantonal licence from the canton of the registered office; cross-border activity also needs a federal licence from SECO. The joint register (VZAVG) currently lists more than 7,400 businesses. Annual statistics can be filed via EasyGov since January 2026; the licence application itself is not yet fully on EasyGov. https://www.seco.admin.ch/de/private-arbeitsvermittlung-und-personalverleih access 2026-09-30.
- KMU portal: same licence split, but it says "more than 6,000" companies and dates its sources to arbeit.swiss (April 2018) and SECO (May 2018). https://www.kmu.admin.ch/de/private-arbeitsvermittlung-und-personalverleih-die-noetigen-schritte access 2026-09-30. Contradicts the current SECO figure. Post uses the SECO figure ("über 7400"), not 6,000.
