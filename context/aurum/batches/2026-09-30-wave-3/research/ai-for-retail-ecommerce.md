# ai-for-retail-ecommerce
Primary query DE / EN: KI im Onlinehandel und Detailhandel / AI for small retailers and online shops
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Shop-system help pages describe notifications, checkout language and resending an order email. Marketing pages sell captions and product slogans. The gap is the office beside a small Swiss shop or webshop: questions the status email does not answer, returns under the shop's own rules, product facts in a second language, supplier orders, and matching a payout to many orders. The returns how-to is a later post. No marketing-copy advice.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- ch.ch, "Returning or exchanging goods": in Switzerland there is no legal right to exchange or return goods; each shop sets its own rules; some shops voluntarily allow a change of mind within 14 days or more. The same rules apply to online trading as to a purchase in a shop. Door-to-door sales and telephone contracts can be cancelled within 14 days if the goods are worth more than CHF 100. German-language URLs tried on ch.ch returned 404 on this date; the English page is the one that loaded. https://www.ch.ch/en/safety-and-justice/returning-or-exchanging-goods access 2026-09-30. Used as one limit, not as the returns procedure.
- Shopify Help, Localization and translation: if a translation is missing, the store shows the primary language. After a language is added and published, checkout and email notifications display the translated content; checkout uses the language the customer browsed in. If a notification translation exists, the customer is sent notifications in the language of the order. Translations can be added with Shopify's Translate & Adapt app, a CSV, or a compatible app. https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/localization-and-translation access 2026-09-30.
- Shopify Help, order details: from an order you can contact the customer, or resend the order confirmation to the customer or to yourself from the timeline. https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/managing-order-details access 2026-09-30.
- Shopify Help, notification variables: in customer-facing notifications, the product name and variant name are translated to the language the customer checked out in, when that language differs from the default store language. https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/orders/notifications/email-variables access 2026-09-30.
