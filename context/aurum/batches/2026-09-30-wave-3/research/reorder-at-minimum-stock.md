# reorder-at-minimum-stock
Primary query DE / EN: Nachbestellung bei Mindestbestand automatisieren / automatic reordering at minimum stock
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Results on 2026-09-30 are product docs (Odoo 17 reordering rules, repleno, Mauve). They explain min/max inside one product. They do not cover the common SME gap: stock that still lives in Excel, consignment, or a service van, nor a human release of one draft per supplier. Gap: when to use the rule already in the reader's system, and when the stock figure is not trustworthy enough to order from.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- kmu.admin.ch, Kennzahlen zur Unternehmensüberwachung: a shorter average time in stock means less capital tied up, and delivery readiness still has to stay high. That needs inventory accounting that shows stock turnover and average time in stock. https://www.kmu.admin.ch/de/kennzahlen-zur-unternehmensueberwachung accessed 2026-09-30. The page also states liquidity ratios and a debtor-turnover band. Those were not used in the post.
- Odoo 17 documentation, Nachbestellregeln konfigurieren: a reorder rule can create a purchase order when quantity on hand falls below a minimum, refilling toward a maximum. The page's own illustration: minimum 5, maximum 25, stock falls to 4, purchase order for 21 units. A multiple quantity can push the order above the maximum. https://www.odoo.com/documentation/17.0/de/applications/inventory_and_mrp/purchase/products/reordering.html accessed 2026-09-30.
- Odoo 17 documentation, Nachbestellregeln: an automatic trigger creates a purchase order or a manufacturing order when the planner runs and forecast quantity is below the minimum, or when a sales order confirmation drops forecast quantity below the minimum. A manual trigger lists the product on the replenishment dashboard instead of creating the order. https://www.odoo.com/documentation/17.0/de/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/warehouses_storage/replenishment/reordering_rules.html accessed 2026-09-30.
Vendor / product features used (URL each): Odoo 17 reorder rules, cited as that product's documentation, not as a feature of the reader's system and not as an integration Aurum has built.
Not verifiable, therefore left out: Repleno and Mauve settings (vendor handbooks, not needed). Proffix, bexio and other Swiss ERP reorder claims. Any savings figure. KitchenCrew time or cost figures. GS1 consignment message codes, which describe retail EDI consignment, not a general stock rule.
