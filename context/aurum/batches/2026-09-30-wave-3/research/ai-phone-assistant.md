# ai-phone-assistant
Primary query DE / EN: KI-Telefonassistent für KMU / AI phone assistant for a small business
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Vendor pages sell a voicebot that answers the line. The official Swiss material that decides the design is the EDÖB page on recording conversations (StGB Art. 179bis, 179ter and the narrow 179quinquies exception) and the EDÖB note that people must be told when they are talking to a machine. The gap is a comparison for a small office: notes after a person has answered, versus a voicebot that answers. No vendor ranking and no per-minute price.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- EDÖB: recording a non-public conversation without the consent of all participants, whether or not one is a party to the call, can be an offence under Art. 179bis and Art. 179ter SCC. Consent has to be clear about the recording and its purpose, and it has to be obtained before the recording so the person can object. https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/de/aufzeichnung-von-gespraechen accessed 2026-09-30.
- Same page: the Criminal Code treats two telephone situations as not punishable: calls with emergency, rescue and security services (Art. 179quinquies para. 1 let. a), and business calls whose content is orders, assignments, reservations and similar transactions (let. b). The EDÖB reads let. b narrowly, as mass business under time pressure, such as an order line or a reservation line. A complaint, or a longer contract negotiation, requires prior information. Recordings under the exception may be used only as evidence. Using them for marketing, training or monitoring how staff sell requires prior information, as does any evaluation beyond proof. Passing such a recording to a third party remains punishable.
- EDÖB on AI: where an intelligent language model communicates directly with a person, that person has a statutory right to know whether they are speaking or corresponding with a machine, and whether what they enter is used to improve the model or for further purposes. https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/de/update-geltendes-datenschutzgesetz-ist-auf-ki-direkt-anwendbar accessed 2026-09-30.
- DSG Art. 19: at collection, the person is informed at least of the controller's identity and contact details and of the purpose. https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de accessed 2026-09-30.
- The FADP applies alongside the Criminal Code when a recording processes personal data, including the right of access. Same EDÖB recording page.
