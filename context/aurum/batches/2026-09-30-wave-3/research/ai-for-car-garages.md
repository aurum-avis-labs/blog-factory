# ai-for-car-garages
Primary query DE / EN: KI in der Garage: Büroarbeit im Autogewerbe / AI for car garages: the office side
SERP (top results seen, what they cover, the gap we fill): Results are mostly workshop-software product pages and general "KI im Autohaus" pieces about diagnostics or marketing. They do not separate what a DMS already does (job, calendar, courtesy car, invoice) from the office work that still arrives by phone and mail. This post is that separation for a Swiss independent garage, including the MFK as a customer reminder, not as the official summons.
Verified facts (one per line, each with URL and access date):
- WERBAS//KSR NET planning: workshop calendar for customer appointments and service and wheel scheduling; appointments can be planned without an active job and later turned into job-related entries; courtesy-car overview and a prefilled courtesy-car agreement. https://werbas-ksr.com/net-planung/ accessed 2026-09-30.
- WERBAS//KSR NET order processing: quotes (Kostenvoranschläge), jobs, delivery notes and invoices; splitting an invoice between customer and insurer for accident, warranty or goodwill cases. https://werbas-ksr.com/net-auftragsabwicklung/ accessed 2026-09-30.
- Canton of Lucerne road traffic office, citing Art. 33 VTS: passenger cars (light and heavy) are first inspected five years, but no later than six years, after first registration, then after three years, then every two years. Vans (Lieferwagen) follow a different interval: first after four years, then after three, then every two years. https://strassenverkehrsamt.lu.ch/strassenverkehr/fahrzeug/fahrzeugpruefungen/pruefungsintervalle accessed 2026-09-30.
- Canton of Aargau: vehicles with number plates are subject to the official periodic inspection (Art. 33 VTS). For the periodic inspection the licensing authority (Strassenverkehrsamt) sends an invitation in time. https://www.ag.ch/de/smartserviceportal/dienstleistungen?dl=periodische-oder-freiwillige-fahrzeugpruefung-bc817f0e-7aa6-4ba8-9ef7-80d4bb067c5f_de accessed 2026-09-30.
- Fedlex consolidated VTS for the article anchor: https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1995/4425_4425_4425/de#art_33 accessed 2026-09-30. The interval list used in the post is the one published by the Canton of Lucerne, not a private summary.
Vendor / product features used (URL each): WERBAS//KSR as an example of workshop software the reader may already have. Aurum has not built a garage-software integration.
Not verifiable, therefore left out: market share of any DMS in Switzerland; APIs of WinCar, C@risma or similar unless on their own page (not fetched); whether every canton uses the same extra check on a change of keeper for cars older than 10 years (TCS describes cantonal differences; not used); tyre-change volume statistics.
