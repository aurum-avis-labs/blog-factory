# compare-supplier-quotes
Primary query DE / EN: Lieferantenofferten einholen und vergleichen / request and compare supplier quotes
SERP: product pages for purchasing suites and public-tender tools dominate this kind of query. The gap is a private SME office method: one identical request to a few suppliers, unlike replies normalised into one table, missing lines flagged, and the award left with a person. This is buying, not chasing quotes the firm has sent to its own customers.
Verified facts: no external statistic or vendor feature is stated in the post. Public procurement rules (BöB) were not used. They govern public buyers, and this page is for a private SME asking a few suppliers.
Not verifiable, therefore left out: any claim that three quotes are a legal duty for private firms. Award criteria from public procurement. Savings from "always taking the cheapest".
