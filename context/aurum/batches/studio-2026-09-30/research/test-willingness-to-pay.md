# test-willingness-to-pay

## Verified

- Swiss practice: ideas as such cannot be protected; execution and branding can (IGE): https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden
- TWINT available through Stripe for online checkout in Switzerland: https://stripe.com/en-ch/payment-method/twint
- Aurum Product Validation Package structure (weeks 1–12, pivots, Go/Pivot/Stop): internal WRITER-BRIEF and service page positioning, not re-sourced here.

## Not verified

- Whether a specific Van Westendorp or Gabor-Granger threshold applies to Swiss B2B SaaS without citing a study (omitted).
- Legal enforceability of informal «yes I would pay» messages without contract advice (stated as weak signal only).

## Do not claim

- Invented conversion rates, «good» WTP survey percentages, or typical pre-order amounts in CHF.
- That a waitlist or Mom Test interview proves willingness to pay (brief and related posts treat these as weaker than money).
- That one paid pilot proves product-market fit or scalable acquisition.
