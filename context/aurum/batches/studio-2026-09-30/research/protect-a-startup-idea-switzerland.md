# protect-a-startup-idea-switzerland

## Verified

- Ideas as such cannot be protected in Switzerland (IGE): https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden
- Software created by an employee in the course of employment belongs to the employer (Art. 17 URG), with external author copyright until written assignment (Rentsch Partner summary): https://www.rentschpartner.ch/ict-law/schutz-von-software
- Swiss trademark filing from CHF 450 for one class, 10 years, renewal CHF 550 (writer brief; recheck IGE FAQ): https://www.ige.ch/de/etwas-schuetzen/marken/faq
- Product validation package facts from writer brief (weeks 1–12, customer owns code, designs, data, documentation).

## Not verified

- Whether a specific founder NDA template is enforceable in every cantonal court context (article stays at general contractual limits, not legal advice).

## Do not claim

- Calendar `apart`: title uses ss; ideas as such are not protected; IGE link required.
- No claim that NDAs stop competitors from building similar products legally.
- No claim that filing a trademark protects the underlying business model or idea.
- No invented patent success rates or typical Swiss pre-seed round sizes.
