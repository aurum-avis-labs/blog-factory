# get-your-first-100-users

## Verified

- Holist-IQ early signals (internal case HTML in WRITER-BRIEF): 11 signups, 5 sessions over four minutes, 3 return visits, 2 hit free credit limit, no credit purchases; campaign figures on source screenshot (5'293 impressions, 299 clicks, CTR 5.65%, avg CHF 0.65 per click, cost CHF 194.81).
- do4me (client case): 17 students, 4 people posting jobs, mostly friends and family; did not prove marketplace liquidity.
- KitchenCrew: warmest door conversations and events in Zurich; Google Ads CPC in HTML CHF 1.80 in Zurich and Zug; 50 emails in first small test; do not invent restaurant count.
- CitySage: launch, Instagram, Product Hunt, light Google Ads produced attention, not a repeatable acquisition channel; do not publish impression/spend figures.
- Postology: about 10 waitlist signups, no active ads.
- Ideas not protectable: https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden
- Swiss cookies / profiling consent context (EDÖB guide 2025) if mentioning retargeting: brief cites Art. 45c FMG pattern; link only if opened in article (used general wording, no EDÖB URL in MDX).
- PVP offer facts: from WRITER-BRIEF service page summary (12 weeks, Azure/React/NestJS, paid Google and LinkedIn in weeks 5–6, analytics from day one, customer owns code).
