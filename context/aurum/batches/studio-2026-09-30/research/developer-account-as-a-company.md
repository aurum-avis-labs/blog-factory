# developer-account-as-a-company

## Verified

- Apple Developer Program organization enrollment requires legal entity status, D-U-N-S Number, legal binding authority, work email on the organization domain, and a public website; seller name on the App Store is the legal entity: https://developer.apple.com/support/enrollment/
- D-U-N-S Numbers are assigned by Dun & Bradstreet; Apple states they are free in most jurisdictions (not required for government organizations): https://developer.apple.com/support/enrollment/
- Apple Developer Program annual membership fee stated as 99 USD (Enterprise 299 USD), local currency where available: https://developer.apple.com/support/enrollment/
- Contract developers: organization enrolls and adds developers to the team; organization is the seller on the App Store: https://developer.apple.com/support/enrollment/
- Apple App Store Review Guideline 4.2 Minimum Functionality (more than a repackaged website): https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- Google Play closed testing for new personal developer accounts created after 13 November 2023: at least 12 testers, 14 days continuous: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en
- Aurum Product Validation Package includes native wrap ready to submit (from WRITER-BRIEF service facts, not a third-party URL)
