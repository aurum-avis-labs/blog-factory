# concierge-and-wizard-of-oz-mvp

## Verified

- Concierge and wizard-of-oz patterns described in Aurum studio content (`how-to-validate-a-startup-idea`, `startup-idee-validieren`): manual delivery of the promised outcome before automation, to learn whether the result is worth paying for.
- Ideas as such are not protectable under Swiss practice: https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden
- Product validation package scope (writer brief): deployed web app weeks 1–4; positioning, landing page, paid acquisition weeks 5–6; measurement and pivots weeks 7–10; Go, Pivot, or Stop weeks 11–12. Not offered as a three-day customer build.

## Not verified

- Whether any single industry source defines «wizard of oz» and «concierge» as distinct formal terms (treated as overlapping product-discovery labels in this article).
