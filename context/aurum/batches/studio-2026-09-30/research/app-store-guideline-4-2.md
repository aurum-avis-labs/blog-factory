# app-store-guideline-4-2

## Verified

- Apple App Store Review Guidelines, section 4.2 Minimum Functionality: apps should include features, content, and UI that elevate them beyond a repackaged website; 4.2.2 discourages pure marketing sites and web clippings: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- Aurum Inklets: React app wrapped with Capacitor, public at https://inklets.app and `/portfolio/inklets` (internal product, per writer brief)
- Product validation package includes web app plus iOS and Android native wrap, ready to submit (writer brief, service page facts)
- Google Play closed-test rules (12 testers, 14 days) apply to many new personal accounts after 13 November 2023: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en (related topic F03, not central to F02)

## Not verified

- Exact wording Apple uses in every 4.2 rejection email (varies by reviewer and build)
- Whether a specific Capacitor or React Native stack changes reviewer behaviour beyond the published guidelines

## Do not claim

- That every wrapped app passes guideline 4.2 (calendar `apart`: Inklets shipped as Capacitor; Apple 4.2 still rejects a thin website wrapper; do not promise a pass)
- Inklets download counts, review anecdotes, or App Store approval as a guarantee for client projects
- That guideline 4.2 is the only reason an app can be rejected
