# cutting-pilot-scope

## Verified

- KitchenCrew scope decision: inventory layer removed from pilot; pilot concentrates on supplier management and ordering (KitchenCrew case study HTML, Aurum writer brief).
- Pilot surface: suppliers, articles, chat or speech-to-text, order drafts, email, spend tracking (writer brief, HTML).
- Built December 2025; feedback-led pilot since April 2026; free to selected participants in exchange for feedback (HTML).
- Warm-lead path: conversations and events in Zurich stronger than paid ads alone (HTML, writer brief).
- Google Ads in Zurich and Zug: CPC about CHF 1.80; first small email test about 50 messages; ads produced clicks, not enough booked pilot calls yet (HTML, writer brief).
- GoldCrew backbone: shared agent structure adapted for gastronomy vertical (HTML).
- Product validation package phases: weeks 1 to 4 build and deploy; weeks 5 to 12 positioning, landing, paid acquisition, measurement, Go/Pivot/Stop (writer brief, Aurum service page).

## Not verified

- Whether removing inventory is the right cut for every restaurant software pilot (case-specific learning only).
- Optimal number of pilot participants before expanding scope (no fixed external benchmark cited).

## Do not claim

- Any count of restaurants in the KitchenCrew pilot (explicitly forbidden in calendar `apart` field).
- That KitchenCrew proved product-market fit, revenue, or repeatable paid acquisition.
- That cutting scope alone fixes distribution when warm conversations are the stronger door.
