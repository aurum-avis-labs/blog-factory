# innosuisse-start-up-coaching

## Verified

- Innosuisse Initial Coaching: voucher up to CHF 10'000 for coaching sessions with Innosuisse-accredited coaches over up to twelve months; entry to start-up coaching and a prerequisite for possible Core Coaching: https://www.innosuisse.admin.ch/de/initial-coaching-fur-start-up
- Eligibility (same page): innovative, science-based business idea; Swiss company founded within the last five years, or ten in justified cases such as time-intensive life-science start-ups, or intent to build in Switzerland with a Swiss address; scalable model, ambitious team, defensible advantage, high market potential, sustainability criteria in evaluation.
- Application via Innosuisse input portal; should include idea description, value proposition, competitive advantage, team, relevant documents such as a pitch deck.
- Evaluation: formal check, independent expert assessment including a short conversation; Innovationsrat decision; typically three to four weeks.
- Coaching process: lead coach invited via portal; voucher redeemed for accredited coach services; completion requires an evaluation report with milestones within twelve months; optional follow-on application for Core Coaching.
- Ideas as such cannot be protected (brief): https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden

## Not verified

- Current Core Coaching voucher amount or rules (page not fully opened for this draft; article names Core only as next step after Initial).

## Do not claim

- That Innosuisse coaching pays for product development, agency hours, or an MVP build.
- That approval implies investment readiness, Innosuisse project funding, or product-market fit.
- Canton-specific schemes (calendar `apart` on sibling rows only; K02 `apart` is empty).
- Package prices for the twelve-week product validation offer.
