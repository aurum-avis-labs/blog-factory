- do4me client case (Aurum case study HTML, writer brief): Zurich everyday help connecting residents with student helpers; 2.5-day build for landing page, web app, listings, profiles, categories, applications, chat, Google Ads, GTM, Microsoft Clarity, QR/UTM links; then 4 days feedback and 1 day iteration; iOS/Google Play wrap prepared but not the first test
- Early signals: 17 students signed up, 4 job offerers (mostly friends and family); did not prove marketplace liquidity; demand side identified as next bottleneck

- Product validation package facts from writer brief (12-week structure, Go/Pivot/Stop, analytics from day one)
