# test-under-your-brand-or-a-new-one

## Verified

- Ideas as such cannot be protected (IGE): https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden
- Trademark filing CHF 450 for one Nice class, 10 years protection, renewal CHF 550 (recheck before publish): https://www.ige.ch/de/etwas-schuetzen/marken/faq
- Employee software copyright can belong to the employer; external authors keep rights until written assignment (Art. 17 URG context): https://www.rentschpartner.ch/ict-law/schutz-von-software

## Not verified

- Exact IGE fee for each additional Nice class beyond the first
- Whether a specific invented product name is free in the Swiss register without a search
- App Store or Google Play policy outcomes for a test app published under a parent company developer account vs a new account

## Do not claim

- That choosing a new brand proves product-market fit, investor readiness, or trademark clearance
- That a weekend Lovable build under the corporate domain is automatically owned by the company without contract review
- Trademark or legal costs beyond the IGE FAQ figures without rechecking the page
- Download counts, ad benchmarks, or «good» conversion rates for brand tests
