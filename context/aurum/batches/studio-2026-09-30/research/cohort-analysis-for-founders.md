# cohort-analysis-for-founders

## Verified

- Cohort analysis as a product analytics method: standard practice (group users by shared start period, compare behaviour over time). No single canonical URL required; article stays methodological, not vendor-specific.
- Ideas as such cannot be protected (Swiss IGE): https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden
- Aurum product validation package (weeks 7 to 10: cost per lead, conversion, drop-off, pivots): from studio batch WRITER-BRIEF / service page distillation.
- Holist-IQ early on-page signals (11 signups, 5 sessions over four minutes, 3 return visits): from WRITER-BRIEF case HTML, not re-checked on dashboards.

## Not verified

- Whether GA4 «Explorations» cohort templates match every founder’s signup event without custom configuration.
- Minimum cohort size for statistical significance in a Swiss B2B niche (article avoids naming a number).

## Do not claim

- A universal «good» week-one retention rate for MVPs.
- That cohort tables alone prove product-market fit.
- Invented industry retention benchmarks (calendar `apart` empty; same discipline as validation-test-metrics).
