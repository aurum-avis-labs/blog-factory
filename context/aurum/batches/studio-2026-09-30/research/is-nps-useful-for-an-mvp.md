# is-nps-useful-for-an-mvp

## Verified

- Net Promoter Score: respondents rate likelihood to recommend 0 to 10; promoters 9 to 10, passives 7 to 8, detractors 0 to 6; score is percentage promoters minus percentage detractors: https://www.bain.com/consulting-services/customer-strategy-and-marketing/loyalty/net-promoter-score-system/
- Sean Ellis «how disappointed would you be if this product went away» and the 40% «very disappointed» heuristic are discussed in our studio post product-market-fit-how-to-find-it (link only, do not duplicate that article).
- NPS measures stated recommendation intent, not observed retention or revenue.

## Not verified

- A single «good» NPS threshold for early-stage B2B SaaS MVPs (vendor benchmarks vary by category and sample).

## Do not claim

- That NPS alone proves product-market fit (Sean Ellis and PMF behaviour live in product-market-fit-how-to-find-it).
- Invented conversion benchmarks or «target NPS» for pre-seed MVPs.
- That a one-off survey replaces cohort retention or activation events.
