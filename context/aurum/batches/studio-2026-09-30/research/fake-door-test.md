# fake-door-test

## Verified

- Aurum Product Validation Package positions against «landing page with a fake button»; it ships a deployed product instead: `aurum-landing-page/src/data/services/en/mvp-validation.ts` (same text in DE).
- Landing Page Package outcomes include a waitlist or lead form, GA4, and Microsoft Clarity; customer owns source code: `aurum-landing-page/src/data/services/en/landing-page-package.ts`.
- Swiss practice: ideas as such cannot be protected: https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden

## Not verified

- Calendar note that the Landing Page Package service page «refuses a fake button» as explicit copy (not found on `landing-page-package.ts`; article states real forms vs deceptive checkout-only doors instead).
- Universal legal requirement in Switzerland to disclose a fake door after click (treated as trust and measurement hygiene, not law).

## Do not claim

- Good CTR, conversion, or click-through benchmarks for fake doors.
- That a fake door proves willingness to pay, product-market fit, or scalable acquisition.
- That one weekend test replaces interviews or a deployed MVP when behaviour in the product is the open question.
