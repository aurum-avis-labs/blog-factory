# pilot-customers-from-your-network

## Verified

- KitchenCrew pilot: warmest door described as conversations and events in Zurich, not ads (internal portfolio HTML, writer brief).
- do4me early signal: 17 students, 4 people posting jobs, mostly friends and family; that did not prove marketplace liquidity (writer brief, portfolio HTML).
- Holist-IQ: early usage signals from a small signup base, not revenue or product-market fit (writer brief).
- Product validation package structure: weeks 1 to 4 build and deploy, weeks 5 to 12 positioning, paid acquisition, measurement, Go/Pivot/Stop (Aurum service page, writer brief).

## Not verified

- A universal number of pilot customers needed before moving to paid acquisition.
- Whether Swiss founders typically exhaust their network in a fixed number of weeks (no external stat used).

## Do not claim

- That do4me's first job posters (mostly friends and family) proved marketplace liquidity or demand-side repeatability.
- Invented pilot conversion rates, restaurant counts for KitchenCrew, or download figures for any product.
- That network pilots replace cold-channel tests or statistical significance.
