# testing-a-niche-product-before-revenue

## Verified

- Holist-IQ internal case (Aurum case study HTML, early 2025): weekend MVP in React and TypeScript; causal-loop diagramming with constrained agents; Google Ads campaign 5'293 impressions, 299 clicks, 5.65% CTR, CHF 0.65 average CPC, CHF 194.81 total spend; 11 signups; 5 sessions longer than four minutes; 3 return visits; 2 users hit free credit limit; no credit purchases; strongest early interest in modelling political interrelations; mobile landing and credit-purchase friction noted.
- Ideas as such cannot be protected: https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden

## Not verified

- No universal signup or session benchmarks for niche B2B or specialist tools; article avoids invented conversion targets.

## Do not claim

- Product-market fit or revenue proof from the Holist-IQ run.
- That political modelling alone defines a scalable business model.
- Download counts, App Store ratings, or paid conversion rates not in the case source.
