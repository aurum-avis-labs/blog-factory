# customer-interview-questions

## Verified

- Mom Test is a customer-discovery method associated with Rob Fitzpatrick's book *The Mom Test* (questions about past behaviour rather than opinions on your idea): https://www.momtestbook.com/
- Swiss founders often lack a technical co-founder; interviews are a common early step before building (internal studio positioning, no external stat claimed).

## Not verified

- Exact wording of the "three rules" as published on the author's site without re-opening the book page section by section.
- Any universal number of interviews needed before you "know" enough to build.

## Do not claim

- Deeper than how-to-validate-a-startup-idea: do not replace that post's four-method overview or rewrite its landing-page and concierge sections.
- Invented conversion benchmarks, "good" interview counts, or Mom Test endorsement by a named Swiss programme.
- That interviews alone prove willingness to pay or product-market fit.
