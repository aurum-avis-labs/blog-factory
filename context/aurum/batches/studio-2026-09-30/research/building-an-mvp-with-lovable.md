# building-an-mvp-with-lovable

## Verified

- Lovable pairs generated front ends with Supabase for auth and data; row level security must be configured and tested, not assumed: general practice; CVE pattern documented in NVD CVE-2025-48757 (missing or ineffective RLS on some Lovable-generated Supabase projects): https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2025-48757
- Supabase project region can include Zurich (eu-central-2): https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/regions
- Apple App Store guideline 4.2 minimum functionality (not a repackaged website): https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- Google Play closed testing requirement for new personal developer accounts after 13 November 2023 (12 testers, 14 days): https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en
- Aurum does not build customer validation packages with Lovable or Supabase (calendar `apart` field, writer brief).

## Not verified

- Current Lovable export or self-hosting options (not needed for this article; stayed on build-in-platform path).
- Exact Lovable plan limits or pricing (omitted per brief: no invented app prices).

## Do not claim

- That Aurum builds customer products with Lovable (neutral tool article only).
- Download counts, review anecdotes, or store approval guarantees for wrapped apps.
- CPC benchmarks or "good" conversion rates for Lovable MVPs.
