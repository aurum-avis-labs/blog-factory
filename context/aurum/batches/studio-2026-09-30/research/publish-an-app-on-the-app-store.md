# publish-an-app-on-the-app-store

## Verified

- Apple App Store Review Guidelines, including minimum functionality (4.2) and marketing / web clipping (4.2.2): https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- Google Play closed testing for new personal developer accounts (12 testers, 14 days continuous) after 13 November 2023: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en
- Apple Developer Program (enrolment required to distribute on the App Store): https://developer.apple.com/programs/

## Not verified

- Current Apple Developer Program fee in CHF (list price is set on Apple's site; not rechecked for this note)
- Whether every organisation Apple Developer account is exempt from Google Play's closed-test rule (calendar row F03 flags this; not stated in F01)

## Do not claim

- No approval promise (calendar `apart`)
- Do not promise that a Capacitor or thin web wrapper will pass review (brief Inklets / 4.2 line)
- Do not invent review timelines, pass rates, or store conversion benchmarks
