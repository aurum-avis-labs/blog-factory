- Product Hunt is a public launch platform where makers publish a product page and the community upvotes and comments: https://www.producthunt.com/
- CitySage (internal Aurum venture): friends and family used the product; a launch on Instagram, Product Hunt, and light Google Ads produced attention, not a repeatable acquisition channel: `context/aurum/batches/studio-2026-09-30/WRITER-BRIEF.md` (case study section; no dashboard figures published).
- Aurum product validation package weeks 5 to 6 include positioning, landing page, and paid acquisition on Google and LinkedIn, not a Product Hunt campaign as a sold deliverable: `context/aurum/batches/studio-2026-09-30/WRITER-BRIEF.md`.
- Production-quality MVPs as validation context: body link slug `why-we-build-production-quality-mvps`.

- Product Hunt ranking rules, launch-time cut-offs, or maker requirements beyond what is on the public site at time of writing.
- Typical upvote counts, visit spikes, or signup conversion from a Product Hunt launch for B2B or Swiss B2C MVPs.
- Whether Product Hunt still drives meaningful B2B pipeline in 2026 (treated as channel fit, not a statistic).

- CitySage impression, spend, like, or signup figures from Product Hunt, Instagram, or Google Ads.
- That a Product Hunt launch proves product-market fit, retention, willingness to pay, or scalable acquisition.
