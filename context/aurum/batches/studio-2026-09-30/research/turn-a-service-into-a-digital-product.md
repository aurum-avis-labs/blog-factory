# turn-a-service-into-a-digital-product

## Verified

- Ideas as such are not protectable in Switzerland: https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden
- Software created by employees in the course of employment belongs to the employer (Art. 17 URG), external authors retain copyright until written assignment: https://www.rentschpartner.ch/ict-law/schutz-von-software
- Product validation package scope (writer brief): deployed web app weeks 1 to 4; positioning, landing page, paid acquisition weeks 5 to 6; measurement and pivots weeks 7 to 10; Go, Pivot, or Stop weeks 11 to 12; customer owns code, designs, data, documentation.

## Not verified

- No single Swiss SME benchmark for «successful» service-to-product conversion rates (none cited).

## Do not claim

- Calendar `apart` field is empty.
- No invented CPC, conversion, or revenue figures for service firms going digital.
- Digitising internal paperwork alone is not the same as a sellable digital product; article keeps that distinction explicit.
