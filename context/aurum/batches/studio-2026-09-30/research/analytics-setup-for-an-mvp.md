# analytics-setup-for-an-mvp

## Verified

- Google Analytics 4 is the current generation of Google Analytics; setup via gtag or Google Tag Manager is documented by Google: https://developers.google.com/analytics/devguides/collection/ga4
- Microsoft Clarity is a free session-recording and heatmap product from Microsoft: https://clarity.microsoft.com/
- PostHog offers product analytics, feature flags, and optional self-hosting; documented at https://posthog.com/docs
- Swiss cookie practice: EDÖB guidance (2025) on when consent is required vs information and objection under Art. 45c FMG for typical site analytics without cross-site profiling (brief distillation; link if opened: https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/)
- Aurum Product Validation Package includes analytics and session recordings from day one (writer brief, service page summary in brief).

## Not verified

- Whether a specific early-stage MVP must register GA4 under Google's EU/Swiss data terms for a given traffic level (depends on configuration and legal review).
- Exact default events PostHog captures without a custom tracking plan.

## Do not claim

- That Aurum installs PostHog as part of the sold package unless the service page explicitly says so (calendar `apart`: package uses analytics and session recordings from day one; do not claim PostHog is what Aurum installs).
- Good or benchmark conversion rates, CPC, or sign-up rates for MVPs.
- That analytics alone proves product-market fit or revenue.
