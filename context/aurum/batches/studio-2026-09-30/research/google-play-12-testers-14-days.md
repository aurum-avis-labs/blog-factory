# google-play-12-testers-14-days

## Verified

- Personal Google Play Console accounts created after 13 November 2023 must run a closed test before production distribution; Production and Pre-registration stay disabled until testing requirements are met: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en
- Closed test minimum: at least 12 testers opted in continuously for at least 14 days when applying for production access: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en
- Internal testing has no minimum tester count; closed testing requires app setup complete: same URL
- Application for production access via Dashboard in Play Console; review usually seven days or less, can take longer: same URL
- Testers who opt out before 14 consecutive days do not count; re-opting in restarts the 14-day window: same URL
- Apple guideline 4.2 minimum functionality (separate from Google): https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/

## Not verified

- Whether organisation (company) Play Console accounts are exempt from the 12/14 closed-test rule (calendar `apart` field; help page title refers to personal accounts only)

## Do not claim

- That organisation accounts are automatically exempt without checking Play Console for that account type
- Download counts, review anecdotes, or that every wrapped app passes store review
- Specific Google Play developer account fees (not opened for this article)
