# paying-a-partner-in-equity

## Verified

- Ideas as such cannot be protected (IGE): https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden
- Employee software belongs to the employer; external author keeps copyright until written assignment (Art. 17 URG), Rentsch Partner summary: https://www.rentschpartner.ch/ict-law/schutz-von-software
- GmbH minimum share capital CHF 20'000, fully paid at formation: https://www.kmu.admin.ch/de/gmbh-haftung-stammkapital-gruendung
- Aurum Product Validation Package: customer owns code, designs, data, documentation; payment may be cash, equity, or mix (service page and writer brief, not a statute)
- Venture studio vs agency framing on aurum site: `brands/aurum/en/venture-studio-vs-agency.mdx`

## Not verified

- Typical equity ranges for Swiss dev shops or studios (brief forbids inventing splits)
- Whether a given GmbH or AG structure is right for a specific equity-for-services deal (needs counsel)

## Do not claim

- Any percentage split for cash versus equity (calendar `apart` field)
- That equity replaces product validation or proves market demand
- Standard vesting terms as if they were law
- Round sizes or valuation benchmarks for pre-seed equity grants
