# swiss-startup-communities

## Verified

- Venturelab describes itself as designing and operating flagship startup programmes in Switzerland, including Venture Kick, Venture Leaders, the TOP 100 Swiss Startup Award, and Innosuisse Start-up Trainings; it states that 90% of Swiss startups have been supported by Venturelab: https://www.venturelab.ch/
- Startupticker.ch is an online platform for young entrepreneurs, innovators and supporters; Innosuisse is named as principal of the platform; the Startupticker Foundation is mandated by Innosuisse to publish news and information on funding opportunities: https://www.startupticker.ch/en/about
- Swiss Startup Association (SSA) describes political advocacy, education, community support, a Startup Desk, network and events, and flagship event Startup Nights in Winterthur: https://swissstartupassociation.ch/
- Innosuisse is the Swiss Innovation Agency; it offers funding and support including accompaniment for start-ups from founding through growth and internationalisation: https://www.innosuisse.admin.ch/de
- Innosuisse Initial Coaching: voucher up to CHF 10'000, up to 12 months, for start-ups up to 5 years (life science 10) with an innovative, science-based idea: https://www.innosuisse.admin.ch/de/initial-coaching-fur-start-up
- Ideas as such cannot be protected (IGE): https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden
