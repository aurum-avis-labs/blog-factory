# find-honest-beta-testers

## Verified

- Google Play closed testing for new personal developer accounts (created after 13 November 2023): at least 12 testers, 14 continuous days: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en
- Apple TestFlight: external builds can require Beta App Review; builds expire after 90 days (Apple developer documentation, cited in studio post testflight-beta-testing).
- Mom Test-style behaviour focus (past use, not compliments) aligns with customer-interview-questions in the same wave; no separate stat claimed for "honest beta".

## Not verified

- Any universal minimum cohort size that "proves" enough for Go/Stop without context.
- Channel-specific response rates for beta recruitment posts in Switzerland.

## Do not claim

- Invented conversion benchmarks, "good" beta retention rates, or fake testimonial quotes.
- That a friends-and-family beta proves acquisition or product-market fit.
- Download counts or review anecdotes for Inklets or other portfolio products unless from approved case HTML (not required for H02).
