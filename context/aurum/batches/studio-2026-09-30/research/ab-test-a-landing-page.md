# ab-test-a-landing-page

## Verified

- One-variable A/B tests on landing pages are standard product practice; Google documents GA4 comparisons and experiments in its help centre: https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9267735
- Swiss cookie and tracking context for analytics on a marketing page: information and objection unless profiling, retargeting, or similar triggers consent (Art. 45c FMG); EDÖB guide 2025 cited in studio WRITER-BRIEF
- Internal method allowed by calendar `apart`: Prozess-Check page ran a hook headline A/B test with three variants and a GA4 funnel; no performance numbers to publish (RESEARCH.md section 4)
- Landing page package facts from WRITER-BRIEF: GA4, Microsoft Clarity, customer owns code, demand test surface

## Not verified

- Whether a specific third-party A/B tool (Optimizely, VWO, etc.) is the best default for Swiss founders at a given traffic level; article stays tool-neutral with GA4 as the common baseline

## Do not claim

- Conversion rate benchmarks or minimum sample sizes presented as universal rules
- Prozess-Check hook test results or impression, click, or signup figures
- That an A/B winner proves product-market fit, payment willingness, or retention
