# app-store-privacy-labels

## Verified

- Apple App Privacy details (privacy nutrition labels) are declared in App Store Connect before submission; Apple documents categories, data types, and linking to privacy policy: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/
- Apple states developers must identify all data the app or third-party partners collect, unless the data meets Apple's narrow «optional disclosure» criteria: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/
- Google Play Data safety form asks what data is collected, shared, and how it is handled; developer documentation: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469
- Google requires the Data safety section to be accurate and kept in sync with the app; users see the form on the store listing.
- Swiss FADP transparency duties (Art. 19) sit with the controller; store forms are not a substitute for a privacy notice (brief distillation; statute: https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de).
- Aurum Product Validation Package includes analytics from day one (writer brief); session replay may appear in real MVPs (companion article in batch).

## Not verified

- Whether Apple or Google rejected a specific Swiss startup in 2026 solely for a mismatch between labels and SDK behaviour (case-by-case; do not invent examples).
- Exact mapping of every Firebase or Supabase default event to Apple's data-type taxonomy without reading the project's SDK list.

- That filling store privacy forms satisfies Swiss law by itself.
- That Aurum's package includes a legal review of your labels (orientation only).
