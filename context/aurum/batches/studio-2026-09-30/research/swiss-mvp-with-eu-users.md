# swiss-mvp-with-eu-users

## Verified

- GDPR Art. 3(2) extraterritorial scope (offer goods/services to people in the EU or monitor behaviour there): https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R0679
- Swiss FADP (nDSG) applies to processing with effect in Switzerland: https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de
- KMU portal plain-language bridge on EU GDPR for Swiss firms: https://www.kmu.admin.ch/de/eu-regelung-zum-datenschutz-neue-eu-verordnung
- Supabase region Zurich (eu-central-2) selectable: https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/regions
- EDÖB 2025 cookies guide (consent when profiling high risk, specially protected data, third-party use, cross-site tracking; otherwise information and objection under Art. 45c FMG): cited in studio WRITER-BRIEF; re-check edoeb.admin.ch before print
- Aurum security articles on scope (calendar crossLinks): `/security/blog/does-the-gdpr-apply-to-swiss-companies`, `/de/security/blog/gilt-die-dsgvo-in-der-schweiz`
