# building-a-saas-inside-an-sme

## Verified

- Software created by an employee in the course of employment belongs to the employer (Art. 17 URG), via Rentsch summary: https://www.rentschpartner.ch/ict-law/schutz-von-software
- External author copyright on software generally remains with the author until a written assignment (Urheberrechtsabtretung), same source
- Ideas as such cannot be protected (IGE): https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden
- GmbH: at least CHF 20'000 share capital, fully paid in at formation: https://www.kmu.admin.ch/de/gmbh-haftung-stammkapital-gruendung
- Product validation package: customer owns code, designs, data, and documentation (writer brief, service page facts)
- KitchenCrew (internal venture): pilot built on existing backbone, inventory removed for pilot scope; warm acquisition via Zurich conversations and events, not proven repeatable ads channel (writer brief case study rules, no invented restaurant counts)

## Not verified

- Typical Swiss KMU IT budget share for a spin-out product; article avoids invented franc bands
- Whether every SME should form a separate legal entity before a pilot; article presents as a decision, not a rule

## Do not claim

- That a successful internal pilot proves SaaS scalability or product-market fit
- That Art. 17 URG alone covers all contractor or agency deliverables without contract review
- Package price or three-day benchmark as a customer promise
