# testflight-beta-testing

## Verified

- TestFlight overview: external testers up to 10,000, internal testers up to 100 App Store Connect users, builds testable for up to 90 days, first external build may require App Review: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/testflight-overview
- Apple Developer Program required to distribute on the App Store (and TestFlight via App Store Connect): https://developer.apple.com/programs/
- App Store Review Guidelines minimum functionality 4.2 and marketing or web clipping 4.2.2: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- Google Play closed testing for new personal developer accounts (12 testers, 14 days continuous) after 13 November 2023: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en

## Not verified

- Typical calendar time for TestFlight Beta App Review on first external build (Apple does not publish a fixed SLA in the overview page)
- Current Apple Developer Program annual fee in CHF (set on Apple's enrolment pages; not rechecked here)

## Do not claim

- No invented conversion rates, tester counts, or «successful beta» benchmarks
- No promise that a thin web wrap passes 4.2 or external beta review
- No approval guarantee for App Store submission after a smooth TestFlight phase
