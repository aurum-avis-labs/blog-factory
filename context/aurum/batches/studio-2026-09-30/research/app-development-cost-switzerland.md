# app-development-cost-switzerland

## Verified

- Apple Developer Program annual membership stated as 99 USD (Enterprise 299 USD), local currency where available: https://developer.apple.com/support/enrollment/
- Apple organisation enrollment requires legal entity, D-U-N-S, binding authority, work email on organisation domain, public website; seller name is the legal entity: same URL
- Apple App Store Review Guideline 4.2 minimum functionality (more than a repackaged website); 4.2.2 not pure marketing or web clipping: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- Google Play closed testing for new personal developer accounts created after 13 November 2023: at least 12 testers opted in continuously for at least 14 days before production access: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en
- GmbH minimum share capital CHF 20'000, fully paid in at formation: https://www.kmu.admin.ch/de/gmbh-haftung-stammkapital-gruendung
- Trademark filing CHF 450 one class, ten years; renewal CHF 550: https://www.ige.ch/de/etwas-schuetzen/marken/faq (recheck before print)
- TWINT available through Stripe: https://stripe.com/en-ch/payment-method/twint
- Supabase region Zurich (eu-central-2) selectable: https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/regions
- Employee software belongs to employer (Art. 17 URG); external author keeps copyright until written assignment: https://www.rentschpartner.ch/ict-law/schutz-von-software
- VAT duty from CHF 100'000 turnover (brief: do not expand without ESTV page)
