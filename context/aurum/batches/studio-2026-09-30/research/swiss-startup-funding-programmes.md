# swiss-startup-funding-programmes

## Verified

- Innosuisse Initial Coaching: voucher up to CHF 10'000, up to 12 months, for start-ups up to five years old (life science up to ten) with an innovative, science-based idea: https://www.innosuisse.admin.ch/de/initial-coaching-fur-start-up
- Venture Kick: Swiss start-up support programme; official site describes staged funding (confirm current amounts on site): https://www.venturekick.ch/
- Ideas as such cannot be protected (IGE): https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden
- GmbH minimum capital CHF 20'000, fully paid in at formation: https://www.kmu.admin.ch/de/gmbh-haftung-stammkapital-gruendung
- Swiss venture capital market 2025: CHF 2.95 billion invested, up 24% on 2024 (CMS note on Swiss Venture Capital Report 2026): use only as context that institutional capital exists, not as a promise of round size

## Not verified

- Current Venture Kick stage amounts (40'000 / 60'000 / 50'000) on venturekick.ch at time of writing; article uses «up to CHF 150'000» unless stages are re-checked on the live page.
- Any canton- or city-specific grant scheme not opened from an official page during writing.

## Do not claim

- Canton or regional funding programmes without re-opening the official page (calendar `apart`).
- Invented pre-seed or seed round sizes, «typical» angel cheques, or CPC-style benchmarks for grant success.
- That a voucher or grant replaces product validation, proves demand, or satisfies investors on its own.
- Innosuisse or Venture Kick eligibility for a reader who only has a marketing idea with no innovation or science link.
