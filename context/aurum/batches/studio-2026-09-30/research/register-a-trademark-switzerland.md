# register-a-trademark-switzerland

## Verified

- Ideas as such cannot be protected under Swiss IP law (IGE guide): https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden
- IGE trademark FAQ states filing fee CHF 450 for one class, protection period 10 years, renewal CHF 550 (per WRITER-BRIEF; recheck before publish): https://www.ige.ch/de/etwas-schuetzen/marken/faq
- Swiss trademarks are filed with the Swiss Federal Institute of Intellectual Property (IGE / IPI): https://www.ige.ch/de/etwas-schuetzen/marken

## Not verified

- Exact fee for each additional Nice class beyond the first (IGE fee schedule not opened in this session)
- Current processing times from filing to registration
- Whether a specific product name is free in the register (case-by-case search)

## Do not claim

- IGE fees other than CHF 450 (one class, 10 years) and renewal CHF 550 without rechecking the IGE FAQ page (calendar `apart`)
- That trademark registration proves product-market fit, investor readiness, or App Store approval
- International protection from a Swiss filing alone without Madrid Protocol or national filings elsewhere
- Lawyer or IGE service package prices not on the IGE FAQ
