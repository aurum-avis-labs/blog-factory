# using-existing-customers-as-testers

## Verified

- Ideas as such cannot be protected under Swiss law: https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden
- Employee-created software belongs to the employer (Art. 17 URG), external author keeps copyright until written assignment: https://www.rentschpartner.ch/ict-law/schutz-von-software

## Not verified

- No single Swiss statistic on how many SMEs run beta programmes with existing customers; article avoids invented benchmarks.

## Do not claim

- That customer testers prove product-market fit or repeatable acquisition.
- Download counts, conversion benchmarks, or CPC figures for customer-beta programmes.
- That every friendly tester session predicts paid uptake from strangers.
