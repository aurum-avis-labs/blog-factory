# event-tracking-plan-for-an-mvp

## Verified

- Aurum product validation package includes analytics and session recordings from day one; weeks 7 to 10 focus on cost per lead, conversion, drop-off, and up to three pivot iterations (WRITER-BRIEF / service page distillation).
- Swiss cookie and profiling practice: consent when profiling is high risk, for specially protected data, when data is passed to a third party for their own purposes, and for cross-site tracking and retargeting; otherwise information and a way to object (Art. 45c FMG). EDÖB guide 2025 cited in WRITER-BRIEF; not opened for this draft.
- Ideas as such cannot be protected (Swiss IGE): https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden

## Not verified

- Whether GA4 recommended events alone cover a typical B2B MVP funnel without custom event names.
- Current default data retention settings in GA4 for new properties (article stays tool-agnostic).

## Do not claim

- A universal list of events every MVP must track.
- Benchmark conversion or activation rates readers should hit.
- That a tracking plan replaces legal advice on analytics consent in Switzerland.
