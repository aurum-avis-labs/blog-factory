# test-a-b2c-idea-with-meta-ads

## Verified

- Meta Ads run across Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and the Audience Network via Meta Ads Manager: https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1601637673431817
- Apple App Store guideline 4.2 (minimum functionality, not a repackaged website): https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- Swiss cookie and tracking: consent for high-risk profiling, cross-site tracking and retargeting; otherwise information and objection (Art. 45c FMG). EDÖB guide 2025: https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/edoeb/de/home/datenschutz/themen/cookies.html
- Holist-IQ internal validation campaign (brief source HTML): 5'293 impressions, 299 clicks, CTR 5.65%, average CHF 0.65 per click, cost CHF 194.81. Used only as internal precedent in writer brief, not repeated as a benchmark in the article.
- Product validation package facts: from WRITER-BRIEF.md (weeks 1 to 12, Azure/React/NestJS, paid Google and LinkedIn in package; Meta is a common founder choice outside that default).
