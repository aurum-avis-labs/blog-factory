# gmbh-vs-ag-swiss-startup

## Verified

- GmbH minimum Stammkapital CHF 20'000, fully liberated at formation (Art. 773 OR): https://www.kmu.admin.ch/de/gmbh-haftung-stammkapital-gruendung
- AG minimum Aktienkapital CHF 100'000; at least 20% liberated, minimum CHF 50'000 at formation (OR 621-622, 632): https://www.kmu.admin.ch/de/rechtsform-aktiengesellschaft-ag
- Employee-created software belongs to the employer (Art. 17 URG); external author copyright until written assignment: https://www.rentschpartner.ch/ict-law/schutz-von-software
- Ideas as such cannot be protected: https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden
- Apple organisation developer enrollment expects a legal entity: https://developer.apple.com/support/enrollment/

## Not verified

- Exact notary and register fees for a given canton on a given day (ranges on KMU pages are indicative, not quoted here as fixed prices).
- Whether a specific investor or bank will accept only a GmbH or only an AG for a given round (case by case).

## Do not claim

- That choosing GmbH or AG validates product demand or replaces an MVP test.
- Invented pre-seed round sizes or that AG is always required for venture capital.
- AG capital figures without the KMU source (use the linked AG page only).
- Legal advice on tax residency, holding structures, or cross-border shareholders.
