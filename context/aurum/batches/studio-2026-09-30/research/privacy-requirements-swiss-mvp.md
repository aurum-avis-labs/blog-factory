# privacy-requirements-swiss-mvp

## Verified

- Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP / DSG) applies to processing of personal data; controller duties include transparency and security appropriate to risk: https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de
- Art. 25 FADP: right to information (access) for data subjects; product teams must know what the app stores when someone exercises it: https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de
- Art. 19 FADP: processor must process only on documented instructions; written contract or equivalent required: https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de
- EDÖB guide on cookies and similar technologies (2025): no general opt-in for all cookies; consent in defined cases including cross-site tracking and retargeting; otherwise information and objection under Art. 45c FMG: https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/de/cookies-und-andere-tracking-technologien
- Supabase platform regions include Zurich (eu-central-2): https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/regions
- Apple App Store privacy nutrition labels and Google Play Data safety are store disclosures; separate from FADP compliance but required for distribution: Apple and Google developer documentation (general obligation, not quoted article-by-article in post).
- Ideas as such are not protectable under Swiss IP law (context for «we only have an idea» founders): https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden
