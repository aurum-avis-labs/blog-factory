# ad-budget-to-validate-an-idea

## Verified

- Holist-IQ internal campaign (writer brief, case HTML): 5'293 impressions, 299 clicks, CTR 5.65 percent, average CHF 0.65 per click, total cost CHF 194.81; early product signals (11 signups, 5 sessions over four minutes, 3 return visits, 2 hit free credit limit, no credit purchases). Friction: mobile landing page, unclear credit purchase.
- Product validation package (writer brief): weeks 5 to 6 include landing page and paid acquisition on Google and LinkedIn; weeks 7 to 10 measure cost per lead, conversion, drop-off; up to three pivot iterations.
- Landing page package (writer brief): demand page, GA4 and Microsoft Clarity, customer owns code.
- CitySage (writer brief): Google Ads used; do not publish impression, spend, like, or signup figures.
- KitchenCrew (writer brief): Google Ads CPC CHF 1.80 in Zurich and Zug in HTML; 50 emails in a first small test; ads produced clicks, not enough booked pilot calls yet. Do not invent restaurant count.
- Ideas not protectable as such: https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden
- Swiss cookies (writer brief, EDÖB 2025): no general opt-in; consent in defined cases; otherwise information and objection (Art. 45c FMG).
