# fixed-price-vs-time-and-materials

## Verified

- Aurum Product Validation Package: weeks 1 to 4 deployed product (Azure, React, NestJS); weeks 5 to 12 positioning, acquisition, measurement, pivots, Go/Pivot/Stop recommendation; customer owns code, designs, data, documentation; payment cash, equity, or mix discussed in discovery, no percentages (writer brief, service page facts)
- Payment models for external development are contract terms, not statutory defaults; copyright and handover remain separate (see who-owns-the-code research, Art. 17 URG via Rentsch)
- Swiss MVP cost context and partner types are covered in existing post mvp-cost-switzerland (agency hourly bands cited there; this article does not repeat franc tables)

## Not verified

- Whether a specific Swiss court template applies to software change orders; article stays on commercial practice, not case law
- Average overrun percentages for fixed-price IT projects in Switzerland (omit figures)

## Do not claim

- A price for the twelve-week Product Validation Package (calendar `apart`: No package price.)
- That fixed price always costs less or more than time and materials in francs
- That either model guarantees on-time delivery or product-market fit
