# measure-activation-and-retention

## Verified

- Holist-IQ early product signals (11 signups, five sessions over four minutes, three return visits, two hit free credit limit, no credit purchases): studio batch WRITER-BRIEF / case HTML, not re-checked on dashboards.
- Ideas as such cannot be protected (Swiss IGE): https://www.ige.ch/de/uebersicht-geistiges-eigentum/ein-leitfaden-fuer-innovative-und-kreative/idee/koennen-ideen-geschuetzt-werden
- Aurum product validation package weeks 7 to 10 (cost per lead, conversion, drop-off, pivots): WRITER-BRIEF / service page distillation.
- Swiss analytics and cookies: information and objection paths under Art. 45c FMG; higher-risk profiling and cross-site tracking may need consent (EDÖB guide 2025, cited in brief, not opened for this draft).

## Not verified

- Whether GA4 «retention» reports match cohort definitions founders use without BigQuery or export.
- Platform-specific default «activation» presets in mobile SDKs for every stack.

## Do not claim

- Universal «good» D7 or D30 retention percentages for Swiss B2B or B2C apps.
- That activation plus retention alone equals product-market fit.
- Invented benchmark conversion or retention targets (calendar `apart` empty; still avoid benchmarks).
