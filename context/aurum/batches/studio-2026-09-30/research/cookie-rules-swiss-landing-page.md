# cookie-rules-swiss-landing-page

## Verified

- Switzerland has no general opt-in for every cookie on a website. Consent is required when profiling is high risk, for specially protected personal data, when data are passed to a third party for their own purposes, and for cross-site tracking and retargeting. Otherwise information and a way to object apply under Art. 45c FMG (writer brief, EDÖB guidance 2025): https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/edoeb/de/home/datenschutz/themen/cookies.html
- Art. 45c FMG (Federal Act on Radio and Television): https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/860_862_863/de#art_45_c
- FADP privacy notice duties for processing personal data (analytics, forms): Art. 19 FADP https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de
- Aurum landing page package: demand test page, GA4 and Microsoft Clarity, customer owns code (writer brief; service page not re-opened)
- Related studio posts on analytics cookies: `brands/aurum/en/analytics-setup-for-an-mvp.mdx`, `brands/aurum/en/session-recordings-and-swiss-privacy.mdx`
