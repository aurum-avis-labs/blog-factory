- Apple App Store Review Guideline 4.2 (minimum functionality; more than a repackaged website) and 4.2.2 (not a pure marketing app or web clipping): https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- Google Play: new personal developer accounts created after 13 November 2023 need a closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 days continuous before production access: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en
- Inklets: React app wrapped with Capacitor, public site https://inklets.app and portfolio `/portfolio/inklets` (from WRITER-BRIEF.md inklets section)
- do4me client project: iOS and Google Play wrap prepared; wrap was not the first validation test (from WRITER-BRIEF.md do4me section)
